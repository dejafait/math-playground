# Two-shift variation source review: retained working record

2026-10-30. Literature-only; no lemma, propagation formula or script.
The exact target is in
[the assessment](literature/2026-10-30-weil-two-shift-cusp-variation-coverage.md).

Read L520's complete inverse/localization argument and L521's complete
closure and recorded audit. L520 (15) retains newly translated forcing
inside a bounded remainder; it does not identify each deeper cusp.
L521 (17)–(20) retains all shifts/collisions in one fixed partition and
obtains theta<=1/2. An iteration of upper norm estimates supplies no
independent signed variation lower bound. This is not an inconsistency
between the two results and does not establish that the conditional
nonzero-trace nullvector exists.

Primary comparison completed so far: Chang-Lara–Saldaña v3 Theorem 4.12,
Lemma 4.14 (4.13)–(4.14), Corollaries 4.16–4.17 and Section 6(1);
Galerne Section 2 Theorem 1 and proof pp.951–953; Biswas et al. v1
Theorems 1.1–1.2 and their stated dimension restriction. Their roles
are upper regularity, the actual difference-quotient criterion, and
a stronger-looking but inapplicable regularity lead, respectively.

New inspected propagation lead: Wang–Yu–Zhang arXiv:2502.19262v1,
(1.1), Theorem 2.1/Remark 2.2, Theorem 4.1 and Section 5.1,
especially (5.2)–(5.4) and the converse regularity argument. The
one-delay parabolic theorem tracks Sobolev order, which improves
with each forward delay. It does not give a variation lower bound
for this stationary two-sided logarithmic operator. Publisher access
failed once; the primary arXiv text was accessible, so this is not
an essential source blocker.

Disposition to record: park the iteration-only implementation, without
asserting summability of its uncomputed signed path profiles. Reopening
needs a new sign/noncancellation mechanism and a controlled depth
remainder; merely counting dense images or iterating L521's upper bound
is insufficient. Preserve the exact assessed target and all old stops.
The independent, unaudited L524 domain/sign argument can be nominated
for a separate conceptual-audit source gate; do not perform that audit
or assume its conclusion in this review.
