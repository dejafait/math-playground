# Global variation source review: retained working notes

2026-10-30, literature-only. No BV assertion or new correlation formula
has been derived. The full fixed signed measure and L520 hypotheses
remain in force. Main gap: L516's eigenvalue-relative signed bound;
global variation would only be an intermediate regularity input.

Inspected primary sources:

- Kulczycki--Ryznar, arXiv:1307.7158v1 (26 July 2013), Theorem 1.1,
  assumptions (A), Definition 2.1, Corollary 1.8, Proposition 6.7 and
  its proof, Example 7.2. The geometric-stable example is covered,
  but the theorem's harmonic/nonnegative hypotheses do not match
  L516's signed inhomogeneous equation. Its Green estimate is
  off diagonal, not an assertion that arbitrary bounded forcing
  produces a globally BV solution.
- Bruno Galerne, *Random fields of bounded variation and computation
  of their variation intensity*, Adv. Appl. Probab. 48 (2016),
  947--971, doi:10.1017/apr.2016.60, Lemma 1 and Theorem 1 with
  proofs, pp.951--953. This is deterministic in Section 2: an L1
  function has finite directional variation iff its translation
  difference quotients have finite liminf L1 norm; in that case
  their signed measures converge weak-* to the derivative measure.
  It does not establish this bound for the present nullvector.
