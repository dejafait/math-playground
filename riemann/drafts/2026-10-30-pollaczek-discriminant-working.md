For clarity, the exact selection can be reproduced without a search.
For s=r^2 and y=s z, divide the cubic by its leading coefficient after
this substitution. The resulting monic polynomial is

    Q_s(y)=y^3-(30+125s/2)y^2
           +(360+510s+2401s^2/4)y
           -(720/q+540s+945s^2/2+3465s^3/8).

Precisely Q_s(y)=(-720/q)P_r(y/s). Translate y=X+10+125s/6.
Then Q_s becomes X^3-H(s)X+J(s), with

    H(s)=-60+740s+4211s^2/6,
    J(s)=1600-720/q-440s-29660s^2/3-162335s^3/27.

Its discriminant is 4H(s)^3-27J(s)^2. Near s=2/25, the choice
720/q=1500 makes J small while H>0; this suggests q=12/25.
The rational radius 9/32 lies near sqrt(2/25), while 1/3 is a
larger radius. L530 certifies the signs at these two exact rational
