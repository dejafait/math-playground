    Psi(s)=C exp(-gamma s^2+delta s)

    0<gamma+sum_j delta_j^2<infinity.

### Primary forward-convolution comparison

Alexander Belton, Dominique Guillot, Apoorva Khare and Mihai Putinar,
*Hirschman–Widder densities*,
[arXiv:2101.02129v2, 9 April2022](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.02129v2),
Theorem1.2 pp1–2, states the one-sided characterization with

    Psi(s)=C exp(delta s) product_j(1+alpha_j s),
    C>0, delta>=0, alpha_j>=0,
    0<sum_j alpha_j<infinity.

Read Proposition2.1 and its entire proof pp6–7. For m>=2 and nonzero
real parameters, it constructs the density by convolution of one-sided
exponentials (reflected for negative parameters). The proof uses their
transforms, convolution closure, Fourier inversion for uniqueness,
and a separate support case. This is forward convolution; it supplies
no division operation or actual-theta factor. No gamma quotient is
identified with a kernel here. One-sided support is an extra condition,
and this genus-zero product is narrower than step647's genus-one class.
