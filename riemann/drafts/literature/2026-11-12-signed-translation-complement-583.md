1. [Oleksiy Karlovych and Eugene Shargorodsky, *Lower estimates for the norm
and the Kuratowski measure of noncompactness of Wiener-Hopf type operators*,
arXiv:2509.20296v2, 28 April 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.20296v2),

compression framework is relevant here; its norm lower theorem is not
imported. It supplies no fixed-cosine restriction, matrix sign, or boundary

2. [Rupert L. Frank, Elliott H. Lieb and Robert Seiringer,
