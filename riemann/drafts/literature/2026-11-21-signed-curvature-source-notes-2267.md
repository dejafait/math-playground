## Three primary candidates compared

1. P.J. Forrester, *Meet Andreief, Bordeaux1886, and Andreev,
Kharkov1882-83*, arXiv:1806.10411v1,27June2018 (internal date28June),
equation(1.7), printed p2; Section2.2 complete proof, p4;
Section2.4 equation(2.4), p5: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.10411 .
Read the determinant expansion, permutation symmetry, transposition
and row integration. The formula has the N! normalization and does
not impose positivity on the functions. Absolute integrability of
the expanded products is needed here for Fubini. Its two-by-two
identity does not identify our three-derivative amplitude automatically
and supplies no sign for oscillatory weights. Only the standard
determinant integration tool is available; no Gram representation
of the desired expression is imported.

2. D.K. Dimitrov and Y. Xu, *Wronskians of Fourier and Laplace
Transforms*, arXiv:1606.05011v1,16June2016 (retrieved text has internal
date21November2021), definitions pp2-3, Lemma2.1 p4, Theorems2.5-2.6
and proof pp5-6: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.05011 . Reused2234 and
read these exact ranges. Theorem2.5 assumes f in L1 with absolute
moment of order2n-2 finite; its correlation formula differentiates
the transform frequency. Theorem2.6 assumes an even nonnegative
measure with moments through order2n-1 finite and concludes positive
definiteness, not pointwise positivity. Our
derivatives are translations in u and the sine weight changes sign.
Neither conclusion supplies the required sign. The parsed Lemma2.1
normalization and displayed Fourier exponents also warrant checking
before any exact import; screenshots returned no inspectable image.
No normalization, strictness or identity from this source is used.

3. E.O. Tuck, *On positivity of Fourier transforms*, Bull.Austral.
Math.Soc.74(2006),133-138, pp133-135, equations(1)-(4), especially
the complete successive-half-period proof p134:
https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/D232B9DEB4819E4D2FF17E8162B8B066/S0004972700047511a.pdf/on_positivity_of_fourier_transforms.pdf .
Reused2257/2260 and rechecked convergence/endpoint assumptions and
the proof. A positive decreasing amplitude gives positive sine
integral by comparing consecutive lobes. It is not a theorem for
our curvature amplitude. Pairing full periods is a candidate way
to retain cancellations; translation derivatives, the double integral
and its period decomposition must still be justified. Parsed equation4
is imperfect; the complete prose proof and prior inspected coverage
are used, not a claimed visual verification.

For dominated differentiation and absolute Fubini, reuse2260's
already inspected standard inputs: K.Conrad, *Differentiating under
the integral sign*, Theorem12.3,p16,
https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/analysis/diffunderint.pdf ;
T.Tao, *245B, notes0*,1January2009, Theorem3(4)-(5), Theorem4(1)-(2),
https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2009/01/01/245b-notes-0-a-quick-review-of-measure-and-integration-theory/ .
Conrad refers out for the proof; Tao omits general proofs. The later
specialization must supply local integrable derivative majorants,
absolute product bounds and summable full-period bounds. Signed
integrands do not qualify for nonnegative Tonelli by themselves.
