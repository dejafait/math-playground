   [Section 2.3 Theorem 1, pp. 381–382](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/epdf/10.1137/23M1616406).
   For a domain D in R^2, f locally Lipschitz in its second variable,
   and differentiable u,v on J=[t_0,t_0+tau] with graphs in D, the
   hypotheses are u(t_0)<=v(t_0) and

   proof, including the local Lipschitz neighborhood, integrating
