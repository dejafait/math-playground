**Primary obstruction.** Min-Hee Kim and Young-One Kim, *On the
Pólya-Wiman properties of Differential Operators*,
[arXiv:1506.00350v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1506.00350v1), 1 June 2015,
Theorem 1.3 (PDF p.3) and Section 3, including Lemmas 3.1--3.3,
their corollaries and the final construction. PDF pp.13--14 were also
inspected for the three inductive properties and (3.7)--(3.8), which
were incompletely displayed in HTML. For any real formal symbol phi
outside LP there exists a genus-zero LP function f such that every
positive iterate phi(D)^m f is defined and has infinitely many nonreal
zeros, counted with multiplicity. The proof constructs finite products
with negative real zeros, retains roots in disjoint closed nonreal
disks, controls coefficient bounds and operator convergence, and proves
the limit is nonzero. The LP characterization used there is cited from
Pólya; its original proof was not audited here. This rules out treating
an arbitrary non-LP exceptional differential factor as having a bounded
cost on arbitrary LP inputs. It does **not** identify those inputs with
G(D)x^d for the actual sector product, nor refute a restricted root-loss
law. No special polynomial symbol or new counterexample is derived.

**Finite-degree interface.** Thomas Craven and George Csordas,
*Location of zeros. Part I: Real polynomials and entire functions*,
Illinois J. Math. **27**(2) (1983), 243--277,
[primary journal scan](https://scispace.com/pdf/location-of-zeros-part-i-real-polynomials-and-entire-z3v2jd50yw.pdf),
pp.257--261, Theorems 3.3--3.4, their proofs and Corollary 3.8.
Walsh's Theorem 3.3 localizes convolution zeros in translates of a
circular region; its ensuing remark proves preservation when both
inputs are real rooted. Theorem 3.4 assumes nonnegative factorial
coefficients, relates an n-sequence to hyperbolicity of phi(D)f for
real-rooted f of degree at most n, and handles vanishing coefficients
by a positive perturbation and Hurwitz. It asserts hyperbolicity on
hyperbolic inputs, not a nonreal-count inequality on arbitrary inputs.
Do not omit that nonnegative-coefficient hypothesis when citing it.
Corollary 3.8 imposes its narrow double sector on **all** symbol zeros
and cites Obreschkoff's monograph p.273; its proof supplies no exception
term. Walsh is also imported there from Grace, not independently proved.
OCR-obscured formulas are not used for an exact coefficient claim;
the attempted screenshots returned no inspectable image in this tool.

**Other geometric comparison.** Cardon, Forgács, Piotrowski, Sorensen

control for the proposed import is supplied. Localization is not a
