### Primary majorant lead: no direct payment

Giovanni Coppola, *On the Modified Selberg Integral*,
[arXiv:1006.1229v1, 7 June 2010](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1006.1229v1),
unnumbered Theorem p1 and Proposition p2, compares Fejer-weighted
short-interval mean squares for f=g*1 and F=G*1. The theorem assumes
integer N,h,Q, even h tending to infinity, h=o(N), Q<=N+h,
real g supported in [1,Q], and |g| bounded by essentially bounded G
on the relevant support. Its conclusion retains Jtilde_F+Nh, with
an arbitrary N^epsilon allowance. The mean is
h*sum_(d<=x+h) g(d)/d, not an imposed constant-density centering.
The variable-support proposition also requires g independent of
N,h,x. Read the definition, theorem, near-frequency/nonnegative
Fejer branch and Section3 completion/remarks, pp5-6. The author
explicitly leaves finding a useful majorant open and warns that
changing its mean can yield a trivial estimate; the same argument
fails for ordinary interval weights with signed Fourier coefficients.
No independent audit of its number-theoretic proof is claimed.

This is a precise observable/hypothesis mismatch, not an impossibility
theorem: no F with a paid centered integral and no reconstruction of
the exact lobe-positive resource is supplied. Inserting a selector
