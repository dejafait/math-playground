### Primary finite-ridge comparison, including correction

representation*, Indagationes Mathematicae 24 (2013), 725-738,
DOI 10.1016/j.indag.2012.10.004: read the author's
[paper](https://pinkus.net.technion.ac.il/files/2021/02/indag.pdf),
definition p727, Theorem2.1/proof pp727-728, Proposition3.1 and

nonparallel directions and profiles in the specified regularity

there is no countable-sum case. Read the author's
[erratum](https://pinkus.net.technion.ac.il/files/2021/02/indag1.pdf):
the classes B and Bd must be linear and translation invariant.
Continuous real functions meet that correction. The target is
complex, countable, has parallel lattice directions, and supplies
fiber data rather than a whole-space finite zero sum. No theorem
application or new specialization is made; no infinite derivative
product is licensed. This is a scope exclusion, not a reproof.
