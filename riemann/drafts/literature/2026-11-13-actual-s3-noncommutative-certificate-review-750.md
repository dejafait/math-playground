### Primary theorem scope and essential proof cases

[J. William Helton, Igor Klep, Scott McCullough, *The convex
Positivstellensatz in a free algebra*, arXiv:1102.4859v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1102.4859v2),
arXiv stamp20October2011 (the PDF also displays13January2019),
Theorem1.1 and definition(4), printed p5. For symmetric real matrix
polynomials, monic concave q and deg(p)<=2d+2, positivity at EVERY
finite symmetric matrix tuple with q>=0 is equivalent to
p in M_(d+1,d)(q). For a monic linear pencil q, deg(p)<=2d+1 permits
M_(d,d)(q). Here M_(a,b)=Sigma_a+sum f_j* q f_j, with square factors
of degree<=a and weights of degree<=b. Boundedness is additional only
for the stated square-free variant. Thus the quadratic target is
within the theorem's representation scale; existence is conditional
on its positivity premise. Read Lemma2.3 p9, Proposition2.5 and its
GNS proof pp10-12, and Section3.3 p15: separation produces a
finite-matrix countertuple to the universal premise. Positive moment
functionals are assumptions of the GNS step. This does not construct
our arithmetic tuple or its relation. No theorem specialization or
reproof is made.

[Helton, Klep, Christopher S. Nelson, *Noncommutative polynomials
nonnegative on a variety intersect a convex set*, author PDF,
15March2014](https://igorklep.github.io/files/hknPosOnIdeal13mar14.pdf),
Theorem1.9 p10, allows nonsymmetric real matrix variables and a
linear pencil L. A finite right chip space C, a left module I generated
in R<x,x*>_1 C, and symmetric p in C*R<x,x*>_1 C are specified inputs.
Nonnegativity on all (X,v) in V(I) with L(X)>=0 is equivalent to
p=sum p_i* p_i+sum q_j*Lq_j+sum(r_k* iota_k+iota_k* r_k),
with p_i,r_k in C, conformable q_j in its matrix amplification, and
iota_k in the (L,C)-real radical intersect R<x,x*>_1 C. The strict-L
case uses the strong radical. Read Proposition2.1/proof p14,
Lemma3.7/proof pp22-23, Section4.1 p24, Corollary4.1/proof pp24-25
and monic Corollary4.4 pp25-26. Null relations and L-positivity remain
premises; separation supplies a violating tuple otherwise. Degree
bounds depend on relation-generator degrees as well as deg(p).
The theorem supplies no particular actual relation module. No import
into complex infinite-dimensional H0 is asserted.
