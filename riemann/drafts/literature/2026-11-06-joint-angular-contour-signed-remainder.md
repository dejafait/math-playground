### Phase optimization: a useful warning, not an existence theorem

deformations*, [Phys. Rev. D110 (2024),014508,
SectionsIIA–C/III, pp2–5](https://journals.aps.org/prd/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevD.110.014508#page=2),
DOI10.1103/PhysRevD.110.014508. Read (4)–(8), the complete variational

Periodic-variable complexification is explicitly allowed. Homological

need not be singularities of that form, although its logarithm is singular.
Local constant effective phase allows pieces with different phases
separated by zeros. A thimble's constant action phase does not fix the
Jacobian phase. The proposed analytic family of normalizing maps is
explicitly conjectural in footnote7; it cannot supply our contour.
The closed-form lower-bound principle (19) requires an actual dominated
closed form. None is provided for G. Strong duality and global contour
existence are not imported; their full proofs were not audited.

Thus the new source comparison rejects an automatic transfer from
holomorphic integrand or locally optimized phase to a positive signed
main term. This is an applicability finding, not a theorem that no good
actual-G contour exists. To pay a future positive scale M_r, one would

all relevant saddle pieces and their Jacobians. No available versus
