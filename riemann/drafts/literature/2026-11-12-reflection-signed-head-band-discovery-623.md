### Primary theorems actually inspected

[Palle E. T. Jorgensen, *Diagonalizing Operators with Reflection
Symmetry*, arXiv:math/9908021v1, 5 August1999](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9908021v1),
Theorem3.1(a), Lemma3.2, pp6-9. For bounded U, a closed U-invariant
subspace K with projection P, and a unitary involution J, the theorem
requires BOTH JUJ=U* and PJP>=0. It constructs a Hilbert-space completion,
a contractive W with W*W=PJP, and bounded self-adjoint S satisfying
SW=WUP and ||S||<=spectral-radius(U^2)^(1/2). Read the statement,
Lemma3.2's graph/contraction equivalence and the existence/boundedness
proof through(3.3)-(3.10). The reflected Cauchy--Schwarz inequality is
used only after PJP>=0; quotienting its nullspace and iteration do not
produce that missing positivity. Uniqueness and the special example in
part(c) are not needed or imported. The local identities with T,U do not
identify a suitable positive compression or invariant K, much less one
whose form is the actual mixed S3 pairing.

[P. Jorgensen, K.-H. Neeb, G. Olafsson, *Reflection positivity on real
intervals*, arXiv:1608.04010v2, 18 December2016](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1608.04010v2),
Definition2.6, Theorems3.2/3.4 and5.8, PDF pp4-6,14. The interval
positive-definite convention uses the sum kernel, not pointwise
nonnegativity. Widder's Theorem3.2 characterizes it by a positive-measure
Laplace transform; Theorem3.4 requires full derivative Hankel positivity.
Its nontrivial implications are cited to Widder/Hamburger, not proved
in this paper; those references were not retrieved or imported here.
Theorem5.8(a) assumes a finite positive measure mu, existence of
L(mu)(|t|) for |t|<=a, and L(mu)'(a-)<=0, and concludes reflection
positivity and a symmetric positive-definite extension. Part(b), for a
nonconstant reflection-positive function, gives a strictly negative
left derivative at some b in(0,a]. Read both proof cases and the
convex-extension argument immediately preceding them. No scalar kernel
or positive Laplace measure representing the complete signed projected
S3 pairing has been identified. Individual exponential response rows
do not cover the prime translations, endpoint defects and induced
middle coupling together. No distributional extension is assumed.

[Maria Stella Adamo, Karl-Hermann Neeb, Jonas Schober, *Reflection
positivity and Hankel operators—the multiplicity free case*,
arXiv:2105.08522v1, 17 May2021](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.08522v1),
Theorem4.5, construction(34)-(35) and proof, pp20-22. The theorem
