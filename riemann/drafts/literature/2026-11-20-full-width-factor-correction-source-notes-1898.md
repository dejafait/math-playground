C033i already proves that this qualified nu_1 does not reflect positivity

identifying positive output with a positive quotient. C033l's capped

## Decision and one bounded continuation

No inspected theorem supplies the proposed full-width positive correction.
Do not rebuild C033j's signed factors or assume a positive quotient from
a positive output. Park an automatic factor-based constructor pending a
specific signed-factor sign/error mechanism and width representation.

A distinct sufficient subclass is independently testable without proving
full-width factor order: laws nu_1*lambda with lambda any Borel probability
on X. This includes every proposed construction that keeps a nonnegative
mass-one quotient after factoring out nu_1, even without restricting that
quotient to a width representation. Its relation to all positive signed
width outputs is deliberately not asserted.

Nominate a single bounded Laplace comparison at s=1 between that subclass
and C033l's exact residual. Use C033i's exact restricted jump measure
P=ell_1(t)dt on [1,3/2] and C032j's finite B_1(1). Seek a strictly positive
uniform error margin on S, with its exact expression and hypotheses; if
the one-observation comparison does not separate, stop this diagnostic.
The sign and margin are NOT evaluated here. This is a known-tool
specialization, not a creative central attempt or a full-class separator.
The ready nomination1898 needs separate approval before mathematics.

If separation is proved later, abandon positive-quotient repair of this
residual; retain the broader output-positive signed-factor question.
