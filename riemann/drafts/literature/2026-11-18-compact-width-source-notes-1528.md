## Primary statements actually inspected

1. Suvrit Sra, *Some positivity properties of Riemann Xi under smoothing*,
   fixed [2611.00345v1](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00345v1),
   Lemma A.4 and complete proof, printed p13, and Proposition3.1 proof,
   p21. A.4 proves normal convergence and real-axis logarithmic growth
   for an admissible kernel; its constant depends on that kernel. The
   proof explicitly uses its strip-decay constant and a summable zero
   count. The Gaussian transform proof provides an absolute integrability
   bound and checks the factor1/2 normalization. Neither statement as
   printed is a theorem about the full signed mixture. Width-uniform
   admissibility constants, compact bounds and joint continuity still
   require a specialization. C032j's fixed-v1 heat-positivity, counting,
   divisor and finite-zero qualifications remain; no source-wide audit
   or new verification of the other preprint was performed.
2. Friedrich Haslinger, *Complex Analysis* lecture notes,
   [author PDF](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~has/scriptneu.pdf),
   Section2.7, Theorem2.39 and entire proof, printed pp67-68; author
   attribution checked through his University of Vienna lecture-note page.
   On an open complex domain and a positive measure space, measurable
   integrable sections, holomorphy in the complex variable, and one
   integrable bound for each compact disk give holomorphy of the integral
   and integration of every complex derivative. The proof uses Cauchy
   formula and dominated difference quotients, including measurability
   of those quotients. It supplies the integration tool; it does not
   supply the local family's uniform bound. A future signed-measure
   application must explicitly use total variation and justify the
   signed-to-positive interface, rather than quote a positive-measure
   theorem without qualification. No reproof of the general theorem
   is proposed.
3. Michael R. Powers and Jiaxin Xu, *A Criterion for Extending
   Continuous-Mixture Identifiability Results*, Journal of Statistical
   Theory and Applications24 (2025), 515-533,
   [doi:10.1007/s44199-025-00124-z](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44199-025-00124-z),
   [publisher PDF](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s44199-025-00124-z.pdf).
   Read Definition1 and Theorem1 with both proof directions, pp520-522,
   and the model on p517. Transfer requires one-to-one continuous
   parameter and transform-variable maps giving equality of the two
   generating functions; the variable map is independent of the free
   parameter. Laplace transforms are explicitly allowed for heavy tails.
   The written proof transports probability mixing densities by a
   Jacobian and uses symmetry for the converse. No such maps are verified
   for C032k's kernels. The model is not arbitrary signed Borel mixing,
   and its theorem is not about cancelling reflected entire transforms.
   Consequently no exclusion or existence conclusion is imported here;
   no assessment of the theorem outside that displayed setting is made.
