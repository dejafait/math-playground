## Prime-dilation criterion: direction of implication

J. Bourgain, P. Sarnak, T. Ziegler, *Disjointness of Mobius from
Horocycle Flows*, author PDF, Theorem2, PDF p2, (1.3)-(1.4):
[primary PDF](https://publications.ias.edu/sites/default/files/disjointness%20b-s-z%20oct.pdf).
Inspected the complete theorem, Notes1-2 and section2's setup. For bounded
scalar F and bounded multiplicative nu, small tau>0, its hypothesis is
|sum_(m<=M) F(p1 m) conjugate(F(p2 m))|<=tau M for every distinct
prime pair <=exp(1/tau), for sufficiently large M. Its conclusion is
|sum_(n<=N)nu(n)F(n)|<=2 sqrt(tau log(1/tau)) N eventually.
No uniform onset in variable F or tau is provided by that formulation.

The desired vector covariance has two independently summed integer
indices, actual mu coefficients in both slots and the weighted translation
inner product. BSZ's correlation is a hypothesis on one common index.
Taking only primes2 and3 does not satisfy its all-pair hypothesis. The
theorem therefore cannot manufacture the needed covariance cancellation.
Its general proof and dynamical application are not imported or audited.
This is a statement-level scope rejection, not a counterexample to the
proposed bound.

Annals of Mathematics183(2016),1015-1056, DOI10.4007/annals.2016.183.3.6,
[Lemma12 and full proof, pp1032-1033](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v183-n3-p06-p.pdf).
Read its complete hypotheses, remainder and proof, including (16), the
p|m correction, endpoint strips and final mean-value argument. It assumes
bounded sequences a,b,c with a_(mp)=b_m c_p when p does not divide m,

polynomials and remainders involving 1/H, 1/P and the prime-free
coefficient square. It is a decomposition estimate, not a power saving
for a prescribed pair of primes. The proof explicitly retains the
a_(p^2 m)-c_p b_(pm) term; it is not licensed to treat mu as completely
multiplicative or to discard common divisibility.
