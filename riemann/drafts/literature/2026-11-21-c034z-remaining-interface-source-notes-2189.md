## Primary statements and proof cases inspected

**Kim--Lee**, *A note on the zeros of Jensen polynomials*,
[arXiv:2105.05386v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.05386v1), 12 May2021:
pp1-2 define real entire functions, the even square transform f_0, order
and Jensen coefficients, and give approximation preliminaries/Theorem3;
p3 contains its polynomial corollary and complete Theorem4 proof;
p4 contains the square-transform remark. Theorem4 requires an even
transcendental real entire f of order<2, T>=1/2, with zeros in
S(T)={z: |Im z|<1/2, |Re z|>=T} union R. It concludes hyperbolicity
for ordinary derivatives through degree 1+4T^2. The proof uses the convex
squared sector, f_0', the auxiliary function z(f_0')^2 and induction
on even derivatives. The final remark separately confines every f_0
derivative to that convex sector and gives the bound
T^2(1+1/(4T^2))^2 for its Jensen degrees. The p2 Jensen convention uses
f^(k)(0), not ordinary power-series coefficients without factorials.
This is the v1 preprint; the published longer version is not assessed.

**GORRTTW**, *Jensen polynomials for the Riemann xi-function*,
[arXiv:1910.01227v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.01227v3), 17 December2020:
pp1-2 give (1.1)-(1.2), RH_m(T) with |Im s|<=T for ordinary xi^(m),
and Theorem1.2 with integer d<=floor(T)^2 and n>=m.
Section5, pp11-12, defines its sector class, states/proves Lemma5.1's
forward derivative closure and states Lemma5.2's transformed-derivative
criterion. Its proof uses Chasse Theorem3.6. The complete Theorem1.2
proof on p12 passes from ordinary derivative zeros to a derivative of
psi(sqrt(z)), asserts a negative-half-plane location, and then uses the
finite sine bound. These inferences are source claims to compare, not
additional audited inputs or general implications inferred here.

**Author accepted manuscript**, [primary PDF](https://uva.theopenscholar.com/files/ken-ono/files/jensenadvmathaccepted.pdf):
pp1-2 retain the same coefficient convention, ordinary RH_m(T) and
Theorem1.2 bound. Section5 spans pp12-13. Lemma5.2's proof explicitly
defines psi_n as the nth derivative in z of xi(1/2+sqrt(z)); its series
uses gamma(j+n)/j!. The complete p13 proof makes the same ordinary-to-
transformed-sector passage and half-plane assertion and displays the
floor of T^2+1/2+1/(16T^2). No intervening bridge theorem is supplied in
these inspected passages. This is the accepted manuscript linked by
C034z; no journal-version equivalence beyond these passages is asserted.
