### Primary theorem and exact scope

Inspected [Behrndt, Hassi, de Snoo and Wietsma, *Antitonicity of the inverse
for selfadjoint matrices, operators, and relations*, arXiv:1403.5704v1,
22March2014](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1403.5704v1), thirteen-page PDF.
Section2.1 defines the inverse of a relation and its operator/multivalued
decomposition. Proposition2.1, printed p4, characterizes order by inclusion
of shifted square-root domains and the corresponding norm inequality.
Corollary3.5, printed p12, states, for selfadjoint relations on a separable
Hilbert space, that 0<=H1<=H2 is equivalent to
0<=H2^-1<=H1^-1. Its supporting Theorem3.4, printed p11, assumes
semiboundedness, H1<=H2 and finite negative index of H1; inverse order
is equivalent to equality of negative indices. The inspected sufficient
proof includes the zero-negative-index case, positive shifts, bounded
inverse comparison and the two strong-resolvent limits. Non-dense domains
are expressly allowed. This is an imported standard result, not a new
inverse-order proof. Proposition2.1 is cited there to earlier work; no
claim of inspecting that earlier work is made.

Reused assessment541/comparison542 for Teschl's closed-form representation
and the bounded Schur theorem; those hypotheses have not changed. Reopened
[Dritschel-Rovnyak0903.3639v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.3639v1), AppendixA,
LemmaA.1 and both proof directions, printed pp28-29: a bounded Hermitian
block is nonnegative precisely when its diagonal blocks are nonnegative
and its off-diagonal block factors through their square roots by a
contraction. The proof does not require a bounded inverse of either
diagonal. A.2 assumes the whole block positive, so it cannot supply a
missing sign here. The unbounded logarithmic form still needs its own
form-domain bridge before this bounded theorem can be applied.
