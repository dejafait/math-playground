## Primary theorem comparisons

1. **Operator factorization.** Dritschel-Rovnyak,
   [The Operator Fejer-Riesz Theorem,0903.3639v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.3639v1),
   Theorem2.1, printed4, assumes bounded operator Laurent coefficients
   on a Hilbert space and positivity at every point of the unit circle.
   It concludes an analytic polynomial square factorization. Equation
   (2.5), printed5, relates the symbol to the infinite Toeplitz form;
   it does not infer positivity from one finite section. Reuse the
   AppendixA.1/A.2 proof coverage in541: shortening starts with a positive
   whole operator, and the block criterion retains the contraction
   obligation. This is the old scope, not new evidence or a new sign.

2. **Signed shared-middle completion.** Reuse541's Teschl closed-form
   representation/coercive-inverse coverage and block comparison. The
   original neutral form domain, both remaining diagonals, actual remote
   block and entire middle response must survive elimination. No new
   formula, completion or negative triple is derived. This known algebra
   alone does not escape the recorded comparison failures.

3. **Finite-size gap propagation.** Gosset-Mozgunov,
   [Local gap threshold for frustration-free spin systems,1512.00088v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.00088v1),
   setup printed2/5, uses finite-dimensional tensor-product chains,
   identical adjacent orthogonal projectors and a nonempty common zero
   space. Theorem1, printed5, gives
   epsilon_m >= (n-1)/(n-2) [epsilon_n-1/(n-1)], n>2, m>n,
   comparing periodic global and open local nonzero gaps. Theorem3
   lowers the threshold to 6/[n(n+1)] for m>2n; at n=3 it remains1/2.
   Section2.1 and Lemma4's zero-space cases, printed6-8, and
   Section2.2(18)-(23), printed8-9, were inspected: projection squares,
   commuting distant positive products and the common kernel matter.
   The optimization completing Theorem3 was not audited or used.

   Those hypotheses are not established for L744(6)'s signed,
   logarithmically unbounded form on a direct sum of cell profiles.
   Its complete prime delays and continuous pairings are retained,
   not replaced by adjacent projectors. No realization or order
   comparison is available. A small positive L2 margin is also not
   the normalized nonzero spectral gap required here. This theorem
   therefore supplies no transfer. This is an applicability finding,
   not a no-go theorem for every possible realization.
