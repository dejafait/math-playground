### 1. Multiple orthogonality with an AT system

R. Kozhan and M. Vaktnas, *Zeros of multiple orthogonal polynomials:
location and interlacing*, [arXiv:2503.15122v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.15122v1),
(1.3), Definition3.5, Theorem3.6, Theorem4.1/Remarks4.2–4.3 and
Corollary4.4: type-II polynomials satisfy the prescribed multi-index
orthogonality relations. The AT hypothesis requires continuous weights
whose weighted monomials form a Chebyshev system on the closed support
interval, relative to a constant-sign measure with infinite support and
finite moments. It gives normality and simple interior real zeros.
Read the complete relevant proofs: Andreief signs the moment determinant;
linear Christoffel transforms characterize roots; positive quadratic
transforms handle nonreal roots and multiplicity. The HTML identifies
v1/19March2025 but displays an internal August2026 date. Numbering here
binds to that accessed text, not an independently audited journal version.
The published PDF introduction was accessible; later requests timed out.

### 3. A Levy-exponent construction from theta moments

to the van Dantzig problem, the Lee-Yang property, and the Riemann
hypothesis*, Ann. Inst. Fourier74 (2024),377–421,

That example was inspected, without importing its parameter specializations
or the earlier full proof cited by the authors.
