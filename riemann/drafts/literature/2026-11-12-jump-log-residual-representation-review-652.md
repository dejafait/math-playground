### Primary theorem coverage and limits

**Exact finite Stieltjes transform.** Tica--Galapon,
*Finite-Part Integration of the Generalized Stieltjes Transform and its
dominant asymptotic behavior for small values of the parameter*,
[arXiv:1703.07979v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.07979v1),
Theorem4.1, p15, (64)-(65): for an entire complex extension f,
integer n>=1 and 0<omega<a, the finite transform equals its
finite-part power series plus the stated correction. For n=1 the
correction is exactly -f(-omega) log(omega), not just the leading
asymptotic coefficient. Read (54)-(61), pp13-15: contour expansion
requires |omega/z|<1; (60)-(61) control convergence strictly inside
omega<a; (62) evaluates the simple-pole contribution.
Finite cosine inputs and their affine reflections meet the entire-input
hypothesis. The whole discontinuous residual does not. This theorem
supplies neither a full-interval H1 constant nor a bound uniform up to
