**Complete roots, including the boundary.** F. Brunault and W. Zudilin,
*Many Variations of Mahler Measures*, ISBN 9781108794459,
[publisher excerpt, Propositions 1.4--1.5, pp.6--7; Lemma 1.6, p.8](https://assets.cambridge.org/97811087/94459/excerpt/9781108794459_excerpt.pdf).
For any nonzero complex polynomial P of actual degree d and leading
coefficient b, its logarithmic Mahler measure is
log|b|+sum_(j=1)^d log max(1,|alpha_j|), with multiplicity. Constants
have an empty root sum. The saved primary reading includes roots
inside, outside and on the circle, including the indented-contour
boundary argument. The lemma is the degree-index binomial coefficient
bound. These are exact identities/inequalities for the whole polynomial,
without a lower bound on this family's root sum relative to D. When
