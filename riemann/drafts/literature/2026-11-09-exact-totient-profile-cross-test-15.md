### Primary arithmetic tools and comparison

Pieter Moree, Sumaia Saad Eddin, Alisa Sedunova and Yuta Suzuki,
*Jordan totient quotients*, [MPIM preprint18-66 (2018)](https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/3557/1/preprint_2018_66.pdf),
Section2 p4 and Section3 pp5–6. Read ConditionTheta1, Lemmas1–2,
the complete proof including beta=-1, and Proposition1. For squarefree-
supported multiplicative theta, put f(n)=product_(p|n)(1+theta_p).
If 0<=sigma<1, kappa,A>=0, and for every real x>=2 one has
sum_(p<=x)|theta_p|/p^sigma<=kappa log log x+A,
Lemma2 states, for fixed real beta,

    sum_(n<=x) n^beta f(n)
      = S_theta M_beta(x)+C(theta,beta)
        +O_(sigma,kappa,A,beta)(x^(sigma+beta)(log x)^kappa),
    S_theta=product_p(1+theta_p/p), M_-1(x)=log x,
    M_beta(x)=x^(beta+1)/(beta+1) otherwise.

The proof treats the harmonic case and both summation-tail ranges.
This supports future reciprocal-totient summations after their hypotheses
are checked; beta is real. It supplies no automatic uniformity for
complex moving exponents or truncated transformed coefficients.
Proposition1 concerns fixed integer-index Jordan quotients, so cannot
be substituted for the moving complex family Q(z). No specialized
profile asymptotic is derived here. Any later use must cite the lemma
and verify its constants; only uncovered finite/parameter details need
proof, with no reproof of its mean-value theorem.
