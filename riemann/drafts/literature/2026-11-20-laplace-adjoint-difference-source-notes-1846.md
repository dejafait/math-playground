Spectral Theory*, Université Paris-Saclay, September2021, Section3.1.1,

argument. In H=L2(0,infinity), the closed nonnegative form

by parts with H1 tests; the zero-endpoint trace forces the Neumann
condition. Its boundary expression is retained before setting that
trace to zero. This is an operator-domain result, with no covariance
or two-copy curvature inequality.
