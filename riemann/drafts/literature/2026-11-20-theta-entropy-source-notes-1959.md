above, with Z=integral exp(V)dx in (0,infinity). It identifies log Z
as sup_nu(integral V dnu-H(nu)), using its differential-entropy

statement, not a new theorem or reproof. Quantitative Theorem1.1

condition. Those hypotheses are not established for the actual
polynomial logarithms. No quantitative import is made.
