### Effective unconditional tail source

Brent–Platt–Trudgian, *Accurate estimation of sums over zeros of the
Riemann zeta-function*, Math.Comp.90(2021),2923–2935,
[published author offprint](https://maths-people.anu.edu.au/brent/pd/rpb276-MC-preprint.pdf),
[DOI10.1090/mcom/3652](https://doi.org/10.1090/mcom/3652),
Lemma1, p2923: for 2pi*e<=T1<=T2 and nonnegative decreasing phi,

    sum'_(T1<=gamma<=T2) phi(gamma)
      = integral_(T1)^(T2) phi(t)log(t/(2pi))dt/(2pi)
        + A*theta*(2phi(T1)log(T1)+integral_(T1)^(T2)phi(t)dt/t),
    |theta|<=1; A=.28 is permitted by footnote1.

The sum counts all upper-half-plane nontrivial zeros, with
multiplicity and half endpoint weights; RH is not assumed. A complex
oscillating product is not an admissible phi. A later use must first
supply a valid nonnegative decreasing envelope. Also read ConditionA,
Theorem1 and Lemma3's Stieltjes, integration-by-parts and endpoint
proof cases, pp2928–2930: their sharper bounds additionally require
nonnegative C2 convex decreasing phi and convergence for the infinite
sum. Lemma1 suffices for the nominated power-envelope calculation;
there is no reason to reproduce the sharper theorem or its proof.
