### Primary statements and applicability

A. Ivic, *On a cubic moment of Hardy's function with a shift*,
[1511.07140v1, 23 November 2015, Theorem 1/(2.1)–(2.4)](https://arxiv.org/html/1511.07140v1),
treats the sharp integral of Z_H(t)^2 Z_H(t+u) over [T/2,T], for real
0<u<=T^(1/2-epsilon). It gives a finite oscillatory divisor sum with
O_epsilon(T^(3/4+epsilon)) error, not its sign. Read (1.2)–(1.6), the
zero-shift qualification, and proof (3.1)–(3.13): right-line deformation,
both connectors, exterior indices and the cubic saddle. Theorem 2 chooses
some phase C in a prescribed interval; it does not bound the required
fixed phase 3pi. The source's historical conjecture is not asserted open
today. Neither three independent complex derivative shifts nor rational
response twists/full tails are in this theorem. Here Z_H denotes Hardy's
function, not the notebook's zeta(5/2+it). No derivative asymptotic is
obtained by differentiating this real-shift formula.

H. M. Bui, *Critical zeros of the Riemann zeta-function*,
[1410.2433v1, 9 October 2014, Remark 1.2, p. 1, Section 5.1/Theorem 5.1, pp. 8–9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.2433v1#page=8),
displays the shifted (2,1) cubic twist with shifts O(1/log T), smooth
weight and lengths h<=T^(4/7-epsilon), k<=T^(1/4-epsilon), or
hk<=T^(3/4-epsilon), k<=T^(1/2-epsilon). Crucially the introduction
and Section 5.1 explicitly designate it an unproved input. It cannot
be imported as an unconditional theorem, including for a fixed twist
without an independent justification. Only v1 is listed in the primary
submission history; failed v2/v3 requests do not establish a later proof.

S. Bettin, H. M. Bui, X. Li and M. Radziwill,
*A quadratic divisor problem and moments of the Riemann zeta-function*,
[1609.02539v1, 8 September 2016, Theorems 1.1–1.2, pp. 2–4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.02539#page=2),
