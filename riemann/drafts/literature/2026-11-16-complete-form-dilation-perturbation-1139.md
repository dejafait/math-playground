### Primary statements and relevant proof cases

* Gerald Teschl, *Mathematical Methods in Quantum Mechanics*, AMS
  GSM99, 2009, online version12February2009,
  [Section6.5, pp149-150](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-schroe/schroe.pdf),
  definition(6.39), Theorem6.24(KLMN) and proof. A closed semibounded
  Hermitian reference form and a Hermitian perturbation on its form
  domain are required. Relative boundedness allows
  |v[f]|<=alpha*q_(A-gamma)[f]+beta*||f||², alpha<1.
  The theorem guarantees a closed semibounded sum, not positivity.
  Its proof retains the additive norm loss and establishes equivalent
  form norms. Neither a value of beta for this defect nor a full
  baseline floor is supplied. Thus small relative bound in the KLMN
  sense does not provide the nomination's homogeneous lower bound.
  This compares hypotheses only; no new KLMN specialization is made.

* Masatoshi Suzuki, *Weil's quadratic form via the screw function*,
  [arXiv:2606.09096v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.09096v3),
  23September2026, Theorem1.3 and Sections4.2-4.4, (4.1)-(4.9).
  The theorem states continuity of the lowest eigenvalue. The proof
  transports Rayleigh quotients to (-1,1), separates a fixed closed
  logarithmic form from bounded translations and continuous-kernel
  terms, uses smooth-core approximation for upper semicontinuity,
  and compactness of bounded logarithmic-energy sequences for lower
  semicontinuity. Read both cases, including varying minimizers and
  prime shifts. This supplies neither an explicit continuity modulus
  nor a full energy-relative constant at the proposed endpoints.
  Strong continuity and compactness are not a quantitative bound on
  all L2 translations. The smooth H1 starting formula cannot be used
  as an H1 assertion about the closed-domain minimizers. No local
  reproof or new domain identification is attempted.
