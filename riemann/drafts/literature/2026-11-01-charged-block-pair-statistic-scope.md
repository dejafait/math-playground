## Inspected primary input and exact interface

Baluyot–Goldston–Suriajaya–Turnage-Butterbaugh, *An unconditional
Montgomery Theorem for Pair Correlation of Zeros of the Riemann Zeta
Function*, [arXiv:2306.04799v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.04799v1),
7 June 2023. Put L_H=log H and w(u)=4/(4-u²). Lemma 5 applies to
fixed real-even r in L1, supported in [-1,1], Lipschitz at zero, and
evaluates the COMPLETE ordered sum

    S_H(r)=sum_(0<gamma,gamma'<=H)
      rhat(i(rho-rho')L_H/(2pi)) w(rho-rho')
      =(H L_H/(2pi)) [r(0)+2 int_0^1 alpha r(alpha) d alpha
                       +O_r(L_H^(-1/2))].

The proof uses the uniform normalized form factor and the Lipschitz
condition to treat its peak near alpha=0. Test-dependent constants are
not uniform in a singular or T-dependent family. The error is additive
O_r(H sqrt(L_H)), not an arbitrarily small relative error in a selected
horizontal contribution. Lemma 3 proves nonnegativity of the complete
form factor by a square integral; it does not sign its individual pairs.

The source's (4.3) gives the triangular test r(alpha)=(1-|alpha|)_+
and its entire transform (sin(pi z)/(pi z))², with value 1 at zero.
This test meets Lemma 5's stated hypotheses; no Fourier reproof or
test optimization is needed. Evaluating this explicitly known function
on a finite multiset remains a separate mathematical calculation.
