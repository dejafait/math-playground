Reuse Rajesh Pereira, *Differentiators and the geometry of polynomials*,
JMAA285(1)(2003),336-348, DOI10.1016/S0022-247X(03)00465-7,
[primary-paper text](https://www.academia.edu/20926761/Differentiators_and_the_geometry_of_polynomials),
Theorem5.4, Corollary5.5, pp345-347, with the entire proof and
multiplicity/compression passages already inspected in1496. For any
degree-n complex polynomial, n>=2, roots z_j and derivative roots w_i
counted with multiplicity, the imported statement is

    sum_i Phi(w_i)/(n-1) <= sum_j Phi(z_j)/n

for every convex Phi:C->R. No real-rootedness or simplicity premise.
The source's rectangular nonnegative matrix has row sums1 and column
sums(n-1)/n. It controls derivative averages from parent averages.
It supplies no inverse cost or theta-specific upper increment. The
signed-log functional is already exactly pi times the height by C028d;
renaming that functional does not furnish a new source hypothesis.
No new height specialization or monotonicity lemma is derived here.
