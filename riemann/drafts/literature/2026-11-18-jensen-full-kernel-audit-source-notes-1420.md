## Exact analytic input

H. Kösters, *On the Occurrence of the Sine Kernel in Connection with the
Shifted Moments of the Riemann zeta function*, arXiv:0803.1141v2,
5 September 2008, [Appendix A, Lemma A.2](https://arxiv.org/html/0803.1141v2#A).
For a real twice differentiable F on a finite interval [a,b], if either
F'' >= epsilon > 0 throughout or F'' <= -epsilon < 0 throughout, it states

\[
 \left|\int_a^b e^{iF(z)}\,dz\right|\le 8/\sqrt{\epsilon}.
\]

The locator is E. C. Titchmarsh, *The Theory of the Riemann Zeta-Function*,
second edition, Oxford University Press, 1986, Lemma 4.4 (reference Ti).
No monotonicity of F', zero endpoint amplitude, stationary-point inclusion,
or minimum interval length is part of A.2. It is an unweighted estimate.
The proposed use in C019/C020c is precisely the already displayed
F(z)=z^2, epsilon=2; the source statement is sufficient for that intended
primitive bound. This comparison does not validate the change of variable
or the weighted use of the bound. A.1's different monotonicity hypothesis
and A.3's integer-frequency sum must not be silently substituted here.
No reproof or stronger general stationary-phase theorem is needed.
