### Primary statements and essential cases read

Hon-Fei Lai, *Characteristic classes of real manifolds immersed in
complex manifolds*, Transactions AMS **172** (October 1972), 1-33,
DOI 10.1090/S0002-9947-1972-0314066-8,
[primary article scan, pp5,16-17,24-25](https://scispace.com/pdf/characteristic-classes-of-real-manifolds-immersed-in-complex-2lh0zj0xb1.pdf).
Section2 uses compact orientable smooth manifolds. Theorem2.3 gives
generic transversality, not constrained transversality. Theorem4.8 is
an identity of universal Grassmannian classes. Theorem4.10 gives, for
an embedding of a compact orientable (2n-2)-manifold in C^n, equal
complex-orientation and opposite-orientation index totals, each half
the Euler characteristic. Read Definition4.9, the opposite orientation
chosen on the negative complex-plane locus, and the reversal argument
in the proof. Theorem5.10 and its pullback proof formulate the bundle
class identity without a transversality hypothesis. For nonisolated
contacts the class is meaningful; an ordinary sum over those contacts
is not thereby defined. Section5's subsequent applications explicitly
deform to isolated points. No constrained deformation is stated.

Franc Forstneric, *Stein domains in complex surfaces*, J. Geom. Anal.
**13**(1) (2003), 77-94,
[Section1 pp78-79 and Section2 pp82-83](https://users.fmf.uni-lj.si/forstneric/papers/2003J.Geom.Anal.pdf).
The local index is winding of the antiholomorphic graph derivative
around an isolated complex point; positive/negative denotes agreement
of tangent complex orientation with surface orientation. Formula(1.1)
is I=chi(S)+chi_normal and 2 I_plus/minus=I +/- c1(X)[S]. Formula(1.2)
uses self-intersection for an embedding. The C^infinity surface
hypothesis is explicit; the C2 target needs a regularity justification,
not an assumed sign-preserving smoothing. Theorem2.1's cancellation
proof joins isolated opposite-index contacts of the same orientation
sign and changes the surface inside a disc, agreeing near its boundary.
It supplies neither our full-form sign nor our prescribed collar.

Forstneric, *Analytic disks with boundaries in a maximal real
submanifold of C2*, Ann. Inst. Fourier **37**(1) (1987), 1-44,
DOI 10.5802/aif.1076,
[Section2 pp11-16](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/aif.1076.pdf).
Read the determinant map on oriented real planes, its zero locus,
the frame/winding argument, coordinate-change Lemma7, Definition2,
Lemma8 and its determinant proof. Definition2 orients the small
enclosing loop by the tangent complex orientation at the contact,
not automatically by the original parameter orientation. Lemma8
allows C1 graph models but assumes an isolated contact. Theorem9
explicitly retains isolation, while explaining why nondegeneracy and
additional regularity can be removed by index properties. It does not
assert localization of an arbitrary nonisolated set. Reuse the already
assessed monomial examples; this review creates no further model.

### Comparison with the exact notebook target

L847 already establishes an oriented embedded torus in X=(C*)^2,
hence also in C2. For a smooth member of this class Lai's stated
conclusion is I_plus=I_minus=chi(T2)/2=0. This direct substitution
is source applicability, not a new topological theorem. It involves
the tangent plane, not the zero divisor of the two-form. Consequently
q=0 alone is not a complex-point census. L849 retains a whole
diagonal density-zero circle, and L855 retains both the divisor
zero and the Jacobian zero at its conditional isolated model.
Those are distinct issues. No assumption that every density zero
is a complex point, or that every contact is isolated, is permitted.

The C2 target has a continuous differentiable Gauss map. Applying the
smooth theorem via a homotopy of Gauss maps may be testable, but the
class identity alone establishes no positivity constraint on such a
homotopy. In particular, neither a generic perturbation of u nor an
isotopy to a totally real torus is an admissible replacement for the
original sign problem. The source's equality of signed totals is
compatible with zero contacts or cancelling contributions. A contrary
conclusion would require a new local sign/localization argument and
a separately justified nonzero contribution.

This is informative negative source evidence against automatic
generic-isolation import, not a disproof of any positive torus and not
a proof that no useful index mechanism exists.
