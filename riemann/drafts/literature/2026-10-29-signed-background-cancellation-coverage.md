### 1. Near-line logarithmic-derivative means

Fan Ge, *Mean values of the logarithmic derivative of the Riemann
zeta-function near the critical line*,
[arXiv:2211.08571v2, 15 March 2023, Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.08571v2).
Read the introduction, (ES 2K), Proposition 2.1 and theorem proof.
For integer K>=1, assume RH and (ES 2K): the multiplicity-counted
2K-tuples at heights [T,2T], with all pairwise distances <=v/log T
and not all entries the same zero label, have count divided by the
total zero count tending to zero, first T->infinity then v->0.
The 2K-th modulus moment at 1/2+a/log T, normalized by
(2a)^(2K-1)/(T(log T)^(2K)), tends to binom(2K-2,K-1), first
T->infinity then a->0+.

Proposition 2.1 also assumes RH. The proof combines its local-zero
expansion, error moments and close-tuple control. It gives no

### 2. Unconditional full-zero pair correlation

Baluyot--Goldston--Suriajaya--Turnage-Butterbaugh, *An unconditional
Montgomery Theorem for Pair Correlation of Zeros of the Riemann Zeta
Function*, [arXiv:2306.04799v1, Theorem 1, (1.2)--(1.4)](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.04799v1).
With multiplicities, their sum uses x^(rho-rho')4/(4-(rho-rho')^2),

square-integral identity, Theorem 1's proof, and Lemma 5's full
statement/proof (3.3)--(3.4). Lemma 5 allows real even integrable r
supported in [-1,1], Lipschitz at zero.

The fixed-scale square integral has neither Xi(t)^2/A(t)^2 nor
