### Primary statements and essential cases

1. Atul Dixit, *Transformation formulas associated with integrals
   involving the Riemann Xi-function*, Monatsh. Math.164(2)(2011),
   133-156, [author manuscript](https://adixit.people.iitgn.ac.in/wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/transformation_formulas.pdf),
   Theorem1.5/(1.15)-(1.16), printed manuscript pp3-4. Its hypotheses
   are -1<Re z<1, alpha,beta>0 and alpha beta=1. The outer factor is
   8(4pi)^((z-3)/2)/Gamma(z+1); the denominator inside the integral is
   (z+1)^2+t^2. On the arithmetic side the final correction is
   -zeta(z)/(alpha*z), not -zeta(z)/alpha^z. The preceding correction
   is -zeta(z+1)/(2*alpha^(z+1)). Keep their sum at zero.

   Read the conversion (4.2), contour shift from Re s=1/2 to3/2,
   pole cancellation (4.4), residue expressions (4.5)-(4.12),
