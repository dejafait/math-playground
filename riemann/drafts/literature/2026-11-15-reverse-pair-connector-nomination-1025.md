### Primary statements and essential proof cases

**Smooth Stokes.** Brian Conrad, [*Math396. Stokes' theorem with
corners*, undated author-hosted handout](https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/diffgeomPage/handouts/stokescorners.pdf),

corners and a smooth compactly supported form of degree one below
the dimension, it gives absolute boundary integrability and Stokes
on the codimension-one strata with induced orientation. Read the
whole proof: partition of unity, sector reduction, coordinate-face
signs and both interior/half-line Fubini cases. Use on a truncated
positive-time parameter box, with pullbacks and scalar tests, real
and imaginary parts separately. The theorem does not require the
map's image to be embedded or the pulled form nonzero. It does not
justify a nonsmooth t=0 limit or discard faces introduced by truncation.

**Homotopy for maps.** Victor Guillemin and Shlomo Sternberg,
[*Semi-classical analysis*, 13January2010 author-hosted version](https://math.mit.edu/~vwg/semiclassGuilleminSternberg.pdf#page=449),
