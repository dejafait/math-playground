1. Mikhail Tyaglov, *On the number of real critical points of logarithmic
   derivatives and the Hawaii conjecture*,

   pp36-37. For `phi` in L-P* (a Laguerre-Polya function times a real

2. Dmitry Gnatyuk and Mikhail Tyaglov, *Hawaii conjecture through the lens
   of Cauchy indices*, [arXiv:2506.22330v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.22330v1),
   version27 June2025, manuscript30 June2025, Theorem4.5 and full proof,
   PDF pp6-9; definitions/equations(1.1),(1.4),(4.1)-(4.6) inspected.
   For a real polynomial p, `p_sigma=p'+sigma p`, real sigma, and
   `Q[p]=(p'/p)'`, the hypothesis `Q[p_sigma]<0` on its real domain
   gives `Z_R(Q[p])=Z_C(p)-Z_C(p_sigma)`, with multiplicities.
   The proof retains shared multiple roots, additional roots of
   p_sigma, both cases p'(lambda)=0/nonzero at the remaining poles,
   and the changed infinity index for sigma=0. Its index signs use the
   imposed strict real-line inequality and polynomial behavior at
   infinity. The actual Euler pair is not this polynomial pencil;
   an entire-function limit or replacement by angular rays is not
   part of the theorem. No required boundary sign is supplied here.

3. A. Khovanskii and S. Yakovenko, *Generalized Rolle theorem in R^n
   and C*, J. Dynamical and Control Systems2(1996),103-123,
   [author PDF](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~yakov/jdcs-96a.pdf),
   Section1.3, Theorem1/Corollaries1-2 pp105-107; Section2.4 proof p113
   and Section2.5 remark p114 read. For functions meromorphic near a
   piecewise smooth curve the controlled quantity is the Voorhoeve
   index, normalized total absolute argument variation. The closed-curve
   estimate has absolute curvature divided by 2pi as an additive cost;
   on a convex boundary the cost is 1. The proof uses the derived image
   curve and a triangle inequality. The zero count is bounded by the
   variation index, not identified with it in general. The convex
   polynomial remark recovers forward Gauss-Lucas when all input roots
   are already enclosed. Neither the positive cost nor that initial
   localization supplies our zero-error inverse comparison.

4. D. Novikov and S. Yakovenko, *A complex analogue of the Rolle
   theorem and polynomial envelopes of irreducible differential
   equations in the complex domain*, J. London Math. Soc.(2)56(1997),
   305-319, [author PDF](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~dnovikov/Papers/jlondon-97std.pdf).

   This is a growth-index estimate, with changed domains and positive
   error, not an exact relative zero count. Theorem4' similarly controls
