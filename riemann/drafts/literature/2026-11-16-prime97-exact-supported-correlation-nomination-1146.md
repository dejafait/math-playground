### Primary statements and limitations

* Takeaki Yamazaki, *On upper and lower bounds of the numerical radius
  and an equality condition*, Studia Math.178(2007),83-89,
  DOI10.4064/sm178-1-5, [publisher PDF](https://www.impan.pl/shop/publication/transaction/download/product/90571?download.pdf=),
  equation(2.3) and its proof, pp84-85. For a bounded operator on a
  complex Hilbert space, its numerical radius equals the supremum
  of the norms of its phase-rotated self-adjoint real parts. The proof
  takes the supremum of the scalar real part over every phase and
  uses the self-adjoint numerical-radius identity. It requires neither
  finite dimension nor real tests. Import this identity by citation,
  not a new lemma or a reproof. Boundedness of any proposed normalized
  operator and coverage between sampled phases remain local duties.

* Zsigmond Tarcsay and Tamas Titkos, *Operators on anti-dual pairs:
  Generalized Schur complement*, DOI10.1016/j.laa.2020.02.031,
  [accepted2020 proof PDF](https://real.mtak.hu/112309/1/1-s2.0-S0024379520301038-main.pdf),
  Theorem2.1, pp4-7, including(2.6)-(2.9) and the minimal-completion
  proof; Corollary2.3, p7. The theorem assumes weak-* sequentially
  complete anti-dual pairs, weakly continuous A,B and A>=0. It
  characterizes existence of a positive block completion by quadratic
  domination/range inclusion and gives its smallest positive completion
  SS*. The Hilbert-space corollary assumes bounded A,B and A>=0;
  its conditions are existence of a bounded positive completion,
  B*B<=mA for some m>=0, and range inclusion in ran(A^(1/2)).
  This covers bounded positive-block comparisons, not a selected
  completion or a fixed numerical threshold. Application to a
  finite-rank defect needs its factorization and a proved positive,
  boundedly invertible comparison base. Different block spaces can
  be embedded in their Hilbert direct sum. Do not assume positivity
  of the block being tested or apply the bounded corollary directly
  to the unbounded J operator. Only bounded capped forms are proposed.

* Victor Hernandez-Santamaria, Sven Jarohs, Alberto Saldana and Leonard

  [arXiv:2311.13079v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.13079v2),
  (1.2), Theorem1.2, Lemmas4.1-4.2 and proofs, Proposition7.2 and
