### Exponential-sum comparison and resonance limitations

[Hiary, Patel and Yang, arXiv:2207.02366v1, Lemma 2.3,

Their introduction explains the erroneous factor two in some older
explicit Kusmin-Landau bounds. The theorem statement and introduction,
