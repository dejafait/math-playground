## Essential statements and cases actually inspected

1. `def:stage-high-data`, `eq:stage-all-height-majorant` (5241–5318).
   The real geometry is fixed before eta. The physical expression is specified
   independently of its high representation. The full correction is holomorphic
   in neighborhoods of the two stated Euler regions. On each fixed real box,
   for q_u<=Z^D it has a bound Z^B_H times a height polynomial of degree J_H,
   uniformly in rows and all imaginary parts. B_H and J_H precede external
   integration-by-parts order. This is a hypothesis of the abstract interface,
   not a consequence of a retained local quotient or nonvanishing of its factors.
   The exact triple identity and absolute convergence are separate requirements.

2. `lem:external-contour-tails` (5337–5392) and its applications (5393–5458).
   The kernel is nonnegative and jointly Borel measurable over a compact set of
   real contour parameters, with every polynomial decay order uniform in Z.
   The arithmetic factor on a Borel domain has a bound C Z^B(1+|t|)^J;
   B,J,C are fixed independently of Z and the requested N. The conclusions
   cover both complements of height boxes and coordinate slices, uniformly in
   the real parameters, and integration over bounded real intervals with the
   corresponding joint measurability. The proof treats integrated tails and
   traces separately, using decay orders exceeding N+J+m and N+J+m-1,
   respectively. This is source extraction, not a local verification.

   For the physical triple kernel, the height variables are Im(s+z), Im(z),
   Im(w), with a fixed invertible transformation. The Gaussian and the two
   Mellin transforms provide the separate decays on compact positive Re(z)
   ranges. For additional separating variables the source requires a fixed
   invertible change of variables and a jointly measurable external density
   with every pointwise weighted supremum bounded uniformly in Z and the real
   parameters (`eq:external-density-contract`). It explicitly excludes inferring
   this trace hypothesis from an internal coefficient measure having only a
   fixed weighted L1 bound; that measure stays inside the arithmetic factor.
   Buffered reciprocal domains must be preserved. Extended axes require
   global/absolute bounds for every factor. Raising external N does not
   differentiate that arithmetic factor.

3. `lem:fixed-bin-contour` (5464–5544), clarification 5546–5551.
   The finite physical bin is fixed before contour motion, q_u is comparable to
   Z^d on fixed positive bounded d-ranges, 0<e<10^-3, and
   T_1=Z^tau>2 with 0<tau<=d_min/100. Box constants are positive, at most 1/2,
   and independent of Z and rows. The source first moves z, then s to a global
   reciprocal line, then w. It discards the physical height complement while
   s is still global, and only then moves the retained s segment. On the
   retained s joins the denominator depends on s alone, so the other two
   axes may be extended using global numerator/correction bounds. No w or z
   join extends a reciprocal s-axis into an unbuffered region. The source
   assigns one finite B_eta before N to the discarded pieces and joins.
   Later auxiliary separating coordinates are not part of this triple contour
   identity; their tails must be justified in the central estimate itself.

4. `lem:bin-contour-accounting` (5662–5799).
   Its central-bound hypothesis is uniform in retained points, row subsets and
   moving labels, with fixed finite A_eta, finitely many correction pieces,
   polynomial logarithmic pointwise multiplicity, and bounded R,g ranges.
   The bin is moved using the full correction before those pieces are formed.
   Its single finite external list includes all three physical Mellin heights,
   including zero coefficients. Every buffered height is expressed as
   gamma_j+sum_k a_jk v_k, with fixed matrix and |gamma_j|<=(3i+1)T_1.
   The source prescribes c_k=1/[2m(1+max_j|a_jk|)] and |v_k|<=c_k T_1.
   It claims a total added allowance at most T_1/2, used once, with fixed
   bounded enlargements handled through the Z threshold. Auxiliary integrals
   must already satisfy the complete central bound, including their discarded
   parts left on global/absolute lines. Pointwise pieces need not be separately
   holomorphic or separately integrated. The proof then uses the central
   hypothesis and a joint L1 test bound; it does not construct the arithmetic
   central estimate or its auxiliary density contracts.

5. `lem:smooth-calculus` (1019–1193), `lem:detector-dyads` (4117–4225),
   `lem:bin-prime-bound` (14192–14254).
   Smooth logarithmic profiles have fixed compact support. The source distinguishes
   weighted Fourier integrals, pointwise Mellin decay and row-parameter Sobolev
   costs. A common density is required before a row l2 estimate is integrated;
   nonsmooth masks are resolved arithmetically, not differentiated. For tails,
   pure norm twists are translated into L-arguments before differentiation.
   Fixed scale/height degrees precede N; only the external seminorm order grows.
   The dyadic proof leaves discarded inverse tails on Re(s+sigma)=2, uses
   buffered inverse bounds on joins, and uses global upper/deletion estimates
   for plain joins. Its internal height ceiling and finite order are fixed
   first. The prime-annulus proof also uses untwisted transforms for joins and
   absolute-line tails, and states that dividing the annular weight by its
   logarithm does not create derivative-dependent scale powers. These are the
   inspected external cases; the moment recursion itself remains unaudited.
