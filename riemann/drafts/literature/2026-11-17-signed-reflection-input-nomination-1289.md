**1. Positive Laplace measure and endpoint criterion.**
P. Jorgensen, K.-H. Neeb and G. Olafsson,
[Reflection positivity on real intervals, arXiv1608.04010v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1608.04010v2),
18December2016, Theorems3.2,3.4,5.8; reuse comparison624.

with full derivative-Hankel positivity at one interior point. Theorem5.8
starts with a finite positive measure whose Laplace transform exists
through a>0; derivative at a from the left <=0 gives local reflection
positivity. Its converse only gives a negative derivative at some smaller
point for a nonconstant function. The proof uses convex extension and
Polya's theorem; it does not construct the initial positive measure.
The primary HTML statements/proof were confirmed. No such measure
