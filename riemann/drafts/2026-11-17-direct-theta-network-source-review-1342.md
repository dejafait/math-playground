## Three primary-source leads

**1. Cyclic walks.** Sergey Fomin, *Loop-erased walks and total
positivity*, arXiv:math/0004083v1, 12April2000,
[primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0004083v1), Section2,
Theorems6.1/6.4, Example6.2, and Theorems7.1/7.2/7.4 statements.
Read the complete Theorem6.1 proof: the two-walk switch, the starting
vertex exception and the lexicographic selection for arbitrary k;
then the complete Theorem6.4 proof, including the reduction from
all-pair to neighboring loop-erasure avoidance. Nonnegative weights
give nonnegative minors only with the prescribed crossing/intersection
condition on ordered endpoints. Example6.2 explicitly has a negative
minor when ordering is incompatible. Section2 first uses formal walk
sums and warns that numerical specialization can diverge. No arithmetic
theta walk matrix, boundary identification or convergence estimate is
provided by these passages. This extends acyclic machinery but does
not pay the actual input. The hitting theorem proof was not separately
audited; it is not imported as a new theorem here.

**2. Signed networks.** E.Y.H.Li, G.M.X.Li, A.L.B.Yang and C.X.T.Zhang,
*A planar network proof for Hankel total positivity of type B Narayana
polynomials*, arXiv:2107.10608v1, 22July2021,
[primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2107.10608v1), Sections1-4,
Lemma3.1, Theorems3.2/4.2; published Adv.Appl.Math.139(2022),102374,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aam.2022.102374).
Inspected the explicit -1,q,1 weights, every involution case in
Theorem3.2, (4.1)-(4.2), the segment definitions and complete
Theorem4.2 proof selecting the first defective forward segment or
last defective reflected segment. Its surviving nonnegative families
prove Hankel positivity for W_n(q)=sum binom(n,k)^2 q^k. Theorem4.3's
two extended recurrences and their replacement weights were also read;
they impose f>=e>=0 or e,f>=1 and a specified constant tail recurrence.
The actual S_(n+2) sequence has not been identified with any of these
families. A signed network can therefore work in a specific model;
generic negative weights are not a universal obstruction or a theta
cancellation theorem. No parameter fitting or new specialization.

**3. Left production.** Xi Chen, Lang Fu and Jiajie Ruan,
*A unified planar network approach to total positivity of combinatorial
matrices and real-rootedness of polynomials*, arXiv:2512.08369v1,
9December2025, [primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.08369v1),
(1.2)-(1.3), Theorems1.3/2.2/2.5, Proposition2.3 and Section3's
proof of Theorem1.3(1)-(2). For invertible lower-triangular A the
left production matrix is Q(A)=A diag(1,A^(-1)); its total positivity
is an input. The proof composes networks for finite leading blocks
of Q(A); their nonnegative realizations come from assumed total
positivity. The network characterization cannot independently supply
theta weights by factoring a matrix whose positivity is the target.
This differs from the stopped right-production Appell array, so its
failure is not inferred from C040a. No actual left-production matrix
or new Hankel implication was computed. The row real-rootedness proof
and Brenti's original characterization proof were not audited and
are not additional imports.
