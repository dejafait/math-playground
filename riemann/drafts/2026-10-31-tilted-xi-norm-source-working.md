## Inspected primary coverage and exact limitations

George Csordas, *Fourier transforms of positive definite kernels and the
Riemann xi-Function*, arXiv:1309.0055v2, 21 February 2014,
<https://arxiv.org/pdf/1309.0055v2> and
<https://arxiv.org/html/1309.0055v2>. Read Definition 1.1/(1.3), printed
p.2; the counterexample preceding Remark 2.1, p.3; Proposition 2.2,
(2.3) and its proof, p.3; Proposition 2.3/(2.4)--(2.6) and its proof,
pp.3--4; and (2.7), (2.11), p.4. The strip class has a nonpositive
Gaussian exponent, real linear exponent and square-summable reciprocal
zeros. The signed-zero identity is away from zeros. The pair-deflation
identity assumes a real entire fixed quotient nonzero at the center;
its small-displacement conclusion is not uniform in varying quotients.
The published example exp(-x^2)(1+x^2) has nonreal zeros yet nonnegative
first Laguerre expression everywhere. None is a disk-norm upper bound.

For the sign question use (2.11) by substitution: multiplying the real
entire function by exp(lambda*z), lambda real, multiplies its product at
x+iy and x-iy by exp(2*lambda*x). At x=0 this factor is one. Thus the
coefficient interpretation uses the same signs, not a new positivity
theorem. This is elementary applicability of the inspected generating
identity, with L571's positive affine rescaling retained. Exact zero
orders are unchanged by the nonvanishing exponential. No LP membership
or zero-free quotient is being assumed for Xi. Full (2.3) specialization
to Xi would additionally use its canonical product; it is unnecessary
for this sign comparison. The source's displayed proof of Proposition
2.3 contains a power typo in its evaluation of f(alpha); use the stated
identity (2.5), not that intermediate sentence, if importing the pair
identity. No reproof or new sign calculation is undertaken here.
