## Primary statements inspected and applicability

**Nested corners.** G. Olshanski, *Projections of Orbital Measures,
Gelfand-Tsetlin Polytopes, and Splines*, J. Lie Theory23 (2013),
1011-1022, [primary PDF](https://www.heldermann-verlag.de/jlt/jlt23/olshla2e.pdf),
Section1 pp1011-12, Corollary3.2 p1017 and its antecedent
Proposition3.1 proof pp1016-17 were inspected. The corner pushforward
is a mixture of unitary orbital measures. Corollary3.2 makes the full
nested eigenvalue array uniform on its interlacing polytope for a
strictly increasing real top row. C030l separately supplies its weak-row
extension and real-field comparison. This is not a joint order theorem
for disjoint two-dimensional corners or a complex tilted measure.
A leading block of size2m is within the nested-array setting only when
2m<n; 2m=n is a full orbit and must be treated separately.

**Complex orbital identity.** C. McSwiggen, *A new proof of
Harish-Chandra's integral formula*,
[arXiv1712.03995v4, 27 April2020](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.03995v4),
(2) p2, Theorem1 and definitions pp3-4, and the analytic-continuation
paragraph after(24) p10 were inspected. Probability Haar and complex
Cartan variables are covered by the discriminant-multiplied identity.
The U(N) trace identity is explained through its SU(N)/central factors.
Coincident spectra require removable limits, not division by zero.
This is an exact formula, not the desired nonvanishing or uniform
adjacent-rank derivative inequality. No general HCIZ reproof is needed.

**All-dimensional polynomial orientation integrals.** B. Collins and
P. Sniady, *Integration with respect to the Haar measure on unitary,
orthogonal and symplectic group*,
[arXiv math-ph/0402073v1, 26 February2004](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0402073v1),
Propositions2.2-2.3/(6)-(10) pp4-5, Corollary2.4/(11)-(12) p5,
and Proposition2.5/pole discussion p6 were inspected. Corollary2.4
integrates equal-degree monomials in unitary entries and their
conjugates using a permutation sum with the Weingarten function(9);
unequal degrees vanish. The proof uses the conditional-expectation
identity and scalar-unitary invariance. Formula(9) retains its
partition-length cutoff; the simplified rational expression requires
the stated pole cancellations outside its nonsingular range.
This permits exact finite polynomial orientation moments in U(2m),
including m=1, without any independence of its subblocks. It does not
itself bound the nonlinear phase average's gradient or its response
under an unrestricted real tilt. Any moment contraction or estimate
needed for TARGET remains work for the research turn, with explicit
dimension and degree substitution. No such specialization is asserted
in this review, and the general integration theorem should be cited.

**Real projection exponential families.** J. Leake and N. K. Vishnoi,

No algorithm was run or proof independently audited.
