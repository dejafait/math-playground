# GMP mpq bridge probe — 19 November 2026

The approved GMP specialization was tested at the primitive boundary. A ctypes
bridge using the installed GMP library parsed and canonicalized a rational with
681k-bit numerator/denominator, then matched Python `Fraction` for addition,
multiplication, division, comparison, and lossless numerator/denominator
extraction. This confirms those GMP primitives are usable locally; it does not
validate an evaluator adapter, any scalar, a radius assertion, the inherited
operation cap, or a speed bound.

The full continuation was not executed. The saved evaluator remains coupled to
`Fraction` in its driver/helpers and JSON serializer, so a primitive bridge by
itself does not fulfill the assessed target. Preserve the 1816 timeout/prefix
and replay artifacts. Next step is the exact saved evaluator with an integrated
GMP rational adapter and complete independent replay; failure of mapping or
replay rejects this backend route. Main-gap status is unchanged: actual-Xi
all-degree positivity and signed-height/cancellation/realization remain open.
