# Uncorrected L473 bulk has no stationary C2 extension

The exact result and full proof are canonical in
[L580](../lemmas/L580-uncorrected-bulk-quartic-stationarity-obstruction.md).
This stop concerns L473 Section 4's uncorrected bounded law only.

WHY IT FAILS: A stationary C2 extension with this first marginal would
automatically have finite fourth jet moments and must satisfy the
known quartic transport identity. The marginal's exact nonzero defect
contradicts that necessary condition. Choosing different conditional
second-height coordinates cannot repair a constraint on the fixed
first marginal. Reopening requires changing that marginal, not another
extension of the same law. This does not reject L473's corrected total
law, whose rare component and covariance correction must be retained,
or any general stationary process mechanism or actual-Hardy/Xi route.
