# Coarse absolute center lower bound — 2026-09-12

The undeformed audit proves the desired relative ratio in [Lemma 136](../lemmas/L136-undeformed-logarithmic-jensen-ratio.md), but disproves the stronger polynomial absolute lower bound proposed as a sufficient condition in L135.

WHY IT FAILS: the fixed-height center value has exponential decay times a fixed power, as proved in the canonical lemma. It therefore cannot be bounded below by a positive constant times any fixed inverse power of the horizontal coordinate. The disk numerator shares its exponential decay, allowing a polynomial relative ratio nonetheless. Replacing that numerator by the horizontally constant integral majorant destroys the needed cancellation. This failure concerns the stronger sufficient estimate, not the logarithmic local count or RH.
