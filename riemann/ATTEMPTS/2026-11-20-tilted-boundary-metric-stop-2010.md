# N=6 boundary-supported metric stop2010

The approved full-coefficient N=6 test is incompatible. Its exact
elimination and coefficient certificate are canonical in
[C029n](../lemmas/C029n-tilted-pollaczek-boundary-metric-obstruction.md).
Independent audit2012 validated the finite obstruction and its exact
certificate; this is no RH or root-count conclusion.

**WHY IT FAILS.** Fixing the five-dimensional bulk metric to the identity
forces its boundary vector to be parallel to the comrade's corrected
first-row tail. Two tail equations then have a nonzero compatibility
determinant for the exact actual coefficients, forcing both remaining
parameters to vanish and contradicting the nonzero Jacobi coupling.
Nonsingularity or inertia cannot repair an inconsistent symmetry system.
This stops this ansatz at N=6 only. Other metric supports and eventual-only
variants are untested; a finite failure is not a global ban. Any new
mechanism requires its own independently testable target and coverage.
Preserve the runtime stops2002/2003, summability park2005 and generic
preservation/common-measure stops in their original scopes.
