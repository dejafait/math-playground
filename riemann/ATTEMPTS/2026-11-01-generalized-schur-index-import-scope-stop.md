# Generalized Schur interpolation does not supply the actual-Xi sign

WHY IT FAILS: The primary finite interpolation comparison in
[the post-C644a assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-11-01-post-c644a-signed-mechanism-discovery.md)
separates existence of suitable interpolants from positivity or a
finite-negative-index theorem for actual Xi. Bolotnikov's hypotheses
and singular-data conditions do not provide that missing actual-function
input. The external safe-sampling criterion already covers qualitative
finite detection but supplies no quantitative vertical-grid order bound.
Thus generic index machinery alone is not a new positivity mechanism.
This is a source-scope stop, not a theorem against all signed methods.
The separately nominated quantitative pole-detector test retains its
simple-zero and normalized-growth assumptions and remains REVIEW_REQUIRED;
it asserts neither a negative actual matrix nor an RH counterexample.
