# Gaussian fixed-prime cancellation obstruction — 2026-10-04

The existing SPECIALIZE assessment approved the large-frequency
sign test and regulator quantifier before this invocation. The
single analytic attempt tried a completely multiplicative phase
assignment on a fixed finite prime set, with every other prime
assigned +1. Its outcome is
[L360](../lemmas/L360-gaussian-finite-prime-completions-retain-nonzero-main-term.md),
an explicit phase-uniform Gaussian main term and error bound.

WHY IT FAILS: this completion retains a nonzero leading factor
uniformly over the selected prime phases. Its value grows as
ε^(−1/4) for fixed support, and a divergent lower bound persists
when the largest selected prime is o(log²(1/ε)). It therefore
cannot furnish a zero-valued template near which to realize L359's
arbitrarily high-frequency shrinking cancellation condition at
fixed ε. This stops the +1-background small-support construction;
nontrivial phases on the remaining primes, larger supports, and
coupled finite-frequency negative signs remain possible. The
actual-frequency realization and all ε-uniform sign conclusions
remain unproved. The new direction uses a shell growing at scale
ε^(−1/2), while retaining the derivative and phase constraints.
No obstruction to RH or to every regulated approximation is claimed.
