**WHY IT FAILS.** Evenness, reality, order at most one, positivity at
zero and simple real zeros outside the verified central interval do
not alone force eventual disjointness for the exact shift/divisor.
L376 tunes all sparse root-pair spacings simultaneously and passes exact
phase equations to a single entire product; common zeros persist at
unbounded heights without vanishing of the shifted value itself.
The geometric inference is therefore stopped under these hypotheses. This does
not exclude a theorem using actual-theta arithmetic, physical
inequalities or a stronger zero-count hypothesis; none is present in
the construction, and no conclusion about RH follows.
