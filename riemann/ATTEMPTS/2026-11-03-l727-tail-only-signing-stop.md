# Completed local-product normalization does not pay the signed margin

The exact quantitative application in
[L727](../lemmas/L727-actual-xi-quadratic-tail-c2-transfer.md) passed the
separate step-226 conceptual audit: cancellation, both infinite tails,
constant 14, onset exp(8), Q''<=0 and the multiple-root identity hold
under its stated hypotheses. No source-access blocker remains for this
application. The earlier approval-only turns added no mathematical
evidence; their repetition does not justify another approval turn.

WHY IT FAILS: L727 bounds the difference from M_Q by (14/log T)P^2,
but establishes no lower bound for M_Q=D1(P)-Q''P^2. Its sufficient
margin (5) is an independent signed assumption. Favorable exterior
curvature and a small error do not establish it. This stops tail
normalization alone as a signing mechanism; it neither refutes the
bound nor rules out a future independent local margin or refutes RH.
No new counterexample is asserted here.

Decision: close this normalization/audit sequence. Reopening signing
requires a materially new actual-local estimate with source coverage
and a discriminating test. The new source nomination uses a full-line
Mobius approximation norm, changing both observable and arithmetic
mechanism. Prior fixed-profile realization and pair-divided
ordinary-moment stops remain preserved in their own files.
