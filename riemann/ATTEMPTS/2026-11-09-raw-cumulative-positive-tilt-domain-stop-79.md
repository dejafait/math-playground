# Raw cumulative positive-tilt substitution: source scope stop

Outcome: the reviewed sources do not authorize replacing L938's v by
U(E)=integral_0^E v on the required lambda interval[0,1/20]. This is a
failed source shortcut, not a counterexample to a cumulative criterion,
actual H2 positivity, L946's compact certificate or RH.

WHY IT FAILS: [DLMF1.14.18–19,27](https://dlmf.nist.gov/1.14#iii)
retains convergence and the initial term in the derivative-transform
identity. L938 supplies integrability of v, but U can approach its
total mass F(0), whose vanishing has not been established. The
available raw-U comparison is on s>0, or lambda<0; analytic
continuation of a quotient does not extend an integral-variation
theorem to s<=0. Subtracting the limiting mass changes the initial
term. The precise comparison and primary citations are retained in
[assessment78](../drafts/literature/2026-11-09-l938-cumulative-zero-count-scope-78.md).
Reopening needs a paid mass/domain argument or a materially changed
observable; the separately nominated tilted primitive is such a
change, with all of its signs and global obligations still unresolved.
