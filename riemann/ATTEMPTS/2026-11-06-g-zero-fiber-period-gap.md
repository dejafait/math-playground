**WHY IT DOES NOT FINISH.** One actual G-zero fiber fixes the angular
mean along a radial line. Profile sampling then bounds every nonzero
h mode by H and imposes finite positivity tests on the opposite-index
profiles; at H=0 it removes all angular dependence of h. But H and
A still enter the period through K=A+H, and no lower bound for A
at -|M|-H has been proved. The actual zero mode of h off the sampled
lines remains free within the original constraints, and angular
independence does not give radial independence. Thus neither the
sharp no-gain inequality nor an admissible strict-gain certificate
has been obtained. A finite Fourier reconstruction is not a feasible
certificate and supplies no counterexample.

The explicit bounded-profile and finite-positivity obstructions meet
the approved intermediate continuation test. Repeating only the
trace identity or relabeling its free mean will not justify another
period attempt. The immediate continuation is independent audit of
C868b; a later period calculation must add materially new quantitative
control with appropriate source coverage. The full class, coupled
graph realization and central Xi-sign problem are not stopped globally.
