# Forced contact is not an automatic divisor/Maslov import

6 November 2026. Primary statements, versions, proof cases and hypothesis
comparisons are recorded in the [completed source review](../drafts/literature/2026-11-06-l858-forced-contact-linking-review.md).

**WHY IT FAILS.** Auroux's divisor formula requires a nonzero boundary
section for a torus in the divisor complement and counts relative-disc
intersections. Pacini's abstract Maslov formula computes an index for
totally real boundary data without forcing a contact. The inspected
torus-linking theorems impose Lagrangian and complement-topology
hypotheses not established for this full vanishing-form problem. None
forces a common F=J zero on the L847 torus with its L849 collar. This
parks automatic import only: it does not refute forced contact or
contour existence, invalidate accepted L858, or ban relative topology.
The separately nominated full-phase/two-period test has not been done.
