# Nonnegative actual-Hermite first variation: initial-interval stop

The approved mechanism proposed B_N>=0 for all T>=1,
N=ceil(16(T+1)^2), t in [T,2T], using actual theta correlations.
The prescribed bounded falsification test stopped at its first point:
[L568](../lemmas/L568-actual-hermite-first-variation-negative-initial-probe.md)
certifies B_64(1)<0 with complete coefficient and infinite-tail errors.
The full certificate passed the independent audit recorded in L568
on 2026-10-31; the original evidence and bounds are unchanged.

WHY IT FAILS: the actual signs of the two jets make each of the three
linear mixed contributions strictly negative at T=t=1. Hence no valid
symmetrization can prove the proposed universal nonnegative sign with
these hypotheses and this cutoff. The negative contribution is tiny,
and a compensating D(P_N) margin or quantitative signed-loss estimate
could still support a different transfer. The result says nothing
negative about D(Xi), higher forms or RH. Repeating the same positivity
claim or expanding its probe grid supplies no new mechanism.

Retain the exact proof and certificate in L568 rather than duplicating
them here. The independent check is complete; it does not reopen the
failed sign proposal or stop distinct compensating-margin mechanisms.
