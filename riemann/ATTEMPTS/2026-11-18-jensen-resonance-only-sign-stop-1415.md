evaluates the exact r=4s, n=one witness prime subfamily of the CA
channel, including source entries and band endpoints. Its positive

one at x=8*pi*m*b, and the original four tapered mollifier factors
give K4=K1/13+O(q^3). There is no arithmetic oscillation to discard
in this selected family. Treating all nonresonant channels termwise
as o(Bq^2), or reading the full numerator's sign from its resonance,
would therefore be unjustified. The total saddle remainder could
still cancel internally. C020a (17) retains that complement, the
unequal-index sum and actual-P residual; the full denominator is
also unpaid. Neither the cBq^2 quotient test nor its O(B) alternative
is resolved. The fixed witness remains a valid target, with coherent
saddle grouping as the refined continuation; this stop is limited
to the stated inference and preserves stops1407/1408/1412.
