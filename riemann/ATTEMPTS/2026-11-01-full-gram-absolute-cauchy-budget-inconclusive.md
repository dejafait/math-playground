# Full-Gram absolute Cauchy budget: inconclusive

The complete full-tail norm and signed extraction estimates are canonical
in [L628](../lemmas/L628-full-gram-complex-shift-and-signed-norm-bound.md),
which is unaudited. This record concerns that specific majorization,
not a counterexample to the target or a global stop on the route.

WHY IT FAILS: Exponential damping in the rational tensor basis controls
the full shifted image and all mixed jets, so no infinite output is
discarded and no restricted first-derivative estimate is iterated.
However, bounding the normalized arithmetic difference by its two
absolute values and then summing all six source terms before Cauchy
extraction yields only the relative constant <10^14. The required
constant is 1/2. This upper bound is inconclusive: it gives neither a
lower bound on the defect nor a negative eigenvalue. Repeating this
absolute majorant supplies no sign information. The new analytic norm
input can instead support a computation retaining the signed
cancellations, including arithmetic degree up to eight when necessary;
that computation is still open. The exact phase, analytic normalization,
common observer, full tail and original coefficient multiplicities must
remain. No averaged positive margin, pointwise sign or RH claim follows.
