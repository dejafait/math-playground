# Absolute counting-envelope nonclosure1554

18 November 2026. The exact proof and constants are in the effective
small-time section of [C032s](../lemmas/C032s-finite-levy-difference-endpoint-control.md).
This is a limitation of one sufficient bound, not a wrong sign of the
actual Levy difference or a ban on counting arguments.

WHY IT FAILS: The explicit absolute counting/displacement envelope is
positive throughout the transformed interval corresponding to t=1/1000,
so its integral gives a negative lower bound for ell_2-ell_1 there.
It therefore cannot close the desired global sign test. It does prove
positivity for 0<t<=1/9996, turning the former existential threshold
into a concrete one. Together with the retained large-time proof, only
(1/9996,1/192) remains. Repeating this same envelope cannot certify the
missing point; tighter cancellation information or a different analytic
input is needed. A Gaussian explicit-formula comparison is nominated
for separate source review. Full cancellation and Xi transfer remain
independent gaps, and stop1553 for the reverse ordering is unchanged.
