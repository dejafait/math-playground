# Moment-only outward Pollaczek preservation fails on the assessed family

The general degree-six two-atom [0,1] moment preservation implication
is false by [L530](../lemmas/L530-two-atom-pollaczek-outward-flow-root-loss.md),
whose finite certificate passed independent critical audit on 30 October
2026. The earlier inconclusive sampling
record and all certificates remain preserved.

WHY IT FAILS: positivity and the stated two-atom moment restrictions
allow the exact measure (13/25)delta_0+(12/25)delta_1, whose alternating
degree-six Pollaczek polynomial loses four real roots between the two
certified radii. Thus these restrictions alone cannot justify the
proposed outward hyperbolicity invariant. This does not refute actual-Xi
root preservation under additional proved theta-specific hypotheses,
nor does it say that the roots remain nonreal at radius 1. Reopening
requires a materially different restriction excluding the certificate,
justified independently for the intended theta application.
