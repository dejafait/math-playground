# The analytic boundary reserve does not close the finite core

WHY IT FAILS: [L913](../lemmas/L913-monotone-exterior-payment-boundary-reserve-unpaid.md)
proves the two-sided exterior payment and all fourteen reduced proper
cuts, but its complete full-theta Gaussian/exponential envelope gives
only C<=U with U between 42.84047/10^6 and 42.84048/10^6. The required
U<15/10^6 fails; even L912's stronger saved raw lower margin cannot
pay this upper estimate. The actual charge and the full reduced core
sign remain unknown. An oversized upper bound is not a lower bound on
the charge or a negative Hall cut. Stop this sufficient closure and
preserve its partial reduction. No lobe quadrature, mesh/degree change,
neighboring-block test, frequency sweep or second envelope campaign
follows. A reopening needs a materially different, independently
testable input and source approval; the historical stop is not a ban
on independent mechanisms. A separate critical audit of the completed
reduction is nominated, without reopening its failed bound.
