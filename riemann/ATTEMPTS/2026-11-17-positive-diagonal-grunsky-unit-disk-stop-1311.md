actual-Xi finite equations exclude every -1<=c11<1, uniformly in all
strictly positive weights and tau. For a normalized holomorphic
univalent map on the full unit disk, the cited classical Grunsky
inequality requires |c11|<=1; the prescription itself gives c11<1.

degree-six witness is valid. The exceptional L branch and the two
unclassified cells cannot evade this contradiction. This stops only
the stated positive-diagonal linear prescription in the stated domain;
it does not refute actual Hankel positivity or RH. No rescaling or
extra sign-cell refinement reopens it. A different quadratic-defect
representation is an unassessed proposal, not a consequence of this
negative result and not a preapproved escape.
