# Direct Suzuki parameter descent: source-scope limit

The [completed source comparison](../drafts/literature/2026-11-04-suzuki-parameter-descent-scope.md)
records exact primary hypotheses and the inspected product formulas.
No mathematical counterexample or new compressed identity was derived.

WHY IT FAILS: Multiplication of the scalar exponential symbols does
not supply a composition law for the truncated Hankel operators.
Suzuki's weighted formula retains a reflection, its untruncated output
need not be L2, and the primary continuous product laws contain
Wiener-Hopf factors and finite-section projections. The inspected
sources do not sign the missing actual-operator defect. L618 therefore
does not acquire fixed-parameter all-window control by this source
import. This is a failure of the proposed direct implementation, not
a theorem that every descent implication is false.

One separately gated small-window square-order diagnostic is nominated
in PROGRESS.md. Its sign has not been calculated. Reopening broad
descent requires an exact applicable compressed relation and an
independently controlled signed input, retaining the original L2 norm;
a scalar identity or another parameter sweep does not suffice.
