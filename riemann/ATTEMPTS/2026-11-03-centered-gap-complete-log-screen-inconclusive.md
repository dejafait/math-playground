# Centered-gap complete logarithm: bounded screen is inconclusive

WHY IT FAILS: The attempted extension of L698 through L697's complex
convolution leaves the full signed angular complement unpaid. The
finite Picard/full-spatial screen in the
[step-127 working record](../drafts/2026-11-03-centered-gap-complete-log-working-127.md)
has negative estimated complete means through n=771, with decreasing
absolute normalized values and positive as well as negative angular
contributions. It certifies neither an infinite distributional-gap
profile nor a logarithmic asymptotic; even the refined grids and
60-digit point checks cannot exclude a small negative limiting rate.
Do not extrapolate the screen or call it a counterprofile. Preserve
[L698](../lemmas/L698-centered-gap-complete-spatial-arc.md) and
[L695](../lemmas/L695-fixed-summable-tail-absolute-angular-cost.md)
with their distinct scopes. The next source-gated scalar logarithmic
certificate is a narrower test of the same compensation problem, with
the exploration budget unchanged.
