# Retained-monomial anchor phase — local mechanism stopped

The complete exact calculation, hypotheses and limitations are in
[L660](../lemmas/L660-retained-moment-anchor-phase-gap.md).
It tests an arbitrary-support extension of L659's prescribed resonance
by choosing one angular position and one retained spatial monomial.

WHY IT FAILS: For every angle, the full-product phase slope at the anchor
is greater than 2n/5. A retained monomial subtracts less than 4n/15,
leaving a slope greater than 2n/15. Neutrality would require degree
greater than n/2, outside k<M<=n/3. Therefore this proposed way of
making the individual summands locally nonoscillating cannot work.
This is only a monomial obstruction: a degree-one polynomial combination
can cancel the first derivative, as explicitly checked in L660, while
its full-support response remains uncontrolled. No bound on H versus D,
admissible all-arcs counterexample, joint-projection stop or RH conclusion
follows. The original logarithmic target remains open; a continuation
must use a different full-response mechanism, not repeat neutrality of
one retained monomial.
