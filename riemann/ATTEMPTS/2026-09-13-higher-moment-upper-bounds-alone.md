# Higher-moment upper bounds alone — 2026-09-13

The scoped attempt was to upgrade L153's large-value measure using additional
higher-moment upper bounds. [Lemma 154](../lemmas/L154-moment-information-and-a-truncated-tail-criterion.md)
proves the exact limitation and a sufficient alternative distributional target.

WHY IT FAILS: Even simultaneous bounds E X^q≤K^q(log(2T))^(q−1) for every
q>1 are consistent with all first-moment mass lying on a set of probability
1/log(2T). The explicit probability-space counterexample is proved in L154.
Interpolation from the established moments also returns this scale. This
obstructs an inference from these upper bounds alone; it does not obstruct a
stronger estimate or exploit the specific series structure. The fixed-cutoff
first-moment tail estimate isolated there remains unproved for S.
