# Inverse-parameter cubic and low-degree cost stop

The approved full degree-at-most-n polynomial-action target remains open.
[L675](../lemmas/L675-inverse-parameter-polynomial-range-and-degree-cost.md)
gives its exact coefficient range test and an explicit angular preimage,
retaining possible zero coefficients and all free-mode qualifications.

WHY THE LOW-DEGREE CONSTRUCTION FAILS: the cubic
P(w)=w*(11*w-10)*(3-2*w) has every prescribed value and strictly positive
real part on the whole closed ordinate segment, and has an exact angular
inverse for every n=12q+3. However every such inverse costs at least
30*(23/8)^(n-1). More generally every normalized polynomial action of
degree at most 3*n/7 costs more than
(1254/1495)*exp((1503/97790)*n), with 1503/97790>1/100. A fixed
polynomial allowance cannot repair this gap. The argument includes all
angular preimages, not only the displayed construction.

Park the cubic and the entire low-degree subfamily for the rate-1/100
task. Continue only with the still-approved higher-degree part of the
same target, using L675's coefficient conditions and paid norm bounds.
No stop for degrees above 3*n/7, all segment-positive actions, the cone,
original unit-residue H or actual Xi follows. L674's older disk stop
and the finite-polar stop retain their own scopes.
