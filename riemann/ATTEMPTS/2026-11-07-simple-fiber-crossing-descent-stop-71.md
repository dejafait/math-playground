**WHY THIS LOWERING ATTEMPT FAILS.** Under L879, at a clean simple
off-diagonal G-zero fiber, every fixed smooth compactly supported
field (chi,0) with nonzero trace moves the phase-normalized density

class-wide sign proof for precisely those fixed fields, not evidence
that all variations increase mass or that a minimizer exists.
Zero-trace, parameter-dependent, coupled or nonsimple-contact
variations are outside this stop. No contact exclusion or RH result
follows.
