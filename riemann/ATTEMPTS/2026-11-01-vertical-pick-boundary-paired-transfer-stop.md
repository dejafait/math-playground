# Vertical Pick concentration: boundary-only paired transfer fails

WHY IT FAILS: [L641](../lemmas/L641-vertical-pick-concentration-and-paired-transfer-obstruction.md)
passes the concentration/rank test, using every local Xi zero and the
exact distinct-pole Gram. But a generic transfer from small boundary
leakage to small exterior complex-pair energy is false: its explicit
unit rational vectors have leakage smaller than every inverse power of
N and exponentially large paired energy of either sign at an allowed
exterior test pair. The proof retains the cancellation of an inner
factor at conjugate points; its cross terms cause the obstruction.
This is not an actual-Xi zero construction and does not impose the
actual local interpolation constraints on those countervectors. Thus
it stops the boundary-only transfer step, not the Rayleigh-witness
target or RH. Any continuation must use additional information from
the actual interpolation nullspace or the signed entire tail. Repeating
boundary concentration, coarse zero counts or independent absolute
evaluation bounds is not a new test. A constraint-sensitive signed
trace is nominated for separate source comparison; no bound for it is
assumed or approved here.
