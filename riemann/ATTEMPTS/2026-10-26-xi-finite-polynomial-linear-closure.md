# Exact polynomial linear Xi closure stopped

The bounded target was a finite homogeneous linear ODE for actual Xi
with polynomial height coefficients and nonzero highest polynomial.
Such a closure could have supplied derivative relations for a coupled
sign mechanism, but would still have required independent defects,
boundary data and the other Laguerre signs.

WHY IT FAILS: [L470](../lemmas/L470-no-finite-polynomial-linear-xi-ode.md)
transfers any proposed equation to the exact completed xi by an affine
change of variable. The imported Valiron normal-type theorem then
contradicts the imported order-one infinite-type statement for xi.
The identity theorem covers an equation proposed only on a real open
interval. This is a classical-result specialization, not a newly
discovered growth obstruction. It assumes neither RH nor simplicity.

Decision: stop this exact class. The separate critical application audit
on 2026-10-27 found no failed implication; its replay is recorded in
`drafts/audits/2026-10-27-l470-critical-audit.md`. Arbitrary analytic coefficients,
inhomogeneous forcing, nonlinear equations and difference systems are
outside the stop. Reopening requires a genuinely changed class with
independently testable inputs and source coverage. No sign or actual
off-line zero is established, and no calculation script was needed.

Recovery comparison: the scalar barrier is a distinct stopped class
(L469); neither stop excludes an arithmetic negative-part estimate for
the actual first Laguerre form. That different quantitative-statistic
mechanism needs its own source comparison. An average estimate would
still leave exceptional heights and all-degree positivity unresolved.
