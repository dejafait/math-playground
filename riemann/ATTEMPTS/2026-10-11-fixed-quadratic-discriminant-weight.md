# Fixed quadratic jet weight does not witness positive discriminant mean

The approved target was a positive leading mean for Delta times the square
of one fixed real linear combination of its three normalized critical jets.
The complete proof and exact coefficient are in
[L394](../lemmas/L394-fixed-quadratic-weights-negative-discriminant-mean.md),
with a separate critical audit pending.

WHY IT FAILS: After retaining the physical error, changed carrier, every gcd
collision, signed sharp endpoints and complete response tails, the leading
coefficient is a positive arithmetic scalar times a strictly negative-definite
real matrix. Thus every nonzero fixed vector has negative coefficient,
against the required positive coefficient. This is stronger than failure of
one selected vector. Stop this fixed quadratic-weight implementation; it
does not rule out positive Delta at other heights, other weights, or another
mechanism. Preserve the earlier coarse-contour failure independently.
