# Line reflection: positive output, insufficient normalized response

The bounded attempt investigated the approved angular detector after
L647 stopped constant-factor grouping. Reflection across Re(a)=-5/4
gives an analytic input with exactly positive output residues, but
[L648](../lemmas/L648-line-reflected-positive-input-rate-stop.md)
proves that its complete normalized response is at most
C sqrt(n) exp(-n/25), at every angle, on the admissible anchor-only
family. The required decay rate is 1/32. The actual operator norm on
that same family is bounded below by 8/sqrt(21).

WHY IT FAILS: reflection cancels each inverse Blaschke phase, but the
reflected input pays for values closer to the disk boundary on the
opposite side of the pole line. The uniform radial gain forces an
input-norm cost exceeding the allowed exponent. Neither angular
selection nor scalar normalization of this input removes that cost.
The diagnostic is not a certificate; the canonical proof establishes
the bound for every angle. This stops the specific reflected input,
not the full detector, the exponential signed-energy comparison, a
modified reflected construction, or RH.

The continuation changes mechanism to source assessment of
mass-sensitive noncancellation and its possible exact-weight transfer.
No such theorem or transfer is assumed. Existing constant-grouping,
inner-input and exact-recovery stops remain intact.
