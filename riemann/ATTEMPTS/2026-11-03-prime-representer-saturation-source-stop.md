# Prime-representer saturation: source-stage stop

The exact source comparison and primary hypotheses are in
[the completed assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-11-03-prime-representer-saturation-comparison.md).
This was literature-only; no new proof or numerical test was performed.

WHY IT FAILS AS A RESIDUAL-CONTROL MECHANISM: Paper 18/v3 (45)--(47)
compares the step-projection deficit with the strong Nyman--Beurling
distance, including a finite support-enlargement cost. It estimates
neither quantity from above by a sequence tending to zero. Bagchi's
Theorem 7 matches the anchored space exactly, so saturation cannot be
assumed from piecewise constancy or L730's neutral density. The source
explicitly leaves the complementary residual unestimated. L729--L731
remain valid in their audited scopes. No disproof of saturation, no
off-line zero, and no failure of a future signed arithmetic estimate is
established. Reopen residual-control work only with a quantitatively new
input or a materially different discriminating mechanism; preserve the
full-update and sqrt(N) source stops.
