# Raw renewal-adjoint coefficient cancellation: stop

2026-10-30. One bounded mathematical attempt under the prior EXPLORE
assessment. The exact residual, complete cutoff limit and qualifications
are in [L518](../lemmas/L518-raw-renewal-adjoint-cutoff-residual.md),
whose separate conceptual audit on 2026-10-30 now passes under the
retained fixed-model hypotheses. The original negative calculation
and the scope of this stop are unchanged.

WHY IT FAILS: The raw renewal density is integrable but outside L2,
and the full operator applied distributionally to its interval
restriction leaves an explicit nonzero residual. The scalar term
has an uncancelled endpoint singularity; each strictly interior
prime-power displacement gives a further one-sided singularity.
The reflected pair cannot cancel these residuals. The legitimate
smooth-cutoff weak equation retains the endpoint trace contribution,
so it supplies no annihilation of either trace. Nonzero residuals
need not have nonzero pairings with a special nullvector. Thus this
stops only the raw-weight coefficient-cancellation implementation,
not the proposed endpoint property, the full relative-flux route or RH.
Reopening requires a materially new corrected-adjoint construction or
an independently justified eigenvector-specific orthogonality input;
discarding the exterior tail or testing with an uncut weight is not
such an input. The O(lambda) rate remains a separate gap even if
endpoint trace vanishing is eventually proved.
