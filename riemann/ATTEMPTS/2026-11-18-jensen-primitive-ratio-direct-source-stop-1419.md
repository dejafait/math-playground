# Primitive-ratio grouping does not meet the direct additive-source hypotheses

18 November 2026. The bounded attempt under unchanged EXPLORE coverage1417
is recorded in [C020d](../lemmas/C020d-real-prime-witness-primitive-ratio-moduli.md).
It groups the entire mixed kernel, including both endpoint functionals,
and resolves every witness-prime gcd case without changing coefficients.

WHY THIS IMPLEMENTATION STOPS: C020d (9) gives actual interior tuples
with coprime witness product and reduced modulus comparable to sqrt(X),
beyond the inspected prime-product additive theorem's X^(1/3) range at
the natural product length. The original joint weights are also not
that theorem's f2 coefficients. Primitive-ratio extraction supplies
neither an inverse-phase transformation nor a separated-weight norm
budget. C020d (10), with its full endpoints, is the first explicitly
isolated unpaid high-modulus expression. Its sign and size are unknown;
it may have a main term. This is an applicability failure for the direct
source implementation, not a lower bound on a residual, a universal
arithmetic obstruction, or a disproof of the fixed witness or RH.

Stops1415-1417 remain unchanged. Reopening this implementation requires
a valid new transformation or a theorem covering the exact weighted
range, with a summable error. An independent conceptual audit of the
inherited analytic reduction is nominated separately; it needs no
prime-product cancellation hypothesis.
