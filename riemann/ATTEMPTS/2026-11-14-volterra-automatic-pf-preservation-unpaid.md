# Volterra automatic PF preservation: source-hypothesis stop884

Comparison884 is recorded in
[assessment883](../drafts/literature/2026-11-14-reciprocal-density-volterra-propagation-883.md).
The scalar Volterra equation is a classical self-decomposable-density
identity. No new mathematical result or counterexample was derived.

**WHY IT FAILS.** The inspected convolution preservation argument
requires the relevant minors of its factors already to have the
desired signs. Scalar positivity and monotonicity of H do not pay
those hypotheses. Assuming complete monotonicity of actual H would
instead assume an RH-equivalent condition. Sra's later v3 finite-order
theorem also makes a low-order actual-density PF test redundant, but
retains a degree-dependent supply of verified factors. Neither theorem
provides the requested arbitrary-order spatial propagation from C466a.
This is a stop for that automatic inference, not a disproof of an
actual-H propagation theorem. The separately assessed cofactor-weighted
retained-zero test884 remains a conditional, unperformed mechanism.
