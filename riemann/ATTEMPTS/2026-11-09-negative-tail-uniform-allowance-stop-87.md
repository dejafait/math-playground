# Fixed negative-tail uniform allowance stops

The full hypotheses, allowance formula, rational certificate and scope
are canonical in
[C947b](../lemmas/C947b-fixed-negative-tail-uniform-allowance-stop.md).

WHY IT FAILS: The approved certificate adds its full uniform kernel
allowance on[4,8] to two nonnegative polynomial/tail contributions.
That allowance alone is more than365 times the available U(4) margin.
The prescribed first test therefore stops before either later
contribution is computed. This supplies no lower bound for B_4 or
the actual replacement error and does not disprove the sufficient
budget target. No degree, precision, grid, split, tilt or error budget
was refined. Retrying requires a materially different cancellation
input and separate source coverage; the signed common-kernel remainder
nomination is REVIEW_REQUIRED, not an established repair.
