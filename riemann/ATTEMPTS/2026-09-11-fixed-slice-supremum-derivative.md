# Fixed-slice velocities do not bound a moving supremum

Date: 2026-09-11.

The audit succeeds in applying static upward selection to simple theta
zeros under a finite positive strip bound; the attempted automatic
passage to a supremum derivative fails. The conditional consequence
and canonical analytic counterexample are in
[Lemma 126](../lemmas/L126-fixed-slice-motion-selection-and-supremum-obstruction.md).

WHY IT FAILS: Selection controls some near-highest zeros, whereas an
upper supremum estimate must control all contenders. Even a bound on
every individual initial derivative is insufficient without a uniform
first-order estimate: the analytic family in L126 has all initial
velocities −1 and supremum right derivative 3. Its branches share one
interval and bounded, summable quartet geometry, so those properties
alone do not repair the interchange. This example is not a heat
solution and rules out only the abstract inference, not a possible
additional estimate using the theta heat equation.
