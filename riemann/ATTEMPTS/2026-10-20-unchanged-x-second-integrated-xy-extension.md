# Unchanged x-second with all temporal identities and zero integrated xy

WHY IT FAILS: [L446](../lemmas/L446-complete-x-second-integrated-xy-incompatibility.md)
forces every integrable cross entry to equal q_H'/(2L) a.e. Its exact
integral retains two endpoint terms and a positive moving-weight term.
On each sufficiently late original cutoff interval [B_j,2B_j), the
complete endpoint defect has cosine average tending to -1/sqrt(2):
the lower endpoint has a prime-2 harmonic and the doubled upper
endpoint has none. Absolute estimates retain the high component,
both intermediates, mass loss, normalization, translation and constant
correction. Thus compatibility fails on a positive-measure set in
each such interval, even without a uniform bound on the cross entry.
The independent step-283 audit in L446 validates this local result. It stops only
the unchanged-density eventual-ray extension, not changed densities
or laws, and gives no actual clipping, global realization or RH result.
