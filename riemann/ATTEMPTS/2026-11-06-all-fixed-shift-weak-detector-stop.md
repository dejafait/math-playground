# All fixed-shift weak-sign detector: finite-class stop

The 6 November 2026 approved cubic test has an exact counterexample
in [L839](../lemmas/L839-cubic-all-fixed-shift-weak-sign-counterexample.md).
This stops the universal implication from separately eventual weak
signs on every fixed-shift ray to real zeros, when finite products and
tied-modulus roots are allowed. It does not stop RH research or versions
with additional hypotheses.

WHY IT FAILS: A tied-modulus conjugate packet can cancel the positive
real contribution exactly on entire rank residue classes without
ever becoming negative. L839 evaluates every positive rank and shift,
including the zero classes and shifts above degree. Thus strengthening
adjacency to all fixed shifts still leaves a weak-sign detector blind
in this class. Infinite asymptotics access is not needed for this
negative result. Strictness, Xi realization and any infinite-degree
exclusion remain separate questions; none is established here.

The independent 6 November audit in L839 replayed the source
normalization, all six subset denominators, every residue class and
all exterior shifts without finding an error. The stop therefore
stands at its exact scope. Whether adding a prescribed higher finite
PF order repairs the detector is a different, unproved question;
its small-angle cubic nomination requires a separate source review.
