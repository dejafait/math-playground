# Equal-amplitude two-wave stationary bulk: exact threshold failure

The full proof is [L576](../lemmas/L576-two-wave-stationary-bulk-zero-clipped-mass.md).
The model meets the complete L473 covariance, exact real-shift
stationarity and bounded derivative support, but its clipped mean is
exactly zero rather than at least 1/288.

WHY IT FAILS: The sum of two equal-amplitude real cosines factors
into a product of two real cosines. Direct expansion, valid even at
zeros and coincident frequencies, makes SS''-(S')^2 a sum of two
nonpositive terms. Thus this bulk has no positive clipped mass to
retain after the proposed sqrt(log H) scaling. A numerical integration
or finer covariance estimate cannot change this identity. Stop this
specific bulk; it does not refute stationary feasibility, L473,
actual negative-mass bounds or RH.

A proposed three-wave test would change the mechanism responsible
for this stop and must receive its own source assessment before
calculation. No threshold, full moment realization or actual-data
transfer for that proposal is asserted.
