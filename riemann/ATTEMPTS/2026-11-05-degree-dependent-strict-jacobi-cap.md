# Degree-dependent strict Jacobi matching: finite-cap stop

5 November 2026, step598. The approved target allowed all four positive
parameters to vary with degree, including free D, and retained both
numerator pairings. [L818](../lemmas/L818-central-strict-jacobi-degree-cap.md)
proves d<=319 under the exact central matching equations and the strict
inequalities A>B+d-1, C>D+d-1 (or the swapped pairing), subject to
L791's inherited audited input contracts.

WHY IT FAILS: the first two equations impose a necessary denominator-pair
inequality, while the strict Jacobi regime forces both numerator
parameters beyond d-1. L791's actual central ratios violate the resulting
necessary inequality for every d>=320. Parameter variation and the second
pairing cannot remove this obstruction. This stops only the specified
strict regime; broader continuous/discrete root regions and direct
root certificates are not excluded. L791's finite success and L817's
fixed-tuple stop retain their distinct scopes. Reopening this same regime
requires changed equations or hypotheses, not a parameter sweep.
