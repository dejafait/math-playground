# Principal Rice in-sector saddle stop1665

WHY IT FAILS. [C048m](../lemmas/C048m-principal-rice-stationary-sector-exclusion.md)
proves that throughout the proposed degree-power annulus and audited
upper sector, the principal main term has Im(F'/F)>=1/(30log|p|)>0
for every sufficiently large d. It therefore has no stationary point
there. The full principal arguments, especially the pi contributed by
Log(-s), are retained; a modulus-only balance would miss this obstacle.
This stops use of an in-region stationary point for that contour idea.
It does not exclude stationary points elsewhere, actual-integrand
stationary points, nonstationary methods, other representations or RH.
The signed Rice integral and sufficient height implication stay open.
Reopening this exact stationary-sector route requires a materially new
observable or region with separately assessed coverage, not larger
numerical degrees. The new nomination concerns theta-boundary
continuation and is unreviewed, not an assumed repair.
