## WHY IT FAILS

Separate positivity would require a nonnegative spectrum for every
primitive family at every frequency. The full diagonal already fails
that threshold. Reflection leaves a derivative jump for each mode;
in the sum of self-pair spectra their squared contributions survive.
The proof retains all modes and controls the infinite remainder, so
neither finite truncation nor a missing common multiplier explains the
failure. The total spectrum contains the omitted off-diagonal
compensation and remains undecided. An exact resummation of this same
diagonal cannot change its Fourier sign. Reopening a positivity route
requires a materially different compensation mechanism and an
independent bounded test; this is not a global ban on regrouping.
