# Four-factor absolute height rectangle — step 45

The approved leading-scale test yields the full-tail reduction in
[L390](../lemmas/L390-discriminant-four-factor-projection-reduction.md),
including its retained k=1 harmonic and controlled error. No beta or
complete square-moment asymptotic is proved.

WHY IT FAILS: The two-factor response-cell contour cannot simply be
reused after squaring the discriminant. Four reflected critical factors
leave E^(-1) in the k=1 term, growing as H^y on the upper rectangle.
The inherited absolute bounds give O(H^2) for its horizontal edge and
O(H^2/log H) for its connectors, above the required o(H log^4 H).
This is a bound-certificate failure, not a lower bound on the integral,
not a proof that all contour methods fail, and not a disproof of the
moment target. The k=2 connector estimate also needs improvement.

Park this absolute-bound implementation. The proposed next mechanism
is a signed fixed-twist unequal-orientation fourth-moment comparison,
with a separate REVIEW_REQUIRED assessment. Complete tails have the
uniform transfer in L390; the signed projection, other coefficients
and positive beta remain unresolved. Historical sharp-source and grid
stops are preserved, without making them a global ban.
