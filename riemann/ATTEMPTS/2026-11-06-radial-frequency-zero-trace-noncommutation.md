# Radial spectral isolation: the normal multiplier does not preserve traces

6 November 2026. The full calculation is canonical in
[L873](../lemmas/L873-radial-frequency-isolation-does-not-preserve-zero-fibers.md),
newly written and unaudited. This is an operation-level NEGATIVE,
not a full-class counterexample or an RH conclusion.

**WHY IT FAILS TO FINISH.** Uniformly bounded individual profiles
do suffice for a tempered nonzero-angular projection; a compact
radial window then isolates an arbitrary chosen nonzero profile
exactly. But its normal convolution leaves the actual zero fiber.
L873 constructs a smooth function zero on every such fiber whose
localized trace is nonconstant, with exact value 1 at a chosen point
where zero preservation would require 0. Thus Fourier calculus and
the original trace identities alone do not license that operation.
The diagnostic has no asserted common ridge structure or admissible
lifts. Filtering only along the fiber does not select the angular
index and does not automatically commute with distributional angular
restriction. No actual-F-specific repair of these operations was
proved. Park this spectral mechanism; any reopening needs new
trace-compatible actual-F information, not the same finite-cutoff
limit, an added norm, or presumed smoothing of a feasible triple.

The independently testable proposed direction is direct heat evolution
of the reciprocal-power Hankel forms at the main positivity gap.
Its source comparison and duplicate screen are not complete; it is
