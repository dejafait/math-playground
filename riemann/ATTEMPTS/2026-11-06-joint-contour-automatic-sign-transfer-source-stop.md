# Joint contour invariance is insufficient for a signed minor bound

2026-11-06, literature-only comparison. Full source scope and citations
are recorded in
[the completed assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-11-06-joint-angular-contour-signed-remainder.md).

**WHY IT FAILS.** Katkova's identity and homological invariance do not
supply an order-uniform signed remainder for k=r-1. The inspected
complex-phase theorem fixes dimension and retains potentially canceling
saddle contributions. Lawrence–Yamauchi's local phase condition retains
the Jacobian and permits different phases across pieces; its proposed
analytic parameter family of normalizing flows is conjectural. No such
family, dominating main term or actual error bound is supplied for G.
These source findings stop automatic contour-to-sign transfer, not all
possible contours and not RH. L775's narrower absolute-complement stop
is preserved. No new mathematical obstruction theorem is claimed.

The independent proposed continuation tests the full-density saddle's
existence in one fixed sector collar, with a separate assessment and
approval gate. It assumes no contour, minor sign or positive potential
and does not repeat the shrinking-core modulus calculation.
