# Mixed fourth moment via coarse arithmetic bounds — 2026-09-13

The exact expansion and proved upper bound are in
[L180](../lemmas/L180-mixed-fourth-moment-kernel-expansion.md).

WHY IT FAILS: Bounding the real and imaginary projections by their complex
moduli and applying the product-frequency Hilbert estimate retains the
factor N²/h multiplying the logarithmic equal-product upper count. It
therefore supplies no improvement over the existing block fourth-moment
scale. The exact expansion also contains phase-dependent kernels, whose
continuous frequency ranges admit stationary points; they cannot simply
be discarded or uniformly treated as separated nonstationary phases.
This records failure of this coarse estimate to improve the bound, not
failure of sharper arithmetic cancellation or a counterexample to RH.
