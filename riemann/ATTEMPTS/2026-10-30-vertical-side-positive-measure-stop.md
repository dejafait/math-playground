# Finite positive vertical-side measures: full-space norm stop

The canonical proof is [L508](../lemmas/L508-vertical-side-positive-measure-norm-obstruction.md). Its independent critical audit of 30 October 2026 passes without repair. It gives a lower bound c_R L^(R/8)/a for every finite nonnegative paired-side measure on [b,B], at every sufficiently large real T. The ratio to the required O_R(a) threshold diverges. This is an informative negative for the approved family, with no novelty certification.

WHY IT FAILS: two separated smooth frequency packets are normalized by their exact side values at height b. The high packet minus the low packet has nonnegative side traces at every y>=b and a positive Q contribution, because cosh(sy)/cosh(sb) changes its monotonicity in s across b. Its real-axis weighted norm is exponentially small. Thus every nonnegative measure reinforces the same witness; endpoint atoms and arbitrarily large finite mass cannot cancel it. The witness vanishes on the side at height b and lies outside H_a, so the result does not refute the constrained Q inequality, general rectangle-supported measures or actual-Xi signs.
