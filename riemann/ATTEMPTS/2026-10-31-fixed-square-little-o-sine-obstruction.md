# Fixed-square little-o defect: unaudited sine-dual obstruction

The first-order bounded sine calculation succeeds at its two budget tests in
[L575](../lemmas/L575-bounded-sine-prime-peano-dual.md), conditional on its named
standard analytic imports. The new specialization itself awaits separate
critical review. All earlier failure evidence remains in force.

WHY IT FAILS: L575's bounded multiplier would force L477's complete residual
and its particular fixed-square absolute defect to have full T(log T)^3 mass.
It pays the surviving stationary chirp and both Taylor remainders, rather than
inferring the full residual from the ratio component alone. If this proof
survives audit, that specific defect cannot be little-o of T(log T)^3. This is
an obstruction to the proposed approximation route, not a counterexample to
RH or a lower bound for negative mass. The current claim is unaudited; different
squares and independent sign mechanisms are not excluded. Audit before relying
on the route stop, and reopen it if that audit finds a material mathematical
error.

Audit update, 31 October 2026: L575 Section 7 now verifies the local
specialization, retaining the precisely cited analytic theorems as inputs.
Both cubic costs, the complete endpoint sum, lattice constants and signed
transfer pass. The fixed-square little-o route is therefore stopped under
those inputs. The initial unaudited status above records the first calculation;
it is superseded by this audit. Negative mass and alternative squares remain
independent gaps. No RH counterexample is claimed.
