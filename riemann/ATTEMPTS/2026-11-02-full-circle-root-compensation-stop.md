[L663](../lemmas/L663-integer-lattice-full-circle-logarithmic-obstruction.md).

the complete grid, conjugated angle and all M spatial moments.

analytic argument and reports a complete replay of its interval
evidence; the source manuscripts and an independent interval backend

sequence lies below the rate needed even when the entire circle is
used without a shrinking-arc measure charge. By L657's exact identity,
the full numerator-zero compensation also has this deficit: no pole
or cancelled zero has been omitted. This stops uniform full-circle
averaging and the stronger full-circle-to-maximum subroute. It does
not upper-bound every localized arc, refute the joint target or bear
on RH directly. A continuation must use angular localization or a
different independently testable mechanism. The earlier zero-evaluation,
fractional-mixture, deweighting and half-turn records remain intact.
