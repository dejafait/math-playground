# Direct gamma/Rice size continuation: a specific domain failure

WHY IT FAILS: [L718](../lemmas/L718-exact-gamma-size-interpolation-and-rice-obstruction.md)
proves that the natural gamma continuation of the moving coefficient
product has infinitely many genuine poles accumulating at the retained
index zero for noninteger real sizes. It therefore fails the finite Rice
theorem's analytic-domain hypothesis, although the integer formula is
exact. Isolating zero gives a convergent analytic interpolation with exact
samples, but its verified pole at q=-1/4+2i/33 can be removed while
preserving every sample. It is a size-interpolation pole, not a located
finite singularity of the generating variable. No global Lindelof growth
bound or signed-rate comparison was established. Stop this particular
template; reopening requires a different exact representation or a
proved growth-and-singularity mechanism. This does not exclude all size
methods, regularized contours or the fixed profile. Preserve the earlier
absolute-contour, positive-blend, step-175 and common-phase stops.
