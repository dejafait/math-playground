# Pointwise quadratic-root counting — 2026-09-14

The exact coprime-sector regrouping and its elementary root estimate are
proved in [L188](../lemmas/L188-coprime-quadratic-roots-in-the-exact-interval.md).

WHY IT FAILS: counting each unit root class separately costs one possible
representative even though the allowed interval is shorter than the
modulus. Summing over all moduli and displacements gives a bound worse
than the inherited divisor estimate. Replacing that one by the interval
length divided by the modulus is not a valid pointwise bound. This
failure concerns the tested counting argument, not the possibility of
averaged distribution or signed cancellation, and is not a counterexample
to RH.
