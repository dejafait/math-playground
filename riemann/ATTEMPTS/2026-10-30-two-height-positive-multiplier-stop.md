# Two-height positive-multiplier certificate: audited stop

The complete obstruction is in
[L505](../lemmas/L505-two-height-positive-multiplier-obstruction.md).
It covers exactly the approved family E_a+alpha P_b+beta P_{2b}, with
arbitrary alpha,beta>=0 at every sufficiently large real T. Its separate
critical audit passed on 2026-10-30 within the stated full-space scope;
the canonical proof contains the replay and common-onset checks.

WHY IT FAILS: choosing a cosine phase with cos(sa)=0 and sin(sa)=-1
eliminates its gap term and makes both clipped boundary integrals
negative. Positivity and monotonicity of both kernels give quantitative
sign margins. A smooth compact-Fourier envelope preserves the three
negative signs, so every nonnegative coefficient reinforces the same
residual. The resulting full-space weighted norm is at least
c_R L^(R/4)/a^2, whose ratio to the required O_R(a) threshold diverges.
The witness is outside H_a: neither the positive-class E bound nor the
weaker Q bound is refuted. No arbitrary-measure or actual-Xi conclusion
follows.

Stop this two-scalar family. Larger or a-dependent nonnegative scalar
coefficients cannot repair the failure. Preserve L504's narrower stop
and the positive-class distinction. This outcome does not authorize a
second coefficient search or general-measure optimization.
