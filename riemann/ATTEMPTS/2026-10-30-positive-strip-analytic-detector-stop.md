# Everywhere-positive analytic detector: Harnack cost stop

The quantitative necessary bound and its hypotheses are canonical in
[L502](../lemmas/L502-positive-strip-harnack-detector-cost.md).
This is a standard-source specialization, not new harmonic-function
theory or a zero-density result. Its stated hypotheses, constant and
class stop passed the independent critical audit on 2026-10-30 recorded
in the canonical result.

WHY IT FAILS: at delta=log log T/log T, positivity of Re f throughout
the entire horizontal zero strip propagates the rectangle's required
value to a real interval of length proportional to log T. Harnack then
forces the K-weighted main term to be at least log T/(128pi), whereas
the requested bound is C log log T (log T)^(-c) for fixed C,c>0.
This is an actual lower bound for every member of the stipulated
class, rather than failure of a construction or a losing upper bound.
Fourier support, evenness and the rest of the rectangle cannot repair
this contradiction. The existing L500/L501 correlation stops are
distinct and remain unchanged.

Park this everywhere-positive detector class at this scale. Reopening
construction requires a
materially changed positivity domain, geometry or signed mechanism
with separate source coverage; merely changing the Fourier support
does not avoid the bound. No failure of signed detectors, the desired
weighted-count estimate or RH follows. Moving-test arithmetic errors,
height/kernel transfers and individual Xi signs remain unresolved.
