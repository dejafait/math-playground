# Direct Laplace saturation: actual-height weighted-transform scope stop

The exact observable, primary statements, parameter restrictions and
comparison are in the [completed source screen](../drafts/literature/2026-10-31-laplace-saturation-source-screen.md).
Canonical actual-function definitions and existing limitations remain
in L477, L481, L482 and L484. No new lemma or mathematical script was
written, and neither alternative for the saturation correlation was
proved.

WHY IT FAILS: the scalar reciprocal Laplace integral is available, but
the inspected Fourier-Laplace theorem evaluates linear prime-block sums
on a good set, with restricted transform parameters. It does not evaluate
the nonlinear all-integer saturation with either residual phase, its
weighted exceptional error or the parameter-integrated signed error at
T(log T)^3 scale. The accessible source therefore does not authorize
the proposed direct evaluation. This is a source-to-method mismatch,
not a counterexample to the desired correlation or a general ban on
Laplace representations. Reopening requires a precise matching joint
weighted estimate or a materially changed mechanism removing its need.
Keep the narrower polynomial, weighted-cubic, sign-leakage and Haar
stops with their original qualifications. The constructive Fourier-dual
nomination is unreviewed and proves nothing about the absolute defect,
negative mass or RH.
