# Specified global Haar truncation: nonvanishing error

The approved diagnostic fixes y=T^(1/1000), the real phase quotient
over p<=y and the rational-frequency rectangle with y-smooth
a,b<=T^(1/100). The completed exact estimate and full proof are
canonical in [L484](../lemmas/L484-real-euler-phase-haar-truncation-obstruction.md).
The proof awaits a separate critical audit.

WHY IT FAILS: vanishing L2 error on the full probability Haar torus
is impossible in this rectangle: L484 gives a fixed positive liminf
for the distance to its entire complex span, retaining the real-part
reflection. Thus a method requiring such a vanishing global error
cannot use this truncation. The evidence stops this implementation,
not the proposed actual-height correlation or the possibility of a
different approximation. No Haar-to-height or residual-weighted
transfer was assumed or proved. The earlier missing source scope
for the full correlation remains preserved.

Reopening this same vanishing-error implementation requires an
identified error in L484. A different norm, support or weighted
height mechanism is a changed target requiring its own source gate;
this historical stop is not a global ban on phase multipliers.
