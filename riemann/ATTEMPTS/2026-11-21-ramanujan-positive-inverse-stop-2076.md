# Ramanujan universal positive convolution inverse: stop2076

The gate-approved one-frequency test fails: [C034q](../lemmas/C034q-ramanujan-positive-convolution-inverse-obstruction.md)
proves v(1)>=4 against the required |v(1)|<=1, with the original
denominator and Gamma recurrence factors retained. This is a
REPRODUCTION using assessment2075, not a new general positivity theorem.

## WHY IT FAILS

Any finite positive inverse measure for the normalized multiplier would
have total mass one, so its two-point Fourier matrix must be positive
semidefinite. C034q gives determinant at most -15. This rejects the
universal positive convolution-removal operation before any proposed
signed modular coefficient can be transferred through it. Signed or
distributional inverses, actual-data-only operations and identities
retaining paid corrections are outside this obstruction. In particular
neither modular methods nor RH are disproved. No frequency sweep or
stronger inverse test is warranted for this operation.
