# Total-frequency integration alone — 2026-09-13

The exact identity and scope are proved in
[Lemma 160](../lemmas/L160-total-frequency-integration-with-a-cutoff.md).

WHY IT FAILS: Removing the nonzero total frequency by integration by
parts differentiates the amplitude-selected weight. With an explicitly
C² cutoff this produces χ''(Q/M)Q'U/M², whose available absolute
bound grows with T at fixed M. Product-frequency Hilbert grouping avoids
a worse termwise estimate but does not close this bound. Collected
primitive coefficients can grow quadratically, so universal algebraic
removal of the denominator also fails. None of these upper bounds or
formal coefficient examples disproves uniform control of the actual
signed correlation; that estimate remains unproved.
