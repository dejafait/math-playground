# Selected signed moments do not force capped nondegeneracy

Step 161's complete prescribed-response counterlaw is canonical in
[L421](../lemmas/L421-prescribed-response-vanishing-capped-cubic-counterlaw.md).
It retains the actual response, curvature and uniform height marginal,
all selected moments and fixed envelopes; its capped coefficient
vanishes for every fixed lambda. Step 163's separate critical audit in
the same canonical file validates the complete selected-relaxation claim.

WHY IT FAILS: A positive signed quartic need not be carried at amplitudes
comparable to the cap. L421 puts its quartic contribution at a higher
amplitude, and supplies the signed cubic separately below the cap.
The latter's subleading second-moment cost can be subtracted from the
bulk covariance without losing its negative-cone support. Thus the
full selected list does not force the proposed positive capped liminf.
This does not evaluate L418's actual integral or rule out additional
actual-function tail/correlation information. A future reopening needs
a specific constraint violated by this law, with separate coverage;
renaming the same moment-only lower certificate is not a new test.
