# One-sided dual attempt: exact reduction, period still open

2026-11-06. The canonical proof is
[L868](../lemmas/L868-one-sided-closed-dual-coefficient-reduction.md),
newly written and awaiting independent conceptual audit. This is not
a stop of the full one-sided class. The audited absolute-class stop
in L867 retains its original scope.

**WHY IT FAILS TO FINISH.** The upper-only inequality leaves a
nonnegative angular-area correction. Closedness couples its radial
derivatives to the other coefficients, so it no longer implies a
holomorphic coefficient. L868 gives a global zero-period example
showing that radial dependence really occurs. The constant angular
mean is exactly the period parameter, but its available absolute
integral bound is weaker than the required threshold. Only the
radial-independent subclass is stopped by L867. No full-class
no-gain theorem or strict-gain certificate has been obtained.

The exact coefficient and closure characterization is a local
research advance, with no originality claim beyond the bounded
source comparison and no RH conclusion. A new period attempt
requires materially new control of the correction; a repeated
holomorphic reduction or zero-period radial ansatz is insufficient.
