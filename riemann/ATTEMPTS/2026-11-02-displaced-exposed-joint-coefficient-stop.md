# Complete joint numerator: exposed-coefficient mechanism stopped

The complete hypotheses, coefficient identities, source citation,
support qualification and proof are in
[L670](../lemmas/L670-displaced-exposed-joint-coefficient-cost-stop.md).

WHY IT FAILS: For a dense set of L666's admissible displacement vectors,
the complete numerator has a rectangular support hull. Every extremal
support coefficient is a corner, so its Akhtari--Vaaler binomial cost
is already zero. Nevertheless the exact rational denominator charge
leaves every such certified lower value at most
log(M/R)-41(n-1)/121, which falls below -n/100-C log n for every
fixed C. This stops that specified clearing and exposed-coefficient
mechanism uniformly; it is not an upper bound for H. Exceptional
support, nonextremal coefficients with their actual binomial costs,
spatial-face restrictions, other clearings and signed angular proofs
remain outside this stop. No finite-angle or full-angular violating
sequence for H has been obtained.
