# Exact endpoint filter: positive fixed-cutoff volume test stops

10 November 2026. The uniform-section attempt tested whether a positive
mean logarithmic determinant could force an expanding direction in a
two-solution family. The precise bounded-average result, division-free
identity and qualifications are in
[L992](../lemmas/L992-endpoint-filter-zero-regularized-volume-rate.md).
Step274 independently audited the bounded-average proof and its
qualifications under the separately approved review272; they pass.

WHY IT FAILS: reflection exchanges the two leading outer coefficients,
and the exact all-index logarithmic ratio has mean zero at every fixed
positive cutoff. The proposed certificate needs a strictly positive
rate. Collision-neighborhood visits can be bounded for a fixed cutoff,
but their uncut logarithmic contribution remains unpaid. Singular
exact rows impose determinant constraints and cannot be removed.
This stops only the positive fixed-cutoff volume certificate; it does
not settle uncut products, growth in individual directions, the fixed
anchor, or the E-to-S implication. Preserve working271's finite
certificate and L991's separate fixed-pivot stop. Reopening this
certificate requires a materially different, solution-specific or
exact-tail input, not a larger finite sample or another cutoff value.
