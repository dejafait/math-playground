# Complete spatial face: degree-index coefficient transfer stopped

The exact family, source imports, all-index envelope and proof are
canonical in
[L671](../lemmas/L671-displaced-spatial-face-binomial-cost-stop.md).

WHY IT FAILS: After the exact denominator normalization, each complete
numerator summand has n-1 pairs of angular factors whose absolute
coefficient polynomial is bounded by (4/5)(1+v)^2. Multiplying the
remaining factors bounds every degree-index normalized coefficient by
(M/R)(4/5)^(n-1). Thus the exact B_face certificate is below
log(M/R)-(n-1)/5, uniformly over all displacements; it cannot reach
-n/100-C log n. This is a proved upper bound on a sufficient lower
certificate, not on H. The undeformed family already demonstrates the
distinction: the certificate fails where H has a much stronger known
lower bound. Sparse support costs, different clearings and genuinely
signed angular information remain outside this stop. Do not repeat
the exposed endpoints or this complete-face binomial transfer.
