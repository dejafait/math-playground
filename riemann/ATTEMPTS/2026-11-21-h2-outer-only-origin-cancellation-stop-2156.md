# Outer-only signed H2 head: exact cancellation stop

21 November 2026, step2156. The fixed five-functional audit is
canonical in [C919c](../lemmas/C919c-outer-rectangle-origin-cancellation.md).
It retains all origin residues, multiplicities and closed-band
boundary prerequisites, including C919b's arithmetic contracts.

**WHY IT FAILS.** The weighted outer contour contributes the origin
residue -2S_k as well as twice the interior opposite-pair sum.
Consequently each of the five I_k is exactly -2 times an exterior
power sum. Reconstructing the signed head still needs the unpaid
S_2,...,S_6 or independent equivalent information. Computing these
outer integrals alone cannot supply the proposed head margin by
this residue identity. C919c also transfers the one-sided opposing
budget to the rectangular complement; that removes a cutoff issue,
but supplies no head sign. This stops the outer-only mechanism, not
every contour method or the target positivity statement. An added
inner observable requires separate coverage and a discriminating
accuracy test; no such computation or inference is made here.

The independent L917/L920/L922 stops and all existing certificates
remain unchanged. This is an informative representation obstruction,
not a disproof of RH or a claim of progress beyond known methods.
