# Reflected multiplicity remainder — pointwise cost stop

The full argument and precise qualifications are in
[L652](../lemmas/L652-reflected-multiplicity-remainder-pointwise-cost.md).

WHY IT FAILS: For n=12q>=24, the admissible single anchor of residue
m=n/3 imposes m Taylor conditions on the reflected-weight remainder.
At u=1 its norm is at least exp(n/25)/(2sqrt(m)), while its positive
residue pairing is m/R. The complete normalized response, including
the negative-part correction, misses squared rate 1/16 with margin
7/400. Yet the constant polynomial gives joint relative energy at
least 21/121 on the same family. Thus a uniform pointwise cost estimate
for this multiplicity remainder cannot supply the intended certificate.
One angle does not control the angular mean; this is no obstruction to
the joint target or RH. The close pair remains polynomially affordable.
The refined test removes unused repeated-jet conditions and evaluates
the full angular cost, retaining the possibility of expensive near
coalescence among distinct unit-residue poles.
