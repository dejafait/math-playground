# Fixed U12 retained-zero box certificate881: parked

One fixed workload with the ten published coarse isolating intervals
failed to certify a lower Gram margin exceeding C750d's epsilon_*.
The full proof, source qualifications, error norms, pairing over complex
coefficients and numerical evidence are canonical in
[C750e](../lemmas/C750e-fixed-u12-retained-zero-box-diagnostic.md).

WHY IT FAILS: Whole-panel integration over the entire uncertain frequency
interval loses the oscillatory cancellations. Every projected sample
box includes zero, and both Gram entry boxes admit the zero matrix.
The finite errors prevent a positive shifted LDL pivot already at the
first step. This is enclosure failure, not a rigorous nonpositive exact
Gram matrix or a proof that the ten actual roots are insufficient.
The fixed evaluator is parked without root refinement, higher precision,
new data or a mode/window change; this stop does not ban other methods.
An independent central mechanism needs separate source coverage next.
