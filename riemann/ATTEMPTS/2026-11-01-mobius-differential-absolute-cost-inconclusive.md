# Weighted Mobius certificate: absolute bounds do not settle the cost

The exact zero-safe identity, full derivative terms and sufficient
threshold are canonical in
[L625](../lemmas/L625-mobius-weighted-quartic-differential-reduction.md).
The approved target retains the raw normalized Mobius amplifier and
one common real observer. No coefficient family or taper was changed.

WHY IT FAILS: Integration by parts gives a nonnegative quartic bulk,
but its derivative-weight correction has no imported sign. Already
for c=(1,0), Cauchy--Schwarz gives the lower bound
4P-2sqrt(PD)-|S|-|C|, with P,D,S,C defined in L625. The available
absolute estimates bound P and D from above, not D/P from above or
P from below. Thus they do not establish the required strict margin
above L622's full transfer error. The ordinary smooth-weight cost is
smaller than the leading transfer-error scale, and completion is
controlled; the first unresolved cost is the actual correlated
amplifier derivative. No negative sign, counterexample to the desired
inequality or general impossibility of integration by parts was proved.

This ends the absolute-bound test, not the whole physical route.
The exact ratio criterion is a refined, independently measurable
continuation inside the same ready source scope: use the complete
moment formulas for the actual P,D with rescaled derivative coefficients.
It needs new quantitative evidence before continuation, not a replay
of bandwidth bounds. The earlier row/freezing/raw-boundary stops are
preserved. Other observers and pointwise Xi signs remain unresolved.
