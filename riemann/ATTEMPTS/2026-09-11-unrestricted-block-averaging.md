# Unrestricted upward block averaging — 2026-09-11

Attempt: extend vanishing full upward block averages from monotone
square-root multipliers to all increasing coordinates with summable
reciprocal squares and strictly increasing bounded heights.

WHY IT FAILS: [Lemma 83](../lemmas/L083-close-pairs-obstruct-unrestricted-upward-averaging.md)
gives widely separated pairs whose internal horizontal and vertical gaps
are equal and tiny. The resulting odd-index contributions force every
large block average to diverge, despite reciprocal-square summability.
Even-index contributions tend to zero, so this refutes the averaging
extension but not the weaker existence of a favorable subsequence.
