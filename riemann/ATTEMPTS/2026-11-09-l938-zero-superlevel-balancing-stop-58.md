# Zero-superlevel preservation under balancing fails

The full statement, source hypotheses, six exact root/sign tables and
rational counterexample are canonical in
[L941](../lemmas/L941-two-block-zero-superlevel-balancing-obstruction.md).

WHY IT FAILS: The actual 34-row polynomial's nonnegative set is not
inward closed even on the approved two-block paths. For m=4,
q0=541/4096 increases toward equality to q1=271/2048, with strictly
positive starting value and strictly negative ending value. The
normalized endpoint is a doubly stochastic mixture of the initial
point and the uniform vector. Complete exact root isolation also finds
inward exits for m=3 and m=5; all sixteen positive roots are simple.
This is a distinct obstruction from L940's negative-valued Schur
witness. No expanded grid can restore the global sign-set assertion
for this unchanged polynomial. L938's integrated-density crossing
count and Xi positivity remain open; other mechanisms are not banned.
