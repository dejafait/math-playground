# Pure height-weight microscopic localization: stop

The approved coefficient-normalized test is settled in
[L620](../lemmas/L620-height-weight-microscopic-concentration-stop.md).
It specializes Montgomery–Vaughan and Hughes–Young rather than proving
a new arithmetic theorem. Coefficients depending on the selected height
are allowed; the fixed smooth denominator is retained.

WHY IT FAILS: For length N<=T^(1/11-epsilon), the mass fraction in
any interval of length 1/log T tends uniformly to zero, whereas this
proposed pure height-concentration mechanism required a fraction
bounded away from zero. The coefficient-summed raw twisted error is
o(T), so a source-error repair cannot fix this concentration failure.
This is no obstruction theorem for weights correlated with Hardy jets
or for the signed integral itself: the observable can be unbounded,
and pure weight mass alone does not control its contribution.

Do not repeat the same concentration test under renamed coefficients.
A distinct continuation would retain the signed arithmetic main terms
and test their spectral structure; its source coverage is still required.
