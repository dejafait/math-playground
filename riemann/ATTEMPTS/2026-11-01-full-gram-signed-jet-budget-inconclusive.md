# Full-Gram signed jet budget: available first-jet cost is inconclusive

The uniform finite-jet reduction, exact coefficients and qualifications
are canonical in
[L629](../lemmas/L629-full-gram-finite-signed-jet-reduction.md).
Its proof and L627/L628 still await separate critical audits.

WHY IT FAILS: Complete six-term cancellation removes the pole problem
and makes arithmetic degrees through eight sufficient, with a uniform
relative O(1/log T) phase/regular-zeta error. However, applying the
available scalar first-jet norm estimate to the exact degree-one
coefficient already spends 64/75+O(1/log T) for the derivative observer,
against a total relative allowance 1/2. The higher degrees remain
uncontrolled in the required signed combination. This upper-bound
cost gives no actual defect, negative eigenvalue or obstruction to
the comparison; higher degrees may cancel it. Repeating the existing
norm estimate inside a termwise absolute budget does not resolve it.

The materially new input is the exact finite signed functional. A
separately reviewed numerical-radius estimate for the centered
compressed first-jet multiplier is an independently testable refinement;
it would leave higher-degree and full signed comparison gaps open.
The full Gram tail, analytic normalization, common observer and actual
Xi transfer must all remain. This stop is not a ban on the arithmetic
route or a claim about RH.
