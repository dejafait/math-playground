# Independent radial contour choices cannot give a nonnegative form

2026-11-06. The full proof and its exact qualifications are in
[L847](../lemmas/L847-actual-g-product-radial-graphs-cannot-be-nonnegative.md).
The independent conceptual audit of 6 November 2026 accepts the proof
at this scope; its detailed checks are recorded in L847. This is the scoped negative
result of the approved full-phase graph attempt, not a solution of
the arbitrary coupled graph problem.

**WHY IT FAILS.** For a product graph the complete oriented density
separates into one factor for each curve times the squared difference.
Nonnegativity of ratios at two fixed points forces both radial curves
onto the same circle. The zeros excluded in forming those ratios are
finite and are restored by continuity. On that circle the forced real
boundary value contradicts the actual G's Fourier coefficients: the
centered case has an unmatched positive coefficient, and the off-center
case has negative coefficient root limit |c|/rho>0 while the positive
coefficients come from an entire remainder and have root limsup zero.
This stops independent one-variable deformations even with no smallness
bound. It does not exclude cross-coordinate dependence or use a
nonzero-Jacobian premise for the unrestricted graph. Preserve the
earlier finite-box, saddle-collar and automatic-sign stops at their
original scopes. A new coupled mechanism would require its own precise
test; merely varying the two independent radial profiles cannot help.
