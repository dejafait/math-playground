# Signed positive-bump rate: exact reduction, comparison still unpaid

WHY IT FAILS: The bounded attempt reduces the actual fixed-profile
R_n(3/20) exactly to the finite signed formula in
[L714](../lemmas/L714-signed-positive-bump-finite-reduction.md), but
does not bound its exponential rate. The real-root factorization
proves nonvanishing of the inverse density of W_n; that property
does not pass through the resolvent convolution or the fixed real
bump integral. The residue's exact rate is therefore not a remainder
rate. Termwise absolute values would erase the two remaining
cancellations and repeat the wrong kind of certificate. No saddle,
relative error, limiting root law or bump-transform lower bound has
been paid. This is an inconclusive attempt, not a competing signed
contribution or a proof that the profile cannot work. Preserve the
absolute-contour and positive-blend stops separately. The newly
exposed polynomial logarithmic limit is a different, independently
testable intermediate input and needs its own source assessment;
even a successful limit would leave the outer cancellation unresolved.
