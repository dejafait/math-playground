# Two signed spatial-growth arcs do not pay the angular complement

WHY IT FAILS AS A GLOBAL COMPLETION: [C708a](../lemmas/C708a-signed-spatial-growth-on-two-inner-arcs.md)
proves bounded B_sp contributions on two fixed arcs and a genuine
relative finite-tail estimate near u=1/2. Its remaining upper bound
is (1-mu(I_+ union I_-))*n*log(65/64)/3+O(1), with a strictly
positive coefficient, whereas the target requires o(n). This does
not lower-bound the actual complementary growth or stop the B_sp
route. Repeating the local radius extension, extrapolating either
signed local limit, or substituting the stopped absolute-envelope
budget would add no complementary estimate. A further attempt must
test the complete signed ratio on an unpaid fixed angular set,
retaining F_n-T_n and the actual S_(n,1) normalization. The original
interpolation and absolute-envelope obstructions remain intact.
