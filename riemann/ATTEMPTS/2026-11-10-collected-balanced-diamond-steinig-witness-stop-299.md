# Balanced collected Diamond-Steinig witness

10 November 2026. The exact review297 full C_n rate test remains open.
The natural witness ell=(m,m,m), n=3m, is settled by
[L999](../lemmas/L999-collected-balanced-diamond-steinig-cancellation.md).

WHY IT FAILS: L998's first-family summand grows exponentially, but
every ordered split contributes with the opposite sign. After full
collection its ratio to that signed first-family term is exactly
-1/binom(3m-1,m); the surviving coefficient has magnitude at most
3^(1-m). Thus this witness supplies no positive exponential lower
rate for C_n. The proof accounts for both families and all weights.
It does not show that C_n is subexponential. Preserve stop296 and
the arithmetic gaps. A uniform root/coefficient upper-bound mechanism
is a different, unassessed lead; do not repeat the balanced-witness
test without materially new input. This is no ban on other vectors,
representations, signed arithmetic estimates or the RH problem.
