# Reverse Levy ordering: stop1553

18 November 2026. The reverse direction is ruled out by the qualified
proof in [C032s](../lemmas/C032s-finite-levy-difference-endpoint-control.md).

WHY IT FAILS: ell_1-ell_2 is strictly negative for all sufficiently
small positive times. It therefore cannot be a nonnegative additional
Levy density, even though C032s now proves finite absolute mass for
either sign of the difference. This stops only this ordering. The
forward difference ell_2-ell_1 is positive near zero and for t>=1/192,
but its intermediate sign remains unproved. No obstruction to every
signed positive output or to RH follows. Stop1302's stronger finite-law
full-cancellation obstruction remains separate and unchanged.
