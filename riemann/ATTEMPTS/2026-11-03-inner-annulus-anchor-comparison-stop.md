# Uniform anchor comparison on the two inner contours

WHY IT FAILS: [L705](../lemmas/L705-inner-annulus-anchor-comparison-obstruction.md)
tests K_0=Q*T_M(x*zeta/R)/R against the complete K for the same fixed
actual L703 profile. At the negative-real points on both |u|=1/4 and
|u|=1/2, the complete nonanchor spatial logarithmic mean is
n*Phi_r(9/20)+O(log n), with Phi_r(9/20)>3/40, whereas the anchor
mean is exactly -log R. Both signed endpoint sums and all spatial
moments are retained. Thus a nonempty open spatial set violates strict
anchor dominance on each comparison contour, even before attempting
radial integration of a zero count. This stops that uniform Rouche
certificate, not the aggregate inner charge or the profile itself.
L705 is unaudited; no actual charge deficit is established. A comparison
retaining endpoints and paying angular transition logarithms is a
materially different possible mechanism and remains unproved.
