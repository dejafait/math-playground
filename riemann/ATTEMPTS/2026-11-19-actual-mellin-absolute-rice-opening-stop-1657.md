# Actual-E absolute vertical Rice opening — fixed-line stop1657

WHY IT FAILS. [C048k](../lemmas/C048k-actual-mellin-rice-vertical-divergence.md)
specializes the finite Rice formula correctly but proves exponential
growth of its weighted actual E on Re(s)=-1/4, for every fixed d>=3.
The two-sided Mellin remainder yields decay of logarithmic size
-(pi+o(1))|t|/log|t|; the reciprocal gamma supplies rate +pi/2.
The polynomial Rice denominator cannot compensate. Thus absolute
vertical integration fails, rather than merely lacking an upper bound.
Independent conceptual audit1659, recorded in C048k, accepts this
calculation without correction. The finite identity
retains all edges. This does not rule out conditional regularization,
other interpolants or contours, or RH. Reopening requires a materially
different representation or a specific cancellation mechanism with an
independent bounded test; increasing the fixed degree does not repair
this absolute integral. Preserve the distinct L718 and prior source
stops.
