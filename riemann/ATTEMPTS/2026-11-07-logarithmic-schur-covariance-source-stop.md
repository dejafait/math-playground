diagonal coefficient. The known cumulant identities do not supply the
missing sign. L044 and L057 remain canonical warnings against generic
Gram transfer and finite-test extrapolation, with their stated scope.

requires an explicit additional actual-law identity or estimate and a
separately checkable bounded discriminator; another generic variance
constant or a finite positive determinant does not fill this interface.
