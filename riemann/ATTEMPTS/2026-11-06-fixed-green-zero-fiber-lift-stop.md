# The fixed Green forcing cannot satisfy one full zero-fiber trace

2026-11-06. The one approved fixed-h lift test has a full-class
obstruction in
[C869a](../lemmas/C869a-fixed-green-forcing-zero-fiber-closure-obstruction.md),
accepted by the separate exact audit of 6 November 2026 in that
canonical file. This is a NEGATIVE result for
realizing L869's h, not a counterexample to RH or a period theorem.

**WHY IT FAILS.** The complete q=r=0 fiber trace forces both
tangential derivatives q_x2 and r_y2 to vanish. The second closure
equation therefore requires h_x2=0 on that fiber. L869's exact
cutoff has a nonzero derivative there at a finite interior point,
for every phase and H>0. C869a gives the full proof and exact
residual. All homogeneous and compatible zero modes are covered
because this is a necessary identity for the original functions,
not a test of a chosen Green solution or finite Fourier ansatz.

Preserve L869's abstract Green counterexample and the older period
stops. The unchanged fixed h cannot be rescued by other modes.
Any later construction needs a materially changed forcing that
satisfies the full tangential trace condition and separate coverage;
whether such a construction retains a negative Green form is open.
Full actual-F admissibility, the period threshold, graph realization
and actual-Xi mixed positivity remain unresolved.
