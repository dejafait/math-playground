# Prescribed exponential top-envelope exhaustion: stop

The approved finite-domain calculation gives a fixed-R,T exponential
lower bound for J_H/a along X_H=H^2+a+B+2. Full hypotheses, constants,
finite-domain regularity and proof are in
[L506](../lemmas/L506-finite-obstacle-top-envelope-divergence.md).
The separate 30 October 2026 critical audit passes, including the exact
Harnack factor, explicit constant and certificate-only interpretation;
the audit details and corrected sharp-Harnack citation are canonical
in L506. It is a local
negative result from standard harmonic comparison methods, not a
new theorem claimed beyond the sources checked.

WHY IT FAILS: A fixed corridor to the right of the finite obstacle
transmits a positive portion of the top datum at a rate slower than
the uniform exponential Fourier envelope grows. A fixed Harnack chain
below the obstacle transfers that portion to a nonzero interval in
the gap. Thus this top-envelope cost cannot remain bounded as the
specified truncations grow; adding other nonnegative envelope costs
cannot cancel it. No admissible function is asserted to attain the
envelope, so this is not a lower bound on its actual negative part.

Stop removal of this prescribed uniform top envelope along this
exhaustion. Preserve the original H_a cost question, finite-height
optimization, different exhaustions and function-dependent estimates
as unresolved. Reopening this stopped calculation needs an audit
defect or a materially changed boundary estimate or geometry, not
another qualitative maximum principle. No actual-Xi conclusion follows.
