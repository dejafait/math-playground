# Logarithmic L2 metric: large-real-zero obstruction (step156)

The complete common-profile estimate and Gram consequence are in
[L909](../lemmas/L909-large-real-zero-common-profile-excludes-logarithmic-l2-symmetry.md).
The step156 write-up was initially unaudited; the separate step157
audit below confirms it. It specializes the previously assessed
endpoint expansion and theta bounds; no originality or RH result is
claimed. It does not use the inconclusive step154 phase calculation.

WHY IT FAILS: The proposed logarithmic L2 form makes sufficiently large
distinct real-zero L-images have a strictly positive real Gram part.
Hedenmalm's pair symmetry would require those same images to be
orthogonal. The uniform norm estimate, nonzero profile and quantified
pair conclusion in L909 give a mathematical obstruction independent of
the parked first-pair and numerical-access attempts. It is not an
obstruction to other weights or nonlocal forms, and does not contradict
the source's conditional theorem or RH.

The step156 decision was to retire this particular metric if the
separate critical audit passed.
The unchanged common-profile target and its prior SPECIALIZE assessment
cover that audit; no further literature is needed absent a new gap.
The original domain, positive-realization import, first-pair phase and
certification stops remain preserved. Reopening this metric requires a
specific error in L909 or changed mathematical data; a different form
would be a new target needing its own assessment.

Audit decision, 9 November 2026 (step157): the canonical L909 audit
passes, including both derivative primitives, the uniform full-norm
envelope, nonzero profile and simultaneous pair threshold. The exact
ODE also verifies that both sides of the pair-symmetry test are defined.
Retire this dt/t metric at the stated scope. No other metric is excluded
by this audit. The next nomination concerns source discovery for an
explicit unbounded nonlocal form and has REVIEW_REQUIRED coverage;
neither an unboundedness necessity theorem nor a replacement is claimed.
