# Witness/upper-tail threshold matching — 2026-10-25

The approved comparison is completed in
[C463a](../lemmas/C463a-witness-upper-tail-intersection.md).
It gives a strictly shorter finite witness interval for fixed choices
3/16<c_0/2<c, with exact rounding and one existential tail onset.
This is a local application of known inputs, not a new analytic bound.

WHY IT FAILS: As a mechanism for crossing the logarithmic threshold or
removing the initial signs, matching these two ranges stops here.
For every fixed admissible pair, the surviving cutoff has leading
coefficient min(c,c_0/2)>3/16. Both separate input families already
approach that infimum; the combination supplies no uniform endpoint
or signs below its remaining cutoff. The bounded-height remainder
has no evaluated overlap with the existential tail onset. This is
not an impossibility theorem for other mechanisms or a refutation of
RH. Reopening needs a genuinely stronger input, such as signs below
the present threshold or uniform endpoint control, not another choice
of the same fixed constants.

The different proposed continuation tests the role of real-root
multiplicity in L257's finite-level masking. It has a separate pending
literature assessment and does not invoke the external manuscripts.
