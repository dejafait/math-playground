# Jensen small remainder budget obstruction1391

The canonical [C019o proof](../lemmas/C019o-moving-first-absolute-moment-and-remainder-obstruction.md)
derives the first absolute moment of the actual moving sum and a lower
bound for C019n's exact weighted remainder. This first derivation awaits
independent conceptual audit; no numerical or RH input is used.

WHY IT FAILS: the ordinary fractional-moment theorem transfers through
a uniformly dominated real Fourier kernel with paid phases, floors and
tails. The resulting concentration below the square-root-log amplitude
scale forces the pointwise-negative diagonal's weighted integral to
have magnitude at least c*B*sqrt(q). The full normalized product is
bounded in magnitude by B/2. Its exact remainder therefore necessarily
cancels a growing part of the diagonal and cannot satisfy the proposed
fixed upper bound c_D*B/2. The diagonal-plus-small-remainder sufficient
closure is stopped, while the sign of the full product and (N) remain
open. A continuation after audit needs a different signed balance or
an independently testable mechanism; another attempt to prove this
same small-R bound is contradicted by C019o if the audit retains it.

The origin/variation stop1387 and earlier stops1345/1355/559 retain
their scopes. This obstruction is neither a refutation of (N) nor a
disproof of RH. Separate review of C019o is the immediate continuation.
