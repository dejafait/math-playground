# Generic admissible critical-point signing stops

The fixed test succeeded in the independently audited canonical
[L738 proof](../lemmas/L738-modulated-admissible-kernel-positive-critical-minimum.md).
Its positive even strictly log-concave kernel satisfies all of
Csordas's admissibility conditions, yet its Fourier transform has a
positive strict stationary minimum in (6,7), with an explicitly
negative first Laguerre expression. The exact parameters were not
changed or swept.

WHY IT FAILS: A proof that signs every nonzero real Fourier critical
value using only these generic shape and decay hypotheses would apply
to L738 and contradict its signed margins. The stationarity restriction
does not repair that generic inference. This conclusion concerns only
this implication; it does not rule out additional theta-specific
hypotheses, an actual-Xi mechanism, or other unrelated routes. Step 265
checked the full analytic proof and corrected the source-convention
wording; all twelve scalar comparisons also passed. The scalar replay
alone checks only arithmetic. Do not promote this route stop
into an RH statement or a blanket ban on critical-point arguments.
