# Adjacent conjugate construction — scoped failure1058

The approved exact adjacent matching class remains open. The actual
conjugate construction and its full proof are in
[C467s](../lemmas/C467s-actual-upper-pair-and-adjacent-trace-mismatch.md),
currently unaudited. It supplies an upper-pair adjoint and j=1 triangle
with all separate complex products and full identities, matching their
outer edge, but fails C467r's prescribed radial value.

WHY IT FAILS: C467s(17) shows that the two selected vertex transports
have a nonzero t^(5/2) difference on a bump-transition mean. Actual
conjugation preserves the background and equations, but it does not
equate the lower and upper graph phases after their different singleton
secants enter the determinant. The zero inner radial flux does not
identify the test values. This stops this natural conjugate selection
only; arbitrary adjacent adjoints may carry different incoming side
data. A class obstruction needs a one-sided characteristic incidence
and uniqueness argument, which has not been established. Preserve
stops1000/1009/1023/1043/1051 with their original, different scopes.
