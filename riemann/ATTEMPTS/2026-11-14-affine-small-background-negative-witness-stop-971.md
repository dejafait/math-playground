# Affine small-background negative-witness search — stop971

WHY IT FAILS: For the chosen family H=H_0+Aq+Bz with arbitrary
complex A,B, the full fixed-time Jacobian separates, and the shrinking
normal radius bounds both the trajectory-angle drift and the tangent
angle uniformly up to every surviving time. The complete proof in
[C466v](../lemmas/C466v-affine-small-background-survivor-sign.md)
gives Re W>=cos(299/19900)|W|>0 under the approved 1/100 condition.
Thus constant complex drift cannot produce the sought negative point,
even arbitrarily close to collision. This stops the affine ansatz;
it is not a stop for coupled quadratics, a proof of the universal sign
conjecture or a result about RH. The new proof awaits independent audit.
An off-diagonal quadratic Hessian introduces genuinely mixed tangent
equations and is outside this affine obstruction. Existing stops927,
932, 941, 947, 948, 959, 961 and 968 keep their recorded scopes.
