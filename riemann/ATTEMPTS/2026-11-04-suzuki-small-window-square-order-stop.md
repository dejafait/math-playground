# Suzuki square-order descent: negative small-window witness

The canonical calculation is
[L739](../lemmas/L739-suzuki-small-window-square-order-obstruction.md).
It proves a strictly negative quadratic form for
K_4[t]^2-K_2[t]^2 on the prescribed normalized constant vector for
every sufficiently small t>0, with a uniform remainder. It is a
source specialization; the separate critical audit in step 269 passed.

WHY IT FAILS: The proposed sufficient order K_2[t]^2<=K_4[t]^2
already fails on shrinking windows of the actual complete causal
kernels. Their different vanishing orders at the origin produce the
negative witness. This stops that order mechanism, not the implication
between contractivity statements: both operators may still be
contractive on small windows. It neither refutes RH nor excludes other
descent mechanisms. Do not repeat a window/vector sweep; reopening
requires materially different independently controlled signed input.
