# Prescribed signed inverse-weight cost: outer-data obstruction

The exact hypotheses, normalization, full proof and source qualifications
are canonical in [L668](../lemmas/L668-signed-finite-deweighting-point-cost-stop.md).
This is a new local method obstruction under the approved EXPLORE
assessment, not a disproof of the fixed-angle logarithmic benchmark.

WHY IT FAILS: the simultaneous inverse-weight interpolant must take
an exponentially large value at an existing outer node inside the unit
disk. Its complete binomial coefficient cost dominates that value even
after every signed row cancellation. A fixed fraction of the actual
grid factors forces a rate strictly larger than 1/100, uniformly over
the independent displacements. The exact moment identity is valid, but
the resulting cost-based lower value cannot meet the proposed rate.
This says nothing adverse about the true Mahler mean; even the audited
undisplaced positive baseline has this cost. Repeating inverse-weight
coefficient cancellation or changing an upper estimate cannot repair
the required point value. Other data or a direct full-root mean are
different targets and require their own coverage and quantitative test.
