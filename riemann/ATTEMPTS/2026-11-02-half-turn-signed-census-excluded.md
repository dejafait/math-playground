# Half-turn phase census: signed suppression excluded

The signed endpoint estimate and full paid-log comparison are canonical
in [L662](../lemmas/L662-signed-phase-endpoint-pair-paid-log-bound.md).
The earlier [absolute-cost stop](2026-11-02-phase-census-absolute-contour-cost-stop.md)
is preserved: it did not settle the signed response.

WHY IT FAILS: As a proposed all-arcs countersequence, L661's exact
half-turn unit-residue census fails the signed endpoint-pair test.
L662 gives a nonzero limiting endpoint multiplier, controls both
horizontal integrals and all rescaled vertical tails, and uses c_0,c_1
to prevent simultaneous endpoint cancellation. After the full spatial
root and angular measure costs, an allowed arc has excess at least
9n/50000-5log n-C over log D-n/16. This is a lower bound for the
actual paid expression, unlike the prior positive absolute cost.
The conceptual audit of 2 November 2026 in L662 section 7 validates
this proof and its required inherited analytic inputs. It excludes
only this census; general integer support, the joint comparison and
Xi signs remain open. Reopening suppression for this same census
requires a mathematical error in the new bound, not another finite
screen or an absolute-value estimate. Different support mechanisms
remain eligible under their own source gates.
