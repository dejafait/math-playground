# Growing-N spectral margin: finite screen is inconclusive

The exact full-arithmetic spectral polynomial, prime-power recurrence,
tail contraction and Gram-coordinate sufficient test are canonical
in [L633](../lemmas/L633-full-arithmetic-spectral-polynomial-and-gram-coordinates.md).
The full computational record is
`scripts/full_gram/growing_n_spectral_probe_certificate.json`.

WHY IT FAILS: The bounded attempt obtains an exact finite normal form
and checks small matrices, but it does not prove a bound on the complete
normalized matrix uniform in N and the coefficient vector. The
floating minimum-eigenvalue and row previews for N<=64 have neither
interval certification nor asymptotic scope. Exact summation of the
arithmetic tail removes a truncation error; it does not turn those
previews into a signed-margin theorem. The local constant-coordinate
cancellation is exact, but the nonconstant entries and their tensor
products still need quantitative control. No negative direction of
the approved inequality was established. This is an inconclusive
bounded screen, not an exhausted arithmetic route or a disproof of RH.

The refined continuation is to estimate the explicitly normalized
prime matrices with the full degree-eight signed functional retained.
It differs from the stopped unscaled row test: the complete reference
Gram form has first been changed exactly to the identity. It also
differs from freezing arithmetic factors or truncating divisor tails.
An oversized bound for this sufficient row criterion would remain
inconclusive for the actual inequality and would require reassessment,
not another claim that a large upper bound is a counterexample.
