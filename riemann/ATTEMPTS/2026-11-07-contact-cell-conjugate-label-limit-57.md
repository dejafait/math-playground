# Four-cell common-line values do not exclude matched conjugate labels

7 November 2026. The exact cell evaluation and conditional survival
calculation are canonical in
[L883](../lemmas/L883-contact-cell-periods-and-label-primitive-phase-test.md).
The independent conceptual audit of 7 November 2026 in L883 accepts

**WHY A UNIVERSAL EXCLUSION DOES NOT FOLLOW.** L883's explicit
boundary-preserving comparison evaluates all four full-form integrals.
It gives necessary primitive-value phase tests, retaining the complete
arc windings. For matched conjugate labels the arc vectors and their
complements are purely imaginary, so all four cell values are real.
Thus common-line cell compatibility cannot distinguish this conditional
pattern. No such actual off-real zero pair or stationary graph is
asserted; signs, graph realization and the d=0 graph phase remain
qualified exactly as in L883. The unmatched and nonconjugate label
tests have not been shown to fail on actual G labels. A new exclusion
attempt needs actual-label separation or additional sign information;
repeating the total-period sum or dropping residue increments supplies
neither. This is not a global ban on relative invariants or the graph
route, and it does not refute RH.
