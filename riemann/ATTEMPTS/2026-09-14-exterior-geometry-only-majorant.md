# Exterior geometry alone after boundary localization

The attempted route was to bring the remaining exterior positive mass
to the Nh scale by summing L213's uniform fixed-pair majorant over
the factor region left by the boundary exclusions.

WHY IT FAILS: [L217](../lemmas/L217-exterior-bulk-majorant-and-arithmetic-threshold.md)
proves that this region contains a fixed box of order N² ordered pairs
with ratio product bounded below by a positive constant. Consequently
the summed geometric majorant is of order Rh, larger than Nh by
N^(1/2). This is an obstruction to that majorant, not a lower bound
on the actual mass or a disproof of a sharper arithmetic estimate.
L217 records exactly the additional weighted-mean suppression required
of the original cell and coprimality weights. That suppression is unproved.
