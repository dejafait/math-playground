# Finite contact-preserving two-fiber replacements cannot lower mass

7 November 2026. The complete conditional proof, explicit coupled
endpoint and remaining interface term are in
[L888](../lemmas/L888-finite-pinned-fiber-mass-bound-and-interface-flux.md).
This is a REPRODUCTION and an informative scoped NEGATIVE, not a new
general variational theorem or an independently audited result.

**WHY THE TESTED CONSTRUCTION FAILS.** Keeping the first radial
component unchanged on both original G-zero fibers leaves each
internal trace homotopy in the actual zero divisor. Each of the three
strip pieces therefore keeps its signed integral. The original graph
already saturates that piece's appropriate constant-phase mass bound,
so every finite replacement in this class has full mass >= the
original mass, including all collars and arbitrary interior coupling.
L888 checks a concrete nonzero coupled endpoint and J!=0. The required
strictly negative mass difference is impossible for this class; no
small-parameter expansion or unchanged total period alone is used.

This stops only replacements satisfying L888(4). A finite replacement
that changes the first component on at least one fiber remains open.
L888(8)-(9),(15) retain its exact signed interface flux and the full
nonnegative phase cost E. Negative interface flux alone is insufficient:
it must be strictly less than -E, and the whole endpoint must be smooth,
periodic, supported inside the strip and have nonzero J.

The approved general finite-replacement target is not exhausted by
this subclass obstruction. Its next test must depin a fiber and pay
the new explicit comparison, not repeat a pinned construction or
L887's fixed-bump expansion. Preserve attempt65/69 and all earlier
qualifications. Configuration/minimizer existence, other contacts,
the sharp mass bound, higher signs and Xi transfer remain unresolved.
