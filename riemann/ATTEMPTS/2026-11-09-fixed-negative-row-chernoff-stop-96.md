# Fixed outer negative-row Chernoff certificate stops

Canonical statement, complete proof, arithmetic qualifications and
reproduction are in [L950](../lemmas/L950-fixed-negative-row-chernoff-outer-tail-stop.md).

WHY IT FAILS: Dropping the positive rows before the fixed increment-one
Chernoff bound loses too much information: even the exact positive-row
cost exceeds the available m-B by more than ten million. All seven
heated moments include both omitted tails, so the result is not an
unpaid-tail artifact or merely unsuccessful quadrature precision.
This supplies no lower bound on actual negative mass and does not
refute B_4<m. No exponent/split/compact-certificate refinement follows.
Any continuation must change the mechanism and pass separate coverage;
signed balanced-simplex localization is only an uncovered nomination.
