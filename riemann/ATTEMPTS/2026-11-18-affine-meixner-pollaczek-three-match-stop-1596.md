# Affine Meixner-Pollaczek three-match stop1596

WHY IT FAILS: [C028k](../lemmas/C028k-affine-meixner-pollaczek-three-coefficient-admissibility-stop.md)
gives an exact feasibility criterion for the unchanged actual P_d:
tau^2/v^3<(d-1)^2/((d+1)*(d-2)^2), with fixed v=S_2>0,tau=S_3>0.
The right side tends to zero, so no admitted common-parameter affine
Meixner-Pollaczek basis can provide all three cancellations along
unbounded degrees. This includes negative scales and arbitrary approaches
to lambda=0, phi=0 or pi, zero scale and unbounded parameters. The
feasibility-first test stops before the fourth/fifth costs; none were
computed. The canonical proof, sufficiency construction, citations and
finite checks are in C028k; independent audit1598 accepted the exact
criterion, strict endpoints and both signed-scale reconstructions. This is a
failure of the stipulated upper-bound construction, not a height lower
bound or a counterexample to RH. Unmatched references are outside scope.

Reopening this exact route requires a mathematical error in C028k or
a changed reference/matching mechanism with an independent discriminator;
a boundary parameter sweep cannot evade its stated criterion. Existing
Laguerre and Pollaczek-flow stops retain their separate scopes.
