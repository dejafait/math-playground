# Global-convex inverse-score comparison: fixed-parameter stop

The two-cancellation proposal in
[drafts/literature/2026-11-09-two-cancellation-integrated-tail-coverage.md](../drafts/literature/2026-11-09-two-cancellation-integrated-tail-coverage.md)
required the actual inverse of h=-sigma to be convex on its entire
image. The gate-approved fixed-parameter test produced
[L916](../lemmas/L916-full-theta-inverse-dilation-score-nonconvexity.md),
with a complete rational full-series certificate. The independent
step190 audit accepts the analytic estimates, global inverse domain
and strict signs; its full record is canonical in L916.

**WHY IT FAILS.** At lambda=1/40 the exact interior point h(1) has
inverse second derivative strictly below -1/26620. Thus the nominated
observable fails the convexity prerequisite even without a null
contact. The result rejects this sufficient global-convex comparison;
it does not determine the signed hinge residual or contact flux.
Changing the order orientation, restricting the observable, or testing
another mechanism would require separate coverage and a discriminating
target. No such replacement is approved by this stop, and it is not
a global ban on contact comparisons or a disproof of RH.
