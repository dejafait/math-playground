# Fixed summable tails: absolute perturbation closure stopped

WHY IT FAILS: The approved complete-logarithm test cannot be closed by
a termwise absolute perturbation of a fixed head. [L695](../lemmas/L695-fixed-summable-tail-absolute-angular-cost.md)
gives one fixed admissible l1 profile with vanishing l1 tails but an
exponentially growing absolute response cost on the original angular
circle, with all spatial moments retained. Its finite-head argument
does justify the ordered-limit sufficiency; it supplies no tail-log
bound. The cost lower bound is not a lower bound on the signed sum
and does not refute the desired A_n inequality. Stop this specific
absolute/dominated closure, preserve the signed problem, and review
the proposed positive-convolution/Fourier-gap transfer in the
[pending assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-11-03-fourier-gap-positive-convolution-transfer.md).
