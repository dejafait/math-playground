# Actual unit lattice: endpoint-dominated localization stopped

The full proof and exact certificate are canonical in
[C663a](../lemmas/C663a-lattice-endpoint-remainder-and-bottom-isolation.md).
This is L663's actual unit-residue lattice with Y=9/20, the full
grid and all M spatial moments, not L662's half-turn phase census.

WHY IT FAILS: At every angle, both endpoints and the entire top/side
contribution are too small to supply the proposed sufficient
two-coefficient bound. C663a bounds the latter by C*n^2*exp(-.029n),
whereas the proposed loss must be below .01305; their strict exponent
gap is at least .01595. Noncancellation of two terms already below
that scale cannot repair the deficit. The exact remaining bottom
integral must carry a response at the sufficient scale if that route
is to succeed. This does not exclude every paid arc, prove the bottom
term small, or turn an additive polynomial error into a logarithmic
error near zeros. Stop endpoint dominance for this lattice and test
the signed bottom contribution as a different, independently
testable mechanism. Preserve the full-circle and earlier census stops.
