# Quadratic periodic differential dual: class obstruction

The gate-approved test required a homogeneous quadratic storage in
(h,h',h(.+1),h'(.+1)), a six-coordinate quadratic majorant for both
clipping branches on all of R^6, and a nonnegative dual on the full
closed interval I. The cost had to be strictly below 1/288.

WHY IT FAILS: [L589](../lemmas/L589-quadratic-periodic-differential-dual-obstruction.md)
proves that every such global certificate costs at least 1/144.
L473's bounded covariance-K law annihilates every permitted quadratic
total derivative while having clipped mean 1/144. It therefore tests
every global majorant, even though L580 excludes its stationary path
realization. The obstruction holds before imposing the cone-dual
condition. It stops this quadratic certificate class, not the general
periodic problem, any larger storage class or actual-Xi signs. Do not
repeat an optimization in this class without changing an explicit
hypothesis. No higher-degree attempt was made in this step.
