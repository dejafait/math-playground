# Literal actual-theta nested thresholds: necessary-premise failure

The approved two-threshold calculation is canonical in
[L812](../lemmas/L812-actual-theta-nested-threshold-rayleigh-obstruction.md).
This is a known-criterion specialization with outcome NEGATIVE.

WHY IT FAILS: A shared scalar and ordered thresholds force the (0,1)
cell to have zero probability, whereas the (0,0) and (1,1) cells have
strictly positive actual-theta mass. The resulting Rayleigh difference
is strictly negative. Thus this literal finite law cannot supply a
strongly Rayleigh premise. Larger finite laws with exactly this
two-coordinate marginal are also excluded; projection stability is
the essential import for that extension. This says nothing about a
different encoding, other correlated constructions or the signs of
the actual averaged polynomials. No matrix/coefficient identity had
been supplied, and failure of this premise does not refute RH.

Abandon this literal encoding. Reopening requires a materially different
realization with independently checkable stability and actual coefficient
matching, not additional thresholds or a degree sweep. Preserve the
earlier interlacing, adjoint and signed-margin stops. A direct actual
coefficient-minor source comparison is nominated separately; its missing
coverage must be reviewed before any new calculation.
