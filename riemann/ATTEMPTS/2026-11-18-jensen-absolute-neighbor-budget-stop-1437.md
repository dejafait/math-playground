Canonical [C020h](../lemmas/C020h-real-prime-witness-absolute-spacing-budget-stop.md)
proves a uniform lower bound c XL^4/(q^4 ell^16) for the complete
absolute near-product budget in Matomäki--Teräväinen Lemma3.3 applied
to frozen original J coefficients. Its ratio to Bq^2 tends to infinity;
the off-diagonal budget alone has the same lower order. Independent
audit1439 in C020h retains the complete proof and its limited scope.
This is evidence against that sufficient estimate, not D.

WHY IT FAILS: products of two original witness primes and four distinct
mollifier primes in [L/2,3L/4] have exact, noncancelling four-taper
coefficients of size comparable to ell^-4. Their original heat
amplitudes stay bounded below. There are too many such distinct indices
for their absolute near-neighbor mass to fit Bq^2: binning at the
source's exact N/U scale forces the lower bound. No improved upper
estimate of this same budget can repair it. The actual translated
complex coefficients can still cancel, and the source upper bound
cannot be reversed. Neither the full real denominator nor RH is
refuted; signed norm estimates and all numerator/actual-P gaps remain.
Stops1407/1408/1431 keep their separate scopes and evidence.
