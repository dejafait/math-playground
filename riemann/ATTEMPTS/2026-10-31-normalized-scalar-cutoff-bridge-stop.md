# Normalized scalar cutoff: compatible-bridge certification stop

The approved test constructs a modified unit bridge retaining the actual
Hardy segment, all L473 tolerances and the coarse derivative bound.
The proof is canonical in
[C582a](../lemmas/C582a-normalized-scalar-cutoff-bridge-obstruction.md);
the separate critical application audit passes with the arithmetic
source proofs still imported.

WHY IT FAILS: A scalar map normalized by phi(0)=0 and phi'(0)=1
cannot change the constructed bridge's second derivative at its
zero-value, zero-slope midpoint. Continuity turns the resulting excess
over the proposed jet radius into positive uniform-phase probability.
Consequently the retained data cannot certify this normalized scalar
method uniformly over compatible bridges. The stop concerns that
generic inference; the original specified L582 quintic, other slopes,
positive clipping and actual-Xi transfer remain unresolved. No RH
counterexample is asserted, and earlier stops and unfinished work remain.
