# Exact grouped variation budget — 18 November 2026

Canonical [C019w](../lemmas/C019w-high-index-variation-budget-obstruction.md)
rules out C019u (8)'s sufficient unweighted variation budget for
the actual signed coefficients, with every original cutoff and heat
time retained. This is new negative evidence about an estimate,
not a disproof of the real-pair squared-mass target.

WHY IT FAILS: indices formed from two source primes near sqrt(X)
and one mollifier prime near L have only two permitted source
assignments. Their shifted heat terms align, so the exact signed
coefficient does not cancel. Counting these distinct indices forces
(1+log Z_*) sum B_k^2/X >= cL/(log X)^4, which diverges. Improving
the d_4 upper envelope therefore cannot pay this particular budget.
The coefficients lie at size comparable to XL; their unweighted
mass is not a growing lower bound for the weighted diagonal or
time norm. The positive upper bound for the off-diagonal cannot
be reversed. A continuation must instead address an independent
weighted diagonal or retain signed integral correlations. C019v's
low block, C019t's qualified transfer and stops1403/1405 are preserved.
