# Stationarity reaches a global line; the single-ray step remains open

6 November 2026. The bounded attempt and full qualifications are in
[L879](../lemmas/L879-stationary-radial-graphs-have-one-phase-line.md),
under the prior
[EXPLORE assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-11-06-stationary-graph-phase-components.md).
This is a partial reduction, not a counterexample to the target.

**WHY THE EQUALITY ARGUMENT STOPS.** Off-zero stationarity yields
componentwise phase constancy. The actual divisor restricts those
phases to one global complex line, but the transverse expansion at a
single odd-order G contact has opposite phases on its two sides.
L879 proves that such a contact in a stationary graph is exactly the
remaining strict-mass-gap alternative. The proof does not establish
or exclude a global graph realizing it. Evenness of the diagonal
factor, positivity-based contact theorems, and the retired ambient
two-period argument cannot supply that missing exclusion. No generic
cosine contour, conditional germ or formal jet is an actual periodic
counterexample. This stops the present automatic-alignment argument
at a precise open branch; it is not a global ban on the graph route.
