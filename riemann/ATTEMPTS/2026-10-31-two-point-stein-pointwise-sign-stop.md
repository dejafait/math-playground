# Two-point Stein pointwise-positivity stop

Date: 2026-10-31. Outcome: NEGATIVE for the proposed pointwise sign mechanism.

[L604](../lemmas/L604-two-point-stein-signed-remainder.md) gives the
complete identity D=M+R with M>=0, all endpoint/Fubini checks, and the
exact oscillatory multiplier in R. For every nonconstant nonincreasing
Stein kernel, some frequency has a strictly negative remainder
integrand on an open set. The multiplier attains -2 exactly; this is
analytic evidence, not a numerical sample.

**WHY IT FAILS.** Monotonicity signs the spatial difference weight but
does not make this two-point remainder pointwise nonnegative. Its
negative part must still be bounded by M plus its positive part, and
this attempt supplies no such integrated estimate. Stop the direct
pointwise-positivity mechanism. A future integrated-cancellation route
would require a specific new estimate and separate coverage; merely
rewriting the same remainder does not reopen it. This is not a negative
value of the full remainder, a counterexample to the generic criterion,
or a statement about actual theta. C233b's different fixed-model stop
remains intact. The independent actual-theta premise has been nominated
for source assessment, without deriving or approving it here.
