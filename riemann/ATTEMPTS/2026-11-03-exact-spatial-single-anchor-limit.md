# Exact single-anchor clearing: full-cube limitation

The successful sparse-support estimate and its precise limitation are
in [L686](../lemmas/L686-exact-spatial-clearing-sparse-displacements.md),
especially Proof Sections 3--5. This record parks only that displayed
certificate as a full-cube completion. It preserves the subfamily
advance and does not prohibit other uses of pole extraction.

WHY IT FAILS: Clearing all possible replacement denominators incurs the
anchor-distance product in L686 (2). At L=2q its guaranteed main term
is smaller than the undisplaced error subtracted there, so the resulting
lower value is negative. This is not an upper bound on the true response,
a countersequence to the joint norm target or an obstruction to every
single-anchor method. The next test needs additional structure, such as
a combined-block signed response with a rigorously paid observation
cost; merely reusing this displayed product does not cover the cube.
