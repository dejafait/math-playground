# Weak discriminant to multiplicity-count implication — stopped

The approved target asked whether D≤0 and L(G)>0 for nonzero real
analytic F,G on an eventual ray force a uniform bounded deficit of
F zeros counted with multiplicity relative to distinct G zeros.
[L373](../lemmas/L373-weak-discriminant-unbounded-multiplicity-deficit.md)
gives an exact counterexample with all real zeros simple.

WHY IT FAILS: F=sin t and G=sin(2t) satisfy the physical inequalities
on the entire real axis, yet G has twice the real-zero density. Their
common zeros are simple for both functions, so multiplicity adds no
missing count. The Wronskian vanishes at those shared zeros. The exact
closed-endpoint deficit tends to infinity after every finite onset
change. Thus neither multiplicity counting nor simplicity alone repairs
L371's stopped generic count implication. A disjoint-zero hypothesis or
an actual-family arithmetic constraint would be a different, uncovered
target; neither is established or approved here. The actual-theta
discriminant and total signed margin remain unresolved. The separate
critical audit on 2026-10-07 retained the canonical counterexample,
including all degeneracies and the arbitrary-onset quantifiers; no
correction or additional obstruction was found.
