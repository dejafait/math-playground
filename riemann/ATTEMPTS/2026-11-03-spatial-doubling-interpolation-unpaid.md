# Spatial doubling: the full-moment interpolation payment is insufficient

WHY IT FAILS: [L707](../lemmas/L707-spatial-growth-transfer-and-cancellation-budget.md),
(4)--(5), computes the full-node Lagrange upper certificate E_n for
the geometric remainder divided by S_(n,1). Its logarithm divided by
n tends to a number greater than 97/300, whereas a vanishing relative
remainder needs an upper bound tending to zero. This fails only the
tested minimum-spacing/absolute-coefficient certificate. A diverging
upper bound does not establish a large actual remainder, a positive
averaged cancellation rate, or a positive B_sp/n. All signed terms,
the anchor, j=0, the inverse angle and M=n/3 moments were retained.
The spatial-growth target remains inconclusive; it is not abandoned
or disproved. A further attempt needs a quantitative signed estimate
or another refinement, rather than repetition of this bound.
