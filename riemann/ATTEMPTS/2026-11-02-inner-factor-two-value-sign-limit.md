The approved degree-at-most-n target remains open. The exact inverse
coordinates and their scope are canonical in
[L676](../lemmas/L676-polynomial-angular-inner-inverse-and-normalization.md).
The unfinished local construction was preserved and independently
reproduced; no duplicate lemma was introduced.

WHY THIS CONSTRUCTION DOES NOT YET WORK: minimizing the angular norm
under P(1)=1 and P(10/11)=0 enforces only two real values. Its exact
polynomial has negative real part at interior segment points for
n=15,27,51, despite finite norms at most n. These rational witnesses
are recorded in L676 Sections 6--7 and its full certificates. The
uniform sqrt(n) bound established in L676 applies only to anchor
normalization, before imposing the extra zero. No uniform cost bound
with both values or eventual sign failure has been proved.

Do not reuse two-value least-norm interpolation as a sign certificate.
Continue the same assessed target through a sign-constrained construction
in the exact inverse coordinates, or a quantified obstruction. A later
onset or different vector is not excluded. L675's low-degree stop,
L674's open-disk stop and the general positive-cone/Xi gaps retain their
separate scopes; this finite test adds no global route ban.
