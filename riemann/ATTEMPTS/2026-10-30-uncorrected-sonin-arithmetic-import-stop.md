# Automatic uncorrected Sonin absorption: source-scope stop

2026-10-30. One literature-only discovery step; no new local theorem.
Exact primary citations, hypotheses and qualifications are in the
[completed assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-10-30-nullvector-arithmetic-slope-discovery.md).

WHY IT FAILS: The narrow archimedean domination theorem cannot be
transported through a non-isometric Euler embedding by keeping its
trace inequality unchanged. More decisively, paper 2 v2 Theorem 6.5
states that the actual uncorrected predecessor-prime Sonin increment
on its critical two-packet tests is subleading to the added negative
prime term. Its upstream source proof has not been independently
audited here. This is a source-level obstruction to automatic local
absorption, not a nullvector counterexample, a sign for the complete
Weil form or a disproof of RH. The explicit pole phase changes the
projection and is outside that increment theorem; testing it at a
common padded cutoff is a materially different, separately nominated
continuation. A collective signed estimate or nullvector-specific
input could also change this decision; repeating regularity, static
positivity or the unchanged source theorem would not.
