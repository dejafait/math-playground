# Pivot-area separate-channel payment — stopped at the fixed pilot

The approved assessment1135 proposed the actual even-source bound
E_j<=P^2(j+1)^5*p_j under M_(j+1)>0. Its P=101,j=2,...,8 pilot
passes with independently enclosed positive cores; the universal
bound remains unproved. The complete result, source definitions,
proof, certificate and qualifications are canonical in
[C750g](../lemmas/C750g-actual-source-pivot-pilot-and-channel-payment-stop.md).

WHY IT FAILS: Applying the adj(G_j) seminorm triangle inequality
after splitting the eliminated u residual into prime, digamma,
exponential and pole channels gives the exact upper budget U_j
defined in C750g(5). Certified LOWER bounds show U_j exceeds the
allowed budget by more than 10^7 in all seven cases and by more
than 4*10^25 at j=8. Independent absolute payments therefore lose
the cross-channel cancellation that the complete signed residual
retains. This is a failure of that specific payment, not a violation
of the proposed inequality. No uniform signed arithmetic estimate
was obtained. Park further growth attempts until a materially new
signed representation or independently testable mechanism is supplied;
more samples, an assumed floor or silent retuning do not qualify.

Audit1138 validated the finite evidence and the direction of the
payment rejection; full proof and qualifications are in C750g Section5.
This does not reopen the stopped payment mechanism. Existing
scalar-floor and generic displacement stops are preserved with their
own scopes. No all-support positivity, Xi realization or RH claim.
