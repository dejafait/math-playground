# The transition-confined characteristic barrier test does not decide range

6 November 2026. The bounded exclusion and qualifications are canonical
in [L854](../lemmas/L854-narrow-transition-characteristic-loop-exclusion.md),
pending separate audit. The existential full-torus linear equation is open.

**WHY IT FAILS.** For sufficiently narrow L849 cutoffs, a smooth closed
characteristic boundary cannot be contained in a single closed transition
strip. A barrier traversing the exterior is not excluded by that proof:
its seam crossings need not be extrema of the separation coordinate.
No such boundary, nonzero source integral or full-torus correction was
certified. The whole bounded test therefore remains EXPLORATION. This
is the third exploration since L851, so the sequence of fixed-cutoff
linear-range tests is parked without resetting its budget. The earlier
inverse and finite-jet evidence is preserved; this is not a global ban
on the equation or on coupled contours. Reopening needs a concrete new
mechanism or an exterior-traversing barrier with independently checkable
boundary and integral conditions, not another singular point or jet.

The independent nominated target concerns an actual nonlinear local
positive graph through a transverse G zero, allowing a complex
Jacobian zero. Essential complex-tangency/index source comparison is
not covered by the linear assessment; it is REVIEW_REQUIRED. No result
for that new target is claimed here.
