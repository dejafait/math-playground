WHY IT FAILS: For the fixed normalized bump, actual predecessor and
successor prime sets, and identical M=log(p)+2 phase, L526 bounds
the corrected Sonin increment by O_h(p^(-1/2)(log p)^(9/10)). Both
traces are separately finite and the added arithmetic term is
exactly -2p^(-1/2)log(p). Hence this local Sonin-only increment
cannot supply the required leading negative contribution. The
bounded-inverse multiplier is obtained only after extracting a
fixed rational factor; omitting that factor or the inverse Gram
operator would invalidate the test. This failure neither refutes
RH nor signs a full Weil form. Reopening mathematical development
requires a materially different observable or collective mechanism;
the pending audit checks this result rather than repeating the test.
