# Direct fixed-polynomial coarse enclosure stop

9 November 2026. The approved [direct-density scope](../drafts/literature/2026-11-09-l938-direct-density-crossing-scope-67.md)
was attempted with the original degree256 polynomial. The canonical
derivation and exact endpoint evidence are in
[L944](../lemmas/L944-fixed-degree-direct-density-endpoint-enclosure-stop.md).

WHY IT FAILS: at E=4 both symmetric singleton enclosures contain zero.
The density center/error ratio is between .0338 and .0339; the
derivative center is negative with magnitude/error between .000220
and .000221. Interval subdivision with those same coarse global
product bounds cannot resolve its closed endpoint. No subdivision
campaign or degree/precision/cutoff increase was performed. This
is an allowance obstruction for that enclosure, not an actual-error
lower bound, counterexample to upward crossing, or global route ban.
A sharper actual-kernel upper bound is a distinct proposed input;
its literature coverage and validity are not established this turn.
