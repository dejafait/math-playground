# Finite signed positive-width smoothed-law mixtures: stop1302

17 November 2026. Research outcome: NEGATIVE for this surrogate route.
The complete qualified proof is [C032l](../lemmas/C032l-finite-signed-smoothed-law-moment-obstruction.md);
the analytic premises were critically audited in
[C032k](../lemmas/C032k-smoothed-law-entire-lift-and-cancellation.md).

WHY IT FAILS: If a finite signed combination of distinct fixed positive
Gaussian widths is a probability measure with any positive exponential
moment, its entire lifted transform must be even. The complete reflected
family satisfies Narasimhan1971 Theorem1, so the forced relation would
make every coefficient zero, contradicting total mass one. Cancelling
finitely many cusp coefficients cannot repair this obstruction. This
retains all C032j preprint/finite-zero/counting/heat-positivity inputs.
It excludes this finite surrogate, not RH, general signed corrections,
zero-width limits, infinite mixtures or other constructions. Reopening
requires a materially different operation and an independently testable
target; increasing the finite number of widths or Taylor conditions is
not a new mechanism. Preserve stop1297 and working1300.
