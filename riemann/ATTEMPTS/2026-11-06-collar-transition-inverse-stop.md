6 November 2026. The canonical result and exact scope are in

**WHY IT FAILS.** Along t=pi the accepted positive collar starts with
G(z_1)<0, whereas the flat cutoff endpoint has G(-R)>0. Every permitted
cutoff therefore crosses an actual G-zero inside its transition. There
the full form, density and matrix M all vanish. Thus resolving the
exterior first does not leave an invertible transition problem. This
is narrower than a range obstruction: L852 checks that the residual's
value-level equation at the zero is compatible, even when its coefficient
vanishes. A tailored smooth flux lift may still exist. The attempt is
EXPLORATION against the global solution-or-range-obstruction test; no
exploration reset or global stop follows from this matrix singularity.
