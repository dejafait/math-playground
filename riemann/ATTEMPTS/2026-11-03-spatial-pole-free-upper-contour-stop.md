# A pole-free scalar contour does not extend to the whole spatial disk

WHY IT FAILS: [L709, Section 7](../lemmas/L709-phase-sensitive-normalization-on-an-imaginary-angular-arc.md)
exhibits a nonzero spatial-resolvent pole crossed by its upper contour
for a spatial point already inside |z|<1. The legitimate scalar
calculation at z=1/2 cannot be used unchanged for either full maximum.
The residue must be retained together with the remaining contour and
the actual real-frequency correction. L709 nevertheless proves a
uniform S_(n,1) lower bound and a relative finite-moment tail estimate
on a fixed arc near i/2. This is a stop only for the pole-free extension;
it neither stops the B_sp route nor proves a positive growth rate.
The next test must compare the complete signed resolvent at both radii,
including this residue, rather than extrapolate the scalar limit.
