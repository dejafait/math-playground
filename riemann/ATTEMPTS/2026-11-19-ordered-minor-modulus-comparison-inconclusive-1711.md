# Ordered two-point minor: modulus comparison is inconclusive

Outcome EXPLORATION. The full exact calculation is in
[C032n, attempt1711, equations (15)-(20)](../lemmas/C032n-odd-kernel-gaussian-width-evolution.md).
It uses the complete actual divisor on the closed width interval and
handles the arbitrarily near-diagonal normalization exactly.

WHY IT FAILS: The attempted estimate controls the absolute value of
the diagonal determinant derivative W by a finite positive constant.
Its resulting enclosure contains both signs and zero. The possible
ratio argument also needs nonvanishing of k_sigma(1/8), which was not
proved at that specified argument. Positivity at the origin alone
does not supply the missing quantitative neighborhood. Thus neither
a strict minor sign nor a counterexample was established. This is a
failure of this modulus estimate, not a mathematical obstruction to
the actual determinant or to RH.

Park this test after its one approved bounded attempt. Reopening needs
a signed quantitative estimate and nonvanishing control, or an
independent rigorous full-divisor certificate. Repeating the derivative
identity, recurrence1534 or a grid does not qualify. A later Chebyshev
application, arbitrary signed cancellation, positive output and actual-Xi
transfer remain unpaid. Gamma park1708 is unaffected.
