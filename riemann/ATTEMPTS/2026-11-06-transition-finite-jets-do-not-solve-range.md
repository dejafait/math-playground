6 November 2026. The finite-jet result, with its actual narrow-cutoff
scope and qualifications, is canonical in
[L853](../lemmas/L853-narrow-transition-transversality-and-source-jets.md).

**WHY IT FAILS.** Transversality and an admissible variation cancelling
the source through the multiplicity order control only finitely many
derivatives at finitely many forced crossings. The remaining bracket in
L853 need not vanish on a neighborhood, and localization introduces
uncontrolled residuals between the crossings. These jets give neither
a smooth PDE solution nor a global range obstruction. Further pointwise
or finite-jet compatibility tests are parked; this is not a stop on the
existential linear equation. The next bounded discriminator is a global
transition flux barrier, with the exact cutoff quantifier retained.
