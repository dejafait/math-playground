## WHY IT FAILS

The selected zero produces a positive divergent retained-frequency
integral, but the complete representation is proved only for Re(z)>1.
Under a hypothetical subpower upper, either the nonnegative deficit's
convergence abscissa lies to the right of the selected real point, or
the difference between the full transform and the retained interval
must have the negative divergence quantified in C034t(8). Its expression
as a nonnegative omitted-frequency integral is established only in the
initial domain. Transporting that identity and sign would require
unproved convergence and control of crossed poles and the frequency
tail. The inspected Landau statement supplies none of these missing
premises. This attempt therefore proves neither the upper nor its
negation; it does not refute the diagonal mechanism in all forms or RH.

Park this upper-contradiction attempt. Reopening needs a materially new
proof of the complete representation at the relevant boundary, or a
signed lower bound on the exact complementary term excluding C034t(8),
with independently reviewable hypotheses. Repeating the local residue,
an initial-domain identity, or the same source comparisons is insufficient.
