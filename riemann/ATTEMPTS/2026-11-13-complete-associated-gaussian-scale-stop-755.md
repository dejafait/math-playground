# Complete actual associated Gaussian scale mixture: sufficient-class stop

The approved target753 was a positive probability representation
A(t)/A(0)=integral exp(-a t^2) dnu(a), for L233's complete actual A,
on finite nonnegative scales. The intended downstream use was its
first Fourier Laguerre sign; higher/mixed signs were independent.

[C233c](../lemmas/C233c-complete-actual-associated-gaussian-scale-stop.md)
records the exact source normalization, finite positive A(0), imported
super-Gaussian complete tail, and the bounded-scale mass contradiction.
This is a known-input specialization and an informative negative for
this representation class, not a new RH obstruction or a sign result.

**WHY IT FAILS.** The actual complete kernel decays faster than every
Gaussian along the real axis. Any positive probability measure on
[0,infinity) has positive mass on some bounded interval of scales,
forcing a Gaussian lower bound. These are incompatible. Atoms at zero
and other finite endpoints do not evade the comparison. This stops
the positive Gaussian scale class only; signed mixtures and other
positive-definiteness mechanisms are not tested or proposed here.
Reopening this class requires a specific error in the cited theorem's
applicability or the exact normalization, rather than another fit.
