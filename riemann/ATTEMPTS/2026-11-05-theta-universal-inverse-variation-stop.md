# Universal inverse theta variation: fixed-square stop

The approved degree-at-most-three inverse test fails the desired
universal inequality v_+(B_3f)<=v_+(f). The actual-law calculation is
canonical in [L822](../lemmas/L822-actual-theta-inverse-quadratic-crossing-stop.md):
the input (1-x)^2 has zero positive-half-line crossings and its inverse
image has two. No numerical moments or truncated theta law are used.

WHY IT FAILS: Nondegeneracy makes the actual moment Cauchy–Schwarz
inequality strict, putting the reciprocal multipliers on the side of
the quadratic discriminant diagnostic that splits the double root
into two simple positive roots. Even-multiplicity inputs belong to
the proposed universal class and cannot be removed after the test.
This parks that universal inverse premise only. Any restricted class
or replacement signed bridge needs its own independently testable
target and source coverage; RH and all-degree mixed positivity remain
open. The earlier forward-variation, common-interlacing and adjoint
stops retain their original scopes.

Step619 independently audited L822's finite inverse, actual-law strictness
and exact half-line counts, accepting the stop without repair. Keep this
obstruction parked. The next source question concerns a coefficient-built
finite-free additive convolution factor, a different realization premise;
no restricted inverse theorem is inferred from that nomination.
