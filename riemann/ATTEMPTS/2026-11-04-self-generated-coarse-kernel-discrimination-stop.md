# Coarse self-generated-kernel discrimination: stop

WHY IT FAILS: Audited [L735](../lemmas/L735-discrete-beurling-self-generated-power-diagnostic.md)
constructs a single locally finite discrete Beurling system whose own
Lambda gives the tested O(x) convolution bound while its psi has a
negative coefficient-1 x^(3/4) term. Thus free-semigroup multiplicative
self-consistency plus this coarse bound does not exclude that defect.
This is a known-construction specialization, not an ordinary-prime
counterexample. Integer-counting regularity, analytic continuation,
the full increment envelope, growing prefix bounds and Xi transfer are
unproved for this system. No stronger impossibility is claimed.
The diagnostic closes at this strength; a new power choice is not a
new mechanism. A specified additional integer-counting hypothesis is
nominated only for source review, without asserting its consequences
or reopening the coarse estimate. Preserve the earlier fixed-kernel
and actual-prime transfer stops.
