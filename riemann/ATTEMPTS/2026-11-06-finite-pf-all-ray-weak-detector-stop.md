# Prescribed finite PF plus every weak solid ray: finite-class stop

The approved small-angle cubic test has a full symbolic certificate
in [L840](../lemmas/L840-small-angle-cubic-finite-pf-all-ray-countermodels.md).
For each finite m the known Schoenberg family supplies PF_(2m+2),
while the specialized finite determinant formula gives weak signs
at every rank and every positive shift despite nonreal roots.
The independent audit recorded in L840 found no error in the
source match or all-rank/all-shift argument; the finite-PF component
is a known-result import.

WHY IT FAILS: Finite PF information cannot remove the tied-modulus
cancellation in this class. The exact sine product in L840 has two
zero rank classes and no negative class; every exterior shift is
zero. Thus adding any prescribed finite PF band does not make these
weak solid signs detect the nonreal pair. The polynomial depends on
the prescribed order. This does not exclude a single infinite-degree
sequence with stronger conditions, strict signs, a no-ties hypothesis
or actual-Xi structure. L838's adjacency and L839's finite-class
stops remain preserved; no RH or Xi counterexample is claimed.
