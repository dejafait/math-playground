# Direct derivative-count and proportion imports stop — step127

**WHY IT FAILS.** The proposed import for K(a,b)=O(T) does not match
the inspected observables. Levinson–Montgomery1974 Theorem1 counts
zeta' zeros and uses a strict critical-boundary real-part sign; the
completed derivative contains H'zeta as well. Levinson1974 and
Wu1206.3737v2 Section3 directly concern xi', but give lower bounds
for its critical-line zeros, not the required signed upper bound
for N'-N-E or a relative lower bound for nonreal derivative zeros.
Subtraction of separate lower bounds is invalid. The precise source
passages, access limits and threshold comparison are preserved in
[the step127 assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-11-09-xi-critical-count-linear-bound-126.md).
[L958](../lemmas/L958-xi-finite-rectangle-critical-count-balance.md)
remains valid. This stops those direct imports, not the O(T) target,
all derivative methods, or RH. A completion-parameter boundary-index
review is a different proposed mechanism and remains REVIEW_REQUIRED.
