# Two-shift cusp iteration: source-scope stop

2026-10-30. One literature-only comparison of the unchanged two-shift
variation target. Evidence, precise primary statements/proof cases and
qualifications are in the
[completed assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-10-30-weil-two-shift-cusp-variation-coverage.md).

WHY IT FAILS: The iteration-only proposal has no independently justified
lower bound for the combined signed profiles. L520's isolated profile
does not give uniform repeated-image remainders; L521 already retains
all shifts/collisions in its strict upper-norm absorption. The inspected
logarithmic regularity results are upper estimates with extra forcing
hypotheses. The inspected delay-propagation theorem changes Sobolev
orders for a different evolution equation and separates a smooth
background; it does not supply additive BV mass. Counting paths or dense
locations cannot fill those missing premises. Park this implementation,
without asserting that its uncomputed path series actually converges,
that cancellations occur, or that the proposed contradiction is false.

Reopening requires a materially new signed noncancellation/lower-bound
mechanism with a controlled depth remainder. A proof contradicting the
conditional BV conclusion could still exclude the assumed nullvector;
the upper bound alone is not a logical ban on that route. This is not
a counterexample to L520/L521, the actual Weil operator, or RH. Preserve
the earlier automatic-Green, automatic-BV, raw-adjoint and transport
stops with their separate scopes. No lemma or script was written.
