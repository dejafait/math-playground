**WHY IT FAILS.** Domination on every radial-graph tangent plane,
including J=0, forces a real two-form into the real part of a complex
top form. Closedness makes its coefficient holomorphic. The global
domination bound allows division by the actual F with removal across
its complete zero divisor; Liouville then forces a constant multiplier
of modulus at most one. Hence the largest absolute torus period in this
class is exactly that of omega. The strict inequality needed for this
obstruction is impossible if L867 survives independent audit.

This stop concerns globally real C1 closed forms dominated for every
z in X and every real A. It does not settle domination on a smaller
realizable class, nonsmooth certificates, any graph's existence, other
orders/shifts or RH. Those changes would require their own coverage and
a materially different test. Keep the older construction stops intact.
