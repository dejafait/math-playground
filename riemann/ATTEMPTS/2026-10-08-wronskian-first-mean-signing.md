# 2026-10-08 — first-mean signing certificate stopped

The approved physical-Wronskian first mean is calculated in
[L380](../lemmas/L380-actual-wronskian-first-mean-cancellation.md),
using L369's already covered weighted mixed mean. The normalization
is A²T²L, with ordinary derivatives of the actual F_0,G pair.

WHY IT FAILS: Exact reflection retains a carrier term, but its t²
real-part diagonal cancels. The complete remaining diagonal is
positive of order H^(-1); the available uniform mixed-mean error
envelope is of order H^(-1/2)(log H)^2. Thus this estimate establishes
a vanishing mean and does not isolate its sign. This is a comparison
of proved bounds, not a lower bound on the actual error, an
impossibility theorem for sharper first moments, or a claim that the
Wronskian vanishes. Even both signs would leave L373's common-only
reset obstruction. Higher moments and noncommon localization need
separate coverage. No RH sign or zero-exclusion range changes.
