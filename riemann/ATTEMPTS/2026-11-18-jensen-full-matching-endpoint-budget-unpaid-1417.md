matches the smooth center amplitudes of all rational mixed-channel
terms with total error o(B). Its exact formula retains the two

WHY THE TERM-BY-TERM DELETION FAILS AS A PROOF: the two domains are
I_mn and I_nm+h. The small endpoint displacement gives only the
absolute budget C020c (13), whose available scale relative to Bq^2
is R/q, tending to infinity. This is an inadequate upper envelope,
not evidence that the actual signed endpoint discrepancy is large.
The full arithmetic sum could cancel it. Neither the full Gaussian
replacement nor a negligible full matching error is justified.
Stop1416's fixed integer families retain their scope. This is not
a global ban, a quotient obstruction, or a disproof of RH.

The materially different proposed discriminator is arithmetic grouping
of the complete rational kernel, with all endpoint terms retained.
Its prime-residue and additive-sum source coverage has not been
assessed; the nomination is REVIEW_REQUIRED before any such derivation.
