## WHY IT FAILS

do not control cancellation between them. L928 keeps the exact complex
cross pairing, both zeta terms and the Hardy phase. Its explicit
relative contraction condition (11) is sufficient and unpaid. Neither
separate lower bounds nor Cauchy-Schwarz provide a fixed contraction
gap. Thus the test stops at a specific finite spectral estimate, with
no joint projection conclusion. A new comparison exploiting this
mixed operator could reopen it; another unscaled row bound, more finite
matrix samples, increased length or more shifts would not resolve the
identified issue. The prior step226 and approval-loop stops remain
separate and unchanged. Audit of the retained moment application is
appropriate; RH-equivalent signs and signed transfer remain open.
