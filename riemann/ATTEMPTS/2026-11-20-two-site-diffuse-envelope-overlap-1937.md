# Diffuse two-site envelope1937: separation-only test stops

WHY IT FAILS: C028x and the exact shared two-site multiplication form
give a uniform spectral envelope: at most two normalized zeros can
contribute nonvanishing cubic/fourth power sums. But the actual target
belongs to that necessary envelope, with a positive unequal two-variable
solution and strict variance slack, under C028c's audited coefficient
contracts. The complete bound, coefficient comparison and qualifications
are in [C028y](../lemmas/C028y-two-site-diffuse-spectral-envelope-and-target-overlap.md).
Thus this necessary-envelope separation does not prove the retained
floor. It also gives no actual sites or vanishing-cost sequence: the
image of the shared Uvarov system may be smaller, and its norm-weighted
residual rates remain uncontrolled. This is new evidence beyond1933,
not a global ban on the family or a conclusion about RH.

The concrete different test is local realization at two prescribed
negative moving sites, with exact finite-degree cancellation as the
threshold. Its proposed uniform C^1 estimate is uncovered and saved
as REVIEW_REQUIRED in review1937. No asymptotic is derived or approved
there. Repeating the separation-only mechanism needs a materially
stronger restriction; all prior collar, parity and recurrence-floor
stops remain within their original scopes.
