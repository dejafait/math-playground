Date: 2026-11-06; source comparison step19.

[completed assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-11-06-theta-connected-partition-interface.md).
The target was an actual-theta signed cancellation for arbitrary-degree
reciprocal Hankel forms, with the already-certified H_2 as a symbolic
discriminator. No determinant expansion or new mathematical test ran.

**WHY IT FAILS.** Leonov–Shiryaev/Burchardt select connected contributions
in a cumulant-of-products identity without imposing positive cumulants.

signs. Zhu's universal positivity requires shifted-cumulant moment
hypotheses, which are not furnished by the actual positive ordinary
theta moments. No inspected source specifies an independently signed
arithmetic-theta pairing at the required nonlinear interface. Thus a
direct positivity import is unsupported; expanding the signed recurrence
or reproducing finite signs would not repair that missing premise.

This is a source-scope stop, not a theorem excluding every theta-dependent
partition construction or a counterexample to an actual form. Retain
the September20 generic-Gram/fixed-cutoff stops, L796 common-Favard stop
and the blocked four-copy GHS source assessment with their original
qualifications. Reopening requires a concrete additional arithmetic
cancellation or signed estimate and its independent bounded test.
Bounded discovery of a materially different mechanism remains available.
