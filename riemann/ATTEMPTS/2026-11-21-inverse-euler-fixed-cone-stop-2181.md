# Universal inverse-Euler cone shortcut stopped2181

The one approved quadratic diagnostic is proved in
[C035a](../lemmas/C035a-inverse-euler-fixed-cone-quadratic-obstruction.md).
It specializes Cardon et al., arXiv:1802.02641v1, Section 4.2, p. 8;
it is not a new general theorem or an actual-Xi counterexample.

**WHY IT FAILS.** For every fixed 0<theta<pi/2 the real quadratic
with roots exp(+/-i theta) is carried by v_j=1/[2(2j+3)] to a
quadratic whose two root arguments have larger absolute value than
theta. Thus sector membership alone cannot be justified by universal
real-polynomial preservation under this inverse. The actual A'' has
additional transcendental and coefficient restrictions which this
polynomial does not meet. Its sector implication is still unpaid.

Stop the prescribed diagnostic after this single family; do not expand
it into a degree sweep or a claim about actual Xi. A future restricted
inverse argument needs materially additional actual-function input and
its own source assessment. This is not a global ban on derivative methods.
