The exact rational observation specialization is canonical in

reflected completion, normalization and original unit residues.
It leaves the signed sample inequality L666 (5) open. The finite

WHY IT FAILS TO CLOSE THE TARGET: exact quadrature represents both E_0
and D with common positive angular weights, but E_0 still contains
the modulus square of the cancelling complex sum. Positivity of those
weights or of a Gram matrix does not compare this sum to the diagonal.
The bounded n=27,51,99 search found smaller responses, but supplies
neither a global minimum nor an unbounded-n estimate with uniform
constant and onset. All floating results are diagnostic. This is an
inconclusive attempt, not a proved failure of scalar angular sampling,
the H criterion, or RH.

One analytic continuation of this exact signed ratio is justified under
the unchanged ready assessment. It must use the permitted unit-residue
family; changing coefficients, discarding cross terms or extending
finite numerical trends would not establish the target. The older
L649/L650/L665 method-specific stops are preserved.

## Analytic continuation and reassessment — 2 November 2026

The allowed analytic continuation has now been attempted; its full
proof and qualifications are in L666 Section 6. The exact straight-path
calculation bounds the change in D by an absolute factor, but leaves
the signed logarithmic energy loss uncontrolled. Its available absolute
fallback supplies only a zero lower bound at the required scale. This
is not evidence that the actual loss exceeds the budget, nor a
counterexample to scalar coercivity or H. Outcome: EXPLORATION.

WHY IT REMAINS UNRESOLVED: the velocity multipliers depend on the
original pole and cannot be factored out of the cancelling sum. Product
magnitude bounds control the diagonal and an absolute derivative, but
do not control the mixed numerator relative to its own energy. After
two unproductive attempts, the scalar sampling mechanism is parked;
reopening requires materially new signed control. The independent
full-spatial fixed-angle benchmark is nominated with REVIEW_REQUIRED
in `drafts/literature/2026-11-02-displaced-full-spatial-fixed-angle.md`.
