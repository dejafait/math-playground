# The specified short mollifier does not witness a positive discriminant mean

Step 76 completes the approved physical assembly in
[L399](../lemmas/L399-complete-mollified-signed-discriminant-mean.md),
using the separately audited transfer and two component limits. The
separate step 78 critical audit validates the assembled result.

WHY IT FAILS: For the exact length H^(1/100) and polynomial P(u)=u^2,
the complete sharp weighted mean has a strictly negative limit. The
carrier limit is zero and the balanced limit is negative; the physical
c^2 factor is retained, and an N-independent absolute tail permits the
fixed-N height limit followed by the response limit. The achieved sign
is therefore opposite to the positive limit required by this diagnostic.
Stop this specified weight as a positive-leading-mean witness; the
separate audit confirms that decision. This does not refute positive discriminant values,
other weights, a pointwise sign theorem or RH. Preserve the independent
L390 absolute-contour, L394 quadratic-weight and repeated-carrier stops.
