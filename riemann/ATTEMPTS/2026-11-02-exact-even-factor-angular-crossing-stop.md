# Exact single-angle even-factor gauge: absolute-contour stop

The canonical argument is
[C673c](../lemmas/C673c-exact-even-factor-angular-crossing-stop.md).
It preserves the proposed gauge's holomorphic square-root branch and
nonnegative interpolation coefficients, then proves a continuous angular
crossing obstruction. The result has not had a separate critical audit.

WHY IT FAILS: normalizing the even part of W at u=-1 does not control the
nearby angle u_*=(-99+20i)/101. For q>=15000, a phase node lies in the
certified band around height 3/20. Every contour in C673c's common branch
and pole-free class crosses its positive horizontal ray, where the exponent
is greater than -1/500, whereas the sufficient criterion needs at most
-1/100. Park this gauge's uniform absolute-contour route. This is not a
lower bound on kappa_e or on the signed remainder, nor an obstruction to
all gauges or finite-n contours. A future angular-pair factor must newly
justify its branch and signs and pass both the real and horizontal crossing
tests; merely changing the old contour shape cannot evade C673c.
