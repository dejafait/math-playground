# Fixed inverse half-plane baseline — stop2034

WHY IT FAILS: the prescribed operator has an exact unique real
degree-six preimage for full U_6, but that preimage has two zeros in
Im(t)<-2, with multiplicity, while the sufficient input threshold was
at most one. Six disjoint rational Rouche disks exhaust its degree and
exclude boundary zeros. See the full canonical proof
[C029t](../lemmas/C029t-fixed-pollaczek-inverse-halfplane-obstruction.md)
and its cited certificate. The resulting de Bruijn bound permits two
nonreal pairs, insufficient for the proposed one-pair mechanism.
This stops only this fixed finite baseline, not an eventual-only law
or RH. No degree/shift/precision campaign is justified. Preserve all
earlier stops; the next nomination audits this finite result.
