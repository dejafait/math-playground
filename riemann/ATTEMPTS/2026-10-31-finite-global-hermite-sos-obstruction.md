# Three actual cutoffs reject global Hermite Gram certificates

The approved complete finite screen succeeds as an obstruction in
[C567a](../lemmas/C567a-certified-finite-hermite-evaluation-obstructions.md):
complete D(P_N) has rigorous negative evaluations at N=64,128,256.
Every allowed-square constraint holds at evaluation. Full proofs,
coefficient/tail qualifications and rational evidence are canonical
there. The independent analytic/full-node/arithmetic audit now passes
without changing those bounds; its complete evidence is recorded in C567a.

**WHY IT FAILS.** A global SOS or global sparse PSD Gram identity must
be nonnegative at every real point. The three complete negative
pairings contradict that requirement at the specified cutoffs. The
evaluation points lie outside L564's [T,2T]; demanding global signs
therefore imposes information unnecessary for that interval. This
stops those finite global certificates, not interval certificates,
other cutoffs, an unbounded separator family, signed Xi transfer or RH.
No uniform extension is justified without a separately assessed,
checkable mechanism. L565/L566's distinct historical stops are retained.
