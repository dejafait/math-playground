The approved restriction/growth test gives a conditional obstruction
for every given f in L504's H_a. The complete hypotheses, cited inputs,
proof and qualifications are in
[L507](../lemmas/L507-actual-negative-part-harmonic-majorant-obstruction.md).
The independent critical audit of 30 October 2026 passes; its checks
are canonical in L507. This remains a classical specialization, not a
new general harmonic-majorant theorem or an RH result.

WHY IT FAILS: A hypothetical finite nonnegative harmonic majorant of
the actual negative part makes both it and its sum with Re f nonnegative
harmonic above the obstacle. Their growth on the vertical ray, the
Fourier symmetries and strict quadrant growth control force f=0,
contrary to the rectangle positivity. This obstruction uses actual
data, whereas L506 only stopped a prescribed uniform envelope.

Stop constructions of such a global majorant under these hypotheses.
The audit found no defect requiring withdrawal. This does not assert H_a nonemptiness,
refute its Q_a inequality, or prove any finite-domain cost diverges.
Any latter inference needs separately covered comparison/compactness
and a specified cost. Other signed certificates and Xi transfer remain
unresolved. Reopening the same existence mechanism requires an audit
defect or materially changed hypotheses, not a repeated growth test.
