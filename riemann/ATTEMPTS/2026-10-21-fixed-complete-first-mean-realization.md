# Fixed complete first means: temporal realization fails

Step 304 tests the exact L449 means under the approved SPECIALIZE
assessment. [L451](../lemmas/L451-complete-first-mean-temporal-defect.md)
gives the complete proof and its qualifications. Step 306 separately
validates its full signed residual, smooth-test passage and every-real-H
bound in the canonical audit section. The law and original cutoff were
not changed.

WHY IT FAILS: The response-phase linear occupation probe has a fixed
positive limiting residual, whereas any trajectory ensemble with these
means would give exactly zero. The high and both intermediate first
absolute moments and the bulk mass loss are too small to cancel the
bounded translation's angular contribution. This rejects the fixed
means on every sufficiently large real-H interval without requiring
atom jumps, actual excursions or a covariance PSD verdict. Altering
only centered covariance cannot repair this defect. Changing means or
using another law is not excluded, and the older selected constraints,
L449 preservation and L450's conditional certificate remain valid.
