# Fixed-q numerator-only baseline stop1961

WHY IT FAILS: On the reference T_d(y)=y^d, the numerator-only power-mean
upper comparison has the exact excess computed in
[C029e](../lemmas/C029e-monomial-fractional-moment-baseline-cost.md).
At any fixed q>0 its excess divided by d grows linearly in d; even
q_d asymptotic to c/d leaves the positive limit Ac for fixed A and c>0.
Thus this comparison cannot have o(d) reference excess at those choices.
The exact escape is q_d=o(1/d). This stops only the fixed-q and
nonvanishing-dq reference rules, not shrinking-q entropy methods.
A large upper-bound cost is not a lower bound on actual H_d.
The actual-family partition upper estimate and central payment remain
unpaid; automatic entropy transfer remains parked by source-limit1959.
