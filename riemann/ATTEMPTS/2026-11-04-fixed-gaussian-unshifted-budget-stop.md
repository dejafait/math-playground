# Fixed Gaussian sufficient-budget stop

The exact fixed-a, n=0 target in the pre-existing assessment
`drafts/literature/2026-11-04-fixed-gaussian-unshifted-jensen-budget.md`
was tested symbolically. The canonical proof is
[L763](../lemmas/L763-fixed-gaussian-unshifted-turan-budget-obstruction.md).

WHY IT FAILS: after the justified Hermite scaling, the nonnegative j=2
summand alone reaches the strict budget's threshold in every degree
d>=D(a), for every fixed a>0. Positivity of the actual gamma_2 is enough;
no high-degree coefficient estimate can cancel a squared summand. This
stops only this sufficient method. Hyperbolicity itself, other signed
criteria, degree-dependent multipliers and RH remain unresolved. A new
signed test must receive its own source review before mathematical work.
