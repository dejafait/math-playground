# Actual-Xi vanishing-amplitude transition: source-stage limit

The [completed source comparison](../drafts/literature/2026-10-31-vanishing-amplitude-saddle-review.md)
finds known saddle expansions allowing amplitude zeros, but no matching
signed transition for actual Xi at a fixed hypothetical quartet with
arbitrary fixed multiplicity. No mathematical proof attempt, coefficient
calculation or impossibility result was performed in this literature step.

WHY IT FAILS: An abstract amplitude-zero expansion does not provide the
height-uniform deflated Xi norm/jets, nonzero signed first term, phase
geometry and smaller complementary-contour error required by the target.
Removing one quartet does not remove other zeros. L463's logarithm and
contour saving have a strict margin beyond 3/8, so they cannot simply be
imported at d<=3/8. Keeping the quotient as an amplitude avoids a logarithm
assumption but does not pay the signed error budget. This is a coverage
and application-input limitation, not a theorem that the route is impossible.

Park the unsupported signed application. A separately nominated exact-model
audit of Note VIII.37 checks a concrete source formula independently of the
missing Xi bounds. Reopening the Xi application needs materially useful
uniform amplitude/contour input or a sign-resolving mechanism; an abstract
expansion or the already known L461 witness does not suffice. The earlier
endpoint and interpolation stops retain their original scopes.
