restored-error comparison nor a signed margin is certified. The inspected
underlying Weil/Watt statements do not supply the missing arbitrary-l2
operator remainder. This is a limitation of the available certificate,
not a proof of positivity, a large actual remainder, or impossibility
of improved arithmetic estimates. Park the exact arbitrary-pair scope
until a specific stronger coefficient-sensitive input or independently
justified margin is available. Preserve all unsigned and spectral stops.
