# Third marginal: conditional iid-square shortcut — stop932

WHY IT FAILS: Under the full three-copy conditional law at S=3B'(1),
each of U1>S/2 and U3>S/2 has positive probability, whereas their
intersection is empty. Thus the pair is neither independent nor a
mixture of identically distributed independent pairs; the precise
support argument is in [C466l](../lemmas/C466l-third-marginal-inversion-and-tail-positivity.md).
The independent-difference modulus-square formula cannot sign K by
factorizing this pair. This stops only that shortcut, not a different
representation of the difference law, common-frequency cancellation,
all-r positivity or RH. C466l obtains local and eventual positivity
by other arguments, with the intervening frequencies still unsigned.
The earlier common-product contour and conditional-core stops remain
unchanged. No negative actual K value or determinant is claimed.
