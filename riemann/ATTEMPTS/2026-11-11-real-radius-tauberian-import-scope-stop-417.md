WHY IT FAILS: The inspected signed Littlewood and Cesaro variants
require genuine unit-disk Taylor convergence and independent coefficient
lower control. HLK assumes nonnegativity; Darboux uses full-circle
regularity. L984 allows an interior off-axis pole while continuing its
quotient along the positive real radius, so values of that continuation
are not automatically Abel sums. The source hypotheses cannot be
treated as paid regularity. This stops these direct imports under the
currently available assumptions, not every real-axis method or actual
zeta-specific estimate. The separately nominated quartic test is still
unperformed and is not evidence for a universal impossibility theorem.
