# Real spectrum alone does not pay the prescribed full Gramian cost

WHY IT FAILS: The all-real-spectrum nilpotent shift has a full-cost
liminf at least 7/16 at the fixed horizon T_d=d^2, by the proof in
[C029c](../lemmas/C029c-nilpotent-full-gramian-positive-floor.md), recorded
pending audit in1951 and independently verified in conceptual audit1953.
Every observation channel is retained. Thus an
argument using only real spectrum cannot justify k_d=o(1) for this
metric schedule. The model has zero imaginary root height: a floor on
the upper majorant is not a lower bound on that height. This stops only
the real-spectrum-alone sufficiency claim, not the actual companion
metric, other horizons or RH. In particular the model is defective;
additional actual-family structure could distinguish it. The actual
endpoint attempts1948/1949 remain parked on their existing evidence.

The canonical proof uses a unitary phase conversion to an all-derivative
polynomial norm, the previously assessed scalar Markov inequality, and
two parity witnesses. It does not replace the full Gramian by a scalar
or terminal-three model. Audit1953 checked the exact normalization,
every derivative channel, endpoint parity and full-norm compression
without a substantive correction; its full details are canonical in
C029c. Repeating the same diagnostic would add no new test.
