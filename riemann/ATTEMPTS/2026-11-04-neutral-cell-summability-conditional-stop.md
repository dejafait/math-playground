# Conditional stop: summable neutral-cell remote couplings

4 November 2026, step 280. The proposed threshold was
sum_{m>=2} beta_m<infinity for the complete energy-normalized
couplings, on cells of length log(3)/2 with both exponential
moments zero. Its definition and full conditional obstruction are
in [L742](../lemmas/L742-neutral-cell-conditional-wiener-obstruction.md).
That specialization has not yet received a separate conceptual audit.

WHY IT FAILS: Under RH, a fixed nonzero neutral smooth profile
has complete translated pairings equal to Fourier coefficients of
a finite positive atomic measure of positive mass. The standard
Wiener theorem gives positive Cesaro mean square, contradicting
the decay forced by absolute summability. Phase aliases add
nonnegative masses; the argument retains every prime power and
the full continuous/polar normalization before applying neutrality.
This parks the proposed summability mechanism in the RH setting.
It is not unconditional nonsummability, a negative Weil vector,
or a disproof of RH. A replacement must account for coherent
spectral interactions instead of requiring this absolute decay;
no such replacement is established or approved here.
