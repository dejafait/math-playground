The complete conditional result, proof, citations and qualifications are
canonical in [C466i](../lemmas/C466i-frozen-hessian-corank-two-zero-balances.md).

kernel indices, mixed normalization, both endpoint-controlled balances,
nonvanishing equivalence and first-contact parity without repair.

WHY IT FAILS: Hankel rank m-2 with an invertible leading m-2 block forces
the compression of A_*' to its two-dimensional kernel to have rank at
most one. The chosen frozen Hessian therefore collapses to one scalar
jet times a lower-degree operator square. Its nonvanishing requires the
additional unproved actual jet condition kappa!=0; even with that
condition, both its H and -H' balances are exactly zero and the weight
must change sign. Thus the proposed strict signed forcing is lost
algebraically, not merely left below an estimated threshold. Stop this
weight without automatic escalation to higher differentials. This is
not an actual contact, a counterexample to PF or RH, or a global ban on
other corank-two mechanisms. Prior rank-one and source-scope stops remain.
