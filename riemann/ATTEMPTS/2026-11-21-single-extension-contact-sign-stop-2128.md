# One positive finite extension does not sign the contact flux

The bounded algebraic specialization is recorded in
[C874b](../lemmas/C874b-single-extension-contact-flux-obstruction.md).
It imports Curto-Fialkow's odd truncated-moment theorem and uses
L874's exact recurrence. This is an informative negative for a
finite-premise mechanism; the actual-theta question remains open.

**WHY IT FAILS.** Positive extension and a null vector force the
auxiliary positive measure onto the null polynomial's roots, but
do not impose the mass-versus-root inequality needed by the flux.
C874b supplies exact positive flux in both lower and full degree,
with all finite PSD and null premises satisfied. The same atomic
examples have positive extensions at every order, so automatically
increasing the extension degree cannot remove them. This does not
establish their realizability as actual theta coefficients. A claim
restricted to actual theta still needs an independent restriction
on those configurations or an independent signed estimate.

audit of this negative result after its separate review gate; a
future actual-theta mechanism would require materially different,
independently testable input. Historical bare-contact stops retain
their original scope. No actual counterexample or RH conclusion
has been obtained. Independent audit step2130 found no error within
the finite-data scope; full checks and qualifications are canonical
in C874b. The stop is retained.
