# Complete selected jet moments do not force the joint sign

Step 139's approved feasibility test has a compatible law in
[L414](../lemmas/L414-compatible-jet-joint-sign-moment-obstruction.md),
validated by the separate step-141 critical audit. All 21 matrix entries, all five scalar
benchmarks, exact ordinary/envelope conversion, response bounds and jet
envelopes are retained. The law is specified by an integral pushforward;
optional atomic replacement supplies existence, not numerical atoms.

WHY IT FAILS: The quartic data are invariant under a common sign reversal
of the critical triple. Exact sign orientation puts their mass on the
allowed support. Rare-state scaling matches the cubic mean, while a
negative-discriminant bulk atom supplies the smaller first-mean scale
without changing quartic limits. The exact curvature terms do not prevent
this construction. Thus this selected information still cannot force
Delta>0 with Bcal<0. This stops the proposed implication only: actual
height/response realization, a uniform height marginal and the first
Bcal mean are outside the relaxation. L410/L412/L413 remain intact;
the actual clipped mean and regulator margin are unresolved.

Step 141 also checks the omission explicitly: this law has first Bcal
mean O((log H)^(-2)), unlike L369's negative constant. This does not
invalidate the selected-information obstruction, whose hypotheses omit
that mean. Simultaneous feasibility after adding it is a distinct,
unapproved target, not a conclusion of the audit.
