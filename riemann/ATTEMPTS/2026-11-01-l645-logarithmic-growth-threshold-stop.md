# L645 logarithmic-grid sufficient threshold: growth obstruction

For each fixed delta in (0,1/2), the proposed standard-growth test gives
an informative negative result for reaching N=floor(log gamma) through
L645 (7). The full proposed proof and all qualifications are canonical in
[C645a](../lemmas/C645a-logarithmic-grid-growth-threshold-obstruction.md);
its independent mathematical audit accepted the stated obstruction on
1 November 2026. The audit is recorded in that canonical corollary.

WHY IT FAILS: The exact completed ratio is bounded below by a constant
times gamma^(1/4), whereas the sufficient threshold grows at an exponent
strictly below log(5/4)<1/4. Thus for stipulated sufficiently high simple
zeros this sufficient threshold is unavailable. This stops only the present
normalization/contraction bound at this grid size; it says nothing about
PSD, the smallest negative witness, multiple zeros or existence of off-line
zeros. Preserve L645's every-n detector and C644a's distinct fixed-reserve
stop. Repetition needs a materially different residual or contraction
mechanism with its own independently testable target and source gate.
