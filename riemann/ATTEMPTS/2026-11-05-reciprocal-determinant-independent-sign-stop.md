# Reciprocal determinant: independent algebraic sign route closed

Step462 applied the already assessed dual Jacobi-Trudi identity. The
complete normalization, reversal sign, radius scaling and singular-case
argument are canonical in
[C785a](../lemmas/C785a-reciprocal-minor-is-original-coefficient-minor.md).
This is a known-result application, not a new determinant theorem.

**WHY IT FAILS.** The residual reciprocal determinant is a negative
strictly positive multiple of the original unpaid coefficient minor.
Signing it therefore requires precisely the original minor sign; changing
coordinates supplies no independent theta estimate. This closes only the
independent algebraic sign mechanism. L785's conditional full congruence
survives, with its two-simple-negative-root and zero-free-complement
hypotheses. Actual-Xi signs, root grouping, all orders and other minors
remain open. Do not extend this identity to larger orders or repeat
fixed-model tests as a response to this stop. A genuinely different
theta-specific input can still be assessed.
