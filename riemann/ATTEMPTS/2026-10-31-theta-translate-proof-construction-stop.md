# Remaining translate-density proof: constructive-source stop

The gate-approved literature review read the complete remaining proof
of Dimitrov–Xu Theorem 3.2, arXiv:1606.05011v1, Sections 3.1–3.3,
printed pp. 13–15. Exact scope and citations are retained in
[the completed assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-10-31-theta-translate-approximation-proof-review.md).

**WHY IT FAILS.** The proposed use required finite translate approximants
with independently controlled L1 error. Sufficiency starts with density;
necessity starts with LP membership and concludes density through Wiener.
The remaining proof provides no such coefficients, translations or error
sequence for actual theta. This closes that previously unread source
lead; it proves neither nonexistence of constructive methods nor any Xi
sign. The source's Fourier-phase consistency also remains unverified;
no result from that criterion is imported. Reopening needs a materially
new approximation estimate. The separate fixed-Gaussian identity audit
is a source-reliability diagnostic, not continuation of the construction.
