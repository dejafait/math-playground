# Uniform half-power lower bound — 2026-09-12

The proposed bound |S(t)|≥d t^(−1/2) eventually, for fixed d>0, is impossible by [Lemma 149](../lemmas/L149-dyadic-mean-square-with-moving-weights.md).

**WHY IT FAILS.** The absolute term mass is not the modulus scale of the oscillatory sum. The rigorous dyadic mean square, including the moving Gaussian weights, is of order T^(−1/2) before division by the interval length. A uniform half-power lower bound would instead force a positive constant lower bound for that integral. More generally every exponent below 3/4 is excluded. This obstruction concerns the proposed estimate, not RH; it supplies no uniform lower bound at the surviving exponents.
