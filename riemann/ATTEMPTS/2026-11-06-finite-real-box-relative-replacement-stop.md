**WHY IT FAILS.** The normalized angular 2-form is closed and vanishes
tangentially on every branch of the specified divisor. Its relative
period is 1 on the coefficient torus and 0 on every separated real-zero
box. Compact reduction of every hypothetical finite relation, including
all complex zeros in its annulus, makes the cited semialgebraic Stokes
and ordinary-homology comparison applicable. Thus no finite real or
integer combination of these boxes equals the torus class. The full
holomorphic form also has a valid relative pairing, but equality of its
integrals alone is not excluded by this different invariant. This stops
the specified finite relative replacement; it supplies no assertion
about infinite chains, other contours, signs or RH. Preserve L775,
L844 and the automatic-contour source stop at their original scopes.
