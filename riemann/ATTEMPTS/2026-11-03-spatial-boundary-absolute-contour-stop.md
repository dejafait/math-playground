# Proposed boundary residue: absolute horizontal-contour isolation stops

WHY IT FAILS: For the same fixed actual L703 profile at u_*=(3+4i)/10
and z=-1, [L713](../lemmas/L713-horizontal-spatial-residue-envelope-obstruction.md)
proves that every fixed horizontal line above the pole i/8 has an
absolute-integral positive-bump remainder envelope with lower rate
greater than 7/1000; the proposed residue's upper rate is below 1/5000.
Thus this triangle-inequality lower certificate is eventually negative,
even before charging the other complete-family terms. Seven exact
checks and all interval rectangles pass; the canonical analytic proof
is not yet independently audited. This is NOT a lower bound for the
signed remainder, an asymptotic for the full G_n, a positive Jensen
defect, or a disproof of RH. A failed upper certificate is not such a
counterexample. Preserve the exact decomposition and stop evidence;
signed integration or a changed contour is a different mechanism.
The proposed signed-remainder rate test needs its own source gate.
