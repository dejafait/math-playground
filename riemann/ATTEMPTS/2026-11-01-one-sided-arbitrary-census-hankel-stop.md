# One-sided support does not suffice for the arbitrary-census exponent

The approved bounded test produced the explicit counterfamily
in [C645i](../lemmas/C645i-one-sided-circle-hankel-counterfamily.md).
It uses fixed delta=2/5, eta=1/100 and C=10, with 10n distinct
unit-residue poles and the exact complex C645g symbol.

WHY IT FAILS: Equally spaced poles on a circle tangent to the anchor's
vertical line all lie on the permitted left side. The exact residue
quadrature in C645i makes their full weighted boundary sum exponentially
close to a term at the circle center. The center has a larger grid
product modulus. Its evaluated gain exceeds delta/8, while the
quadrature error is uniformly smaller on the entire boundary. Thus
even the largest Hankel norm, not merely a scalar sum or a least
singular value, is o(exp(-delta*n/8)). Positive unit residues and a
rightmost anchor alone cannot give the asserted bound for every fixed C.

The separate conceptual audit on 1 November 2026 verified the full proof
and arithmetic without a mathematical correction; its record is canonical
in C645i. This stop is accepted for the stated synthetic claim. Preserve C645g's
small-census result and C645d's different scalar obstruction. The
family has no claimed actual-Xi realization, and gives no off-line
zeta zero. Any reopening of the failed universal extension needs a
new quantitative restriction excluding this family or a different
independently testable detector, with suitable source coverage.
