# Density Peano dual: direct derivative implementation parked

The exact positive pairing and full finite derivative calculation are
canonical in [C574a](../lemmas/C574a-peano-dual-chirp-derivative-budget.md).
The invocation-start EXPLORE assessment covered this one attempt.
All existing linear-frequency, stationary-involution, moment,
saturation and grouped-certificate stops remain in force within their
stated scopes. None is a global prohibition on full-residual research.

WHY IT FAILS: the actual quotient produces a bounded globally C^1
multiplier, but the proved total-variation budget is at most 2T log T.
Every nonzero-coefficient chirp phase is nonstationary, including exact
cutoff endpoints. Nevertheless, after paying the amplitude derivative
and summing every ordered pair, the explicit derivative budget is
O(T^(3/2)log T); the trivial coefficient budget is O(T^(3/2)). Both
miss c T(log T)^3/2 by diverging factors. The already known stronger
L477 bound O(T(log T)^3) remains available, with no certified constant
at most c/2. Thus these upper estimates cannot complete the separation.
They do not prove the leakage large, rule out a sharper quotient
estimate, or give any Xi sign or RH conclusion. Boundary terms and
zero diagonal coefficients cause no missing case.

Repeating this variation estimate is not justified. Reopening it needs
a materially new bound exploiting the actual quotient, or another
independently testable mechanism. The nominated bounded sine of the
prime-block probe changes the amplitude and proposes arithmetic
expansion control instead of generic quotient variation. It is only a
REVIEW_REQUIRED source target; its weighted signed density moment,
complete chirp correlation, finite expansion errors and stationary
cases are unproved. No transfer of the result for D/Q is assumed.
