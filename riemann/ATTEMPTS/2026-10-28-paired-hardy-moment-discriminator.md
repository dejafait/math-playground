# Two-height Hardy moment discriminator: feasibility obstruction

The tested discriminator was the complete second/fourth moment system
for the six normalized Hardy coordinates at t and t+1/log H, including
all cross moments and four power-saving same-height odd second errors.
The threshold was E(x_0 z_0-y_0^2)_+>=log H/288.

WHY IT FAILS: [L473](../lemmas/L473-paired-hardy-jet-moment-feasibility.md)
constructs a positive law with clipped mean log H/144+O(1) while meeting
every constraint. Its strictly positive leading covariance permits an
exact correction of the rare component's entire second matrix; no
reflection changes the cross quartics. Thus this specified finite data
cannot rule out the threshold. The application awaits independent audit;
the cited arithmetic theorems, including Bui--Hall's omitted Proposition 3
proof, remain imported. The law is abstract and supplies no actual-zeta
lower bound, sample paths, global realization or RH conclusion.

Do not repeat this identical finite-data test. Reopening needs a genuinely
additional constraint that the constructed law need not satisfy, with
its own source coverage and bounded test. This is a stop for the exact
discriminator, not a global prohibition on correlated-height methods.
