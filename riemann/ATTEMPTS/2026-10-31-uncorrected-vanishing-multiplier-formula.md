# Uncorrected vanishing-multiplier formula: bounded rejection

The approved fixed-model audit is proved in
[L570](../lemmas/L570-fixed-vanishing-multiplier-source-formula-audit.md).
For A(z)=z-1, B(z)=exp(z), N=n>=1 the coefficient is exactly zero,
whereas the uncorrected Note VIII.37 expression predicts a nonzero
exp(n)n^(-3/2) term. This is already covered by the authors' 2019
erratum; the local audit reproduces known correction evidence.

WHY IT FAILS: At a vanishing amplitude the phase-dependent correction
can cancel the amplitude term. The applicable O'Sullivan rule has
alpha_0=alpha_2=0 here. The short uncorrected expression misses that
cancellation. The original nonnegative-A theorem is not contradicted;
the audit concerns the note's explicit signed/vanishing extension.
See L570 for full hypotheses, calculations, source access qualifications
and the erratum specialization. No general replacement was derived.

Park the uncorrected expression. The fixed-model result supplies no
height-uniform Xi quotient/jet or complementary-contour bounds and no
Xi sign. Reopening the actual-Xi application requires materially new
application input; repeating this elementary audit would add no evidence.
