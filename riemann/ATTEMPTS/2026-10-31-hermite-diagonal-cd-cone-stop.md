# Actual Hermite diagonal-CD cone: exact coefficient stop

The approved highest-two-weight diagnostic is completed in
[L565](../lemmas/L565-actual-hermite-diagonal-cd-cone-obstruction.md).
It uses the complete actual-coefficient discriminant, including the
mixed contribution. The highest weight is positive and the next is
strictly negative for every integer N>=2; no large-N asymptotic or
effective onset is necessary. The independent finite application audit
in L565 now passes, including N=2 and L564's moving cutoffs, without
repair. This is a local obstruction, not a claimed literature novelty.

**WHY IT FAILS.** The actual coefficients of the two highest Hermite
summands have opposite signs. In the unique diagonal-CD expansion,
the contribution proportional to the square of the highest coefficient
cancels at the next degree, leaving a strictly negative product of
the two positive b coefficients. Thus this nonnegative-weight cone
cannot certify any of L564's moving truncations. This does not refute
positivity of the discriminant, other mixed-term certificates, or RH.
Reopening this specific cone would require changing its mathematical
certificate, not sharpening coefficient asymptotics. The absolute-tail
bound and the prior parity/Pollaczek stops retain their own scopes.
