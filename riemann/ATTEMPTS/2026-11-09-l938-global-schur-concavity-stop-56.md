# Global Schur-concavity of the fixed symmetrized H2 polynomial fails

The full statement, exact averaging, hypotheses, criterion citation,
witness and complete finite certificate are canonical in
[L940](../lemmas/L940-symmetrized-h2-polynomial-schur-concavity-obstruction.md).

WHY IT FAILS: The approved 48 rational two-block tests contain fifteen
exact positive Schur–Ostrowski discriminators. In particular the
interior tuple (16/27,1/27,...,1/27) has a strictly positive
discriminator, and the standard sum-preserving binary mixing argument
contradicts global Schur-concavity. The full S_12 arithmetic average,
all 34 rows, factorials and twelve factors were retained; neither a
normalization change nor an unsigned bound creates the obstruction.
The proposed global value-order mechanism is closed for this polynomial.
No enlargement of this grid can restore it. This does not decide
one-crossing of the integrated density, positivity of its transform,
or RH, and it leaves the earlier contact/score/cell stops intact.

The selected witness has negative polynomial value. A distinct
zero-superlevel balancing property is not settled by this proof.
Its proposed six-ray root/sign test receives REVIEW_REQUIRED source
coverage separately; no such roots or signs were computed in this step.
