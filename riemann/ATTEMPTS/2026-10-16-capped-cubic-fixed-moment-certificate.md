# Capped cubic: fixed magnitude moments do not decide the coefficient

Step 157's complete calculation is canonical in
[L420](../lemmas/L420-capped-cubic-power-window-and-moment-budget.md).
It keeps L418's actual rho, full response, curvature, fixed lambda and
sharp average. The imported fixed-p bounds localize any leading
contribution to rho between ell^(2-delta) and ell^(2+delta), for every
fixed 0<delta<=1/2. This is a local application of known moment bounds,
not a new zeta-moment theorem.

WHY IT FAILS: On each side of the exact radial cap, the moment
certificate has exponent 2+(4-p)^2/4. Its minimum is exactly 2 at p=4,
with no evaluated constant or lower bound. Discarding the two outer
power regions leaves the cap-transition window uncontrolled at the
required scale. Fixed Holder interpolation supplies no improvement.
L409's signed cubic does not evaluate this correlated absolute
observable. Hence this certificate neither proves nor contradicts
F_lambda=beta_* ell^2+o(ell^2) for any fixed lambda. In particular it
does not disprove L418/L419's conditional construction in general.

Stop repeated magnitude-envelope or renamed shell tests with these
same inputs. A reopening needs new restricted response/direction
information and a discriminating coefficient estimate, with separate
coverage if its target changes. The independently testable next target
is L418's outstanding critical audit, beginning with separate source-
scope approval. Existing sign-only, direct-lift and clipping stops,
and every actual/global/regulator gap, remain in force at their scopes.
