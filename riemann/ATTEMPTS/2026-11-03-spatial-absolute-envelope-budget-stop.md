# The stronger spatial absolute-envelope budget fails on a fixed arc

WHY IT FAILS: [L708](../lemmas/L708-inner-arc-absolute-envelope-cancellation-obstruction.md)
gives a uniform positive exponential separation of the full prescribed
S_(n,1) from A_n on a fixed positive-measure radius-1/2 arc, hence a
positive linear lower bound for Cbar_n. The proof keeps the actual
L703 profile, unit masses, anchor, inverse angle, j=0, both finite
tails and all M moments. This stops L707's sufficient premise
Cbar_n=o(n). The separate conceptual audit in L708 Section 9 (step 164)
accepts this restricted obstruction under its stated fixed-profile hypotheses.
It does not stop the weaker B_sp=o(n) or J_sp=o(n) targets, refute
the profile, or resolve RH. The earlier divergent interpolation
certificate remains a different, narrower failure; it was not used
as evidence for this actual cancellation bound. The new obstruction
has no effective arc, rate or onset. The original step-163 result and
auxiliary certificate are preserved; the audit supplies no stronger stop.
