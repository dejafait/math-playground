# Standard-input portfolio audit — 2026-09-21

Outcome: no justified technical reopening was identified in the recorded standard inputs. This is a review of the repository’s available mechanisms, not a theorem that these inputs cannot prove RH. No new lemma or equivalent criterion is proposed.

## Target and scope

The required result is nonnegativity of every actual mixed form cᵀH_dc, for every degree and real vector, supplying C032a’s missing premise; an independent exclusion of every off-line zero would bypass that premise. The whole proof overview, global DAG, existing changes, standard-input file and parked-route audits were inspected. Existing unfinished work is preserved.

## What the input portfolio supplies

Meromorphic continuation, the functional equation, identity theorems, gamma nonvanishing and unique factorization underpin the existing localization, Euler product and alternating representation. The [continued-prime audit](2026-09-09-continued-prime-positivity.md) already excludes continuation of the positive-series inequality into the strip. The [alternating-interval screen](2026-09-09-positive-laplace-kernel.md) leaves a strict head-minus-tail margin unproved at every point of the target half-strip. Neither an identity theorem nor reflection supplies that missing inequality. The functional equation is also already used in the heat center analysis: the [functional-equation audit](2026-09-12-functional-equation-lower-bound.md) records that the leading transformed expression returns the original oscillatory series, rather than a lower bound.

The Gaussian integral, Poisson summation and Euler’s gamma integral supply the exact theta representation. Locally uniform convergence theorems justify its manipulations. Hadamard factorization supplies reciprocal-zero summability after growth has been proved. These inputs have already been used in the retained reduction; they do not themselves assert positivity of the signed Newton expressions. The [theta audit](2026-09-20-theta-logarithm-relevance-audit.md) identifies precisely that missing passage. The fundamental theorem of algebra is likewise already used in the generic kernel obstruction, not an unused source of actual-theta zero exclusion. This does not exhaust possible new uses of these theorems.

The remaining quantitative inputs are also active in existing results. Complex Stirling estimates supply undeformed center estimates and the negative-heat expansions; their phase-sensitive use has not established the required all-height lower bound. Montgomery–Vaughan’s generalized Hilbert inequality is used in the fourth-moment and cutoff analysis. L154 explicitly shows why the resulting moment information does not remove exceptional heights. Even its unproved stronger tail hypothesis would give positive proportion, not every height.

The second- and third-derivative tests have already been screened in L174: their pointwise O(N^(5/6)) bound and averaged O(sqrt(N)) bound do not give the required o(sqrt(N)) first moment. L175 uses the B-process with an error O(log N)=o(sqrt(N)), but the transformed main sum still needs the same first-moment saving. A sufficiently small transform error is not a bound on that main sum. Fejér’s theorem is already used to recover continuous periodic functions from their Fourier coefficients (for example in L140 and L204); that convergence statement supplies no cancellation estimate for the growing arithmetic sums. At the later Fourier endpoint L231’s best recorded bound is O(N²h log N), while L230 needs F_H=o(B_0), with B_0 at least a constant times N²h/log N. The resulting available relative upper bound O((log N)²) is not o(1).

These local targets are not themselves RH criteria. The [heat/Fourier audit](2026-09-20-heat-fourier-relevance-audit.md) additionally requires control of exceptional centers and a valid whole-plane moving-height argument. Improving a derivative estimate without addressing those separate gaps does not justify reopening the full route. The [comparison-energy audit](2026-09-20-comparison-energy-transfer-audit.md) likewise still lacks an actual-theta auxiliary equation and compatible boundary conditions; none is supplied by the standard-input list.

## Decision

No new all-degree sign estimate or all-height nonvanishing statement was obtained. The achieved actual positivity remains the six scalar signs and finite H_1/H_2 certificates under their existing contracts. The required range is all degrees and vectors. No candidate or certified off-line zero has appeared.

**WHY IT FAILS.** Treating an already-used standard transform, convergence theorem, or upper bound as a new mechanism leaves the same missing sign or lower bound. The portfolio contains no identified unused result that meets those thresholds, and the existing audits already explain why the proposed transfers fail. Stop cycling through internal route audits or adding local estimates without a completion mechanism. Retain every branch; a new primary-source input or an explicit new argument would be needed to justify technical reopening. This is a research decision about the present notebook, not an impossibility claim about RH.

## Stop/go decision after the source screens — 2026-09-21

This review uses the recorded source assessment and existing proofs; it does not repeat the literature search or independently validate abstract-only claims. The precise target remains cᵀH_dc≥0 for every d and real c for the actual nodes. C032a supplies the downstream implication to RH. The existing working-tree changes, including strengthened L055/L057 and the L025/L030 convergence explanations, are retained.

The screened mechanisms fail the reopening test for different reasons, which must not be conflated:

- The full weighted adjacent-moment family, together with any prescribed finite Hankel cutoff, is insufficient in the comparison class by L055/L057. Their quantifiers are “for each cutoff there exists an example”; they do not assert one nonreal-zero example passing every cutoff. This is a proved obstruction to a generic implication, not an obstruction to using exact theta arithmetic.
- The shifted-Jensen estimates have a domain restriction excluding the required unshifted high-degree endpoint. They provide no error bound there. Their formal extrapolation cannot be used either as a valid upper bound or as evidence of nonhyperbolicity. Thus improving only a positive shift cutoff is not a completion mechanism.
- The associated-kernel source leaves positive definiteness as a premise. Its positive integrand establishes a different kind of positivity. The screened higher-curvature and contour-Hankel claims supply no stated passage to all mixed forms; their abstract-only status is retained. Unlike the weighted family, these proposals are not all refuted by the recorded comparison example. They are unqualified for reopening because the necessary implication or premise is missing.

None of these sources supplies the auxiliary equation and boundary conditions required by the comparison-energy route, the full-strip noncancellation required by the alternating representation, or the uniform center and global evolution estimates required by the heat route. In particular the heat discrepancy bound O((log N)²) relative to B_0 remains short of the required o(1), and fixing that bound alone would leave the independent global gaps. Combining these incomplete interfaces supplies no recorded implication bridging them.

**Decision: no-go for technical continuation from this screened portfolio.** The achieved actual-node result remains the finite tests under their stated contracts; no all-degree lower bound has been obtained. End the audit/search cycle as well as its technical extensions. Another pass over the same sources, another equivalent criterion, or another finite test has no identified downstream use. No new lemma or DAG input is warranted. No candidate or certified off-line zero is present.

**WHY IT FAILS / admission condition.** A technical reopening must identify a specific additional identity or estimate, justify its hypotheses for actual theta data, and exhibit its passage to the all-degree target or independent all-zero exclusion. For the heat route it must also address the separately recorded uniformity and evolution gaps. Merely naming a new representation or asking for the missing sign does not meet this condition. No such mechanism is currently in hand; this is an explicit stop decision for the available work, not a theorem of impossibility or a request to resume formalization.
