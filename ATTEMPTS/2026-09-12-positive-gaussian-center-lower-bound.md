# Positive Gaussian averaging does not supply a center lower bound

Date: 2026-09-12.

The negative-heat convolution identity is established in [Lemma 137](../lemmas/L137-negative-heat-convolution-and-cancellation.md). The attempted shortcut was to transfer the undeformed center lower bound through the positive Gaussian average.

WHY IT FAILS: The averaged values are complex and positivity of the averaging weight supplies an upper bound only. L137 gives an exact comparison from a positive even finite Fourier measure with a nonzero input center value and an exactly zero averaged center value. Its cancellation factor is zero despite positive absolute mass. For theta itself the new absolute O(a) error bound also gives no uniform relative estimate at exponentially decaying undeformed centers. Neither observation disproves the desired theta-specific relative bound; it requires quantitative phase control or another lower-bound argument. A common strip on a heat interval remains unproved as well.
