# Common frozen block law with preserved moments — 2026-09-13

The proposed shortcut was to approximate every midpoint-frozen block by
one common probability law, retaining its first moment, and thereby
justify common cutoff means.

**WHY IT FAILS.** [L170](../lemmas/L170-frozen-block-variance-profile.md)
proves a nonconstant limiting mean profile and a fixed positive lower
bound on the best uniform common-mean error. Thus a common law with
uniformly preserved first moments is impossible. This does not refute
a common weak limit without uniform integrability or equality of the
specific bounded cutoff means: neither follows from means alone, and
the actual signed cutoff covariance remains unresolved.
