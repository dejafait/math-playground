# Generic growth does not force upward-contribution vanishing

The proposed inference was that finite nonattained strip height and order-at-most-one symmetric paired-product growth force E(w_n)→0 whenever simple-zero heights approach the supremum. [Lemma 68](../lemmas/L068-sparse-product-with-large-upward-contribution.md) disproves that inference.

## WHY IT FAILS

Global growth bounds limit the number of zeros but do not impose a lower bound on distances within a sparse cluster. Two vertically close simple zeros can therefore have an arbitrarily large upward interaction even in an order-zero entire product. The canonical proof above constructs such clusters and verifies their growth and exact zero set. This failure concerns the universal vanishing estimate only; it does not decide net interaction, existence of a favorable subsequence, a heat-envelope law, or RH.

## Full-interaction strengthening (2026-09-11)

[Lemma 69](../lemmas/L069-positive-divergence-of-the-full-sparse-interaction.md) now also disproves a universal nonpositive limiting full interaction along every near-supremum sequence for these generic products. Downward terms do not repair this example: their exact local part stays bounded, and all other clusters have a vanishing absolute bound. A favorable sequence, heat evolution, and theta-specific conclusions remain outside this obstruction.
