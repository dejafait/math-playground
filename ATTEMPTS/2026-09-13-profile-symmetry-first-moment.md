# Profile symmetry alone does not decide the first moment

Date: 2026-09-13.

The attempt was to extract first absolute moment decay from the symmetric
and antisymmetric profiles of the fixed dual involution. The rigorous
relations and inference countermodels are in [L179](../lemmas/L179-profile-eigenspaces-and-first-moment-obstruction.md).

WHY IT FAILS: after a common phase rotation, the two components are real
and imaginary up to a vanishing L² error. Their absolute value is the
Euclidean norm of two amplitudes, so their opposite transform signs do
not cancel that norm. Exact versions of these phase identities with the
same second moments permit both constant radius and rare large radius,
with respectively constant and vanishing first moment. These are only
countermodels to inference from those identities and moments, not
counterexamples for the actual arithmetic sums or for RH.
