# Full-core coprime density with an absolute Möbius error

Date: 2026-09-14.

The recovered long-cell draft led to the exact square-root window and
finite divisor identity in [L199](../lemmas/L199-full-core-square-root-window-and-coprime-error.md).
The lower bound in L198 remains unproved.

WHY IT FAILS: each full-core window in the central strip has real
length less than one. Its density main term is smaller than one,
whereas the standard absolute Möbius endpoint-error budget is at
least one per quadruple. Summing and subtracting this budget cannot
produce a positive lower bound, let alone the required N³ count.
This does not refute the actual count or exclude cancellation of
endpoint errors; it identifies precisely why assigning coprime
density to each bounded window is insufficient.
