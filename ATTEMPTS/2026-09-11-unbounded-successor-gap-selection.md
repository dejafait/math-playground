# Unbounded successor gaps as a common-selection condition

Date: 2026-09-11.

Outcome: false for the interpolation of Lemma 100, as proved in
[Lemma 103](../lemmas/L103-unbounded-successor-gaps-do-not-give-common-selection.md).

WHY IT FAILS: A large distance to the first higher coordinate controls
one term but need not control the total number of higher coordinates
at comparable distances. The constructed prescribed set has unbounded
successor coordinate gaps while retaining enough nearby higher points
that a small height increment produces a fixed positive full upward
sum. Sparse placement makes these adversarial increments summable for
any proposed subsequence. Height-dependent selection remains valid;
unbounded gaps alone do not provide the asserted common selection.
