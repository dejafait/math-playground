# Hodge conjecture: argument overview

The exact target is surjectivity of the rational cycle-class map for every smooth projective complex variety and every codimension, as recorded in [the primary-source audit](foundations/01-target-and-scope.md). No complete candidate argument has been developed.

The named standard inputs are Lefschetz (1,1), Hard Lefschetz, and the cohomological functorialities in [foundations](foundations/02-standard-inputs.md). They cover codimensions 0, 1, n-1, and n, hence all dimensions at most three. For a fourfold, L001 separates Hdg^2 into its primitive part and the algebraic summand h Hdg^1. This locates the first unresolved case without reducing the general conjecture to dimension four.

## Unresolved gap

Even on an arbitrary fourfold, no argument constructs algebraic cycles for every primitive rational (2,2)-class. Higher dimensions and codimensions also remain unresolved. The actual threshold is surjectivity for all such classes; examples of algebraic primitive classes do not reach it.

Universal generation by products of divisors cannot fill this gap. On a smooth quartic self-product, L002 gives an algebraic primitive class outside that span. L003 identifies Hdg^2 modulo divisor products with End_Hdg(T(S)), where T(S) is the cup-product orthogonal complement of rational divisor classes. The diagonal contributes the scalar identity, so the quotient after adjoining it is End_Hdg(T(S))/Q id. Every class modulo divisor products has a primitive representative in T(S) tensor T(S).

The exact requirement on this family is algebraic realization of every Hodge endomorphism of T(S). Scalar endomorphisms are already realized by the diagonal. They suffice when the endomorphism space is Q id, but C003a constructs an algebraic non-scalar graph on the Fermat quartic, ruling out a universal divisor-plus-diagonal span. No construction realizing every endomorphism for arbitrary S, or covering arbitrary fourfolds, is supplied here.

[Buskin's theorem](foundations/03-buskin-isometry-theorem.md) supplies algebraic classes for rational Hodge isometries of K3 second cohomology. L004 tests its linear span when the full transcendental endomorphism field E is totally real: the only self-isometries on T(S) are +id and -id. Even closed chains of isometries through other projective K3 surfaces give no further actions. Thus this mechanism returns exactly the divisor-plus-diagonal span, leaving [E:Q]-1 residual dimensions when [E:Q] > 1. This conditional calculation does not construct a quartic with prescribed E and does not exclude other algebraic correspondences.

A nonisometric construction does fill the residual gap for the very general member of [van Geemen–Schütt's quadratic RM family](foundations/04-degree-two-rm-family.md), which is not assumed to be quartic. L005 checks a degree-two rational self-map, correcting the direction of a displayed coordinate scaling. Its graph acts on T by U with U^2 = 2 id and q(Ur,Us) = 2q(r,s). Since the full field is Q(sqrt(2)), the identity and U span it. After justifying the residual calculation for these projective K3 models, divisor products, the diagonal, and this graph span all rational (2,2)-classes. Arbitrary K3 surfaces and arbitrary fourfolds remain outside this result.

For the very general member of [the cubic RM family](foundations/05-cubic-rm-family.md), L006 resolves and normalizes a dihedral-cover correspondence Z. It acts by U with minimal polynomial z^3+z^2-2z-1. The diagonal, Z, and the algebraic composition Z^(circ 2) therefore realize the full field Q(zeta_7+zeta_7^(-1)); the residual criterion makes every rational (2,2)-class algebraic on this self-product as well. This three-dimensional family does not exhaust its four-dimensional RM deformation space.

L007 identifies the non-lci crossing at the pair of zero-section points over infinity and gives the exact ideal-sheaf lifting obstruction. L008 accounts for the whole fibre at infinity: C has three transverse double points, and its normal sheaf is the pushforward of the normal bundle of its smooth normalization's map to S x S. An embedded first-order lift forces the two double-cover involutions, and hence their dihedral action, to lift. The allowed Weierstrass coefficients then give only the Dickson family. Retaining the exceptional (-2)-curve excludes the otherwise invisible A_1 resolution tangent. The obstruction kernel on the four-dimensional NS-fixed RM tangent space is exactly the three-dimensional family tangent, so the transverse direction is obstructed for this support even though its class stays of Hodge type. This leaves different cycle representatives and algebraic extensions of the class unresolved.

L009 shows that adding the diagonal does not remove that obstruction. The union acquires two elliptic double curves with local smoothing parameters. A global lift would turn its diagonal branch into a meromorphic vector field with at most simple poles on those two fibres. Projection to the base has degree four but must vanish at the 21 nodal values, forcing the smoothing residues to vanish and the diagonal to persist. The residual lift of C extends over the three isolated points by the Hartogs property of its normal sheaf. Thus this union still lifts in exactly the three Dickson-family directions, leaving the fourth RM direction unresolved for other representatives.

L010 tests the different union C with F_+ x F_+. Its double curve is the diagonal elliptic curve D in that fibre product B. A lifted B branch has normal displacement in H^0(B,N_B(D)), but N_B is trivial and genus-one Riemann–Roch makes this space equal to H^0(B,N_B). Thus the apparent simple poles cannot occur. A product of moving fibres persists, and its residual ideal gives a flat lift of C. This union again has exactly the three-dimensional kernel V_D; adding this reduced component supplies no transverse extension of U.

L011 tests the specified length-two thickening B_2 along the base conic. In the local ideal (s,r^2 z), comparison with a reference lift still permits only a simple pole along the Cartier intersection z=0. The two nilpotent layers each have the elliptic no-pole property, so every normal displacement extends regularly. A possibly deformed length-two component persists, and cancellation in its residual ideal extracts a flat lift of C without flattening the double structure. The kernel remains V_D. This nonreduced representative also fails to extend U transversely.

## Partial results

The standard low-dimensional cases and the precise primitive fourfold reduction have full proofs in L001. The correspondence calculation now measures exactly what the tested generators miss on quartic self-products: the divisor-plus-diagonal span has dimension rho^2 + 3, whereas Hdg^2 has dimension rho^2 + 2 + dim_Q End_Hdg(T(S)). The Fermat inverse graph makes the residual dimension at least one and has an algebraic primitive representative. These generator obstructions rule out stronger spanning assertions, not the Hodge conjecture.

For the very general quadratic RM family, rho = 10 and L005 reaches all 104 rational (2,2)-dimensions, compared with 103 from divisor products and the diagonal. This proves the codimension-two conjecture for that family of self-products from a published family theorem and an explicit graph; it is not a new general resolution or a novelty claim.

For the very general cubic RM family, rho = 4 and L006 reaches all 21 rational (2,2)-dimensions, compared with 19 from divisor products and the diagonal. The construction is published; the normalization, composition, and exact spanning calculation are checked here. L008–L011 give rank-one embedded obstructions on the four-dimensional RM tangent space for C and its tested unions with the diagonal, smooth fibre product, or specified length-two fibre thickening. These representatives do not extend the achieved span to the missing direction. The family results leave the universal target unresolved.

## Known traps checked

- All target coefficients are rational; integral counterexamples are irrelevant to this test.
- The test variety is smooth and projective, with the explicit ample class h_1 + h_2.
- The class outside divisor products is itself algebraic. Primitivity and failure of divisor generation are not evidence of nonalgebraicity.
- Rationality is essential when excluding mixed divisor/transcendental Hodge tensors; the complex (1,1)-part of T(S) has not been set to zero.
- The tensor/endomorphism identification constructs cohomology classes, not cycles. The displayed residual quotient is a vector-space quotient, and full algebraic realization remains a separate requirement.
- Buskin's isometry hypothesis is retained. The totally real test concerns the full endomorphism field and rational isometries; it is neither an integral-lattice argument nor an obstruction to nonisometric correspondences. No contradiction with the known CM consequence is asserted.
- The degree-two self-map is resolved before computing its graph action. Exceptional divisors contribute no transcendental part, and the pairing scales by two on T; no degree formula on all of H^2 for a naive rational pullback is assumed. The full quadratic endomorphism field is asserted only for a very general member, not for every specialization.
- The dihedral correspondence is defined by proper pushforward from a smooth resolution. Trace is a sum over two sheets, fixing the factor 1/2 for the pushed symmetric graph sum. Its square means composition of correspondences, and its polynomial relation is asserted on T, not in the Chow ring. The full cubic field is used only at very general parameters; persistence of a Hodge class is not assumed to deform its algebraic cycle.
- A smooth source for a pushforward cycle is not assumed to be its embedded support. The cubic support is non-lci at three double points. Its global obstruction is computed using simultaneous normalization of these specific locally rigid crossings, not a smooth-support semiregularity theorem. The finite double-cover and involution arguments retain all points over infinity.
- Weierstrass coefficients alone do not determine an arbitrary infinitesimal resolution of an A_1 point. NS-fixedness lifts the exceptional curve and removes that ambiguity. The resulting obstruction concerns this embedded support; it does not obstruct every algebraic representative of its Hodge class or give a Hodge counterexample.
- The diagonal union has locally smoothable curve crossings, so local rigidity of C alone cannot decide its deformation. Its global obstruction uses the same deformation in both ambient factors, the nodal-fibre zero count, and Hartogs for N_C. No simultaneous normalization of the union or automatic flatness of a residual ideal at its three-branch points is assumed.
- The fibre-product union has its own local smoothing parameter. Its failure uses the absence of genuine simple normal poles along a single diagonal in an elliptic product, an explicit reference lift, and a flatness calculation for the residual. It is not deduced merely from local rigidity of C or from a vanishing of H^1(N_B). The argument does not cover nonreduced added components or higher pole orders.
- The length-two calculation separately treats the nilpotent normal module and Cartier intersection. Regularity is proved on both layers, and containment uses injectivity of localization rather than reduced density. The residual calculation allows r^2=epsilon b and does not assume the thickening can be made constant by coordinates. No obstruction for every nonreduced representative is asserted.
- The Fermat graph is an algebraic cycle over C with rational cohomology class even though its equation uses i. Its inverse-graph convention is checked explicitly.
- Inverse Hard Lefschetz is used only to recover a rational (1,1)-class, which is algebraic by the named theorem; no algebraic inverse correspondence is assumed.
- The separation uses a complex holomorphic form to test equality of rational classes. It does not identify all complex (2,2)-classes with rational Hodge classes.
- Neither the standard low-dimensional cases nor the fourfold reduction establishes the exact universal target; no verified resolution is claimed.
