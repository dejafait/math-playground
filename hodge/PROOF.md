# Hodge conjecture: argument overview

The exact target is surjectivity of the rational cycle-class map for every smooth projective complex variety and every codimension, as recorded in [the primary-source audit](foundations/01-target-and-scope.md). No complete candidate argument has been developed. The named standard inputs are Lefschetz (1,1), Hard Lefschetz, and the cohomological functorialities in [foundations](foundations/02-standard-inputs.md). They cover codimensions 0, 1, n-1, and n, hence all dimensions at most three. For a fourfold, L001 separates Hdg^2 into its primitive part and the algebraic summand h Hdg^1. This locates the first unresolved case without reducing the general conjecture to dimension four.

## Unresolved gap

Even on an arbitrary fourfold, no argument constructs algebraic cycles for every primitive rational (2,2)-class. Higher dimensions and codimensions also remain unresolved. The actual threshold is surjectivity for all such classes; examples of algebraic primitive classes do not reach it.

Universal generation by products of divisors cannot fill this gap. On a smooth quartic self-product, L002 gives an algebraic primitive class outside that span. L003 identifies Hdg^2 modulo divisor products with End_Hdg(T(S)), where T(S) is the cup-product orthogonal complement of rational divisor classes. The diagonal contributes the scalar identity, so the quotient after adjoining it is End_Hdg(T(S))/Q id. Every class modulo divisor products has a primitive representative in T(S) tensor T(S).
