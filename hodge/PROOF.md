# Hodge conjecture: argument overview

The exact target is surjectivity of the rational cycle-class map for every smooth projective complex variety and every codimension, as recorded in [the primary-source audit](foundations/01-target-and-scope.md). No complete candidate argument has been developed. The named standard inputs are Lefschetz (1,1), Hard Lefschetz, and the cohomological functorialities in [foundations](foundations/02-standard-inputs.md). They cover codimensions 0, 1, n-1, and n, hence all dimensions at most three. For a fourfold, L001 separates Hdg^2 into its primitive part and the algebraic summand h Hdg^1. This locates the first unresolved case without reducing the general conjecture to dimension four.
