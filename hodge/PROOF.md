## Assembly and unresolved gap

The [exact target](foundations/01-target-and-scope.md) is surjectivity of the rational cycle-class map for every smooth projective complex variety and every codimension. Lefschetz (1,1), Hard Lefschetz and functoriality cover the recorded extremal codimensions; L001 isolates the primitive (2,2) gap on fourfolds, without reducing the general conjecture to that case.

No argument here constructs algebraic cycles for every primitive class, even on arbitrary fourfolds. L002/L003 show why universal divisor-product generation, or adjoining just the diagonal, is insufficient on the K3 self-product family. Special algebraic classes do not establish the required uniform realization of Hodge endomorphisms.

## Partial results

The current branch tests hypothetical bundle/kernel data on identical marked K3 factors with fixed connection and Chern character. Its cup, concentration, stability and trace calculations are conditional restrictions on that branch. Audited L159 excludes two extremal z=0 branches using the actual opposite-coefficient harmonic map, square +1 and strict normalized trace signs. It neither improves the retained z<=119 relaxation nor gives an independent lower comparison. Allowing positive z retains the ordinary twisted top sheaf W and changes essential concentration/edge hypotheses.

The existence of the equality-kernel F, the proposed positive-z obstruction, other stable summands, transport and global primitive Hodge realization remain unproved. All specific markings, twists, integral classes and audit qualifications live in the canonical statements; do not replace their actual cup maps with arbitrary matrices.

## Known traps checked

- A Hodge-theoretic decomposition or finite-dimensional compatible model is not algebraic realization. Riemann–Roch identities alone do not construct the required cycle or sheaf.
- Preserve actual twists, ordinary versus derived top terms, parallel reduction, edge degrees and all independent Euler/trace signs. A z=0 exclusion cannot silently be reused for positive z.
- Restricted K3 calculations do not settle arbitrary fourfolds, higher dimensions or all codimensions. No complete candidate is asserted.
