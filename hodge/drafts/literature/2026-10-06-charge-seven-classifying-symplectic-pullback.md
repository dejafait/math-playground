## Inspected primary family formula

sheaves via the Atiyah class*, arXiv:math/0703264v2, 19 October 2007,
Section 2, pp. 8--10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0703264v2#page=9).
Theorem 2.2 concerns a smooth projective complex Y, stable or simple
sheaf moduli M, and a class in H^(n-q-2)(Y,Omega_Y^(n-q)). Its
Atiyah/Yoneda/trace formula gives a closed two-form on M's smooth
locus. For a smooth affine parameter B and B-flat family, equations
(13)--(14) express pullback by the family Kodaira--Spencer map.
The proof splits the Atiyah class into parameter and Y components,
identifies the relevant Kunneth term of its traced power, and proves
closedness. Section 2 explicitly admits simple-sheaf charts and the
analytic counterpart; neither separatedness nor a global universal
sheaf is a hypothesis of the family calculation. Smoothness must
still be checked before applying the assertion about M's smooth locus.
For K3 factors the surface case uses a holomorphic two-form and the
trace of the Yoneda product. This is imported coverage, not an
evaluation for the saved F.

## Normalization and descent reference

1997 text, printed pp. 219--225 and 227--228, PDF pp. 231--236
and 238--239](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/files/HuybrechtsLehn.pdf#page=231).
Section 10.1.6 identifies traced Atiyah powers with factorial-scaled
Chern-character components. Sections 10.1.7--10.1.8 give relative
trace, graded signs and the Kodaira--Spencer construction.
Definition 10.3.1 and Lemma 10.3.3 identify the family form through

Kodaira--Spencer/Yoneda/trace. Lemma 10.3.4 handles tensoring by a
parameter bundle; Theorem 10.3.5 normalizes a quasi-universal family
by its similitude. Section 10.2 describes descent using local families.
Proposition 10.4.1 proves the nondegeneracy criterion by Serre duality;
Corollary 10.4.2 and Theorem 10.4.3 treat trivial canonical bundle.
These are supporting references; their projective stable-moduli setup
is not an existence theorem for irrational-h algebraic moduli.
