## Primary statements and essential proof cases

**Holomorphic formality already covered.** Bandiera--Manetti--Meazzini,
*Formality conjecture for minimal surfaces of Kodaira dimension 0*,
Compositio Math. 157 (2021), 215--235,
[1907.10690v2, 2 August 2020](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.10690v2),
Theorem 5.1 and full proof, p. 15: a coherent sheaf on a complex projective
surface with trivial canonical bundle has formal derived-endomorphism
DG Lie algebra if its automorphism group is linearly reductive.
Re-inspected the equivariant resolution, cyclic pairing, splitting and
rational finitely supported representation cases; reuse Theorem 2.3,
pp. 5--6, from the ready rank-five assessment for split DG-module transfer.
This avoids an unassessed substitution of irrational slope polystability
into an ample-line-bundle theorem. L094 already supplies the split bundle
and vanishing of the off-diagonal Hom spaces. The future proof must check
reductivity and the trace-free module splitting; neither specialization
is newly derived here. An abstract formal equivalence is not a convergent
unitary, metric or quaternionic chart. Import these general results rather
than reprove them.

**Direct connection-formality comparison.** Meazzini--Onorati,
*Hyper-holomorphic connections on vector bundles on hyper-Kähler
manifolds*, Math. Z. 303 (2023), article 17, DOI
10.1007/s00209-022-03176-4; read
[2204.04075v2, 26 July 2022](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.04075v2).
Theorem 4.3, pp. 19--20, gives associative Dolbeault formality for a
projectively hyperholomorphic connection, without a stability hypothesis;
its proof uses the strong two-differential lemma and derivations.
Corollary 4.2 supplies holomorphic quadraticity. In contrast, Corollary
3.11/Question 3.15, pp. 15--16, do not establish general quaternionic
Dolbeault formality. Proposition 3.16's special case requires homotopy
abelianity, which is not supplied here. Definition 2.8 distinguishes
metric hyperholomorphic from general autodual connections. Definition
5.1 and Theorem 5.8, pp. 22--24, concern complex Artin bases and
infinitesimal autodual deformations. Corollary 5.9 and its complete proof,
p. 24, use Proposition 3.18's surjectivity and Proposition 3.19's tangent
comparison; no convergence or Hermitian condition is supplied for the
lift. Thus none of these statements imports the desired unitary
quaternionic surface germ. This is a scope comparison, not an assertion
that the paper's question remains globally open in 2026.

The subsequent [Meazzini--Onorati 2509.03180v1, 3 September 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.03180v1),
*Deformations of twisted sheaves and formality results*, Theorem 5.5,
p. 20, and its complete proof, extends endomorphism Dolbeault formality
to projectively hyperholomorphic twisted bundles. Its three proof cases
are the derivation property, the two differential identities and the
strong differential lemma. It does not replace those conclusions by a
fixed-metric quaternionic connection model. No twist is needed or assumed
for the present ordinary split bundle.

**Finite analytic equations and multiple parameters.** Reuse
Buchdahl--Schumacher, *Polystable bundles and representations of their
automorphisms*, Complex Manifolds 9 (2022), 78--113,
[Proposition 2.6 and (2.4), pp. 82--84](https://d-nb.info/1262812674/34),
DOI 10.1515/coma-2021-0131. Re-inspected the correction uniqueness,
elliptic residual bound, holomorphic harmonic obstruction and equivariance
passages. On a fixed compact Kähler manifold, for Sobolev p>2 dim_C X,
a small harmonic parameter has a uniquely corrected integrable Dolbeault
operator exactly on this finite analytic zero locus. E is already
polystable at the retained real h. The trace-free restriction and an
actual determinant trivialization must be justified in the future proof.
No quotient lift or exact ASD moment normal form is imported.

Michael Artin, *On the solutions of analytic equations*, Invent. Math.
5 (1968), 277--291, Theorem (1.2), p. 277, remains the precise original
citation from the ready realization assessment. Its original full proof
was not newly retrieved. Directly read the multivariable statement and
inductive proof strategy in Guillaume Rond, *Artin approximation*,
J. Singularities 17 (2018), 108--192,
[Theorem 2.1, p. 122, proof reductions pp. 123--126](https://www.journalofsingularities.com/volume17/rond.pdf#page=15).
For a characteristic-zero valued field and finitely many convergent
equations f(x,y)=0, a formal solution vanishing at zero has convergent
solutions agreeing modulo (x)^c for any c. There is no one-parameter
restriction; over C, c=2 can preserve a two-parameter first jet. It
requires an actual formal solution of the complete equations. It neither
proves convergence of that series nor preserves an additional differential
quaternionic/isometry condition absent from the finite equation system.
No such compatible formal map has been constructed in this review.
