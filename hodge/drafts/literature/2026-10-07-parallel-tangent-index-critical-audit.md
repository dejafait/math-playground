## Primary source comparison

[Huybrechts--Lehn, *The Geometry of Moduli Spaces of Sheaves*,
Aspects of Mathematics E31, Vieweg (1997), Section 10.2,
Theorem 10.2.1, printed pp. 222--223](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/files/HuybrechtsLehn.pdf#page=234):
positive-rank stable sheaves on a smooth projective surface, restricted
to the open locus with vanishing trace-free Ext^2. The construction

relative Ext, trace and cup product descend. The proof verifies base
