8.1, p. 103, assumes a reference connection with scalar contracted
curvature, a sufficiently small harmonic parameter polystable for the
reference automorphism group, and exactly the correction of Proposition
2.6. Its constant depends on the reference connection. The theorem gives
a complex gauge producing scalar contracted curvature; integrability
must separately hold to infer holomorphic bundle polystability. The
minimal-norm replacement and stable/reducible reductions on pp. 103--104
are proof devices, not extra hypotheses demanding that L102's original
parameter itself minimize its orbit norm. The uniform closure step on

direction-dependent, would be inadequate here.
