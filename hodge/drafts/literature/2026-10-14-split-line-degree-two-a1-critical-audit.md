## Exact primary cases

G.-M. Greuel, C. Lossen and E. Shustin, *Introduction to Singularities
and Deformations*, Springer (2007), ISBN 978-3-540-28380-5,
[Chapter I, inspected 2026-10-14](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/greuel.pdf).
Definition I.1.1 and Remark I.1.1.1(4–5), p. 9, cover analytic algebras,
automatic locality and convergent substitution; Exercise I.1.1.4,
p. 19, gives uniqueness and convergence proof instructions. These
cover the ring-map/holomorphic-germ bridge, not its application to tau.

Theorem I.1.8, p. 12, divides convergent series by a series general of
finite order in the last variable, with a unique convergent quotient
and polynomial remainder of smaller degree. For the cited divisor
y^2-xz, place y last: at x=z=0 it is y^2, so the applicable order is two.
Read uniqueness p. 13 and the complete norm-estimate proof pp. 17–18;
the proof establishes convergence, beyond formal division.

Theorem I.1.20, p. 27, equates surjectivity of an analytic algebra map
with surjectivity of its cotangent map. Its complete proof uses
Theorem I.1.10 and Corollary I.1.11, pp. 13–14. Normality, smoothness
and flatness are not hypotheses. The audit must verify the actual
map A to R and its cotangent classes.
