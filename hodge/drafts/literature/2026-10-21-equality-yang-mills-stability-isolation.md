### Primary results and essential proof cases

Jean-Pierre Bourguignon and H. Blaine Lawson Jr., *Stability and
isolation phenomena for Yang–Mills fields*, Communications in
Mathematical Physics **79** (1981), 189–230,
[DOI 10.1007/BF01942061](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01942061), was read
in the [author-uploaded full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/38329079_Stability_and_isolation_phenomena_for_Yang-Mills_fields).
Theorem (7.12), pp. 217–219, treats weakly stable SU(2), SU(3), U(2)
fields on the round S^4. Theorem (10.1), pp. 227–229, treats SU(2)
on compact oriented homogeneous Riemannian four-manifolds, concluding
self-duality, anti-self-duality or abelian reduction. Its proof contracts
curvature with Killing fields; homogeneity supplies evaluations spanning
each tangent space. Hessian nonnegativity and a trace-zero identity
then force commuting self-dual/anti-self-dual curvature algebras.
This is not an eight-dimensional product-block decomposition.

The general Hessian formula (6.5), pp. 212–213, follows by differentiating
curvature twice and using the Yang–Mills equation to remove the
acceleration term. It alone gives no sign-improved mixed equation.
Theorem (5.5), pp. 205–206 and its following discussion use a positive
lower curvature-operator bound to dominate a cubic term for small
curvature. Theorem (5.20) is spherical; (5.26), pp. 211–212, assumes
half-conformal flatness and **positive** scalar curvature in dimension
four. Neither is a Ricci-flat-product theorem. Damaged OCR constants
are not used as exact thresholds.

Mark Stern, *Geometry of minimal energy Yang–Mills connections*,
Journal of Differential Geometry **86** (2010), 163–188,
[primary PDF](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/21/852-JDG-86-1-A5-stern.pdf),
Theorem 1.1, p. 164, extends the adjoint splitting conclusion to compact
groups on complete oriented nonnegatively curved homogeneous
four-manifolds, for finite-energy locally minimizing connections.
The introduction explains why Killing contractions are essential;
(2.5)–(2.6), p. 167, give the general second-variation inequality.
Section 6, pp. 184–186, instead uses a parallel (3,0)-form on a
Calabi–Yau threefold to turn the (0,2) curvature into a one-form.
Theorem 6.21 requires stability for E'=4||F_A^(0,2)||^2; its proof
retains bracket constraints before concluding parallelness of the
non-(1,1) sector. Our base has complex dimension four, and HYM already
annihilates that complex-type sector. A product-mixed (1,1) block is
not the sector addressed. No theorem is specialized across that change.
