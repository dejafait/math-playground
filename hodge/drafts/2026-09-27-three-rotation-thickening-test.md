## Reasoning saved before completion

At the generic point of C^(2), the ordinary square of its regular codimension-two ideal has quotient length 1+2=3. Consequently the fundamental cycle is [C]+3[C^(2)]+[C^(3)]. The normalized rotation actions sum to -id, so this cycle acts as 2U^2-5 id, a non-scalar endomorphism. This passes only the preliminary relevance test.

At a finite crossing of a reduced component (z,r) with the squared component (z,q)^2, the exact intersection is

    I=(z^2,zq,rq^2).

Writing B=C[[w,r,q,z]]/I, its syzygies are generated by q*f-z*g and rq*g-z*h for f=z^2, g=zq, h=rq^2. A homomorphism phi:I -> B has unique expressions

    phi(f)=rq*A(r)+z*B0(r),
    phi(g)=q*C(r)+q^2*D(q)+z*E(r),
    phi(h)=q*M(r)+q^2*N(q)+z*P(r),

with coefficients also depending on w. On the reduced branch, its normal r-displacement has residue M(0)/q. On the thickened branch away from r=0, the normalized trace of its rank-three algebra has q-displacement

    epsilon*(M(r)/r+E(r))/3.

The prospective common residue is therefore M(0) on the reduced branch and M(0)/3 on the trace section of the thick branch. This is the new local mechanism; using a symbolic square or a ribbon would change it.

For a first-order deformation of a rank-three square-zero algebra with basis 1,x,y, associativity forces the constant coefficients of the deformed x^2,xy,y^2 to vanish. Their normalized traces vanish as well, and trace/3 is a ring homomorphism. This should give an intrinsic section over the first projection on the dense complete-fibre locus, without assuming a nilpotent filtration or a reduced component lifts. Its second projection reduces to the original elliptic-fibre isomorphism. Complete fibres then force equality of the two j-invariants on this trace section, as on each separate reduced sheet.

Near the crossing, J_0(t)-J_0(s)=h*r*q with h a unit. Equality on the reduced and trace sections should give, with the same critical-point difference d,

    d+h*M(0)=0,     d+h*M(0)/3=0.

Thus M(0)=d=0. There are mixed crossings for the critical pairs {2,3} and {1,3}; if justified, their equalities also kill the ordinary reduced--reduced smoothing at {1,2}. The recovered reduced C would then extend across the three infinity points by L008's normal-sheaf Hartogs property.

Remaining checks before a kernel claim: prove the trace construction and residue calculation for every allowed ideal lift, justify its complete-fibre use, check reduced-component recovery and gluing, and prove the reverse inclusion for the full ordinary-square ideal at infinity. There the exact ideal is

    (J_1 intersect J_-1) intersect (J_2 intersect J_-2)^2
        intersect (J_3 intersect J_-3).

Relative finite-group linearization should make this entire ideal constant along the Dickson family; it must not be replaced by the intersection of the individual squared graph ideals. No completed kernel result is asserted at this checkpoint.
