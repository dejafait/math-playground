# L110 — Actual split-fibre first variation gives higher-image length one

## Hypotheses

Retain the entire conditional family of corrected L108: p:S_1 x B -> B
is the projection of the identical marked projective K3 surfaces, with
the fixed common hyperkähler metric and full tensor tau_D. The hypothetical
locally free rank-two F is polystable at the real product polarization,
has integral c_1(F)=0 and ch_2(F)=-14e_1-21e_2-tau_D, and carries L071's
compatible connection. Set V=End_0(F), C=Rp_*V and W=R^2p_*V.
The split parameter set Z is finite and possibly empty, as in L108.

At any closed z in Z the actual fibre is E_0=L direct sum L^{-1},
with c_1(L)=k, k^2=-14 and h.k=0. Use L094's split cohomology and
actual-family harmonic/Dolbeault comparison and L095's actual injective
harmonic derivative, including at z. Use L108's algebraic perfect
pushforward and derived base change, retaining the distinction between
ordinary stalks and derived fibres. For the stated defect-budget
consequences also use audited L109's q_z<=w_z. No point of Z, F, reduced
defect structure, ordinary base change at z or cotangent rank is assumed.

## Conclusion

For R=O_{B,z}, maximal ideal m and residue field kappa=C, one has

\[
                       W_z\simeq R/m,
             \qquad \operatorname{length}_R(W_z)=1.       \tag{1}
\]

The isomorphism to R/m involves a choice of a generator; (1) canonically
says that the stalk is one-dimensional over kappa and is annihilated by m.
In particular, writing s=|Z|, corrected L108 and L109 give

\[
 w=s,\qquad 0\le q\le s,\qquad
 c_2(N)=(800-s-q)\eta,
 \quad 800-2s\le c_2(N)/\eta\le800-s.                    \tag{2}
\]

For Z empty this means w=q=0; the assertions at z are vacuous. No bound
on s, metric extension, semistability, F existence, transport or primitive
Hodge realization follows. This is a conditional ADVANCE / REPRODUCTION
of the assessed deformation and local-algebra tools. The separate critical
audit below preserves it, with outcome EXPLORATION / REPRODUCTION: no
substantive error or stronger conclusion was found. It is not a complete
candidate for the Hodge conjecture.

**Minimal complex and the map whose rank is needed.** Put
V_0=End_0(E_0). L094 gives

\[
 V_0=O h_0\oplus L^2\oplus L^{-2},\quad
 h_0=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1),\qquad
 (h^0(V_0),h^1(V_0),h^2(V_0))=(1,52,1).                 \tag{3}
\]

Indeed each off-diagonal summand has h^1=26 and zero endpoint
cohomology, while the trivial diagonal line has cohomology (1,0,1).
L108's minimal representative over R can therefore be taken as

\[
 K:\quad R\xrightarrow{d^0}R^{52}\xrightarrow{d^1}R,
       \qquad d^i\equiv0\pmod m,                       \tag{4}
\]

in degrees 0,1,2. In particular W_z=H^2(K)=R/I, where I is the
ideal generated by the entries of the last row d^1 and I subset m.
This is a presentation of an ordinary stalk obtained from a perfect
complex, not an ordinary cohomology-and-base-change assertion at z.

Let T=Hom_kappa(m/m^2,kappa)=T_zB. Each u in T gives the actual
infinitesimal base map R -> A=kappa[epsilon]/(epsilon^2),
f -> f(z)+epsilon u(f-f(z)). Denote the linearized last row by

\[
 \ell(u):K^1\otimes kappa\longrightarrow K^2\otimes kappa.
                                                               \tag{5}
\]

The exact sequence of complexes 0 -> K_kappa -> K_A -> K_kappa -> 0
uses epsilon for the first injection. Since K_kappa has zero
differentials, its connecting map in degrees 1 to 2 is exactly (5):
lift b to K_A^1, apply d_A^1=epsilon ell(u), and divide by epsilon.
Under derived base change these are H^1(V_0) and H^2(V_0). We next
identify this connecting map for the actual coefficient deformation.

**Actual first variation, including the split point.** Pull back F
by this infinitesimal base map and call the bundle F_A on S_1 x Spec A.
It gives the exact coefficient sequence

\[
 0\longrightarrow V_0\xrightarrow{\epsilon}
     \operatorname{End}_0(F_A)\longrightarrow V_0\longrightarrow0.
                                                               \tag{6}
\]

This is also an exact sequence of coherent O_{S_1}-modules after
forgetting A along the finite product projection. Thus the usual
projective GAGA cohomology comparison, with its natural maps, compares
its algebraic and analytic connecting homomorphisms on S_1 itself.
No assertion about ordinary base change of R^1p_*V at z is needed.
The functorial derived-base-change identification for C identifies
the triangle of (6) with that of K_A; connecting-map naturality
therefore identifies its degree-one connecting map with ell(u).

In the Dolbeault realization of the actual bundle deformation choose
a smooth splitting to write its fibre differential as
bar-partial_{E_0}+epsilon a_u, with bar-partial a_u=0. Its class is
the actual determinant-fixed KS class kappa_z(u) in H^1(V_0).
Trace-free representatives can be chosen using the determinant
trivialization; a scalar component would commute with every coefficient
and would not change the following calculation. The induced differential
on degree-j endomorphism-valued forms b is

\[
 \bar\partial b+\epsilon[a_u,b],\qquad
 [a_u,b]=a_u\wedge b-(-1)^j b\wedge a_u.                \tag{7}
\]

This is the adjoint module, rather than the defining module or
one-sided multiplication. Conjugating the smooth splitting changes
a_u by a Dolbeault coboundary, so changes no induced map in cohomology.
Trace splitting commutes with (7). Computing the connecting map of
(6) using (7), with the same epsilon-injection convention as (5), gives

\[
                  \ell(u)[b]=[a_u\wedge b+b\wedge a_u]
                         \quad\hbox{in }H^2(V_0).       \tag{8}
\]

The actual KS used here is injective. To check that this is L095's
derivative even at the split point, use L094's smooth unitary family
comparison r_u=dot A_u-d_A H_u. Its (0,1) part differs from the
actual Dolbeault derivative by bar-partial H_u. L095 makes r_u
harmonic, and L094 identifies real skew-adjoint harmonic one-forms
with Dolbeault harmonic forms by r -> r^{0,1}, with inverse
b -> b-b^*. This is an injective real-linear identification. The
quaternionic comparison gives r_{Iu}=I_c r_u and I_c acts as i on
the (0,1) component. Hence the real derivative is the underlying map
of the complex-linear KS map T -> H^1(V_0). Its kernel is zero by
L095's norm identity ||r_u||^2=2lambda||u||^2 with lambda>0.
This uses the actual family before the stabilizer quotient, not a
coarse-moduli tangent at a reducible point.

**Graded Serre transpose and full rank.** Use the fixed fibre two-form
sigma and the nonzero normalization c=(2pi i)^(-2) from L108/L109.
The forms

\[
 \Omega(x,b)=c\int_{S_1}\sigma\wedge\operatorname{Tr}(x\wedge b),
 \qquad
 P(h,t)=c\int_{S_1}\sigma\wedge\operatorname{Tr}(h t)             \tag{9}
\]

are respectively the nondegenerate alternating Serre form on H^1(V_0)
and the perfect pairing H^0(V_0) x H^2(V_0) -> C. Nondegeneracy
uses the actual coefficient trace pairing and surface Serre duality.
Since H^0(V_0)=C h_0, the map t -> P(h_0,t) identifies H^2(V_0)
with C. In particular it is not zero: on the diagonal line
Tr(h_0^2)=2, and pairing H^2(O) with the nonzero sigma is perfect.

For closed degree-one representatives a,b, graded cyclicity gives
Tr(h_0 b wedge a)=-Tr(a h_0 wedge b). Consequently (8) gives the
exact transpose identity

\[
 P(h_0,\ell(u)b)
   =c\int\sigma\wedge\operatorname{Tr}
          (h_0 a_u\wedge b+h_0 b\wedge a_u)
   =\Omega([h_0,a_u],b).                                \tag{10}
\]

The plus sign in the degree-one bracket in (8) is essential. For
example an upper-right a and lower-left b give twice their wedge
under the h_0 trace, agreeing with ad_{h_0}(a)=2a.

On H^1(V_0)=H^1(L^2) direct sum H^1(L^{-2}), ad_{h_0} acts by
+2 and -2. There is no diagonal H^1(O) sector. It is therefore
invertible on this full 52-dimensional space. If ell(u)=0, (10)
and the nondegeneracy of Omega imply ad_{h_0} kappa_z(u)=0;
invertibility and KS injectivity imply u=0. Thus

\[
             T\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}(H^1(V_0),H^2(V_0)),
                  \quad u\longmapsto\ell(u)
                 \quad\hbox{is injective}.              \tag{11}
\]

This tests b in all of H^1(V_0), not just in the image of KS.
It is consistent with the vanishing of the quadratic obstruction
on the actual tangent plane in L094; that narrower restriction
does not assert that (10) vanishes for every b.

**Cotangent generation and length.** After choosing the generator
of K^2 in (4), the linear last row is a map

\[
             H^1(V_0)\longrightarrow m/m^2.
\]

Its transpose is (11), using the same generator of H^2(V_0).
Finite-dimensional duality and dim_C T=2 show that the row spans
all of m/m^2. Thus I+m^2=m. The finite R-module m/I satisfies
m(m/I)=(m^2+I)/I=m/I. Nakayama gives m/I=0, so I=m and
H^2(K)=R/m. This proves (1) with actual and required cotangent
ranks both equal to two; the ordinary stalk length is exactly one,
not inferred from the derived-fibre dimension one.

Finally W is supported on the finite set Z and has length one at
each of its points, so w=s. L109 gives 0<=q_z<=1 and therefore
0<=q<=s. Substitution into corrected L108 gives (2). These are
only defect-budget consequences; controlling s and extending the
chosen metric or proving semistability are independent unresolved tasks.

The following checks the actual comparison and all rank-to-length steps.

First check naturality before assigning a formula to the first variation.
After restriction to Spec R, a finite affine cover of the separated
surface S_1 gives a bounded Cech complex J for V. On each affine
intersection the coefficient is a finite projective module over the
product coordinate ring, which is flat over R. Thus the terms of J are
R-flat. Tensoring J with A or kappa computes the corresponding coefficient
cohomology: these are the actual base-changed vector bundles and their
affine Cech complexes. This concretely realizes the derived base-change
isomorphism of [Stacks 0B91](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B91),
whose proper-flat and perfect-coefficient hypotheses hold here.

Choose a comparison K -> J representing their isomorphism in the derived
category. K is bounded free, so the comparison can be represented by a
chain map; both complexes are bounded and flat, so its scalar extensions
are quasi-isomorphisms. The same map gives a commutative diagram with
exact rows

```text
0 -> K tensor_R kappa -> K tensor_R A -> K tensor_R kappa -> 0
     |                  |               |
0 -> J tensor_R kappa -> J tensor_R A -> J tensor_R kappa -> 0.
```

The left arrows in the rows are multiplication by epsilon and the right
arrows are reduction. Consequently the connecting maps agree by 0117.
The bottom row is the Cech realization of (6), since End_0 commutes with
this base change and is A-flat. The three sheaves in (6), regarded over
S_1 by the finite projection, are coherent. The named projective GAGA cohomology comparison
is natural on this exact sequence, and the Dolbeault resolution computes
its analytic connecting map. This checks the triangle comparison in the
proof without confusing a fibre of an ordinary higher image with derived
cohomology. The identifications on the two residue-field ends are fixed
by K -> J; they do not vary with the tangent direction u.

The actual first-order bundle is represented in a smooth splitting by
bar-partial+epsilon a. On endomorphism-valued j-forms its differential
is exactly bar-partial+epsilon(a wedge (-)-(-1)^j(-) wedge a),
by the induced Hom differential. In degree one its epsilon coefficient
therefore has a plus sign between the two products. Its scalar trace
vanishes by graded cyclicity, so the coefficient remains in End_0.
Replacing a by a+bar-partial s changes [a,b], for closed b, by
bar-partial[s,b]; hence the connecting map depends on the actual KS
class and not on the smooth splitting. The FIM and BW inputs cited above
allow this nonsimple fibre; their role here does not include a stable
zero-Chern resonance theorem.

Next check the real/complex bridge at the actual reducible connection.
For a real base vector v, L094's identity gives
dot A_v^{0,1}=r_v^{0,1}+bar-partial_A H_v. The infinitesimal Dolbeault
deformation class is therefore [r_v^{0,1}]. L095 makes this representative
harmonic, and L094's inverse b -> b-b^* shows that it is zero only if
r_v is zero. Moreover r_{Iv}^{0,1}=i r_v^{0,1}. Under the standard
identification of the real tangent with the underlying complex tangent,
u=(v-iIv)/2 belongs to T^{1,0}_zB and the complex-linear extension gives

\[
 \tfrac12\bigl([r_v^{0,1}]-i[r_{Iv}^{0,1}]\bigr)
                      =[r_v^{0,1}].
\]

Thus there is no factor two or conjugation ambiguity in the relevant
kernel. L095's positive norm excludes every nonzero real v, and hence
every nonzero complex tangent u, from that kernel. This comparison is
before quotienting by the stabilizer; a change of its framing conjugates
both the KS class and the action and cannot change the rank.

For an independent sign check, write closed off-diagonal representatives

\[
 a=\begin{pmatrix}0&\alpha\\ \beta&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 b=\begin{pmatrix}0&\gamma\\ \delta&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

All four entries are degree-one forms in the appropriate line bundles.
Their products in the diagonal line obey

\[
 a\wedge b+b\wedge a
   =\operatorname{diag}(t,-t),\quad
 t=\alpha\wedge\delta-\beta\wedge\gamma,
\]
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}\bigl(h_0(a\wedge b+b\wedge a)\bigr)
   =2t=\operatorname{Tr}([h_0,a]\wedge b).
\]

This verifies both odd-degree signs and the factor two in (10).
H^1(O)=0 means these representatives cover all of H^1(V_0), not a
selected tangent subspace. The trace form pairs L^2 with L^{-2}
perfectly and has diagonal norm Tr(h_0^2)=2. Surface Serre duality
therefore makes Omega perfect and P(h_0,-) nonzero on the one-dimensional
H^2(V_0). The fixed scalar c is nonzero; no positivity premise is used.
For any nonzero u, injectivity of KS and the two weights +2,-2 give
[h_0,kappa_z(u)] nonzero. Perfectness supplies some b in the full H^1
with Omega([h_0,kappa_z(u)],b) nonzero. Thus ell(u) is nonzero. Testing
only b in the tangent plane would not justify this conclusion and is
not what the proof does; L094's compatible quadratic tangent remains
consistent with the result.

Finally, the derived fibre dimensions (1,52,1) give precisely the
minimal ranks in (4), with every differential entry in m. No other
degree contributes to H^2(K), so W_z is the ordinary cokernel R/I.
If f_1,...,f_52 are the last-row entries, the dual of
b -> sum_j b_j (f_j mod m^2) is exactly u -> ell(u), after the fixed
choice of the degree-two generator. Its injectivity gives rank two,
equal to dim(m/m^2). Therefore I+m^2=m. The module m/I is finite
because R is Noetherian, and its equality with m(m/I) is the exact
hypothesis of Nakayama. This proves I=m over the algebraic stalk itself;
no completion, radical replacement or reduced-support assumption enters.
Actual and required cotangent ranks are both two, and the local length
is exactly one, not merely at most one.

Summation and audited L109 give w=|Z| and 0<=q<=|Z|. Substitution into
corrected L108 retains both negative defect signs in c_2(N); the bounds
in (2) have the stated directions. For Z empty all defects vanish.
The audit does not determine q_z, bound |Z|, extend a metric or prove
semistability, F existence, transport or a new algebraic Hodge class.
It verifies this known-tool specialization without claiming a new
mathematical advance beyond the already recorded result.
