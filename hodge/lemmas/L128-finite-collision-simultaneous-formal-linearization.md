## Hypotheses

Suppose F is holomorphic locally free of rank two and
Omega-slope-polystable, with integral c_1(F)=0 and

\[
             \operatorname{ch}_2(F)=-14e_1-21e_2-\tau_D.
\]

Use the actual opposite algebraic classifying map of L118,
f:B -> M=M_H^s(2,0,-19), and its actual common smooth harmonic

\[
 \nabla^{f^*TM}_u(df(v))=df(\nabla^g_u v).                 \tag{1}

## Conclusion

There are one formal holomorphic chart k_M from the completed tangent
space V at zero to the completed smooth ambient germ (M,z), and
independent formal holomorphic source charts k_i from (V_i,0) to
(B,b_i), all with identity differential under these tangent
identifications, such that every actual completed branch map satisfies

\[
                 k_M^{-1}\circ\widehat f_i\circ k_i=L_i.
                                                               \tag{2}
\]

Put S=widehat(Sym_C V^*) and S_i=widehat(Sym_C V_i^*), with
completion at their positive-degree ideals, and let
I(P_i) in S be the ideal generated by the linear forms vanishing on P_i.
In the coordinates (2), the actual completed restriction map is

\[
 \rho:S\longrightarrow\prod_{i=1}^r S_i,
              H\longmapsto(H\circ L_i)_i,                    \tag{3}
\]

and the actual image completion is

\[
      \widehat{\mathcal O}_{Z,z}
          \simeq S\big/\bigcap_{i=1}^r I(P_i),               \tag{4}
\]

compatibly with its injection into the completed finite normalization.
This is an equality up to the specified formal coordinate isomorphisms,
not a claim about convergent or algebraic coordinate changes.

The result is a conditional ADVANCE / REPRODUCTION, specializing the
inspected compatible-coordinate theorem. The separate critical audit
below preserves it. It computes no arrangement defect or global bound, establishes
neither F nor a collision, and supplies no transport, cycle or resolution

The inverse of the actual local biholomorphism f_i:B_i -> N_i

\[
