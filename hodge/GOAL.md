# Hodge conjecture

Apply ../GOAL.md as the shared research policy.

## Exact target and traps

For every smooth projective complex variety, prove or disprove that every rational Hodge class of type (p,p) is a rational linear combination of classes of algebraic cycles of codimension p. The integral Hodge conjecture is not this target.
