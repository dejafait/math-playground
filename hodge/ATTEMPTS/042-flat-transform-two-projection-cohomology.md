# 042 — Flat-image two-projection test is compatible

2026-10-05. The approved bounded attempt is recorded in
[L058](../lemmas/L058-flat-transform-opposite-projection-compatibility.md).
It establishes an actual rank-nine opposite transform conditional on
F, its complete character and cohomology, and the forced acyclic
rank-seven residual summand. This is a reproduction of known tools.

WHY IT FAILS: The flat image gives cohomology compatible with
O_S^2 plus a polystable acyclic rank-seven bundle of c_1=0,c_2=14.
Its stable-summand ranks and RR characters produce no contradiction;
in particular the formal single rank-seven stable branch requires
only zero, never negative, cohomology dimensions. No residual or
kernel existence has been proved. Repeating these same two
characters, indices or cohomology tests cannot force tau_D=0.
Stop this mechanism while retaining the exact rigidity question.
The separate determinant-pairing/orthogonal-bundle possibility needs
source comparison before any derivation; no such structure is assumed
or proved in this attempt. All earlier recipe stops remain preserved.
