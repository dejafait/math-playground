# 044 — Nonflat two-projection test forces stability but remains compatible

2026-10-06. The approved bounded attempt is recorded in
[L060](../lemmas/L060-nonflat-opposite-projection-compatibility.md).
It proves actual opposite all-fibre IT and preservation, evaluates
the full signed character and both Leray sequences, and excludes
every reducible rank-three polystable alternative at (-10,-11).

WHY IT FAILS: The required stable rank-three image has c_1=0,c_2=8
and cohomology (0,2,0), consistent with the tested surface RR.
Forcing stability does not prove existence or contradict this
character. Repeating the same indices and line-summand test cannot
exclude the branch or supply a global kernel. Stop this bounded
mechanism for this pair, preserving ATTEMPTS/041--043 and the
separate flat-source blocker. A low-rank projective-lift test could
use additional structure, but neither a symmetric form on this image
nor a spin/projective lift or its obstruction is proved here. Those
changed hypotheses require a separate source review before research.
