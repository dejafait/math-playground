# Attempt 020 — Doubled source with divisible presentation terms

Date: 2026-09-27. Outcome: the tested generic construction is excluded;
the unrestricted doubled-source target remains unresolved and its
three-turn construction window is closed.

The test sought actual mixed presentation maps for I_C direct sum I_C
in L025's final W, with an integral terminal twist and invariant first
two Chern classes. Unlike full evaluation, proper generating sections
allow mixed maps. [L030](../lemmas/L030-rank-divisible-doubled-presentations-fail.md)
constructs actual presentations of ranks (8,10,8;4) by fourth powers
of product line bundles and an actual twist making c_1 zero.

**WHY IT FAILS.** Making every factor class divisible by the terminal
rank r guarantees a convenient integral twist, but also makes the
normalized mixed correction divided by r an integral lattice operator.
Invariance would give it the cubic eigenvalue 2(lambda-2)/r, of norm
-56/r^3. This is not an integer for r>=3. The rank-four construction
therefore fails the Chern test for every final chamber, before any
stability assertion. This is a new scoped application of known tools,
not a general nonexistence theorem for doubled-source bundles.

Arbitrary factor residues and possible rank-two presentations are
outside this exclusion. The earlier pre-chamber virtual class remains
valid within its limited scope. No complete compatible construction
has emerged during the bounded window, so continuing with further
residue or multiplicity searches is not justified by this result.
The main gap and the three-versus-four deformation threshold remain.
