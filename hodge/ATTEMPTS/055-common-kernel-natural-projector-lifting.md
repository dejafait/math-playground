# 055 — Natural kernel-projector lifting stops

2026-10-11. The complete conditional test is in
[L086](../lemmas/L086-common-kernel-natural-projector-nonlifting.md).

WHY IT FAILS: Stability of the prescribed common kernel makes all its
parallel endomorphisms scalar. Both actual higher-direct-image maps
send these to the same scalars; neither can produce the nontrivial
trivial-isotypic projector, for either allowed multiplicity. No general
inversion or fullness theorem remedies this mismatch under the retained
hypotheses. This is nonlifting, not nonexistence of the kernel. Stop this
mechanism and preserve the independent block and transport gaps.
