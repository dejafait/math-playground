# Product-line indices for the corrected rank-two data

Date: 2026-10-04. Outcome: NEGATIVE for using this test family to
narrow L048's surviving arithmetic region. The necessary indices
are evaluated for every integral product-line twist, with the full
rank-two character and fixed mixed action retained.

WHY IT FAILS: Surface-line HRR and the integral full mixed tensor
make every twisted index differ from the untwisted one by an integer,
as proved in
[L049](../lemmas/L049-corrected-rank-two-product-line-indices.md).
Thus the six-divisibility condition already imposed by L048 is the
entire product-line condition. Further sampling of twists cannot
exclude another surviving pair. This closes only that arithmetic
filter; it neither proves nor rules out an actual rank-two bundle.
Finite-rank topological conditions and holomorphic construction,
stability and transport remain separate mechanisms to assess.
