2026-10-11, step 224. The exact (-10,-17) exclusion is recorded in
[L091](../lemmas/L091-flat-exception-determinant-cup-square-exclusion.md),

the audit itself is EXPLORATION / REPRODUCTION.

WHY IT FAILS: The forward actual image is O_S^2. Charge-thirteen
stability on every opposite fibre gives a rank-nine actual preserved
polystable image, whose sections form exactly its O_S^2 trivial part.
Both relative determinant/Serre forms restrict perfectly to these
two-dimensional section spaces. Through actual Leray, restriction and
trace maps they measure the two scalar components of the determinant
self-cup of the same degree-one class. One vector makes both components
nonzero, contradicting the rank-two square-zero identity. This retires
only this conditional recipe, preserving older topology and factor
results; it supplies neither cycles, transport nor Hodge nonalgebraicity.
