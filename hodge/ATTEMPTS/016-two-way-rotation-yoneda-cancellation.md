# Two-way mixed motion of the first two rotation sheaves

The tested object is O_C direct sum O_(C^(2)). Its first-order
motion has arbitrary mixed Ext^1 classes in both directions and
zero self components. The ambient product is constant modulo
tau^2, with the same later coefficient in both factors. No
filtration or splitting is imposed on the sought sheaf lift.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L024](../lemmas/L024-two-way-rotation-products-cannot-cancel.md)
excludes simultaneous cancellation by the two global Yoneda
products for every coefficient outside V_D. A local flat matrix
reference turns the two curve coordinates into an order-two
normal residue. Complete fibres at the critical pairs where only
one summand occurs force all those residues to vanish. The
resulting embedded lift of C extends across all three infinity
points by Hartogs, regardless of the punctual mixed classes,
and retains C's transverse obstruction. The admissible coefficient
set is exactly the three-dimensional V_D against four RM
directions required. This differs from the fixed-extension test
and stops this mixed-only order-two mechanism; it is not a claim
about self motions, other bundles or all algebraic representatives.
