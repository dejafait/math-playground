# 039 — Minimum correction pair: product realization fails

2026-10-05. The exact (-9,-10) product-bundle target is excluded by
[L054](../lemmas/L054-minimum-pair-relative-direct-image-obstruction.md).
The ready SPECIALIZE assessment was reused; this was one mathematical
application, not another source search.

WHY IT FAILS: Every first-factor restriction would be stable with
second Chern number five. All-fibre cohomology and perfect base change
make the actual first higher direct image a line bundle, but relative
GRR forces a character incompatible with a line's finite-rank identity.
The canonical proof retains the alternating sign and full mixed square.
Individual factor existence and topological realization do not provide
the missing global compatibility. This stop applies to the fixed
polystable locally free product bundle; other corrections, other
representatives and the rational Hodge conjecture remain open.
