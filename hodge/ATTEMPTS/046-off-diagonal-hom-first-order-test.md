# 046 — Relative-Hom first-order test leaves separation open

2026-10-07. The approved attempt and full calculations are in
[L066](../lemmas/L066-charge-seven-relative-hom-first-order-test.md).
Zero degree-zero derived-fibre cohomology at every distinct parameter
pair was the required threshold; it was not reached.

WHY IT FAILS: The Euler value -20 permits both isomorphic and
nonisomorphic fibres. The degree-zero cohomology sheaf is zero even
where derived Hom is nonzero. The actual linearized Hom equation tests
extension of a central isomorphism, not its existence. Its rank can be
four, and L066's local perfect complex retains an isolated central jump
with that rank and the tested symplectic constraints. The model is not
a counterexample satisfying the full F hypotheses: the actual higher
differential and global constraints remain unknown. Repeating these
indices or first-order constraints cannot settle separation. Preserve
ATTEMPTS/041--045. Connection/metric rigidity is a different mechanism
requiring source review; no total geodesy or metric identity is assumed.
