# Extensions between all three reduced rotation supports

The tested objects are every middle sheaf in an extension of O_C direct sum O_(C^(3)) by two copies of O_(C^(2)). The filtration, support and presentations may be forgotten in a lift. This is a module problem, separate from the earlier ordinary-square union.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L017](../lemmas/L017-rotation-sheaf-extensions-retain-obstruction.md) computes the full 28-dimensional extension space, including the punctual contributions at infinity. At a nonsplit finite crossing a rank-one union summand meets one extra rank-one copy of the second component. The actual unfiltered lift has residue mu on the first branch and mu/2 on the trace centre of the second. Their necessary fibre j-invariant equations force mu to vanish. Split self-extension blocks and the normal-sheaf Hartogs property then recover a lift of C for every extension class. Conversely every class lifts along V_D. The exact kernel is therefore three-dimensional against four required, although the action U^2-3 id is non-scalar. This stops these sheaves at first order and leaves other representatives and the universal rational Hodge target unresolved.
