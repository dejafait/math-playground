# Three-line first-curvature-derivative restriction test

2026-10-18. The approved finite order-one test is computed in
[L124](../lemmas/L124-three-line-first-curvature-derivative-full-range.md).

WHY IT FAILS: For every positive angle configuration of the three
quaternionic lines, differential Bianchi and the complete differentiated
normal equations allow all 36 real components of the admissible source
derivative triple. Quintic interpolation supplies a real extension for
every prescribed triple, with a 16-dimensional fibre on their two-plane.
Thus this first-order restriction test adds no collision condition after
the independent order-zero data have passed L123. It supplies no metric
germ, actual branch, collision, F or Hodge class. L124's separate step-371
critical audit preserves this stop without finding a substantive error
or stronger conclusion; it is not a second mathematical advance.
Higher-order or global tests are outside this stop and need their own
discriminating target and coverage; simply choosing other first-derivative
values or angles does not reopen this mechanism.
