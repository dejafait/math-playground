# Attempt 037 — Symmetric pure corrections at rank two

The corrected rank-two character with a=b=-10 passes the earlier
Bogomolov and all product-line index tests. Apply the finite-rank
quaternionic-line criterion on the spin eight-manifold S x S,
using the full mixed tensor and the tangent Pontryagin class.
The resulting residue is 6 modulo 12 against zero required.
See [L050's canonical proof](../lemmas/L050-corrected-rank-two-su2-realization-region.md).

WHY IT FAILS: The characteristic number vanishes exactly when
(a+4)(b+4) is 6 modulo 12. For symmetric corrections this product
is a square, which never has that residue. Thus larger symmetric
corrections cannot repair the obstruction. This stops the entire
fixed-tensor symmetric SU(2) recipe, including its earlier index
survivors, before any holomorphic or stability test. It is not a
Hodge counterexample and does not stop nonsymmetric corrections:
(-7,-6) has an actual topological SU(2) realization by the cited
existence theorem. Its holomorphic compatibility remains open.
