# Attempt 001 — Universal generation by divisor products

Date: 2026-09-24. Outcome: rejected as a universal spanning mechanism.

The proposed intermediate assertion was that every primitive rational (2,2)-class on a smooth projective fourfold is a rational sum of products of divisor classes. Together with the Lefschetz (1,1) theorem, it would supply algebraic cycles for the primitive gap isolated by L001. The exact discriminating example and full proof are in [L002](../lemmas/L002-primitive-diagonal-outside-divisor-products.md).

**WHY IT FAILS.** A primitive correction of the diagonal of a smooth quartic surface is algebraic but is not a divisor-product class on its self-product. Its action on a holomorphic two-form separates it from every divisor product, including products involving arbitrary divisors. Repeated intersections with ample divisors stay within the same divisor algebra and cannot remove this obstruction. The failed assertion is stronger than the Hodge conjecture; the result does not obstruct mechanisms using more general algebraic correspondences. Such mechanisms require their own tests.
