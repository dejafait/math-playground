# 040 — The entire five-charge product-realization slice fails

2026-10-05. The approved a=-9, variable-integer-b target is excluded in
[L055](../lemmas/L055-five-charge-relative-direct-image-slice-obstruction.md).
This widens the fixed-pair stop while preserving its evidence.

WHY IT FAILS: Every first-factor fibre must be stable with second Chern
number five, independently of the other factor's behaviour. All-fibre
cohomology and perfect base change force an actual line bundle as the
first higher direct image. Full relative GRR forces a zero first
character and a second character that vanishes only at the nonintegral
coefficient b=-106/7. Every allowed integer coefficient therefore fails
the line identity. This stops the slice under the exact fixed metric,
mixed tensor, rank-two local freeness and product-polystability
hypotheses; it does not contradict topological or individual factor
existence. Larger charges, other representatives, transport and the
rational Hodge conjecture remain open. The proposed six-charge
direct-image connection test requires separate source coverage.
