# 043 — Flat residual orthogonal parity/index test is compatible

2026-10-06. The approved determinant-duality, orthogonal topology and
adjoint-index attempt is recorded in
[L059](../lemmas/L059-flat-transform-orthogonal-residual-constraints.md).
It proves a parallel symmetric residual pairing, narrows the stable
summand cases, and evaluates the tested necessary constraints.

WHY IT FAILS: The single stable orthogonal rank-seven branch remains
compatible with the total characteristic relation and adjoint RR.
The inspected rank-five real-bundle theorem supplies matching rank-seven
topology after stabilization, and the required adjoint cohomology has
nonnegative dimensions. Neither fact supplies a holomorphic acyclic
residual or a compatible global kernel. Eliminating a rank partition
does not eliminate this surviving branch. Repeating the same parity
and index identities cannot force the mixed tensor to vanish. Stop
this bounded mechanism, preserving ATTEMPTS/042 and all earlier stops;
a projected-connection curvature test would be a different analytic
mechanism and needs its own source comparison.
