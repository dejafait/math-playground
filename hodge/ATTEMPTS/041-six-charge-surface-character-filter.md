# 041 — Six-charge surface filter leaves four numerical cases

2026-10-05. The approved complete a=-10 slice filter is recorded in
[L057](../lemmas/L057-six-charge-direct-image-character-filter.md).
It excludes every input integer except -9,-11,-13,-17 using the
specified directional stable/split tests. Earlier stops are preserved.

WHY IT FAILS TO CLOSE THE SLICE: Relative GRR gives an actual
rank-two polystable transform of charge b+17. Charges eight, six
and four pass the stable Euler bound; charge zero has the genuine
surface alternative O_S^2. These numerical survivors supply no
global kernel, and their existence is not assumed. Repeating this
character or these inequalities cannot decide them. A proposed
flat-transform rigidity test for b=-17 is materially different:
it would need a theorem relating a trivial actual transform to the
kernel's nonzero mixed action, without assuming an equivalence.
That implication lacks essential source coverage and remains
unproved; it requires separate review before mathematical work.
