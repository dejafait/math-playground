# Attempt 005 — Deforming the reduced cubic support alone

Date: 2026-09-25. Outcome: stopped in the transverse NS-fixed RM direction.

The proposed route was to extend the reduced support C of the cubic dihedral correspondence from its three-dimensional Dickson family to the missing fourth RM direction by a flat embedded deformation. The complete first-order test is [L008](../lemmas/L008-cubic-support-global-obstruction.md).

**WHY IT FAILS.** All three singular points are transverse two-branch surface crossings, whose first-order embedded deformations are locally trivial. A lift therefore gives a simultaneous normalization with two finite flat double-cover maps. Their deck involutions retain their dihedral relations because the normalization has no infinitesimal automorphisms. The resulting symmetry restricts all Weierstrass coefficients to the original three-dimensional family; NS-fixedness retains the exceptional curve and excludes the hidden A1 resolution tangent. Thus the obstruction has kernel dimension three, whereas extending this support in every RM direction would require dimension four. This does not obstruct a different cycle representative, a union with additional algebraic components, or the rational Hodge class itself.
