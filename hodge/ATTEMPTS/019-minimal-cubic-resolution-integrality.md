# Attempt 019 — Mixed resolution in the minimal cubic divisor subspace

Date: 2026-09-27. Outcome: stopped at integral Chern-class compatibility.

The tested sequence has three product-line-bundle presentation terms
for I_C, original factor classes in L025's W, and an arbitrary
integral terminal twist. The full proof is
[L028](../lemmas/L028-cubic-subspace-resolution-parity-obstruction.md).

**WHY IT FAILS.** Invariant first two Chern classes would force the
mixed ch_2 operator on NS(S) to be integral and self-adjoint, with
characteristic polynomial f(z)(z-gamma) for an integer gamma.
Modulo 2 the divisor pairing is nondegenerate alternating, while
the irreducible cubic and linear factors force orthogonal primary
spaces of dimensions three and one. The one-dimensional space
contradicts nondegeneracy. This excludes the exact W recipe before
any map or stability test, for every rational chamber conjugation.
It does not exclude rational algebraicity or all possible
transcendental coefficients of a bundle representative.
