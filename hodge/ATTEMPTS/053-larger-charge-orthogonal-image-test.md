attempt is recorded in

then tests every allowed stable block at the unchanged real metric.

WHY IT FAILS: The formal combination of a trivial line and two copies
of a stable determinant-trivial rank-two charge-four block in the p_2
image, opposite a trivial line and a stable symmetric rank-five
charge-ten block, satisfies the prescribed characters, both Leray
cohomologies, pairing signs, simple/adjoint dimensions and necessary
orthogonal characteristic relations. These checks do not construct
either block or a common kernel. Repeating the same indices cannot
exclude this remaining branch. Stop this bounded necessary-constraint
mechanism, preserving the older stops and flat source blocker; the
conditional argument receives a separate critical audit before any
different construction or representation test is selected.
