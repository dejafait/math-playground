# 049 — Pure point corrections cannot rescue the fixed mixed tensor

2026-10-08. The direct actual-curvature attempt is recorded in
[L070](../lemmas/L070-all-survivor-mixed-curvature-obstruction.md),
whose separate critical audit on 2026-10-08 preserves the exclusion.

WHY IT FAILS: Under L052's retained product-bundle and compatible
connection hypotheses, invariant fibre curvature kills the pure
block contribution to the contraction with the hyperkahler triple.
The remaining mixed contribution has the negative of a nonnegative
raw unitary pairing, including its central part. The fixed mixed
tensor instead forces the scale -2lambda<0, independently of a,b.
Thus changing only the pure point corrections cannot rescue this
recipe, even with reducible restrictions. The audited proof supplies
an all-survivor obstruction; it neither reverses actual
topological realization nor proves a Hodge class nonalgebraic.
Other representatives, changed data and transverse transport remain
outside the conclusion. The narrower charge-seven stop is preserved.
