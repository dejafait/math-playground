# Attempt 038 — Polystability of the boundary topological survivor

The pair (-7,-6) passes L050's finite-rank topological SU(2)
criterion. Test its holomorphic locally free polystability at the
fixed common metric before attempting transverse transport.
The canonical proof is [L051](../lemmas/L051-boundary-survivor-factor-polystability-obstruction.md).

WHY IT FAILS: A polystable product splitting has divisor-only mixed
character and cannot realize the prescribed cubic action. In the
stable branch full-class rotation invariance supplies a hyperholomorphic
connection. Its restriction to either quaternionic K3 factor is
Hermite--Einstein, hence polystable. The restricted second Chern
numbers 3 and 2 are incompatible with either stable locally free
bundles (Riemann--Roch and dual stability) or slope-zero line sums
(parity and effectivity). This excludes the fixed survivor at this
metric, preserving its topological existence. It does not exclude
other corrections, unstable holomorphic bundles or arbitrary cycle
representatives, and it is not a Hodge counterexample.
