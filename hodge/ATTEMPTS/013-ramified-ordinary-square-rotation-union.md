# Ramified order-two lift of the ordinary-square rotation union

The tested scheme is I_C intersect I_(C^(2))^2 intersect I_(C^(3)), with its entire ordinary square at infinity. The ambient product is constant modulo tau^2 and has a transverse RM coefficient at order tau^2. Every first-order motion of the subscheme is allowed.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L016](../lemmas/L016-ramified-rotation-union-retains-obstruction.md) proves that the reduced supports and the first-order trace centre of the squared support stay fixed. Complete non-isotrivial elliptic fibres force the remaining first-order algebra multiplication into a single vertical cubic direction. Its quadratic trace defect vanishes, so actual graph sections exist through order two. A flat mixed-crossing reference then gives the same unequal residues on the reduced and thickened sheets, forcing them to vanish. Recovering C and extending across infinity puts the ambient coefficient back in V_D, of dimension three against four required. This stops the prescribed ramified test; it does not rule out other sheaves, other representatives or higher ramification orders.
