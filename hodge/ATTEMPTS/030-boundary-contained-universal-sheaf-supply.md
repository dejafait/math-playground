# Boundary-contained universal-sheaf supply

The proposed supply is a regular map S -> M_H(2,0,-1) whose
entire actual degree-three weighted-support image lies in
the collision locus. L039 and L040 reduce its normalized
universal action to that support cycle, including exceptional
Hilbert-cube images. This was a different test from the
fixed one-moving-point recipe; no stable lift was assumed
for an arbitrary prescribed support map.

## WHY IT FAILS

The full bound is [L041](../lemmas/L041-boundary-contained-sheaf-maps-are-scalar.md).
The collision normalization has a unique geometric preimage,
so its finite pullback descends to two regular maps a,b on
the normal parameter K3 even for nondominant or entirely
triple images. The actual weighted action is 2a^*+b^*.
Each regular component acts by zero or a scalar sign on T(S),
under the full totally real cubic-field hypothesis. Thus the
entire boundary-contained supply spans at most one direction
against three required by E. Punctual lift data cannot change
that leading-cycle action. Stop this supply; generically
distinct support maps and arbitrary correspondences remain
outside the exclusion. This is a reproduction of known tools,
not a nonalgebraicity result or a new general Hodge theorem.
