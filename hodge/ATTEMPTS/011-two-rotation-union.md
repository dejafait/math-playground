# Reduced union of two rotation images

The proposed representative was Y=C union C^(2), with the second support the reduced image of (g_0,g_0 sigma^2). Its non-scalar action is U^2+U-2 id, and its four finite elliptic double curves provide genuine local smoothing parameters.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L014](../lemmas/L014-two-rotation-union-retains-cubic-obstruction.md) computes the global compatibility of those parameters. An embedded lift preserves the fibre j-map relation. At the two other critical-point pairs the union is smooth, forcing equality of the critical j-variations; their differences are the four smoothing residues. All residues vanish, so component recovery and Hartogs extension yield a lift of C even across infinity. The kernel remains the three-dimensional V_D, against four required. This stops this first-order representative and leaves other cycles and possible ramified higher-order families unresolved.

Adding all three reduced supports would lose the non-scalar action, since their three eigenvalues sum to -1. A nonuniform thickening on those supports is therefore a distinct proposal requiring its own source review and cycle check; no calculation for it is supplied here.
