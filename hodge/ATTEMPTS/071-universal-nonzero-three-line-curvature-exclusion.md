# Universal nonzero-curvature exclusion at a triple collision

2026-10-18. The approved pointwise test, including real structure,
all normal components, arbitrary angles and the common source scale,
is computed in [L123](../lemmas/L123-three-line-real-gauss-curvature-compatibility.md).

WHY IT FAILS: A real hyperkähler algebraic tensor on H^2 has the two
orthogonal coordinate lines and their diagonal curvature-invariant,
with nonzero unit-frame restrictions (A,A,A/2). Thus nonzero curvature
on all three branches does not contradict pointwise Gauss-Codazzi.
The exact compatibility space has a 10-dimensional restriction range
in the general case and dimensions 11 or 15 in the exceptional cases.
This stops the universal exclusion based only on nonvanishing. It does
not settle the actual unknown K3 curvature values or angles, realize a
metric germ or collision, or supply F. A different test using actual
geometric data would require those inputs and separate coverage. The
separate critical audit in L123 (2026-10-18, step 367) preserves the
formula, real witness and this bounded stop; it supplies no stronger
geometric exclusion.
