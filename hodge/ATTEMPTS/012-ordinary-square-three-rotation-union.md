# Ordinary-square union of all three rotation images

The tested scheme has ideal I_C intersect I_(C^(2))^2 intersect I_(C^(3)). The middle square is ordinary, including at infinity. Its fundamental cycle has multiplicities 1,3,1 and acts as 2U^2-5 id, so it is a relevant non-scalar representative.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L015](../lemmas/L015-ordinary-square-rotation-union-retains-obstruction.md) shows that each reduced--thickened crossing has the same potential displacement residue on its reduced sheet and one third of that residue on the thickened sheet's normalized-trace section. Both sections must preserve the fibre j-invariant, forcing the residue to vanish. These equalities also kill the reduced--reduced smoothing parameters. A lift of C is recovered and extended across infinity, so the lifting kernel is again the three-dimensional V_D, short of four required. This stops the specified first-order representative; it does not exclude ramified higher-order lifts, where the linear trace argument has not been established.
