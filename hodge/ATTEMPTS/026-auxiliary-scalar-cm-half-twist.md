# Auxiliary scalar CM extension and half twist

Tested 2026-09-27. Outcome: NEGATIVE. Classification: REPRODUCTION.
Stop the recipe with V=T(S) tensor_E E(i) and scalars in type (0,0).

## Proposed use

Produce an effective polarized weight-one half twist of V,
then investigate an abelian realization and algebraic comparison
cycles carrying the cubic RM action. Algebraic inclusion and
retraction correspondences would still be required after a
successful Hodge-theoretic test.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L035](../lemmas/L035-scalar-cm-extension-has-no-effective-half-twist.md)
shows that V^{2,0} occupies both conjugate K-embeddings above
the real embedding acting on T^{2,0}. Every CM type leaves a
one-dimensional forbidden top piece; the known criterion
requires dimension zero. The formal shift has types (2,-1)
and (-1,2), so it fails the effective weight-one prerequisite
before polarization or comparison cycles are at issue. This
does not exclude other CM actions or auxiliary representations
and does not show nonalgebraicity of any rational Hodge class.
