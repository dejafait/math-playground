# 045 — Spin lift excluded; nonspin lifting/index alternatives survive

2026-10-06. The approved necessary-constraint attempt is recorded in
[L061](../lemmas/L061-nonflat-rank-three-projective-lift-constraints.md).
It establishes the actual parallel symmetric image pairing, identifies
the projective torsor, and excludes w=0 by stable rank-two RR.

WHY IT FAILS: A nonzero SL2 obstruction need not have nonzero Brauer
image. Ordinary lifts with unrestricted non-even determinant pass the
necessary isotropic index; the fibre determinant gives a formal witness
to these equations. Brauer-twisted lifts have a compatible simple
self-Ext index, and the actual obstruction classes are not determined.
Neither passing case supplies the exact-h image or a global kernel.
Repeating the same charge, parity and index equations cannot exclude
them. Stop this bounded mechanism; preserve ATTEMPTS/041--044 and
the separate flat-source blocker. A charge-seven classifying-map
symplectic pullback test is a different geometric target and requires
its own source approval before mathematical work.
