# Dihedral-plus-diagonal stable lift on the original K3

Date: 2026-10-03. Outcome: stopped at the stable-lift test.

The proposal was to use the degree-two fibre cycle of C
plus the diagonal point as the actual length-three support
of a map S -> M_H(2,0,-1). A stable universal-sheaf family
would provide another representative for a later transverse
deformation test. This did not assume that L009's embedded
union was flat over the first projection or that its
deformation obstruction classified all sheaf families.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L042](../lemmas/L042-dihedral-plus-diagonal-has-no-stable-lift.md)
constructs the regular norm map on every resolved fibre,
but computes the full image ideal of norms at each of
the three points over infinity as (uv,u^3,v^3)^2. This
nonzero ideal is primary for the height-two maximal ideal
and cannot be invertible. L040's necessary criterion,
valid because the generic support is distinct, rules out
the stable lift on S. This failure precedes any transverse
deformation question. Stop the unchanged recipe; a modified
parameter surface is a distinct target requiring a prior
literature review, and no such family is constructed here.
The result is a scoped reproduction of known tools, not
nonalgebraicity or a resolution of the Hodge conjecture.
