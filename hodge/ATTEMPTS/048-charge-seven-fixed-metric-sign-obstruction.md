# 048 — The exact charge-seven fixed-metric family is excluded

2026-10-08. The separate critical audit preserves the scoped sign
obstruction in [L069](../lemmas/L069-charge-seven-metric-sign-obstruction.md).

WHY IT FAILS: For the hypothetical locally free rank-two F with the
retained L052/L060 data, stable charge-seven fibres, identical marked
factor metrics and fixed mixed operator, the metric-normalized family
form forces L067's positive pullback scale to be -2lambda in (-3,-2).
The audited frame, natural KS projection and Chern–Weil conventions
retain this contradiction. The exact representative recipe is therefore
excluded under those inputs. Earlier compatible index tests remain
valid necessary conditions; they do not supply existence. This is
neither nonalgebraicity of the Hodge class nor a Hodge-conjecture
resolution. Other correction pairs with potentially reducible fibres
require separate coverage and have not been excluded by this audit.
