# One moving point in the universal-sheaf kernel model

The tested recipe fixes p,q in S and three distinct quotient
lines, then takes the kernel of O_(S x S)^2 onto the sum of the
diagonal and the two constant point sections. The audited generic
moduli model justifies its stable locus, without supplying a
global stable extension or a non-scalar action.

## WHY IT FAILS

The full calculation is [L038](../lemmas/L038-one-moving-point-sheaf-map-is-scalar.md).
The actual family is flat but has unstable fibres when the moving
point collides with either fixed point. More decisively, its
normalized universal action is id on T(S); even a hypothetical
stable replacement only at those two parameter points has the
same action. It therefore remains in the scalar diagonal span,
one transcendental dimension against three required in the cubic
field. Stop this specific supply recipe. No exclusion of other
three-point families, boundary maps or non-scalar correspondences
is asserted.
