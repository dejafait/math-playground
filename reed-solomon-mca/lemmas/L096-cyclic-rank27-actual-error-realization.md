# L096 — Conditional cyclic rank-27 realization by actual errors

## Hypotheses

Put k=F_97 and F=F_(97^20), and let H be the order-sixteen subgroup
of k^*. Fix any bijection h:Z/16Z -> H. All indices are modulo sixteen.
Use exactly the cyclic blocks and unshifted columns

    B_s={s,s+1,s+4,s+6},
    v_i=(1,h(i),...,h(i)^7)^transpose,
    U_s=span_F{v_i : i in B_s}.

Let M be L088's full 96-by-28 alternating matrix for these spaces,
and assume rank_F M=27. Use L088 and L089 with their completed
separate audits. Let C be the length-sixteen, dimension-eight RS code
on H over F, and use precisely the event in
[the frozen affine-line model](../foundations/02-pinned-affine-line-model.md),
at any radius 1/4<=delta<5/16. No preassigned input words or chart
are part of the hypotheses. No labeling of rank 27 is known.

## Conclusion

There exist words a,b in k^H, sixteen distinct parameters gamma_s in k,
and words e_s in k^H such that

    supp(e_s)=h(B_s),
    a+gamma_s b-e_s belongs to C,
    b restricted to H minus h(B_s) does not belong to
        C restricted to H minus h(B_s).

In particular every gamma_s is bad on that same twelve-coordinate
agreement set. For this newly constructed pair, L010's D and all
sixteen coordinate locators are nonzero polynomials. Its entire bad
set over F is exactly {gamma_s : s in Z/16Z}, so its error contribution
is exactly 16/97^20. There are no further bad extension-field parameters,
including singular or lower-weight events. The locator equality
conditions in L010 hold.

This is a conditional realization, not an example of rank 27 or an
unconditional attainment statement. It decides no other family, uniform
rank bound, triangle bound or challenge-definition qualification. This
application passed the separate step-238 critical audit below.

### A rational chart containing all sixteen directions

By L088 the nonzero kernel is generated by a decomposable tensor
over k. Its image gives a two-dimensional k-plane S_k whose scalar
extension is the residual plane S in F^8. By L089, for each s the
intersection S intersect U_s is the line F r_s, where

    r_s=sum_(i in B_s) w_(s,i) v_i

has all four w_(s,i) nonzero in k. These sixteen lines are distinct.
Each r_s is rational by L089's explicit formula; it belongs to S_k,
since a rational vector in S_k tensor_k F lies in S_k (extend a
k-basis of S_k to one of k^8 and inspect its other coordinates).
Thus the sixteen rational lines k r_s in S_k are distinct as well.

The two-dimensional space S_k has 98 one-dimensional subspaces:
in any basis they are k(1,t), t in k, and k(0,1). Choose a line
l_infinity different from these sixteen lines, and a nonzero linear
functional lambda:S_k -> k with kernel l_infinity. Then
lambda(r_s)!=0 for every s. Choose u in S_k with lambda(u)=1 and
choose a nonzero v in ker lambda. The vectors u,v form a basis.
There is a unique gamma_s in k with

    z_s=r_s/lambda(r_s)=u+gamma_s v.                    (1)

The gamma_s are pairwise distinct: equality would make the two
normalized r_s equal and hence their lines coincide. This chooses a
new chart for new inputs; it makes no assertion about any prescribed
original chart or the parameters of another pencil.

### Shifted moments and full actual errors

For i in B_s define

    e_s(h(i))=w_(s,i)/(h(i) lambda(r_s)),               (2)

and set every other coordinate to zero. Every denominator is nonzero:
h(i) belongs to H subset k^*, and lambda was chosen nonzero on r_s.
Every numerator is nonzero by L089. Hence e_s has exactly the four
claimed nonzero coordinates.

The shifted column of sigma at h(i) is h(i)v_i, not v_i. Consequently
(2) gives, as an equality of all eight moment coordinates,

    sigma(e_s)=sum_(i in B_s) e_s(h(i)) h(i)v_i
              =r_s/lambda(r_s)=u+gamma_s v.           (3)

This division by h(i) is essential; no change to the syndrome-space
chart or permutation of its moment rows is being made. Surjectivity
of sigma over k gives a,b in k^H with sigma(a)=u, sigma(b)=v.
Equations (1)–(3) imply sigma(a+gamma_s b-e_s)=0 over F, so
a+gamma_s b-e_s belongs to C. All eight moments and the full errors
are now established before invoking any locator/event bound.

### Nonzero determinant and every coordinate locator

For any fixed x=h(i), L087 gives exactly four containing blocks,

                    product_(y in h(B_s))(x-y) != 0.  (5)

Thus every ell_x is a nonzero polynomial. This argument proves the
polynomial hypotheses by an omitted-block evaluation; it does not
