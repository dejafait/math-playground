# Matching-two six-edge/eight-vertex criterion fails

Date: 2026-10-05.

WHY IT FAILS: [L025](../lemmas/L025-matching-two-small-union-counterexample.md)
gives a simple, degree-four, four-uniform sixteen-edge family on sixteen
vertices with matching number two and no six edges on at most eight
vertices. Its cyclic-gap proof is independent of the exact search; the
selected-family certificate additionally finds minimum union eleven.
Thus the universal incidence criterion cannot supply the proposed small
block for an affine-restriction argument. This stops that criterion,
not RS compatibility work: the example is not a moment-compatible pencil,
and no reduction of arbitrary equality supports to this family is claimed.
L024's exact doubled-family exclusion remains valid; the main error
interval remains 11/q–16/q.
