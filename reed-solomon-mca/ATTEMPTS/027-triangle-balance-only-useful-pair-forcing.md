# Triangle balance alone does not force a useful additional pair

2026-10-12. The approved finite incidence test succeeds as a witness:
[L072](../lemmas/L072-triangle-balanced-linear-support-witness.md)
gives sixteen degree-four blocks containing the prescribed triangle,
with pair intersections at most one among all blocks.

WHY IT FAILS: Coordinate balance and the fixed triangle cannot by
themselves force an additional pair with union five or six, because
every additional pair in the explicit witness has union seven or eight.
This stops that implication only. No actual equality pencil, field
labeling compatible with moments, nonzero weights or original finite
parameters has been constructed. Moment constraints are a materially
different possible test; uniform triangle twelve and useful-pair
extraction using stronger hypotheses remain unresolved.
