# Three disjointized four-row candidates are finite

2026-10-26. The three predeclared tests in
[L158](../lemmas/L158-three-eligible-four-row-loci.md) have exact
rank-one loci of cardinalities 2, 2 and 0 over every extension of F_97.
All obey the two-disjoint-pairs and pairwise-linear support conditions.
The complete original matrices, nonzero minors, kernels, homogeneous
equations and quotient restrictions remain in the canonical certificate.

WHY IT FAILS: Replacing L115's intersecting second pair by the tested
crossed-overlap pairs removes its free common-factor family. In the
two rectangle tests the complete projective kernel is a line, but
decomposability cuts it to two points; the broken rectangle has zero
kernel. Thus none supplies infinitely many exact-rank-one planes.
This stops these three explicit constructions, not all rectangle
patterns, universal finiteness, or the challenge. No fourth sample or
rank-only census is justified by this experiment. A structural proof
on arbitrary original quadruples remains an independently testable
mechanism within the unchanged approved target; its coincident and
distinct kernel-line cases are both unresolved.
