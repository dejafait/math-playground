# Varying the prescribed two-block construction to attain sixteen

Tested 2026-10-04 within the ready actual-moment resultant assessment.
The proposed candidate generator varied the two five-point blocks and
their six-point complement in L008's construction, seeking a resultant
c*P^4 satisfying every equality condition over F_(97^20).

WHY IT FAILS: [L013](../lemmas/L013-two-block-locator-and-fiber-obstruction.md)
identifies the actual determinant locator as a scalar divided difference
of the two block polynomials. Its possible additional bad parameters
come from a single fiber on the six-point complement; the total is ten,
eleven or fifteen, never sixteen. This is a uniform proof over extensions,
not an inference from 256 sampled partitions. It stops this prescribed
generator as an equality witness. Arbitrary weighted inputs, arbitrary
affine moment pencils, the full resultant feasibility problem and the
grand challenge remain open; the older orbit and source failures remain
independent evidence.
