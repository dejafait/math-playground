# Five-coordinate pencils as a sixteen-count generator

Tested 2026-10-04 within the ready actual-moment assessment. The
generator allowed arbitrary weights on a five-set, including pencils
through two four-error representatives with three shared coordinates.

WHY IT FAILS: [L015](../lemmas/L015-five-coordinate-projective-reduction.md)
reduces any such nonpersistent pencil to at most five coordinate
cancellations and a rational configuration of 330 external-support
points. Sixteen would require eleven of these points on one line.
The [complete finite test](../drafts/2026-10-04-five-coordinate-constrained-test.md)
enumerates every five-set up to its proved free multiplicative action
and reports a maximum six. This stop explicitly depends on that finite
enumeration; L015 proves the reduction and threshold. It covers
extension-field weights through the rational-line passage, rather than
through sampling those weights. Its best actual line is a specialization
of the already covered two-block family, and
[C013a](../lemmas/C013a-eleven-challenge-partition-specialization.md)
certifies eleven by the existing fiber theorem. The lower bound improves,
but this generator cannot supply the needed sixteen in the tested finite
configuration. Pencils requiring at least six coordinate columns and
the grand challenge remain open; no parked four-block calculation or
July retrieval was reopened.
