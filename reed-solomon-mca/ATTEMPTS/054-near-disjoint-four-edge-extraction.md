# Unconditional S-marked four-edge extraction fails

2026-10-26. The approved attempt sought four two-plus-two edges with
one singleton S intersection and five empty intersections in every
12–14-edge linear family of maximum coordinate degree four. The full
explicit twelve-edge counterexample and direct proof are canonical in
[L166](../lemmas/L166-near-disjoint-seed-extraction-counterexample.md).
All 66 pair and 495 four-subset records passed same-step independent
replay. A separate critical audit on 2026-10-26 also passes; its full
evidence and exact qualifications are recorded in canonical L166.

WHY IT FAILS: Isolated S labels are permitted. L166 uses only six S
labels, while the proposed four edges would use seven. Its J pairs
realize all the remaining incidence constraints, so this is an actual
abstract hypergraph counterexample, not merely a graph or degree
sequence. This stops the unconditional extraction implication only.
The witness is not asserted to be an actual coding equality family or
extend to sixteen supports. Stronger hypotheses require a new reviewed
target; removing the isolated label without a complete witness or proof
does not reopen this claim. L152/C152a and L165 remain unchanged.
