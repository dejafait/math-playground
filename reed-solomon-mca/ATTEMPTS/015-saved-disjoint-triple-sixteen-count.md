# Saved disjoint triple cannot attain sixteen

Date: 2026-10-04.

The complete restricted theorem is
[L022](../lemmas/L022-disjoint-triple-complete-exclusion.md), with its
[rational-branch certificate](../scripts/coefficient-feasibility/rational-branch-audit-result.json).
The separate nonprime and singular-family certificates remain preserved.

WHY IT FAILS: Every rational fourth parameter of a nonzero recurrence
determinant has a rank-three kernel. Extension-scalar descent and explicit
projective basis changes reduce every full-weight occurrence to a supplied
actual pencil whose safe-chart resultant fails the necessary fourth-power
identity. Nonprime parameters are excluded by L017 and the four zero-
determinant support families by L018–L021. Hence no input pair with the
prescribed supports at 0,1,2 has sixteen bad parameters. This stops witness
search for this fixed triple, not arbitrary disjoint triples, other triples,
or research on the challenge. Another fixed triple alone would not explain
whether the tested class is unavoidable; the next source-screened direction
is the balanced-support matching implication.
