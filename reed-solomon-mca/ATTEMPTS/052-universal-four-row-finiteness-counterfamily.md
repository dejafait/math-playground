# Universal four-row finiteness encounters an explicit infinite family

2026-10-26. The approved structural attempt sought finiteness for every
pairwise-linear original quadruple whose supports split into two
disjoint pairs. The residual singleton coefficient test checked 864
scalar configurations after L161's reduction. It retained 24 simultaneous
survivors (12 for h=8, 12 for h=12, zero for h=22), whereas exclusion
required zero. Full evidence is in
`scripts/seven-coordinate/residual-pencil-singletons.json`; these
necessary equations alone were not a realization or a finiteness test.

WHY IT FAILS: One structurally selected survivor gives the original
quadruple (35,522,477,566) and cross-ratio 26. The canonical construction
in [L162](../lemmas/L162-explicit-four-row-infinite-family.md) exhibits
infinitely many distinct algebraic-closure planes with four exact ranks
one. Thus finiteness cannot follow from just the specified support and
rank conditions. L162 passed its separate critical audit in step 505;
its direct proof does not assume L159–L161's reductions. Additional coding or
later-row constraints would be a materially different target requiring
coverage; another attempt at the same unconditional finiteness claim is
not justified by the present evidence. This is not a counterexample to
twelve-row exclusion or the MCA challenge.
