# Saved common-coordinate triple as a sixteen-count generator

Tested 2026-10-04 under the existing SPECIALIZE actual-pencil assessment.
The prior determinant-only test was blind because of a polynomial
zero-syndrome kernel. This attempt removes it before full-weight tests.

WHY IT FAILS: [L016](../lemmas/L016-shared-coordinate-triple-quotient.md)
reduces every extension-field full-weight pencil with the prescribed
supports {1,8,12,18}, {1,8,22,27}, {1,33,47,50} at 0,1,2 to affine
rational fourth-support equations. The complete exact certificate leaves
at most one isolated support and one universal support, giving at most
five bad parameters in this fixed class. Its numerical classification has
explicit finite-certificate dependence; it is stronger than a prime-field
sample. An actual five-count pencil passes the full event and exact-field
audits, but it is below the existing eleven and cannot attain sixteen.
Stop this fixed triple, preserve other zero-determinant families and
extension-root branches, and leave arbitrary support triples and the
grand challenge open. This class stop is not a global research ban.
