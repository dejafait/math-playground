# Complete two-row ranks do not stratify disjoint pairs

2026-10-25. [L153](../lemmas/L153-complete-disjoint-pair-two-row-census.md)
records the complete approved finite census and independent verification.
All 79,380 original two-block stacks have rank twelve and entire
nine-dimensional kernels. No pair or deficient kernel was discarded.

WHY IT FAILS: Rank alone supplies no strata on this complete domain and
cannot exclude any pair. Equal ranks do not identify the different
complete kernels or transport L110/L125's later-row loci. The saved
registry may feed a materially different many-row gated test, but such
a test needs its own coverage assessment and must retain decomposability,
rank-one projections, full weights, incidence restrictions and original
chart. This stops two-row rank stratification only. It is no obstruction
to the challenge and no global ban on independently specified many-row
mechanisms; L104's local survivors and ATTEMPTS/041–043 remain preserved.
