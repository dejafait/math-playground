# Stop evidence: all-disjoint exclusion by triple unions alone

Date: 2026-10-07. The approved target is the L033 twelve-incidence bound.
The bounded profile test established
[L039](../lemmas/L039-six-coordinate-equality-four-matchings.md): equality
would force profile (0,12,0), with four perfect matchings on six outside
labels and disjoint tag pairs. This is a useful necessary restriction,
not a proof of that bound or a failed proof of the main challenge.

WHY IT FAILS: L039 supplies an explicit abstract family of the remaining
form, and proves that every admissibly paired family of that form has
only the selected union-six triple below union size nine. The witness
has degree four at every vertex, the full outside pair packing, all six
same-tag pairs disjoint, and no forbidden triple or six-support union.
Consequently these support conditions alone cannot exclude the surviving
form. This does not show that its actual moments, weights, locators or
finite parameters can be realized. Continue only by adding such
compatibility information or a distinct independently testable condition;
do not repeat this support-only exclusion. The stop is local to this
mechanism, not a ban on the approved quantitative target.
