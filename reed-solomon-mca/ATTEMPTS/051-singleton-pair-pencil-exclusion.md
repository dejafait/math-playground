# Singleton-pair pencil counting does not exclude the last branch

2026-10-26. The structural argument and complete certificate in
[L161](../lemmas/L161-two-dimensional-pencil-coordinate-obstructions.md)
exclude a dimension-two L160 kernel for two or more shared S-coordinates.
With exactly one overlap they force a common quadratic pencil generated
by the two singleton-coordinate pairs. All 105 such pencils and all
3780 J-pair memberships were checked, including independent arithmetic
and coverage checks.

WHY IT FAILS: The hoped-for bound of fewer than four J-pair quadratics
per pencil is false: nine coordinate-pair choices yield four, representing
three distinct constant-product pencils h=8,12,22. Proportional evaluations
within each of two pairs do not imply proportionality between the pairs.
The necessary condition therefore does not exclude the sole-overlap
branch. These pencils are not infinite families and do not refute
four-row finiteness. Preserve the complete membership evidence; another
count of the same pencils cannot decide it. The remaining actual-map
singleton/overlap equations, followed by exact-rank reconstruction, are
a concrete different test within the unchanged finiteness target.
