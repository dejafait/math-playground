   Theorem 1 and its entire proof in the
   [author-uploaded primary article](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/249879499_Generalizing_flocks_of_Q_3q).

   without an algebraic-closure hypothesis. The proof of Theorem 1

   The institutional repository record has no attached article; it is
