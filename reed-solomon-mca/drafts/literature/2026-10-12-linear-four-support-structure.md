## Inspected theorem and scope obstruction

16_4-configurations*, Rev. Roumaine Math. Pures Appl. 50(5–6) (2005),
483–493, [arXiv:math/0505205v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0505205v1),
11 May 2005: inspected Section 2, printed pp. 2–3, Theorem 2 and its
essential Section 3 proof cases, pp. 3–9, and Section 4, pp. 9–10.
Its configurations are rank-three matroids with lines of size at most k,
exactly k k-point lines through each point; general position permits only
k-point and two-point lines. Realizability and pseudoline realizability
refer to the real projective plane; the latter corresponds to orientability.
Theorem 1 imposes n>k^2+k-5, and Theorem 2 excludes real and pseudoline
16_4 realizations. Euler's formula, absence of digons and ordered crossing
regions are essential proof inputs, including the final grid cases in
Lemmas 7–8. Section 4 reports nineteen abstract types from Betten–Betten.

The real arrangement hypotheses are not supplied by the frozen RS model.
Its syndrome line meets four-column spans in an eight-dimensional space
over characteristic 97. No bridge to a real pseudoline arrangement is
established. Consequently the nonrealizability theorem is inapplicable;
nonorientability is not a field-independent representability obstruction.
No RS theorem is imported or reproved here.
