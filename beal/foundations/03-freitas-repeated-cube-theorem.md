# Freitas's repeated-cube exclusion

Audited on 2026-09-25. Nuno Freitas, *On the Fermat-type equation x^3+y^3=z^p*, Commentarii Mathematici Helvetici **91** (2016), no. 2, 295–304, DOI [10.4171/CMH/386](https://doi.org/10.4171/CMH/386), **Theorem 3**, printed p. 296 ([publisher PDF, page 2](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/43415#page=2); [arXiv:1601.06361, page 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1601.06361#page=2)).

For a prime p >= 17 with Legendre symbol (-3/p) = -1, the equation x^3+y^3=z^p has no integer solution with xyz nonzero and gcd(x,y,z)=1. Equivalently, the exponent condition is p congruent to 2 modulo 3. The definitions of nontrivial and primitive are on printed p. 295; signed integer coordinates are allowed.

The theorem is unconditional. Its proof is in section 2, printed pp. 297–298. The full theorem is the cited input; the modular and symplectic ingredients inside its proof are not reproved here. The publisher's text extraction drops some minus signs; the arXiv statement preserves them and agrees on the prime range and residue class. The abstract alone omits the lower bound, which must be retained when applying Theorem 3.

The imported statement supplies no application at p=5 or p=11, or at primes congruent to 1 modulo 3. This is a boundary of this citation, not a claim about the literature status of those cases. No density computation or earlier theorem quoted in the introduction is imported as a separate exclusion.
