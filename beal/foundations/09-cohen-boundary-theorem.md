# Cohen's fourth-power/cube boundary theorem

Imported on 2026-10-03 using the source assessment completed before this research turn. Henri Cohen, *Number Theory, Volume II: Analytic and Modern Tools*, first edition, Graduate Texts in Mathematics **240**, Springer, 2007, **Section 14.6.3, Proposition 14.6.6, pp. 484–485**.

## Exact statement and source scope

The proposition states that neither equation

\[
X^4+Y^4=Z^3,\qquad X^4-Y^4=Z^3
\]

has a solution in nonzero coprime integers X,Y,Z. In particular, its minus clause excludes every pairwise coprime nonzero integer triple satisfying the difference equation. It imposes no height bound, parity restriction, or exclusion of coordinates of absolute value one. This is an unconditional fixed-signature theorem.

The statement and both printed proof cases were read in Cohen's text through a [book transcription](https://dokumen.pub/number-theory-volume-2-analytic-and-modern-tools-2-0387498931-9780387498935.html), most recently during the prior source-recovery turn on 2026-10-03. The [publisher's chapter record](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-0-387-49894-2_6) confirms the volume, edition, and chapter identity, but supplies metadata rather than the proposition. The transcription's mathematical-symbol rendering is imperfect; publisher-hosted theorem text was not retrieved. The exact statement and its two cases are preserved in the prior assessments, rather than inferred from search snippets.

Cohen's proof treats the odd-Z difference case together with the plus case by rank-zero elliptic curves, and treats the even-Z difference case using cubic Fermat. Those source arguments, parametrizations, and rank computations are cited, without an independent reproduction. Only the minus clause is needed for the boundary application. The plus clause overlaps an existing notebook exclusion and is not counted as an additional result here.
