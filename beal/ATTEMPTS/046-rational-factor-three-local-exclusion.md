# Rational-factor systems: standalone three-adic exclusion retired

2026-10-19. Under the prior EXPLORE assessment, the bounded modulo-27
test gave exact primitive three-adic points in both complete systems;
see the canonical proof in [L099](../lemmas/L099-rational-factor-three-adic-solvability.md).

WHY IT FAILS: With m=3 and a=1 or 2 fixed, lifting u after substituting
C preserves the full simultaneous system and has a unit derivative.
Both exact points have B,C units and v3(A)=1. Increasing three-power
precision cannot exclude these points. This stops standalone
three-adic nonexistence, without classifying all local valuations or
settling integer lifting, global compatibility or other algebra types.
A new global compatibility restriction would be needed to reopen
exclusion using three. L099's separate independent critical audit was
completed on 2026-10-19 and passed without correction; see its canonical
audit subsection and `scripts/rational-factor/three-lifts-audit-certificate.json`.
The earlier finite arithmetic cross-check alone was not that audit.
