# Bounded quadratic five-adic pruning is exhausted

2026-10-24. The approved individual family theta²+a theta+b with
|a|,|b|<=8 has been exhausted; see
[L125](../lemmas/L125-bounded-quadratic-five-adic-exhaustion.md) for the
full proof, exact sources, complete certificates and arithmetic replay.

WHY IT FAILS: Of 289 values, thirteen are S-units, seven have square
rational norm and four pass both real and dyadic point images. All
seven norm-square values are square in every actual five-adic factor,
so L119 rejects none of the four survivors. The cancellation in
theta²+3 is explicitly certified. This is new negative evidence for
this finite family, not a global ban on five-adic pruning. Repeating
these values or merely increasing precision offers no new test.
Products, larger boxes, other supported classes and point methods are
outside the completed target; a new mechanism needs separate coverage.
Full supported arithmetic and rational/integer point control remain
unresolved. The separate critical mathematical audit in L125 now
confirms the complete finite stop without a mathematical correction.
