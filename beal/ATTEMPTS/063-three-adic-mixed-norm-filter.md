# Norm-restricted mixed coefficient filter at 3 — stopped

2026-10-26. The canonical result is
[L144](../lemmas/L144-three-adic-mixed-norm-kernel-trivial.md);
its separate conceptual critical audit was accepted on 2026-10-27.

WHY IT FAILS: The actual quartic algebra over Q_3 is a product of
Q_3 and a totally ramified cubic with residue field F_3. Rational
square classes restrict isomorphically to the cubic. After quotienting
by every diagonal scalar, norm is an isomorphism, so the entire
norm-trivial coefficient kernel is already the identity. The elliptic
origin represents that identity. Thus restricting the B image to
square norm leaves no nonidentity class for an extra mixed filter
to exclude, independently of point sampling or full image algorithms.
This stops only the norm-restricted coefficient test at 3, not every
mixed descent, the unconditioned image, other primes or Beal itself.
Reopening needs a materially different, separately assessed target,
not a larger point search in this zero kernel. Preserve ATTEMPTS/062
and the real/11-adic saturation evidence independently.
