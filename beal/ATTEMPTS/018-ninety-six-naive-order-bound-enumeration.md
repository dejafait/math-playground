2026-10-10. [L051](../lemmas/L051-ninety-six-order-class-generation-bound.md)
certifies a sufficient order-based cutoff about 1.65*10^118, exceeding
the approved 10^6 workload threshold.

WHY IT FAILS: Enumeration at this sufficient cutoff fails the bounded
entry test, so that implementation is parked. This is not a lower bound
on necessary enumeration or the field Minkowski bound. The nonmaximal
order and conservative signature can greatly inflate it. Maximal-order
refinement and other unconditional certification methods remain open;
class/unit and Selmer completeness are unproved. Reopening requires a
certified smaller cutoff and rigorous ideal backend. Preserve previous
stops and unfinished arithmetic. FNS Remark 7.13's smaller Jacobian
factor is the next proposed source lead, with applicability unassessed.
