# Split-isogeny obstruction after exact same-coordinate equations — stopped

2026-10-26. The bounded finite-kernel specialization and point comparison
are canonical in [L142](../lemmas/L142-marked-split-isogeny-kernels.md).
The full attempt record is
[the working note](../drafts/2026-10-26-split-isogeny-kernel-attempt.md).

WHY IT FAILS: Once rational B- and E-points have the same T-coordinate,
their equations already give the rational marked lift h=3W/R; L142
handles the nonzero denominator and the ramification fibers. The
reverse-isogeny connecting condition then vanishes on their normalized
Abel classes, so it supplies no extra exclusion on those exact point
pairs. The forward connecting map has the wrong target for this test.
This is a redundancy result, not a failed source retrieval or a Beal
counterexample. The explicit finite modules remain available for a
different, separately assessed test on quotient Jacobian classes. No
nonredundancy against the existing supported/fake descent images, full
joint descent, rank gain or point completeness was proved. Reopening
this shortcut requires a concrete mixed test on weaker class data with
demonstrated extra content, not another restatement of the lift equation.
