# Rational-factor systems: standalone five-adic exclusion retired

2026-10-19. The bounded approved feasibility test gave exact primitive
five-adic points in both complete systems; the canonical proof is
[L102](../lemmas/L102-rational-factor-five-adic-solvability.md).

WHY IT FAILS: With m=u=1 both cube equations have unit-derivative seeds.
In II the full complementary identity first proves the exact integral
normalization for the fixed tuple, retaining the factor 5^11. Hensel
then gives one common tuple in each system, with m,C units. Increasing
five-power precision cannot eliminate these exact points. This retires
standalone exclusion at five, without claiming integer lifting, global
compatibility or a local valuation classification. A specified additional
global compatibility mechanism is needed to reopen exclusion using five.
L102's separate independent critical review remains pending.
