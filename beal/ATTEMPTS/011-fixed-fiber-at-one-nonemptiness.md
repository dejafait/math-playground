# Fixed fiber at one cannot supply local-image nonemptiness

2026-10-08. [L038](../lemmas/L038-fixed-fiber-at-one-local-algebra-mismatch.md)
certifies every factor: A has degrees [1,11], whereas B_1 has degrees
[6,6], all unramified. Both dimensions are twelve.

WHY IT FAILS: Although L036 admits the scalar 1 as a primitive common-j
witness, it fails the independent whole-fiber condition. An isomorphism
cannot change the primitive-idempotent field degrees. Thus this scalar
cannot establish nonemptiness of either restricted local image. This is
a stop for j=1, not for complete-fiber comparison, other scalars, descent,
or Beal. An independently certified fiber at another admitted scalar
remains a discriminating test; no nonemptiness or emptiness is inferred
from this one rejection. Preserve ATTEMPTS/008–010.
