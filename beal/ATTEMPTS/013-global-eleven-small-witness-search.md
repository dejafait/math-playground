# Global eleven projection: small witnesses and missing class data

2026-10-10. [L045](../lemmas/L045-global-eleven-projection-ideal-criterion.md)
proves an exact ideal criterion and a both-or-neither intersection
dichotomy. The exact bounded search of 103,532 linear, quadratic and
cubic elements produces no norm supported only at five and eleven.
Preserve its full certificate and the canonical criterion.

WHY IT FAILS: These finite polynomial boxes need not contain suitable
S-units and do not cover classes with nonzero fifth-multiple valuations
outside S. Their failure cannot prove either class nonvanishing or an
empty projection intersection. The required quotient classes remain
unevaluated, and no certified class data are available. Park this bounded
search and dependent global arithmetic; enlargement of the boxes alone
is not a justified continuation. Reopening requires a specified different
exact-witness construction or unconditional ideal-class certificates.
An independent two-adic whole-fiber power test is proposed for separate
source assessment. This is not a ban on global descent or on that local
mechanism; ATTEMPTS/008–012 remain intact.
