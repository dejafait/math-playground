so the rational-root ratio parametrizes rational orbits throughout the
positive interval. Primitive integer lifting fixes the reduced numerator
and denominator and reimposes precisely the original simultaneous fifth-
and cube-power tests and gcd conditions. Quotient rationality alone thus
provides no further arithmetic pruning or positive-genus curve. This
parks the direct quotient shortcut, not nonlinear covers, supported class
descent, either integer system or the whole rational-factor branch. A
future exclusion-oriented reopening needs a specified extra mechanism
and a test beyond the equivalent lifting conditions; independent audit
of L101 is verification of this stop, not such a reopening.
