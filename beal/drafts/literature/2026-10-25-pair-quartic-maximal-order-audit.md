- [Peter Stevenhagen, *The arithmetic of number rings*, MSRI Publications
  44 (2008)](https://library.slmath.org/books/Book44/files/08psh.pdf),
  Lemma 6.4 p.221 and Section 9 pp.233–235, especially Proposition 9.3
  and its proof. The lemma uses a nonzero finitely generated stable
  module. Section 9 permits nonmonogenic orders; p must not be a unit.
  It computes the radical by iterated Frobenius with p^k exceeding the
  residue-algebra dimension. The multiplier lies between R and p^(-1)R;
  the proposition guarantees enlargement if R is not p-maximal. Its
  proof uses a largest power of the radical and intersection with the
  p-maximal extension. The following construction uses the action
  R/pR -> End(I_p/pI_p). No lower bound on p is imposed.

  2020-07-19](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf),
  Lemma 2.23 p.33 and Remark 2.25 p.34, including the basis-change proof.
