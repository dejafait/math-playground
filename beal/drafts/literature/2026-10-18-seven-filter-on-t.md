## Primary-source coverage and exact applicability obligations

Read on 2026-10-18, retaining the versions already assessed:

- Stacks Project, [Lemma 31.18.6, tag 0BD2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BD2),
  statement and complete proof. Finite locally free morphisms of positive
  degree have a determinant norm compatible with arbitrary base change.
  The field algebra D/F meets the finite-free hypothesis. Its use must
  retain the entire base-changed algebra, rather than a chosen factor.
- J.S. Milne, [Algebraic Number Theory, v3.08, 19 July 2020](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANTc.pdf),
  Proposition 8.2 and Corollary 8.4 with complete proofs, printed p.136
  (PDF page 138). Finite separability suffices: completion gives the
  product over all extending places and the norm is the product of
  their norms. The proof factors a primitive-element polynomial and
  uses determinant base change; normality is unnecessary. Apply with
  the fixed embeddings from L090, checking their correspondence before
  assigning components. L074's even valuations outside 2,11 concern
  arbitrary field classes, including negative valuations and class lifts.
- Milne, [Class Field Theory, v4.03, 6 August 2020](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFTc.pdf),
  III Proposition 1.2, Lemmas 1.3–1.5 and complete proof, printed p.98
  (PDF page 107). For finite unramified extensions of local fields the
  unit norm is onto and reduces to the residue-field norm. The proof
  retains higher unit filtrations and their convergent corrections.
  L090 supplies an unramified entire field and its quadratic subfield;
  verify the actual norm domain and base separately for each factor.
  Surjectivity alone is not a square-character or containment theorem.
- Keith Conrad, [Finite fields](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/galoistheory/finitefields.pdf),
  Theorem 4.6 and complete proof pp.9–10, Section 5 introductory
  discussion, Theorems 5.5–5.7 pp.10–11. Theorem 5.7 states the
  norm formula a -> a^((q^n-1)/(q-1)) and surjectivity for arbitrary
  finite base F_q. Section 5 leaves the analogous proofs to the reader;
  the inspected Theorem 4.6 proof uses all Frobenius conjugates and
  multiplicative cyclicity. No version date is identified. This is
  explicit source coverage for the nonprime residue base, not permission
  to replace a product norm by componentwise squareness.
- Pete L. Clark, [Algebraic Number Theory II, 161-page notes](https://plclark.github.io/PeteLClark/Expositions/8410FULLL.pdf),
  Corollary 1.77 and its proof, printed p.57, and Theorem 1.79, p.58.
  Completeness, integral coefficients and a simple residue root suffice
  for lifting; the stronger statement requires v(f(a))>2v(f'(a)).
