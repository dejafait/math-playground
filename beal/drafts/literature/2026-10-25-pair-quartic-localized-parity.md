## Source statements and comparison

L. Claborn, *Dedekind domains: Overrings and semi-prime elements*,
[Pacific J. Math. 15 (1965), 799–804](https://msp.org/pjm/1965/15-3/pjm-v15-n3-p06-p.pdf),
Corollary 1.4 and Proposition 1.5, p.800: for a Dedekind domain A
with fraction field F and an intermediate ring A subset B subset F,
extension of fractional ideals induces a surjection Cl(A) -> Cl(B).
Its kernel is generated by classes of prime ideals P with PB=B.
The complete proofs were inspected: surviving prime ideals lift by
contraction, principal ideals extend to principal ideals, and a class
becoming principal has its remaining fractional-ideal factors among the
killed primes. Negative valuations are allowed; no positivity or narrow
class condition occurs. Proposition 1.2 supplies the overring's Dedekind
property. These are imported, not reproved.

P. L. Clark, *Elliptic Dedekind Domains Revisited*,
[Enseign. Math. 55 (2009), Theorem 5, pp.217–218](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/44188),
is a matching modern formulation, attributed to Claborn. Section 2.1
identifies Pic with ordinary ideal classes for Dedekind domains. Its
proof includes both the surjectivity and kernel directions. No elliptic
curve assumption enters this theorem. Claborn's original theorem was
followed and read, rather than relying on a snippet or the paper title.
The older UGA URL failed; the publisher copy and original are accessible.
A guessed MSP p03 URL was unrelated and is excluded from the evidence.

[Stacks Lemma 10.12.10, tag 00DF](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00DF)
says tensoring a right-exact sequence of R-modules by any R-module
preserves right exactness. Its full proof was inspected again; flatness
