Adeles*, [161-page author notes](https://plclark.github.io/PeteLClark/Expositions/8410FULLL.pdf),
accessed 2026-10-17, no dated revision identified. Read Theorem 1.75,
Corollary 1.77 and its proof, printed p.57, and Theorem 1.79, p.58.
