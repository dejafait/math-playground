## Primary coverage inspected

[Dembélé–Voight, *Explicit methods for Hilbert modular forms*, arXiv:1010.5727v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1010.5727v2), Theorem 2.8 (printed p. 7), computes the entire parallel-weight-two cuspidal Hecke module for strict-class-number-one fields; it permits matrix output and arbitrary constituent eigenvalue fields. Theorem 3.9 (p. 11) assumes quaternion discriminant D coprime to N and identifies its image with the D-new space. D=(1), available in even degree, gives the whole space. Section 2 (pp. 6–7) identifies normalized eigenvalues with integral Fourier coefficients. Section 4 (pp. 12–16) counts all ideal neighbours, divides norm-element counts by unit stabilizers, and uses ideal-class enumeration and a mass termination check. These are completeness requirements, not checks performed here. Its narrow-class-number hypothesis must be justified for this specialization in any implementation; no new class-number proof is supplied here.

[Magma V2.29, Operators](https://magma.maths.usyd.edu.au/magma/handbook/text/1788), dated 3 August 2026, supplies HeckeOperator(M,P) and rational matrices with a fixed basis in parallel weight two. [Creation of Full Cuspidal Spaces](https://docs.magma-maths.org/ModularArithmeticGeometry/HilbertModularForms/creation.html) requires a level ideal in the maximal order and weights at least two of matching parity. A full-space quaternion algebra must be unramified at every finite prime; the definite implementation uses a maximal order and accounts for the level internally. This differs from the direct Eichler-order model in the paper. Neither interface restricts constituent coefficient fields. An implementation would still need exact field/ideal identities, the intended prime rather than an unspecified factor above 29, the complete cusp-space basis, and integral characteristic-polynomial validation before reduction at 7. No lattice reduction or mod-7 semisimplicity is being asserted.

[PVT arXiv:2512.17845v1, Section 7.4, Theorem A proof](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17845v1#S7.SS4) says that MagmaInput creates Data.txt from possible motive traces, including degenerate parameters. Theorem A excludes p=7 from its stated range. Table 7.2 alone supplies no Hecke operator. L024's exact-level location and unrestricted coefficient fields are reused; neither Theorem A nor the field-F column supplies the desired exhaustion.
