## Primary theorem comparison

[Pacetti–Villagra Torcomian, arXiv:2512.17845v1, 19 December 2025, Theorem 7.8 and proof, Section 7.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17845v1#S7.SS2), explicitly places an irreducible plus representation in a parallel-weight-two Hilbert newform space of trivial character at level 3^i(sqrt(5))^j, i,j in {2,3}, with coordinate-dependent formulas. The source uses (a,b,c)=(X,-Z,Y), p=7 and t=-X^5/Y^3, as in L019. Theorem 2.4(1) supplies modularity of all plus specializations for q=5,r=3; Remark 2.2 retains the geometric Tate normalization. These are known results, not a claim of new modular location. Direct citation of Theorem 7.8 is a matching theorem-level lead; the present task audits its BD interface rather than independently reproducing its full proof or rejecting the theorem.

[Breuil–Diamond, *Formes modulaires de Hilbert modulo p et valeurs d’extensions entre caractères galoisiens*, Ann. Sci. ENS 47(5) (2014), 905–974, Theorem 3.2.2, author PDF printed pp. 36–37](https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~breuil/PUBLICATIONS/extensions.pdf#page=36), has the following load-bearing requirements. Its Section 3.1 also fixes a totally real base field, a continuous totally odd residual representation and a sufficiently large coefficient field. The theorem assumes p>2, residual modularity and irreducibility on G_(F(zeta_p)); its extra projective-image exception occurs only at p=5. It prescribes a character psi lifting the residual determinant with psi/epsilon of finite order, a finite set S containing the p-adic and residual/determinant ramification places, a subset T of the p-adic places, Weil–Deligne types at S and characters mu_v at T and at places away from p with nonzero monodromy. For every v in S a local lift must satisfy all five conditions:

1. At v dividing p it is potentially semistable of Hodge–Tate weights (0,1) at every local embedding, in the source convention.
2. At v dividing p it is potentially ordinary exactly when v belongs to T.
3. It has the prescribed Weil–Deligne type.
4. At v in T, or v not dividing p with nonzero monodromy, it contains a line lifting mu_v times the residual cyclotomic character; after dividing by epsilon this line has finite inertia.
5. Its determinant on inertia equals psi on inertia.

The conclusion is a modular lift with the same local prescriptions, unramified outside S, and **global** determinant psi. Thus local determinant compatibility is an inertia condition; silently requiring equality of whole local characters would strengthen the hypothesis. Conversely, residual determinant compatibility alone is insufficient. The proof's pp. 37–38 cases include nonzero monodromy and ordinary-component choices; they do not remove the local-lift premise.

The global numerical and representation inputs are available: F is totally real, p=7>2, modularity has a named source, L019 fixes cyclotomic determinant and the unramified quadratic base completion at 7, L020 supplies absolute irreducibility, and L021 supplies restriction irreducibility. Coefficient enlargement is allowed by BD and does not authorize dropping one embedding. Total oddness, the choice psi=epsilon and each S/T/type/line prescription still need to be explicitly recorded in a mathematical application. At 3 and 5 the original geometric characteristic-zero component is an available lift with cyclotomic determinant and conductor at most three, as already used in L019. Prescribing those existing types is distinct from replacing them by types of minimal residual conductor.

## The newly inspected local-lift sources

[Fontaine–Laffaille, *Construction de représentations p-adiques*, Ann. Sci. ENS 15(4) (1982), 547–608, Section 9, particularly 9.10–9.12, printed pp. 604–606](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_1982_4_15_4_547_0.pdf#page=59), classifies finite flat p-groups over W(k) using filtered modules with the two filtration degrees 0 and 1. Section 9 assumes absolute ramification index one, coefficient ring Z_p and perfect residue field. For p unequal to two it includes all finite flat p-groups, without a unipotence restriction. Proposition 9.12 identifies the generic-fibre points with the associated contravariant Fontaine–Laffaille functor; its full proof identifies the two Hom descriptions and compares their lengths. Theorem 8.4, printed pp. 595–596, gives crystalline admissibility in the small filtration range. These hypotheses match the underlying finite-flat setting of L019, but the rank-two coefficient action and the determinant specialization are further applicability obligations, not conclusions stated verbatim by Section 9.

[Clozel–Harris–Taylor, *Automorphy for some l-adic lifts of automorphic mod l Galois representations*, Publ. Math. IHES 108 (2008), 1–181, Section 2.4.1 and Lemma 2.4.1, printed pp. 33–35](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1007/s10240-008-0016-1.pdf#page=33), treats an unramified local base and a coefficient field containing all its embeddings. Its coefficient-linear Fontaine–Laffaille functor is exact and fully faithful. Before Lemma 2.4.1 it assumes the residual representation lies in that functor's image and each graded multiplicity at each embedding is at most one. The lemma proves liftability by lifting the filtered direct summands and divided-Frobenius maps across small Artinian extensions. Its full proof was read. It is not a theorem that arbitrary residual representations have weight-two lifts. Corollary 2.4.4, printed p. 37, identifies the rank-one crystalline deformation tangent space with unramified classes; it does not itself prescribe an arbitrary determinant.

This is primary support for testing the local bridge: transfer L019's finite-flat coefficient action into the filtered category, justify filtration ranks using its cyclotomic determinant (or the already written L021 inertia cases), use liftability with the required weights, and verify determinant compatibility. The CHT covariant functor is defined using a dual and a Tate twist in its Section 2.4.1; the convention conversion must be written rather than guessing the Hodge–Tate sign from the word “covariant.” No claim about crystalline reduction of the original J in every coordinate case is needed or established by these sources. The proposed lift may be a different characteristic-zero representation with the same local reduction.
