# Beal conjecture

Apply ../GOAL.md as the shared research policy.

## Exact target and traps

Prove or disprove: if A^x + B^y = C^z for positive integers A, B, C, x, y, z with x,y,z > 2, then gcd(A,B,C) > 1. A disproof requires an exact integer solution with gcd(A,B,C) = 1, not merely failure of pairwise coprimality.
