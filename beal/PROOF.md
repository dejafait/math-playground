# Beal conjecture: argument overview

The exact target is the nonexistence of a positive primitive solution to A^x + B^y = C^z with x,y,z > 2. The primary statement and the equivalence of primitivity with pairwise coprimality for an actual solution are recorded in foundations/01-target-and-scope.md. No complete candidate proof or disproof of Beal has been developed. L029 imports the full Catalan–Mihăilescu classification to exclude every positive solution with a unit base, for arbitrary original exponents greater than two and without assuming primitivity. A unit right-hand base and two unit summands are excluded directly; a single unit summand gives consecutive powers, whose unique exception 9−8=1 forces the right-hand exponent to be two. All power representations of the exception are checked in L029. The theorem is cited without a cyclotomic reproof; Mathlib coverage is not checked. L002 reduces the target exactly to exponents in S = {4} union {odd primes}, by taking divisors of each exponent and raising the corresponding bases to positive integer powers. C002a then excludes equal-exponent signatures, every placement of (p,p,3) and (p,p,4) for odd primes p >= 5, and the ordered signature (4,4,3). These exclusions use Fermat's Last Theorem and Darmon–Merel; Breuil–Conrad–Diamond–Taylor's full-modularity theorem discharges the latter's historical cube-clause hypothesis. Precise named sources are in foundations/02-standard-results.md.
For the residual family a^3+b^3=c^p, L003 factors the left side into s=a+b and q=a^2-ab+b^2. The exact possibilities are (s,q,c)=(u^p,v^p,uv), with 3 not dividing uv, or (3^(p-1)u^p,3v^p,3uv), with 3 not dividing v; in each case gcd(u,v)=1. The identity 4q=s^2+3(a-b)^2 gives reversible square-discriminant systems preserving positivity and primitivity. This supplies a further integer constraint, without excluding either branch.

L005 imports Kraus's necessary restrictions through precise published restatements: for prime p >= 17, a primitive nonzero cube solution has c congruent to 3 modulo 6 and v_2(ab)=1, without a condition on p modulo 3. The source-access limitation and exact citations are in foundations/04-kraus-divisibility-restriction.md; Kraus's original full text was not retrieved.

L007 retains the simultaneous power conditions through factorization in the norm-Euclidean Eisenstein ring. With W=3^(p-2)u^p it extracts (d+W sqrt(-3))/2=((r+t sqrt(-3))/2)^p, where r,t are odd and coprime and r^2+3t^2=4v. The coefficient condition is t U_p=3^(p-2)u^p for the integer recurrence U_0=0, U_1=1, U_(n+2)=r U_(n+1)-v U_n. It forces v_3(t)=p-2+p v_3(u), and the only common factor of t and U_p is possibly p, with v_p(U_p)=1 when p divides t. Thus U_p must be a signed pth power or p times one. The converse retains the strict positivity bound on the trace T_p. This is an exact global reduction, not an exclusion.
