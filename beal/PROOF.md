## Assembly and unresolved gap

The target is absence of positive primitive solutions to A^x+B^y=C^z for x,y,z>2, with scope in [foundations](foundations/01-target-and-scope.md). L002 reduces exponents to 4 or odd primes; L029 excludes unit-base cases using the cited Catalan–Mihailescu theorem. Named classical exclusions remove selected signatures, not the infinite residual family.

The repeated-cube factorization gives reversible square-discriminant systems with positivity and coprimality retained. Later number-field and descent routes constrain fixed remaining systems. They do not yet classify all supported arithmetic or rational/integer points, nor eliminate every exponent signature.

## Partial results

The recorded octic route supplies an actual marked model, maximal-order/support calculations, local images and restrictions on bounded trial classes. Its genus-two and elliptic quotient maps and rational two-torsion computation are tools for possible descent/rank comparisons. In particular L110/L111/L113 concern the actual quotient models and maps, while audited L126 supplies the torsion constraint. A target-specific Jacobian isogeny/rank relation and upper-rank or point control remain unestablished here.

Finite trial failures and auxiliary-field map obstructions only exclude their tested constructions. Unit and S-class contributions, full descent, compatibility with retained fifth-power equations, recovery of original integer solutions and all other residual systems remain separate obligations. Read each canonical statement for its particular field, twist, places and audit status.

## Known traps checked

- A local obstruction for a trial class is not exhaustion of the global supported group or point set. A quotient norm condition is not the entire Kummer image.
- Trivial rational two-torsion does not bound rank or prove emptiness; irreducibility alone does not imply maximality of a polynomial order.
- Preserve primitive/positive boundary cases, all support primes, scalar quotients, actual twists and both retained systems. No general Beal candidate is asserted.
