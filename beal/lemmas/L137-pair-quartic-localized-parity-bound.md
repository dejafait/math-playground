# L137 — Localization adds no rank to the existing quartic parity matrix

## Hypotheses

Let K=Q(alpha) be L133's degree-four field, with polynomial

    g(X)=X^4+64X^3+150X^2−32500X−109375.

Use audited L134's full maximal order O and ordered integral basis
(1,alpha,beta,gamma), with beta=(alpha^2+14alpha+25)/50 and
gamma=(alpha^3+239alpha^2+4475alpha+19375)/18750. Use audited L135's
ordered prime ideals T=(P_0,...,P_31), including their actual full
O-basis lattices, and audited L136's complete 182 accepted valuation
vectors. Let V be their image span in F_2^32. Put B=O[1/330], and let
e_i denote the i-th standard coordinate vector, with zero-based indices.
All class groups are ordinary class groups. There is no GRH, narrow-class,
monogenic-order, full-relation-lattice or descent-support assumption.

## Conclusion

The complete set of primes inverted in B is

    P_1, P_26, P_27, P_28, P_29, P_30, P_31.

All seven corresponding e_i already belong to V. Thus

    dim_F2(V + <e_1,e_26,e_27,e_28,e_29,e_30,e_31>) = 30,
    dim_F2 Cl(B)/2Cl(B) <= 32−30 = 2.

The required rank 32 is not reached, and this augmentation gives no
improvement over L136's ordinary-class bound. The two nonpivot columns
give no lower bound for either actual class quotient. No class number,
unit basis, descent, rank, point, lift, integer/degree-nine recovery or
Beal exclusion follows. A possible later descent application would need
allowed support containing every prime above 2,3,5,11; that has not been
established. This is a specialization of known quotient theory, with a
new finite negative test of this particular proposed improvement. It is
not a claim of mathematical originality beyond the inspected sources.
The separate critical proof audit of 2026-10-25 accepts precisely this
statement, with no mathematical correction or new dependency.

## Proof

### Imported localization theorem and the exact support

Import L. Claborn, *Dedekind domains: Overrings and semi-prime elements*,
[Pacific J. Math. 15 (1965), 799–804, Propositions 1.2–1.3, p.799,
Corollary 1.4 and Proposition 1.5, p.800](https://msp.org/pjm/1965/15-3/pjm-v15-n3-p06-p.pdf).
For a Dedekind domain A with fraction field F and an intermediate ring
A subset D subset F, the overring is Dedekind and ideal extension gives
a surjection Cl(A) -> Cl(D), with kernel generated by classes of primes
P such that PD=D. The kernel and surjectivity proofs were inspected in
the prior assessment. A matching formulation is P. L. Clark,
*Elliptic Dedekind Domains Revisited*, [Enseign. Math. 55 (2009),
Theorem 5 and proof, pp.217–218](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/44188).
These results are imported, not reproved.

Here O is the maximal integer ring by L134, hence Dedekind, and B is its
localization at {330^n:n>=0}, embedded in K. The denominators are nonzero.
For a prime P, PB=B exactly when P meets that multiplicative set.
Indeed 1 in PB implies, after taking a common denominator, that some
330^n is in P; conversely such a member becomes a unit in PB. Primeness
then says this happens exactly for primes above 2,3,5,11. This is an
ideal-localization statement, not inversion of a chosen ideal generator.

L135's complete maximal-ideal enumeration in O/pO gives respectively
one, two and three primes above 2,3,5. Their full lattices are precisely
P_26,...,P_31. At 11 the index [O:Z[alpha]]=937500 is prime to 11, so
L135's imported Milne Remark 3.43 applies. Its complete factorization
g mod 11=(X+5)^4 gives one distinct prime, P_1=(11,alpha+5)O. The four
copies encode ramification, not four ideals. These statements cover all
primes above the inverted rational primes, not only primes of small norm.

The calculation binds the full ordered factor base to L135's SHA-256
`c5363d1b846188e37fc53590b61de34923bb981da25ddd895cd82e14dda9b73b`
and every relation to L136's SHA-256
`fdb376f96717188690de8c849197d8b64834afdcdc56d789f427f2cf186c5501`.
It checks equality of the entire ordered ideal records in both inputs.

### The partial-quotient implication

L135 gives a surjection Z^32 -> Cl(O) sending the i-th basis vector to
[P_i]. L136's R_box lies in its kernel because each retained vector is
the full factorization of a principal ideal. Compose with Cl(O) -> Cl(B)
from Claborn. Each e_i for an inverted prime now also maps to zero.
Writing E_Z for their integral span gives a surjection

    Z^32/(R_box+E_Z) -> Cl(B).

Import [Stacks Project Lemma 10.12.10, tag 00DF](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00DF),
right exactness of tensor product. Tensor over Z with F_2. The result is
a surjection

    F_2^32/(V+<e_1,e_26,...,e_31>) -> Cl(B)/2Cl(B).

Thus its target has dimension at most 32−30=2. Flatness is unnecessary.
There is no claim that R_box is the full relation lattice, so equality
of the actual dimension with two is not justified. In particular,
nonpivot columns 17 and 24 do not prove surviving classes. This completes
only the stated localized upper bound and the failed finite improvement.
