## Conclusion

and then eta evaluated at the four increasingly ordered alpha roots.
Write a negative sign as 1 and a positive sign as 0. The real Kummer image
W=delta_infinity(J(R)/2J(R)) is exactly

    W=span_F2{(0,1,1,1,1),(0,0,0,1,1)}.

Let E be the even-parity subspace of F_2^5, the real product norm kernel,
and let s:V -> E be the sign map. The induced map V -> E/W is onto and
has rank exactly two. Consequently the ambient subspace satisfying this
