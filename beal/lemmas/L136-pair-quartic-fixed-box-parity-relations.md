# L136 — Fixed-box principal-ideal parity rank in the pair quartic

## Hypotheses

Let K=Q(alpha) be L133's field with irreducible polynomial

    g(X)=X^4+64X^3+150X^2−32500X−109375.

Use audited L134's maximal order O and its ordered basis

    b=(1,alpha,beta,gamma),
    beta=(alpha^2+14alpha+25)/50,
    gamma=(alpha^3+239alpha^2+4475alpha+19375)/18750.

Let T=(P_0,...,P_31) be the ordered 32 prime ideals in audited L135's
`scripts/pair-genus-two/quartic-class-certificate.json`, whose SHA-256 is
`c5363d1b846188e37fc53590b61de34923bb981da25ddd895cd82e14dda9b73b`.
The first 26 entries are its regular linear-factor primes in increasing
rational-prime/factor order; the final six are its exceptional primes at
2,3,3,5,5,5 in the displayed order there. The full lattices, not only their
rational norms, specify T. Every N(P_i)=p_i is a rational prime, and their
ordinary ideal classes generate Cl(O). No narrow-class or monogenic-order
assumption is made.

For each nonzero a in {-2,-1,0,1,2}^4 put x_a=sum a_i b_i. Retain a
precisely when the complete prime factorization of (x_a) is supported on T.
Let R_box be the subgroup of Z^32 generated by the retained complete
valuation vectors (v_(P_i)((x_a)))_i. Both signs, units and repeated or
zero parity vectors are retained.

## Conclusion

Exactly 182 of the 624 elements are retained. Of the others, 438 have
a rational norm cofactor outside the rational primes under T, and four
pass that filter but have an omitted prime-ideal factor. The image of
R_box in F_2^32 has rank exactly 30. Consequently

    dim_F2 Cl(O)/2Cl(O) = dim_F2 Cl(O)[2] <= 2.

The required full parity rank 32 is not attained by this box. This supplies
no positive lower bound for either dimension, no class number or complete
class presentation, and no information on narrow classes. The box is
finished without enlargement. Units, descent, quotient ranks, points,
marked lifts, integer recovery, degree-nine recovery, L095's systems and
other residual Beal signatures remain open. This is an exact-instance
specialization of inspected standard theory, not a new general theorem
or a Beal solution. Independent arithmetic replay and the separate critical
proof audit of 2026-10-25 accept this statement; the audit is recorded below.

## Proof

### Imported ideal and quotient results

Import J. S. Milne, *Algebraic Number Theory*, v3.08 (2020-07-19),
[Theorem 3.7, pp.49–50; Corollary 3.13 and following divisibility remark,
p.51; Propositions 4.1(c), 4.2, pp.69–70](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf).
Nonzero ideals of O factor uniquely; ideal containment reverses
divisibility, ideal norms multiply, and N((x))=|N_(K/Q)(x)|. Ideal norm
is lattice index. These apply to the full maximal order in L134, including
ramified primes and primes dividing the power-order index.

Import Peter Stevenhagen, *The arithmetic of number rings*, MSRI
Publications 44 (2008), [Section 7, pp.224–225, and Section 12,
pp.248–249](https://library.slmath.org/books/Book44/files/08psh.pdf).
The multiplication determinant is the element norm. For a generating
factor base T, the valuation map from T-units has image the full relation
subgroup in Z^T, with quotient Cl(O); the partial-relation quotient maps
onto Cl(O). L135 establishes the generating hypothesis unconditionally.
Every retained x_a is a T-unit and supplies such a relation, so

    Z^32/R_box -> Cl(O)

is surjective. Import [Stacks Project, Lemma 10.12.10, tag 00DF](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00DF)
for right exactness of tensor product. Tensoring this surjection over Z
with F_2 gives a surjection from F_2^32/im(R_box) to Cl(O)/2Cl(O).
The ordinary class group is finite, as follows from the cited Minkowski
theorem used in L135. The standard structure theorem for finite abelian
groups identifies the dimensions of Cl(O)/2Cl(O) and Cl(O)[2]. Thus the
computed rank 30 proves the asserted upper bound two; it does not prove
that missing relations do not exist outside this box.
