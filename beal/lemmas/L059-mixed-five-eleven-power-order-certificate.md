# L059 — Mixed prime-five/eleven power-order certificate

## Hypotheses

Use L033's degree-twelve field K=Q(k), with

    m(X)=X^12-6X^10+6X^9-6X^8-126X^7+104X^6+468X^5
          +258X^4-456X^3-1062X^2-774X-380.

Put R=Z[k] and O=O_K; do not assume R maximal. Use L038's complete
five-adic factors Q5 and U_11, the latter unramified of degree eleven.
Let q0 and q11 denote their maximal-order primes, respectively. Use
L041's complete eleven primes P1,P2,P3, ordered by residues 8,2,10,
and L045's quotient

    V=Cl(K)/(5Cl(K)+<[q]: q above five>),  u=image([P1]).

Set J=(11,k-8)R and Q=(5,k)R. For the conditional certificate suppose
beta in K* and positive integers h,t with 5 not dividing h are supplied,
and a literal equality of additive R-ideal lattices is verified:

    beta R=J^h Q^t.                                      (1)

The approved finite attempt restricts h,t to {1,2,3,4}, but the implication
below holds for the stated positive exponents. No identity is presumed
to exist, and no R-ideal inverse or certified maximal-order basis is used.

## Conclusion

The exact extensions are QO=q0 and JO=P1. Identity (1) implies

    (beta)=P1^h q0^t,  h[P1]+t[q0]=0 in Cl(K),  u=0 in V.

Moreover [beta] belongs to L033's K(S,5), S consisting of all primes
above five and eleven, and rho([beta])=(h,0,0) modulo five. Thus both
L045 local vectors (2,0,0),(0,2,0) survive the global valuation projection.
This settles only the conditional projected-survival question. No
witness existence, class value without a witness, simultaneous unit/local
compatibility, global modular point, integer solution or branch emptiness
is claimed. The bounded candidate family has no completeness guarantee.

## Proof

### Imported results and exact prime-five specialization

Import J. S. Milne, *Algebraic Number Theory*, v3.08, July 19, 2020,
[author PDF](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf), Theorem
3.29, p.57 (O is Dedekind), Corollary 3.13, p.51 (localization equality),
Example 3.19 and Theorem 3.20, p.53 (DVR ideals and prime factorization).
The complete-field factor correspondence is used with L038's proved
factorization; its coprime lifting and local degrees were imported there
from Milne Theorem 7.33/Remark 7.36 and Corollary 7.42, pp.121–124.
These general theorems are cited, not reproved. K/Q is finite separable
in characteristic zero, and k is integral since m is monic. They concern
O, rather than unique factorization or invertibility in R.

At q0, L038's unique linear lifted factor has k reducing to zero.
Its completion is Q5, hence ord_q0(5)=1 and ord_q0(k)>=1.
The ideal generated by 5,k has DVR exponent exactly the minimum, one.
At q11 the actual residue field is F_(5^11), and the residue of k is
a root of L038's irreducible degree-eleven H with H(0)=1. It cannot
be zero, so k is a unit and the ideal exponent is zero. These are all
places above five, their local degrees totaling twelve. At every finite
prime away from five, 5 is a unit. Thus localization equality gives
QO=q0 with exponent exactly one, without assuming a power-order
prime decomposition or using an index computation. L058 already proves
JO=P1 using all three ordered eleven places and all other finite primes.

### Conditional class and valuation consequence

Ben Lynn, *Extension and Contraction*, [author notes](https://crypto.stanford.edu/pbc/notes/commalg/extcon.html),
operations item 3, states extension preserves integral ideal products
for every ring homomorphism. Apply it to R -> O and the positive powers
in (1). Extension of beta R is beta O, by its defining O-span, so
(beta)=P1^h q0^t. Ideal classes are taken only in O. Passing to V kills
[q0], and the principal class gives hu=0. Since 5V=0 and gcd(h,5)=1,
choose integers a,b with ah+5b=1; then u=a(hu)+b(5u)=0.
The quotient convention is precisely Sharifi, *Iwasawa Theory*, Chapter
1, [author HTML](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~sharifi/notes/iwasawa-ch01.html),
Definition 1.3.19, as specialized in audited L045. Neither this source
nor ideal extension supplies beta or its existence.

The displayed principal ideal has zero order away from S, and eleven
orders h,0,0. Thus [beta] is in K(S,5). Both local vectors survive by
L045. Explicitly choose an integer a with ah=2 modulo five: beta^a
has vector (2,0,0), while 11^2/beta^a has vector (0,2,0), because
L041 gives rho([11])=(1,1,10)=(1,1,0) modulo five. Negative a is allowed
in K*, without inverting any ideal in R. Only q0 occurs in (1), whereas
L045 permits all prime-five exponents and arbitrary fifth-power ideal
factors elsewhere. The implication is sufficient and not necessary.

### Exact finite verification interface

Use the fixed coordinates (1,k,...,k^11) and multiplication matrix T
of k. The full generator matrices are [11I|T-8I] and [5I|T]. Build
each power and mixed product from every pairwise product of basis
columns, reducing by the entire m. L058's lattice argument shows this
Z-span is the literal ideal product. For each compression from G to
a twelve-column B, verify integer X,Y with GX=B and BY=G, nonzero
det(B), and integer C with TB=BC. These certify the complete full-rank
R-ideal without adding saturation or maximal-order closure.

Reduction supplies only candidates. With basis columns transposed to
rows, check the integer transform and integer inverse in both directions,
determinant +/-1, and every exact Gram–Schmidt size and Lovasz inequality
at delta=3/4. Test exactly the twelve resulting rows and bi+bj,bi-bj
for every i<j, 144 candidates per pair. For a nonzero beta form its
entire multiplication matrix M_beta. Verify integer C_beta such that
M_beta=B C_beta. Acceptance requires an integer reverse inclusion
M_beta D_beta=B as well. If |det(C_beta)|>1, this candidate is a proper
sublattice: a reverse inclusion would force C_beta D_beta=I and hence
integer determinant +/-1. Therefore such rejection uses the complete
inclusion, rather than a norm-only test. No finite rejection decides
principality or u.

Full product, transform and candidate matrices must be saved and replayed
independently. The scripts under `scripts/power-order-mixed-five-eleven/`
implement this interface for the sixteen approved positive pairs only.
The computation's outcome below is separate from the conditional
mathematical implication.
