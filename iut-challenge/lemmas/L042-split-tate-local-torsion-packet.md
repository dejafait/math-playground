# L042 — Full torsion and exact degrees of a split Tate local packet

## Hypotheses

Work in a fixed algebraic closure of Q_3, with absolute valuation v_3(3)=1. Let zeta_60 be primitive, H=Q_3(zeta_60), q=3^30, and E_q/H the standard untwisted split Tate curve. For a finite extension K of Q_3, write e(K/Q_3) and f(K/Q_3) for its ramification index and residue degree. Let N=H(E_q[7]) denote the field generated by the coordinates of **all** geometric 7-torsion points. Roots mentioned below lie in this fixed closure; changing a primitive root does not change its field.

## Conclusion

All geometric 30-torsion of E_q is H-rational. For any alpha with alpha^7=3^30,

N=H(zeta_7,alpha)=Q_3(zeta_420,3^(1/7)).

Here

e(H/Q_3)=2, f(H/Q_3)=4, [H:Q_3]=8;
e(N/Q_3)=14, f(N/Q_3)=12, [N:Q_3]=168;
e(N/H)=7, f(N/H)=3, [N:H]=21.

In particular this packet passes the tested local full-30-torsion prerequisite and has ramification divisible by 7. This does not construct global initial data, satisfy L041's global hypotheses, identify native inputs/actions or demonstrate an essential IUT flaw. The separate 2026-10-10 critical audit below validates this unchanged local conclusion.

## Proof

### Imported inputs and qualifications

Import named Tate uniformization, as stated Galois-equivariantly in [Kedlaya, Introduction: the Tate curve](https://kskedlaya.org/18.727/tate-curve.pdf), fall-2004 author handout, Theorem 1, p.3, hypotheses p.2 and proof discussion pp.3–4. For every finite extension K of H it identifies E_q(K) with K^times/q^Z, compatibly with Galois actions. The handout defers surjectivity to Silverman V.3.1/V.4; no reproof of uniformization is claimed. Use the **standard** Tate coefficients in [Milne, Elliptic Curves, second edition (2021)](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf), III.3, (24)–(26), pp.102–103; the differing extracted a4 expression in Kedlaya is not imported. Theorem 2 of Kedlaya, p.4, supplies split multiplicative reduction. Here q is nonzero and |q|_3<1, as required. Finite-extension uniformizations give the algebraic-closure statement by taking their union. There is no restriction that the torsion order be prime to residue characteristic. The field has characteristic zero; Milne EC2 II.6.3, pp.70–71, also gives geometric n-torsion of rank two for every positive n in this characteristic.

For local degrees use [Milne, Algebraic Number Theory, v3.08 (2020-07-19)](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf), Theorem 7.38, Corollary 7.42 and Remark 7.43, pp.123–125 (completeness, extension valuation and degree=e f); Proposition 7.50, Corollaries 7.51–7.52 and Example 7.54, pp.127–129 (unramified/residue correspondence and prime-to-3 cyclotomic fields); Proposition 7.55, pp.129–130 (Eisenstein total ramification). Finite p-adic fields and their finite residue fields meet the perfectness/completeness assumptions. Root lifting and exact order preservation are also covered by [Clark, Algebraic Number Theory II](https://plclark.github.io/PeteLClark/Expositions/8410FULLL.pdf), 161-page version accessed 2026-10-08, Proposition 2.30, p.76. These general results are imported; the following computations only specialize them. No general Kummer irreducibility theorem is needed.

### Every torsion class and equality of the coordinate field

Fix n>=1 and t with t^n=q. In the multiplicative quotient a class [u] is killed by n exactly when u^n=q^m for some integer m. Write m=kn+b with 0<=b<n. Then (u/(q^k t^b))^n=1. Thus every torsion class is [zeta_n^a t^b], 0<=a,b<n, and every such class is killed by n. These n^2 classes are distinct: equality makes the quotient a q-power; its valuation is (b-b')v_3(q)/n, forcing b=b' and that q-power to be 1, then a=a'. This accounts for the entire torsion, not merely a cyclic subgroup.

For n=30 choose t=3. Since H contains mu_30 and 3 belongs to H, every representative is in H, and uniformization over H makes every 30-torsion point H-rational. This includes the 3-primary torsion; reduction modulo 3 is never used.

For n=7 choose t=alpha. Put M=H(zeta_7,alpha). All representatives lie in M, so N is contained in M. To prove the reverse inclusion, compare Galois kernels over H. An element sigma of Gal(Q_3-bar/H) fixes all torsion coordinates exactly when it fixes all torsion points. By Galois equivariance, in particular it must fix [zeta_7] and [alpha]. The first condition says sigma(zeta_7)/zeta_7 is in q^Z; this quotient has valuation zero, so it is 1. The second says sigma(alpha)/alpha is in q^Z; its seventh power is 1 since sigma fixes q, so again its valuation is zero and it is 1. Thus sigma fixes both generators of M. Conversely fixing both generators fixes every displayed torsion class and hence all coordinates. The two pointwise-fixing subgroups coincide, and the fixed-field correspondence gives N=M. Both are finite separable fields: M is generated by roots of two finite polynomials, while N has finitely many algebraic torsion coordinates. This argument proves equality rather than one containment or a subgroup-field approximation.

### Cyclotomic stages

Write U_m=Q_3(zeta_m) for m prime to 3. Prime-to-3 root lifting identifies U_m as the unramified extension whose residue Frobenius orbit has length ord_m(3). For m=20 the successive powers are 3,9,7,1 modulo 20; hence U_20 has degree and residue degree 4. Since 60=3*20 with coprime factors, extracting powers of zeta_60 gives primitive third and twentieth roots, and Bezout recovers zeta_60 from them. Therefore H=U_20(zeta_3). If x=zeta_3-1 then x satisfies x^2+3x+3=0. Over U_20 the uniformizer is 3; this polynomial is Eisenstein. Its degree-two extension is totally ramified, giving e(H/Q_3)=2 and f(H/Q_3)=4.

For m=7 the powers of 3 are 3,2,6,4,5,1 modulo 7, so ord_7(3)=6. The prime-to-3 compositum U_20(zeta_7) equals U_140: roots of coprime orders 20 and 7 generate a primitive 140th root, and its powers recover each field. Equivalently, the unramified/residue correspondence gives compositum degree lcm(4,6)=12. Explicitly ord_140(3)=lcm(ord_20(3),ord_7(3))=12 by the Chinese remainder theorem. No assumption of linear disjointness is made.

Put B=H(zeta_7)=U_140(zeta_3)=Q_3(zeta_420). The same translated polynomial is Eisenstein over unramified U_140. Consequently B has absolute e=2 and f=12, with degree 24. H is a subfield, and the valuation and residue towers yield e(B/H)=1, f(B/H)=3 and [B:H]=3.

### Radical stage, without an Eisenstein shortcut

The absolute valuation of alpha is 30/7. The normalized integer valuation on B is v_B=2v_3, so its extension takes v_B(alpha)=60/7. In a finite extension N/B of ramification index r, the extended v_B has value group (1/r)Z. Since gcd(60,7)=1, the presence of alpha forces 7 to divide r. On the other hand alpha satisfies X^7-3^30 over B, so [B(alpha):B]<=7. The degree=e f theorem gives r<=[B(alpha):B]<=7. Thus r=7, [N:B]=7 and f(N/B)=1. This proves the requisite irreducibility and total ramification for this particular polynomial; it is not claimed to be Eisenstein over B.

Finally set theta=alpha^4/3^17. Then theta^7=3^(120-119)=3 and theta^30=alpha^(120)/3^510=alpha, using alpha^119=3^510. Hence B(alpha)=B(theta), giving the stated root field. The tower now gives e(N/Q_3)=7*2=14 and f(N/Q_3)=1*12=12, so [N:Q_3]=168 and [N:H]=21. It also gives e(N/H)=7 and f(N/H)=3. All these are exact degrees, not merely necessary divisibility bounds.

### Separate critical audit — 2026-10-10

This audit reuses the approved primary-source coverage above and checks
every specialization independently. It imports named Tate uniformization;
it neither claims a new proof of that theorem nor treats the unread
Silverman chapter as inspected. The standard untwisted curve is essential:
no descent or twisting identification is being assumed.

For full torsion, take an arbitrary algebraic representative u. The
equation u^n=q^m and Euclidean division of m (including negative m)
give the displayed representatives. If two representatives differ by
q^k, then (b-b')/n=k. Since |b-b'|<n, k=0 and b=b'; their root-of-unity
factors are then equal. There are exactly n^2 distinct classes. Every
algebraic point and representative occurs over some finite extension,
so the finite-extension uniformizations suffice for the algebraic
closure. In characteristic zero this applies to n=30 as well as n=7;
no prime-to-3 torsion restriction or reduction argument has entered.
For n=30, t=3 and mu_30 subset H prove rationality of every point.

For the coordinate field, fixing every point fixes both [zeta_7] and
[alpha], not merely their generated subgroup as a set. For either
generator its quotient with its conjugate is a root of unity, so has
valuation zero. The only member of q^Z of valuation zero is 1 because
v_3(q)=30 is nonzero. Conversely, fixing the two generators fixes all
49 classes. The coordinate field's absolute Galois fixing subgroup is
therefore exactly that of M. Both fields are finite separable, so
fixed-field correspondence proves N=M with both containments justified.
An x-coordinate-only field would not support this argument; N includes
both coordinates of every point, as stipulated.

For the cyclotomic stages, the displayed powers show exact orders 4
and 6, since neither list reaches 1 earlier. The Chinese remainder
theorem gives order 12 modulo 140, hence unramified degree 12. This
does not multiply 4 and 6. The coprime-order root identities give H,
B and Q_3(zeta_420) inside the fixed closure, independent of which
primitive roots are chosen. Over both U_20 and U_140, 3 is a
uniformizer: X^2+3X+3 has monic leading coefficient, both other
coefficients divisible by 3 and constant coefficient not divisible by
9. Thus both quadratic stages really are Eisenstein and totally
ramified. In particular, e(H)=e(B)=2, f(H)=4 and f(B)=12; the
relative B/H extension has e=1, f=3 and degree 3.

For the radical stage, v_B(alpha)=60/7 in the extension of the
integer-normalized base valuation. Its value group in N is
(1/e(N/B))Z. The denominator 7 therefore divides e(N/B).
The polynomial bound [B(alpha):B]<=7 and e<=degree force both
quantities to equal 7, with residue degree 1. This argument uses no
unproved disjointness and does not label X^7-3^30 Eisenstein.
The identities theta^7=3 and theta^30=alpha follow respectively from
alpha^28=3^120 and alpha^119=3^510. Hence both radical-field
inclusions hold. A different seventh root of 3 differs by an element
of mu_7 already in B, so the stated field is independent of that
choice as well.

Finally the tower gives absolute degrees 8, 24 and 168 for H, B and
N, with e(N)=14 and f(N)=12; division by the H invariants gives
e(N/H)=7, f(N/H)=3 and degree 21. Their products agree at every
stage. The auxiliary check v_H(q)=60 is prime to 7 and mu_4 subset H
also survives. ANT 7.42/7.43's labels are corrected above to Corollary
and Remark. No mathematical statement or local dependency changed.

The audit passes exactly this local result from known theorems. It
does not establish algebraic/global realization of the local curve,
the global F/F_0 degree condition, selected-place realization,
actual/native inputs or actions, pilot transport, essentiality or
the original finite B>=A comparison. Earlier exact-field exclusions
remain intact. Full matching Mathlib coverage remains unchecked.
