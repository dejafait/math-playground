## Conclusion

One consistent reciprocity identification defines, for every
alpha in Gamma_G, a full model MLF×-pair reference lift
(alpha,C_alpha). Its restriction c_alpha to U_N is continuous,
preserves T, and induces a continuous Z_3-linear automorphism
h_alpha of the genuine rank-168 lattice J. These lifts and actions
respect composition and inverses. Their Q_3-linear extensions to N
are exactly the source's reconstructed additive actions under the
same reciprocal identification. In particular

    h_alpha(log u)=log(c_alpha(u))  (u in U_N),
    h_alpha(J)=J,                 h_alpha(I)=I.

Torsion is retained in the full pair and in c_alpha; it is divided
out only in U_N/T -> J. No integral presentation basis, field
coordinates, residual irreducibility, orbit radius, actual native
permitted-action identification or input/pilot membership is asserted.
This is a REPRODUCTION of a known method on the retained field,
not progress beyond the cited results or a demonstration of an IUT flaw.

**One choice for both unit objects.** Let U_rec be the reconstructed
fixed-field units, U_bar,rec the reconstructed algebraic-closure
units, and V_rec the reconstructed additive space. Denote the source
identifications by

    r: U_N -> U_rec,        R: M_bar -> U_bar,rec,
    F_add: N_+ -> V_rec.

The maps r and F_add are those of Proposition 3.11; R is built
from these same reciprocal maps at finite subextensions of Omega
using Definition 4.1 and Proposition 4.2. Let i:U_N -> M_bar
and j:U_rec -> U_bar,rec be the base-field inclusions. In particular

    R i = j r.

Explicitly, for each finite N<=L<=Omega let r_L identify U_L with
the reconstructed units of G_L=Gal(Omega/L). For L<=L', write
t_(L,L') for the transfer-induced transition on these units.
Lemma 1.7(iii) gives

    t_(L,L') r_L(u)=r_(L')(u),    u in U_L,

where u on the right is its actual field inclusion into L'. The
maps r_L therefore induce R on the union M_bar=union_L U_L.
The term L=N occurs in this system, and r_N=r. Its canonical
map to the direct limit is j, proving R i=j r at that term.
Each transition is injective by this same identity and injectivity
of field inclusion, so j is injective. Lemma 1.7(iv) identifies
conjugation on this system with the G-action on M_bar, proving
the equivariance of R used below. No choice of a lift for an
individual alpha is involved in constructing R.
Let ell:U_rec -> V_rec be the natural rationalization map. The
fixed-field logarithm diagram says F_add log=ell r, with codomain
V_rec. Its image on the left is F_add(J), not all F_add(I).

For alpha in Gamma_G, functorial reconstruction gives maps B_alpha
on U_rec, Bbar_alpha on U_bar,rec, and A_alpha on V_rec, satisfying

    Bbar_alpha j = j B_alpha,       A_alpha ell = ell B_alpha.

Concretely alpha maps the term indexed by an open subgroup H to
the term indexed by alpha(H), by the isomorphism alpha|_H.
Naturality of transfer gives a well-defined map on the direct
limit. At H=G this is B_alpha, which proves the first identity.
Rationalization of B_alpha proves the second. Composition of these
term maps is the term map for the composite, and the identity
induces the identity; their inverses are induced by alpha^(-1).
Conjugation by g is transported to conjugation by alpha(g), so
Bbar_alpha is equivariant with that change of the G-action. Define

    C_alpha = R^(-1) Bbar_alpha R.

Equivariance of R and reconstruction gives
C_alpha(gx)=alpha(g)C_alpha(x), so this is a full model pair lift.
The fixed sets of M_bar for G equal U_N. Surjectivity of alpha
and the same assertion for alpha^(-1) show C_alpha(U_N)=U_N.
More precisely, using the inclusion identities gives

    C_alpha i = i r^(-1) B_alpha r.

Thus c_alpha=r^(-1)B_alpha r. It is continuous with continuous
inverse, since r is a topological isomorphism and B_alpha is the
map induced by the continuous abelianized Galois automorphism on
the reconstructed inertia image. This argument requires no topology
on completed algebraic-closure units. Keeping R fixed for every
alpha gives C_(alpha beta)=C_alpha C_beta and
C_(alpha^(-1))=C_alpha^(-1) directly from functoriality.

**Torsion and the genuine integral image.** By Lemma 1.2(i,v),
ker(log:U_N -> N)=T, and U_N/T is topologically isomorphic to
Z_3^168. Here the finite 3-primary factor is allowed and has not
been set to zero. L042's primitive zeta_420 has a power of order
three, so that factor is indeed nontrivial. Its exact order is not
needed. As a group isomorphism, c_alpha preserves every element
order and hence T, in both directions. Consequently

    [u] -> log u: U_N/T -> J

is a well-defined continuous bijection. The source's unit structure
and logarithm continuity make its domain compact; J lies in the
Hausdorff space N. Thus it is a topological isomorphism. In
particular J is compact and abstractly a free rank-168 Z_3-module.
Its Z_3 structure agrees with scalar multiplication in N: this is
true for integers by additivity of log and hence for Z_3 by
continuity: if integers n_m tend to a in Z_3, then n_m[u] tends
to a[u] in U_N/T, while n_m log(u) tends to a log(u) in N.
This argument uses powers on the quotient, not a choice of roots
in U_N. Lemma 1.2(iv)'s rationalized isomorphism shows J spans
N over Q_3, so J is a full lattice in N. No presentation vector
has been used as an integral basis vector in this argument.

Put h_alpha=F_add^(-1) A_alpha F_add on N. It is Q_3-linear
and invertible by the cited reconstructed additive action. The
choice identities give, for every u in U_N,

    F_add log c_alpha(u)
      = ell r c_alpha(u) = ell B_alpha r(u)
      = A_alpha ell r(u) = A_alpha F_add log u.

Injectivity of F_add proves h_alpha(log u)=log c_alpha(u).
The torsion check above independently verifies that this formula
does not depend on the representative u. Hence h_alpha(J) is
contained in J. Applying exactly the same formula to alpha^(-1)
gives h_alpha^(-1)(J) contained in J, and therefore h_alpha(J)=J.
The restriction is continuous and Z_3-linear, either from the
quotient identification or from its Q_3-linear extension to the
finite-dimensional field N. Composition and inverse compatibility
hold because they hold for A_alpha. Finally Q_3-linearity gives
h_alpha(J/6)=h_alpha(J)/6=J/6. This does not divide torsion
elements by 6 inside the multiplicative pair.
