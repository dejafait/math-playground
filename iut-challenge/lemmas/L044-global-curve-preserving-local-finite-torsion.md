# L044 — A global curve preserving the local finite-torsion packet

## Hypotheses

Fix the algebraic closure of Q_3 used in L042 and an embedded primitive
zeta_60. Set k=Q(zeta_60), let v be its induced 3-adic place, and
identify k_v with H=Q_3(zeta_60) inside that closure. Retain the standard
untwisted Tate curve E_q with q=3^30 and
N=Q_3(zeta_420,3^(1/7)) from L042. For any elliptic curve over H,
H(E[7]) means the embedded field generated by both coordinates of
every geometric 7-torsion point, not merely x-coordinates or a subgroup.

## Conclusion

There exists a nonsingular elliptic curve E_0/Q such that, for
E'=E_0 tensor_Q k,

E'_H[30] subset E'_H(H), and H(E'_H[7])=N

as fields inside the fixed algebraic closure. Indeed its full
210-torsion is isomorphic to that of E_q as a G_Q_3-module before
restriction to G_H. This is an existential known-theorem
specialization; no explicit congruence precision or coefficient tuple
is obtained. The separate 2026-10-11 critical audit below validates
this unchanged existential conclusion.

No global rational 30-torsion over k, exact-curve realization, global
initial theta data, permitted input/action identification, pilot
transport, essentiality or original finite B>=A comparison is asserted.

### An integral squarefree cubic over Q_3

Write Milne's standard equation over Q_3 as

y^2+xy=x^3-b_2(q)x-b_3(q).

The b_i here are Milne's series labels in (24), not the usual
Weierstrass b-invariants. Both series have integer coefficients and
zero constant term. Since q=3^30, they converge to elements of Z_3.
The resulting pointed curve is the standard E_q used by L042, now
viewed over Q_3 before base change to H. Its discriminant is

Delta=q product_(r>=1)(1-q^r)^24 != 0.

Indeed the factors lie in 1+3^30 Z_3 and approach 1 with increasing
valuation of their differences; the convergent product is a unit.
In particular the curve is nonsingular.

The Q_3-linear projective coordinate change
[X:Y:Z] -> [X:Y+X/2:Z] fixes O=[0:1:0] and is invertible.
In affine coordinates put t=y+x/2; it gives

t^2=f(x), where f(x)=x^3+(1/4)x^2-b_2(q)x-b_3(q).

Thus f is monic of degree three in Z_3[x]: 2 and 4 are units in Z_3.
It is squarefree. Otherwise a repeated root r in the algebraic
closure would satisfy f(r)=f'(r)=0, making (r,0) a singular affine
point of t^2=f(x), contrary to the invertible change from E_q.
The projective cubic has its unique point at infinity O; the partial
derivative with respect to Z of
T^2 Z-X^3-(1/4)X^2 Z+b_2 XZ^2+b_3 Z^3
equals 1 at [0:1:0]. Hence no singularity at infinity has been hidden.
Its smooth projective curve C_f is the pointed genus-one curve E_q
under this coordinate change, and satisfies Theorem 2.2's hypotheses.

### Integer approximation with degree retained

Apply Theorem 2.2 with ell=3 and m=210. Choose its precision M
large enough also to retain squarefreeness, as permitted by its proof.
Choose integers c_2,c_1,c_0 representing respectively the residue
classes of 1/4,-b_2(q),-b_3(q) in Z_3/3^M Z_3, and set

g(x)=x^3+c_2 x^2+c_1 x+c_0 in Z[x].

Residue representatives exist by the definition of Z_3; no
approximation over a larger coefficient field is required.
Its leading coefficient is exactly 1, so deg(g)=deg(f)=3 and
g-f belongs to 3^M Z_3[x]. The theorem gives a squarefree g and

J(C_g)[210] congruent to J(C_f)[210]

as G_Q_3-modules. This also shows directly that the local smooth
projective model has the same genus one.

Define E_0/Q by the projective cubic

T^2 Z=X^3+c_2 X^2 Z+c_1 XZ^2+c_0 Z^3,

with O=[0:1:0]. Squarefreeness over Q_3 implies that the nonzero
polynomial discriminant of g in Q remains nonzero over Q-bar.
In characteristic zero the affine singular-point equations again
require a common root of g and g', so no affine singular point occurs.
The partial derivative in Z at O is 1; O is the only point at
infinity. The cubic is geometrically irreducible: g(x) cannot be a
square in Q-bar(x), since its pole at infinity has odd order three,
so T^2-g(x) is irreducible there and hence also in Q-bar[x,T].
The standard smooth-plane-curve genus formula (Milne I.4, (6),
p.32) gives genus
(3-1)(3-2)/2=1. Thus this is a nonsingular pointed genus-one curve,
and hence an elliptic curve E_0/Q. Its Q_3 base change is C_g.

The pointed Jacobian identifications above and the pointed coordinate
change from C_f to E_q convert the theorem's conclusion into a
G_Q_3-equivariant abelian-group isomorphism

phi:E_0(Q_3-bar)[210] -> E_q(Q_3-bar)[210].

The point at infinity is defined over the respective base field, so
these identifications are Galois-equivariant, not just geometric
group isomorphisms. No assumption about the approximation preserving
the original infinite q-parameter or j-value has been made.

### Restriction and the full embedded coordinate field

Base change E_0 to the specified embedded k and then to its completion
H. The result E'_H is E_0 tensor_Q H.
The completion identification is compatible with the fixed embedding:
the closure of k in H contains Q_3, since Q is dense in Q_3,
and contains zeta_60, hence contains Q_3(zeta_60)=H. Conversely k
lies in the complete field H, so its closure is contained in H.

Regard
G_H=Gal(Q_3-bar/H) as the actual subgroup of G_Q_3 in this fixed
closure; phi remains equivariant after restriction to this subgroup.

For each n dividing 210, the subgroup killed by n inside E[210]
is exactly E[n]: a point killed by n is killed by 210, and the
reverse inclusion in this description is definitional. Every
abelian-group isomorphism commutes with multiplication by n.
Consequently phi restricts to G_H-equivariant isomorphisms of the
complete 30-torsion and 7-torsion groups. This uses all torsion at
both levels, including the 3-primary part of 30-torsion.

L042 shows that G_H acts trivially on E_q[30]. For P in E'_H[30]
and sigma in G_H, equivariance gives
phi(sigma P)=sigma phi(P)=phi(P), so sigma P=P. Hence all these
points are H-rational. In affine coordinates, fixed geometric
points have both coordinates fixed and therefore in H; O is already
H-rational.

For any sigma in G_H, equivariance and bijectivity on 7-torsion give

sigma fixes every point of E'_H[7]
  if and only if sigma fixes every point of E_q[7].

For either curve this pointwise-fixing subgroup is exactly the
subgroup fixing the field of all torsion coordinates: fixing both
coordinates fixes an affine point, and conversely fixing every point
fixes its coordinates; O contributes no extension. These coordinate
fields are finite separable extensions of H, generated by the
finitely many algebraic coordinates. The fixed-field correspondence
therefore identifies them inside the chosen closure. L042 gives
H(E_q[7])=N, whence H(E'_H[7])=N as embedded fields.
Neither an abstract field isomorphism nor equality of degrees is
being substituted for this kernel argument.

### Separate critical audit — 2026-10-11

The verdict is a pass for the stated existential finite-torsion result,
with no changed mathematical conclusion. The audit uses the pre-existing

`drafts/literature/2026-10-11-finite-torsion-global-curve-critical-audit.md`.
The primary Theorem 2.2, its complete proof and Notation 1.5 were

**Model and signs.** The inspected Milne III.3 (23)–(26) agree with the
signs used above. Integral coefficients and zero constant terms give
v_3(b_i(q))>=30: every positive-degree term has valuation at least 30,
and the valuations tend to infinity. For the discriminant product,
v_3((1-q^r)^24-1)>=30r; tails of its partial products tend to 1.
Its limit lies in 1+3^30 Z_3, so it is a unit and Delta is nonzero.
Expanding (y+x/2)^2 gives y^2+xy+x^2/4, hence exactly the displayed
f with its positive x^2/4 coefficient and negative b_i coefficients.
The homogeneous transformation has determinant one and fixes O.
A repeated root of f would give both affine partial derivatives zero
at (r,0); this contradicts smoothness of E_q. At infinity, Z=0
forces X=0 and T is nonzero, giving precisely O, where the Z-partial
is 1. Thus no projective singularity is lost by completing the square.

**Approximation and global curve.** The theorem is applied to that
actual Q_3 model, not to an arbitrary H-coefficient polynomial. Each
nonleading coefficient has an integer residue representative modulo
3^M, and the leading coefficient remains 1. Increasing the supplied
precision retains the module isomorphism and guarantees squarefreeness.
For the resulting integral g, its discriminant is a rational number
nonzero in Q_3, hence nonzero over any characteristic-zero extension.
The same affine derivative test and the Z-partial at O prove geometric
smoothness of E_0. The odd pole order of g at infinity excludes a
square in Q-bar(x), proving irreducibility of T^2-g(x). Its projective
homogenization has no extra component at infinity: such a component
would require a factor Z, excluded by the term -X^3. The geometric
curve is therefore irreducible. Milne I.4 (6), applied over Q-bar,
gives genus one; O is Q-rational. These facts justify an elliptic
curve E_0/Q and its stated smooth Q_3 model, rather than merely an
affine or geometrically disconnected curve.

**Pointed Galois modules.** Milne I.4 Proposition 4.10, including its
injectivity, surjectivity and group-law proof, and the p.36 Galois
passage apply to both smooth genus-one curves. The geometric Jacobian
points are their degree-zero divisor classes, and the base-rational
O makes P -> [P]-[O] equivariant. The pointed Q_3 coordinate change
also preserves this identification and hence the elliptic group law.
Consequently the imported Jacobian isomorphism does give the claimed
G_Q_3-module isomorphism on all 210-torsion. No compatible polarization,
chosen torsion basis or isomorphism of curves is needed for this claim.

**The actual completion and restricted kernels.** The closure of the
embedded k in H contains Q_3 and zeta_60, and is contained in H;
it equals H. Thus the restriction is to Gal(Q_3-bar/H) in the fixed
closure. For n=30 and n=7, both dividing 210, the subgroup killed
by n in the 210-torsion is the full n-torsion. The group isomorphism
carries these subgroups bijectively and remains G_H-equivariant.
Trivial action on L042's entire 30-torsion therefore transfers to
E'_H[30], including its 3-primary part. Fixed affine points have both
coordinates in H since the fixed field of G_H is H.

For 7-torsion, write rho_0 and rho_q for these restricted actions.
The same equivariant bijection gives
rho_0(sigma)=identity if and only if rho_q(sigma)=identity for every
sigma in G_H. Each kernel is exactly Gal(Q_3-bar/K_i), where K_i is
the embedded field of both coordinates of every 7-torsion point on
the corresponding curve. Indeed a sigma fixes the generators of K_i
exactly when it fixes every such point. These are finite separable
extensions of H, so equality of those subgroups gives equality of
K_i by the fundamental theorem of Galois theory. L042 identifies the
second with N. Neither a normalizer, a setwise-stabilized subgroup,
a single torsion point nor equality of extension degrees is used.
This independently checks the load-bearing embedded-field inference.
