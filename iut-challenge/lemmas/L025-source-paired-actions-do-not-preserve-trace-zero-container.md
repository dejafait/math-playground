## Proof

### Exact integral transport of the source words

Set e_i=y_(2i), f_i=y_(2i+1), 1<=i<=13. These are a Z_3-basis of the actual M by L024, not an arbitrary choice of coordinates in K. L019's reference lift acts on each log(u_j) by log(rec_K^(-1)(alpha_ab([x_j]))). Abelianizing the source words therefore gives precisely the following actions on M:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
u_i:&f_i\mapsto f_i+e_i,\\
v_i:&e_i\mapsto e_i-f_i,\\
w_i:&e_i\mapsto e_i-f_i+f_{i+1},\quad
      e_{i+1}\mapsto e_{i+1}+f_i-f_{i+1}.
\end{array}
\]

Every unlisted basis vector is fixed. In particular the long word for b_i under phi''_i abelianizes to b_i: both a_(i+1) and b_(i+1) contributions cancel. The two other long words give respectively a_i-b_i+b_(i+1) and a_(i+1)+b_i-b_(i+1). This checks all affected cases, including each end of the chain. Products and inverses correspond to products and inverses of these actions because reciprocity conjugation and descent through log are functorial. Continuity on J gives Z_3-linearity by limits of integer multiples; since J spans K, the extension to K is unique.

Thus these actions preserve M integrally and act on V. Let Gamma be the subgroup they generate in GL(V). No larger mapping-class or full symplectic image is assumed. On M each generator has determinant one: u_i and v_i are elementary shears; w_i fixes all f's and adds their combinations to e's, giving identity diagonal blocks in the ordering of all e's followed by all f's. Their inverses also preserve M.

### Irreducibility of the exact residual subgroup

Reduce this basis and these formulas modulo 3. Let W be a nonzero Gamma-invariant F_3-subspace of M/3M. Choose a nonzero vector

\[
z=\sum_i A_i\bar e_i+B_i\bar f_i\in W.
\]

If some B_i is nonzero, (u_i-id)z=B_i bar(e_i) belongs to W, so bar(e_i) belongs to W. If all B_i vanish, some A_i is nonzero, and (v_i-id)z=-A_i bar(f_i) belongs to W, so bar(f_i) belongs to W. Applying v_i to bar(e_i), or u_i to bar(f_i), and subtracting the original vector shows that both bar(e_i) and bar(f_i) belong to W for this pair.

If i<13, apply w_i-id to bar(e_i). Its value is -bar(f_i)+bar(f_(i+1)); subtracting the already available term gives bar(f_(i+1)), and then u_(i+1) gives bar(e_(i+1)). If i>1, apply w_(i-1)-id to bar(e_i). Its value is bar(f_(i-1))-bar(f_i); this gives bar(f_(i-1)) and then bar(e_(i-1)). Iterating along the finite chain reaches all 13 pairs. Hence W=M/3M, proving irreducibility of this paired subgroup itself. No generation theorem for a larger symplectic group is used.

### Residual irreducibility forces same-space scalar homothety

Here is the known criterion's required direction with its lattice obligations explicit. Let N be any Gamma-stable full Z_3-lattice in V. Full lattices are commensurable: in bases of N and M, clear the finitely many denominators in the change-of-basis matrix and its inverse. Choose the least integer a such that N'=3^a N is contained in M. Such integers exist and are bounded below by the reverse inclusion; minimality implies N' is not contained in 3M, since otherwise 3^(a-1)N would be contained in M.

The nonzero image of N' in M/3M is Gamma-stable. By the preceding irreducibility it is the full quotient, so M=N'+3M. The finitely generated Z_3-module Q=M/N' therefore satisfies Q=3Q. Nakayama's lemma over the local ring Z_3, whose maximal ideal is (3), gives Q=0. Thus N'=M, or N=3^(-a)M as subsets of the same V. This supplies exactly scalar homothety, not just an isomorphism of abstract modules.

For compatibility with the cited compact-representation formulation, the closure of Gamma in GL(M)=GL_26(Z_3) is a compact profinite group. Evaluation on V is continuous. If a closed full lattice N is Gamma-stable, it is also stable under this closure: approximate an element and its inverse by Gamma, and use closedness of N. Both M and C are closed full lattices, being finite free Z_3-lattices in the finite-dimensional Q_3-space V. The direct proof above already works for Gamma, so no closure enlarges the required group assertion.

### The actual field lattices are not homothetic

L019's power-basis trace calculation gives Tr(sum_(r=0)^26 c_r pi^r)=27c_0. Together with L016's O=Z_3[pi], this identifies

\[
D=\bigoplus_{r=1}^{26}\mathbb Z_3\pi^r,
\qquad C=3^{-4}D.
\]

In particular these are full closed lattices in V. Let P_13:K->Q_3 be the coefficient of pi^13 in this power basis. L016 gives the COMPLETE equality J=H+sum_(j=1)^13 Z_3 q_j, H=pi^14 O. The pi^13 coefficient of H lies in 3Z_3. In its full table only q_1 and q_13 have nonzero pi^13 coefficient, each equal to 1. Thus P_13(J) is contained in Z_3, not merely so for a sampled sublattice. Moreover

\[
q_{13}=\pi^{12}+\pi^{13}\in J\cap V=M
\]

has P_13(q_13)=1. Consequently P_13(M)=Z_3. Also P_13(D)=Z_3. If M=3^k D for an integer k, applying P_13 would give Z_3=3^k Z_3, forcing k=0.

But the other exact representative

\[
q_4=\pi^4+\pi^8+(\pi^9+\pi^{12}+\pi^{24})/3

belongs to J by L016's proved complete-image and H-valued-error argument. Its constant coefficient is zero, so q_4 belongs to M. Its pi^9 coefficient is 1/3, so it does not belong to D. Therefore M is not D, and the only possible scalar homothety has been excluded. M is not homothetic to D or to C. These explicit representatives are genuine logarithms; no reciprocal field coordinates for the y_i have been assigned.

Here arbitrary Q_3 scalar homothety reduces to the displayed powers of 3: every nonzero scalar is 3^k times a Z_3-unit, and multiplication by such a unit fixes each Z_3-lattice as a set. Thus the coefficient comparison excludes all scalar homotheties.

### Noninvariance and an attained existential field witness

If C were Gamma-stable, the known criterion just proved would make C homothetic to M, contradicting the exact field comparison. Thus C is not Gamma-stable. If every displayed forward generator preserved C with equality, their inverses and composites would also preserve C, yielding that contradicted stability. At least one forward generator h therefore has h(C) unequal to C.

For this generator, even containment h(C) subset C is impossible. Indeed such containment gives an integral matrix in the displayed Z_3-basis {3^(-4)pi^r:1<=r<=26} of C. Its determinant is the basis-independent determinant of h|V, which is one by the source-word matrices above. Its inverse is integral by the adjugate formula, so containment would imply equality. Hence h(C) is not contained in C.

If all 26 basis vectors of C had images in C, Z_3-linearity would imply h(C) subset C. At least one basis vector c=3^(-4)pi^r therefore has image outside C. Reference trace preservation ensures h(c) is in V. Since C=3^(-4)O intersect V and O consists exactly of field elements of valuation at least zero, this failure is exactly v_3(h(c))<-4. This proves the asserted attained existential witness, without identifying its r or h in field coordinates.

**Qualification.** The contradiction decides only invariance of this valuation container. A point in C need not belong to pi I or to the smaller literal logarithm/ideal union. In particular the conclusion does not contradict L020's tested-map enclosure, does not realize L022's chosen shear, and does not revive the excluded involution of L018–L019.
