# L090 — Every surviving member's ordered native field has index four

## Hypotheses

Keep the fixed integers c,h,d_*,e_*, the original monic family, fixed
closure Q-bar inside Omega, and both domains of audited L089. A
survivor here means any original member E/Q counted there: its short
coefficients satisfy A>0 and B odd in one of L089's rectangles, and

In particular no named member from L087 is substituted for this E.

For any ordering o=(j_1,j_2,j_3) of the three distinct roots r_1,r_2,r_3

    D_o=r_(j_2)-r_(j_1), t_o=(r_(j_3)-r_(j_1))/D_o,
    E_o: V^2=X(X-1)(X-t_o),
    G_o=Q(t_o)(iota,E_o[15]), iota^2=-1.

and IUT IV Proposition 1.8/Theorem 1.10. Normal closures are over Q

    F_mod=Q,
    F_tpd=Q(E[2])=Q(r_1,r_2,r_3)=Q(t_o),

### An arbitrary member and its geometric native object

Fix one arbitrary survivor E. L089 gives

    j(E)=1728*4A^3/(4A^3+27B^2), 0<j(E)<1728.

Thus j(E) is rational and differs from both exceptional values. Its
pointed geometric automorphism group is exactly {+1,-1}. The original
monic cubic is separable by L089. Put K=Q(r_1,r_2,r_3)=Q(E[2]); the
equality follows because its nonzero two-torsion points are precisely
(r_m,0), with rational origin. K/Q is a finite Galois splitting field.

Fix any ordering o. Distinctness gives D_o nonzero and t_o different
from 0,1. Choose s_o with s_o^2=D_o in Q-bar, without requiring it
to belong to H. The actual mutually inverse projective changes are

    [x:y:z] -> [(x-r_(j_1) z)/D_o:y/(D_o s_o):z],
    [X:V:Z] -> [r_(j_1) Z+D_o X:D_o s_o V:Z].

Their matrices are invertible and fix [0:1:0]. Substitution gives
g(r_(j_1)+D_o X)=D_o^3 X(X-1)(X-t_o). They therefore give a pointed
geometric group isomorphism phi_o:E -> E_o by the cited Weierstrass
theorem, including the entire torsion sets in both directions. No
Q(t_o)-rational isomorphism is asserted.

Both projective curves are nonsingular and geometrically irreducible.
An isomorphism of their geometric punctured opens, or of any conjugate
such opens, extends uniquely to the projective completions by Milne's
theorem over Q-bar. Extending the inverse shows these extensions are
inverse. They map the unique omitted point to the unique omitted
point; conversely a pointed isomorphism restricts to the opens. Thus
the unmarked punctured moduli object is exactly the pointed elliptic
one here. E/Q is a descent of this same object for every o. The
native j-field description gives F_mod=Q.

Since its pointed automorphisms are plus and minus one, both trivial
on E[2], IV 1.8(iii) makes the two-torsion action independent of the
chosen rational descent. We may therefore compute the native field
on the actual E/Q, obtaining F_tpd=Q(E[2])=K in this embedding.

To identify Q(t_o), first t_o belongs to K. If sigma in Gal(K/Q)
fixes t_o, extend sigma to Q-bar. The two orderings of E[2] given
by (r_(j_1),r_(j_2),r_(j_3)) and their sigma-images have the same geometric
coarse ordered parameter. Antieau–Meier's result supplies a pointed
geometric automorphism of E carrying the first ordering to the
second. It is plus or minus one and hence fixes every two-torsion
point. Thus sigma fixes all roots and is the identity. The stabilizer
of t_o in Gal(K/Q) is trivial, so finite Galois correspondence gives
Q(t_o)=K. This holds for each ordering without an equivariant rational
Legendre section. It proves the two asserted native-field equalities.

### This member's finite division ambient field

Temporarily label the arbitrary roots 1,2,3 and choose radicals

    alpha^2=r_2-r_1, beta^2=r_3-r_1, gamma^2=r_3-r_2.

Choose a primitive zeta_60 with zeta_60^15=iota and set

    k=K(zeta_60),
    M=K(iota,alpha,beta,gamma,E[15]).

All signed difference radicals belong to M, as reversing a difference
multiplies a radical by a sign of iota. Yelton's general theorem
applies to this monic separable cubic and gives

    Q(E[4])=K(iota,alpha,beta,gamma).

It follows that M=Q(E[4],E[15])=Q(E[60]). For the last equality,
one inclusion follows from E[4],E[15] subset E[60]. Conversely, for
P killed by 60, [45]P is killed by four, [16]P is killed by fifteen,
and their sum is [61]P=P. The Q-defined group law puts both
coordinates of all such P in M. Thus M/Q is finite Galois.

L089 supplies the actual full level-120 image of this original E.
CRT and surjective reduction GL_2(Z/8) -> GL_2(Z/4) give

    Gal(M/Q)=GL_2(Z/4) times GL_2(Z/15).

Reduction is onto because any entrywise lift of an invertible
mod-four matrix has odd determinant. This uses the joint image,
not a product inferred from unrelated marginal images. Write an
element (p,b). Fixing K means p=Id modulo two; fixing zeta_60 means
det(p)=1 modulo four and det(b)=1 modulo fifteen. Hence precisely

    U=Gal(M/k)=P_4 times SL_2(Z/15),
    P_4={Id+2X: X in M_2(F_2), tr(X)=0}.

Here det(Id+2X)=1+2tr(X) modulo four, and multiplication adds X.
Thus P_4 has eight elements and is elementary abelian.

Every p in P_4 fixes the roots and iota, giving three radical-sign
characters chi_alpha,chi_beta,chi_gamma. Their combined map into
{+1,-1}^3 is injective: trivial signs mean fixing every generator
of Q(E[4]), hence identity on full E[4]. Equal orders make the map
bijective. Radical signs depend only on p because the radicals are
in Q(E[4]); the full product image realizes every sign triple
jointly with every b in SL_2(Z/15). No generic independence or
recorded cyclic Frobenius is used.

### Complete pointwise kernels for all six rows

For h=(p,b) in U, put chi_o(p)=h(s_o)/s_o. It fixes every root and
D_o. Applying h to phi_o changes its y-denominator by chi_o(p).
Consequently, in the basis transported by this actual pointed map,
the action on full E_o[15] is

    rho_o(h)=chi_o(p)b.

Indeed h(phi_o(P))=[chi_o(p)]phi_o(h(P)) for every P, including
all torsion points. This checks the imported twist action on the
actual coordinates. Fixing both coordinates of every E_o[15] point

    H_o=Gal(M/G_o)={(p,b) in U: b=chi_o(p)Id}.

This includes both uniform scalar sectors and excludes nonscalars
as well as CRT scalars with opposite signs at three and five. Both
uniform scalars have determinant one and are distinct modulo 15.

The six denominator differences are plus and minus alpha^2,beta^2,
gamma^2, each unordered root pair occurring twice. Reversing a
difference multiplies its square root by a sign of iota, fixed by U,
so it does not change the character. Thus exactly three subgroup
types occur, denoted H_alpha,H_beta,H_gamma, given by the respective
character in the displayed formula. The eight possible sign triples

    +++, ++-, +-+, +--, -++, -+-, --+, ---.

In each H there is one element per triple, its b forced by that
column, so |H|=8, with four elements in each scalar sector. In any
pair of columns equality holds for four triples, giving pairwise
intersection order four. All three columns agree only for +++ and
---, giving common intersection order two. In particular the three
subgroups are distinct. This is the complete finite count.

### Every conjugate and the normal-closure threshold

    [M:G_o]=8, [M:G_o^nor]=2,
