## Proof

### Torsion and the genuine all-unit logarithm

Put U_1=1+pi O. There is no primitive third root zeta_3 in L.
Indeed eta=zeta_3-1 satisfies the Eisenstein polynomial
eta^2+3eta+3=0 over Q_3, so v_3(eta)=1/2. This is the
Eisenstein valuation conclusion of **Pete L. Clark, Algebraic Number
Theory II**, inspected 161-page author-hosted version,
**Proposition 2.9/Corollary 2.10**, p. 65
([primary notes](https://plclark.github.io/PeteLClark/Expositions/8410FULLL.pdf)),
also used for L031's model. But 1/2 is outside (1/27)Z. Hence
zeta_3 cannot lie in L. Any nontrivial 3-power root of unity would
give one of order three by taking a power, so none lies in L.
The total degree 108 is not used to exclude a quadratic subfield.

The principal-unit filtration makes U_1 pro-3: each finite quotient
is a 3-group, the quotients U_j/U_(j+1) are the additive residue field
F_81, and the intersection is {1}. These are L031's cited filtration
inputs. A finite-order element of U_1 therefore has 3-power order.
For completeness, its finite cyclic subgroup is separated by these
quotients; since it is finite, one quotient is injective on that subgroup,
so its order divides a power of 3. The preceding exclusion shows
that U_1 is torsion-free.

L031's prime-to-3 lifting input gives O^times=mu_80 times U_1,
with trivial intersection. Log is zero on mu_80. Its kernel on all
units is exactly torsion: choose a positive integer m annihilating
the finite quotient O^times/U_14, where U_14=1+pi^14 O. If log u=0,
then u^m lies in U_14 and has logarithm zero. The inverse deep
log/exp theorem in L031 forces u^m=1. Conversely a torsion unit
has zero logarithm because log is a homomorphism into a characteristic
zero additive group. It follows that log:U_1 -> J is bijective.
It is a topological isomorphism by compactness and Hausdorffness.
Continuous Z_3-powers correspond to scalar multiplication: integer
powers give the equality, and both sides are continuous in their
Z_3 parameter. All residue-unit directions are accounted for by mu_80;
the old decomposition with only two signs is not used.

### Full presentation and integral generation

Local reciprocity identifies the maximal abelian pro-3 quotient A of
G_L with the pro-3 completion of L^times. The usual decomposition
L^times=pi^Z times mu_80 times U_1 therefore gives

    A = Z_3 direct-sum U_1 = Z_3 direct-sum J.

In particular A is torsion-free, and the projection of G_L^ab onto
A is injective on the reciprocal U_1 factor. This follows because
pro-3 completion kills mu_80 and is the identity on the compact pro-3
group U_1. A uniformizer is used only for this abstract decomposition,
not to prescribe the images of the presentation generators.

Apply maximal abelian pro-3 quotient to the full admitted presentation.
Before imposing relations the free module has symbols S,T,X_0,...,X_108.
The tame relation gives T=81T, hence 80T=0 and T=0 since 80 is a
Z_3-unit. The fixed word x'_0 has abelianized image aT+bX_0 for
a,b in Z_3. The wild relator has no S term and its commutators vanish.
Thus its remaining relation is cX_0-3^s X_1=0, with c=1-tb. Precisely,

    A = (Z_3 S direct-sum direct-sum_(i=0)^108 Z_3 X_i)
        / Z_3(cX_0-3^s X_1).

This is the closed integral relation submodule, not its saturation.
Abelianization sends the closed normal subgroup generated by the
defining relators to the closed module generated by their images.
The displayed relation span is closed, being a continuous image of
compact Z_3. There are no other relations: assignments to any finite
abelian 3-group with T=0 and cX_0=3^sX_1 satisfy both full defining
relators, hence yield continuous homomorphisms by the presentation.
Conversely every such homomorphism satisfies them. Finite quotients
separate elements of the compact displayed module, proving the equality.

Necessarily c is a Z_3-unit. Otherwise the nonzero relation vector
v=cX_0-3^s X_1 is divisible by 3. Let k=min(v_3(c),s)>=1,
with v_3(0)=infinity, and w=v/3^k. Its class is killed by 3^k but
is nonzero: w=dv for d in Z_3 would force 1=3^k d by any nonzero
coordinate. This contradicts torsion-freeness of A. This reasoning
includes c=0 because the X_1 coefficient is nonzero.

Consequently X_0=(3^s/c)X_1 in A. Since x_i have reciprocal images
in U_1 and the projection is injective there, applying log gives
y_0=(3^s/c)y_1 in the actual J. In particular this is an integral
elimination, not a rational-span calculation.

The wild reciprocal image is exactly U_1. Indeed Lemma 1.7(i)
identifies the inertia image with O^times, while Lemma 1.5(ii)
identifies I_L/P_L with a product of Z_l for primes l different from 3.
The image of P_L in O^times is a closed pro-3 subgroup, hence is
contained in U_1. The quotient of O^times by that image is a continuous
quotient of I_L/P_L and therefore has only prime-to-3 finite quotients.
The image of U_1 in this quotient is pro-3, so is trivial: its finite
quotients would have both 3-power and prime-to-3 order, and finite
quotients separate elements of a profinite group. Thus the image
contains U_1 as well. Compactness of P_L ensures its image is closed.

Normal topological generation of wild inertia by the x_i now implies
topological generation of its abelian reciprocal image U_1 by the u_i:
conjugation disappears in abelianization. Thus y_0,...,y_108
topologically generate J. The map Z_3^109 -> J sending (a_i) to
sum a_i y_i has compact, hence closed, image. It contains the integer
sums and their closure, so its image is exactly J. Eliminating y_0
now shows J=sum_(i=1)^108 Z_3 y_i. These 108 generators span L
because J is full by L031; dimension 108 forces Q_3-linear independence,
and hence Z_3-linear independence. They are a basis of the entire J.
No finite logarithm samples or scalar extension of the old basis are used.
