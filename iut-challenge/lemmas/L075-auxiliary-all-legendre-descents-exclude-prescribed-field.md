# L075 — Every literal auxiliary Legendre descent excludes the prescribed field

## Hypotheses

Fix the same auxiliary witness E=E_aux/Q and embedded number field
F=F_aux of L074, in the same algebraic closure Q-bar. Thus

    E: y^2=g_aux(x)=product_(m=1)^3(x-r_m),
    F=Q(zeta_60,E[30]),

with distinct r_m in F and the origin at infinity. Division fields
contain both coordinates of every geometric torsion point. Retain
L074's one actual tau in G_F and its six ordered-root equations
E_ijk. Set a=r_2-r_1, b=r_3-r_1, lambda_0=b/a. In particular
i=zeta_60^15 belongs to F and i^2=-1.

Let T be any number field contained in this fixed F. Let

    E_L/T: V^2=X(X-1)(X-lambda), lambda in T, lambda!=0,1,

with origin at infinity, be geometrically pointed isomorphic to E.
The equation is literal and monic: no additional quadratic multiplier
is included. All geometric points and fields use the fixed Q-bar.
No rationality of E_L[15] over F is assumed.

## Conclusion

The F-base change of E_L is exactly one of L074's six equations,
with the same infinity origin. The same tau acts as -Id on its full
15-torsion. Consequently

    T(i,E_L[15]) is not contained in F,
    F != T(i,E_L[15]).

These assertions hold for every T and literal pointed Legendre
descent in the hypotheses. This is an auxiliary admissibility screen
by known methods, validated by the separate step296 critical audit
below. It is not an IUT flaw, a retained-witness verdict or a complete
candidate resolution.

### Exhaustion and the actual base change

All fields have characteristic zero. The separability of g_aux and
lambda!=0,1 make both Weierstrass curves nonsingular. Since T is
inside F inside the fixed Q-bar, a geometric pointed comparison can
be taken over that same algebraic closure. Apply Milne's converse
to an isomorphism E_L -> E. It has the form

    x=u^2 X+r, y=u^3 V+s u^2 X+t, u!=0,

with coefficients initially only in Q-bar. Both equations have
Weierstrass coefficients a_1=a_3=0. The coefficient identities and
invertibility of 2 therefore force s=t=0. Thus substitution gives

    g_aux(u^2 X+r)=u^6 X(X-1)(X-lambda).

It follows that r, r+u^2 and r+u^2 lambda are three distinct roots
of g_aux: they correspond to the three distinct roots 0,1,lambda
of the right side. They exhaust its roots, so for an ordering (i,j,k)
of (1,2,3) they are r_i,r_j,r_k, respectively. Therefore

    u^2=r_j-r_i=d_ij,
    lambda=(r_k-r_i)/(r_j-r_i)=t_ijk.

This is a necessary specialization of the imported coordinate theorem.
It does not assert u in F or descent of the comparison isomorphism.
Indeed L074 excludes each required square root from F.

In L074's row order, the six possible t_ijk are exactly

    lambda_0, 1/lambda_0, 1-lambda_0, 1/(1-lambda_0),
    (lambda_0-1)/lambda_0, lambda_0/(lambda_0-1).

Here (lambda_0-1)/lambda_0=1-1/lambda_0, so this list matches
the cited general-field orbit with no missing value. Every denominator
is nonzero. L074 supplies v_3(j(E))=-30, hence nonexceptional j;
no exceptional-automorphism hypothesis is silently changed. The root
assignment argument itself does not need a distinctness claim about
the six parameter values.

Now extend E_L from T to F using the actual inclusion T subset F.
Its coefficients, coordinate symbols and infinity origin are unchanged.
Since lambda=t_ijk, its equation over F is literally E_ijk's equation.
The identity on X,V is consequently a pointed F-isomorphism between
these F-curves. This is equality of the target equations, not descent
of the geometric E_L -> E comparison. The full geometric 15-torsion
sets and G_F-actions agree under this identity: the equations and
group laws define the same multiplication-by-15 map in Q-bar.

### Full-coordinate field contradiction

For that row, L074 proves that the same tau in G_F acts as -Id
on E_ijk[15] in a transported full basis. By the identity just checked,
this is also its action on E_L[15]. Choose a basis point Q of exact
order 15. Then tau(Q)=-Q!=Q: otherwise 2Q=0, contradicting its
order. Such Q is affine, since it is not the origin. Thus at least
one of its two affine coordinates is moved by tau. An x-coordinate
field alone could miss this test; the full-coordinate field cannot.

Put S=T(i,E_L[15]) inside the fixed Q-bar. If S were contained
in F, all coordinates of every E_L[15] point would be in F.
As tau fixes F pointwise, it would fix Q, a contradiction. Hence
S is not contained in F, which in particular excludes equality.
The inputs T subset F and i in F have been checked; neither requires
T to be Galois or F_tpd, and no enlargement of F is made.
