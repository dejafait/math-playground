\[
S_A=\sum_{h\in\Gamma}\mathbb Z_3h(A)
=27\mathbb Z_3y_1\ \oplus\ 3^{-1}M,
\qquad
S_B=\sum_{h\in\Gamma}\mathbb Z_3h(B)

The fixed line is the actual presentation-defined line Q_3 y_1; these embedded span descriptions retain it. Nevertheless, for every admitted presentation the actual finite-word orbit minima are exactly -7/3 and -10/3, respectively. Hence

\[
\min_{h\in\Gamma,\ x\in(A\cup B)\setminus\{0\}}

\qquad
\operatorname{hull}(\Gamma(A\cup B))=\pi^{-90}O.

The sharp radius is 3^(10/3), strictly smaller than 81=3^4, and independent of the admitted presentation. The hull means the smallest centered closed field valuation disc, not equality of the orbit and its span or its hull. Attainment is existential by actual finite words and actual literal inputs; no explicit word attaining the radius is identified.

## Proof

### A finite source word supplies integral projections

By L024, J=Z_3 y_1 direct-sum M and K=Q_3 y_1 direct-sum V. On the ordered basis (e_i,f_i), L025's source maps have matrices

\[
U=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
W=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Here U is u_i and W is v_i; composition u_i v_i means apply v_i first. Direct multiplication gives

\[
UW=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
(UW)^2=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
(UW)^3=-\mathrm{Id}_2.
\]

Both maps fix every other pair and y_1. Therefore the finite word

\[
s=\prod_{i=1}^{13}(u_i v_i)^3\in\Gamma
\]

fixes y_1 and is -Id on V. The factors have disjoint supports in this actual basis and commute as linear maps; any fixed increasing order defines the required source word. No assertion about generating a larger symplectic group is needed. Set P=(Id+s)/2 and Q=(Id-s)/2. They satisfy P+Q=Id, P^2=P, Q^2=Q and PQ=QP=0. On K, P projects onto Q_3 y_1 with kernel V, and Q projects onto V with kernel Q_3 y_1. In particular P(J)=Z_3 y_1 and Q(J)=M.

For either E=A or E=B let S_E be its ordinary orbit span, consisting of finite integral linear combinations of h(x), h in Gamma, x in E. Left multiplication by any group element permutes these summands, so s(S_E)=S_E. Since 1/2 belongs to Z_3, both P and Q preserve S_E. Thus

\[
S_E=P(S_E)\oplus Q(S_E).
\]

Indeed every element is the sum of its two projected elements in S_E, and their intersection is zero by the complementary vector subspaces. This verifies the integral preservation obligation for the known idempotent splitting method. It does not average over the infinite group Gamma, and neither P nor Q is asserted to be a group action or to preserve E itself.

### Fixed-direction normalization from the complete logarithm image

Write c_r(x) for the coefficient of pi^r in x, 0<=r<=26. L025 gives Tr(x)=27c_0(x). By the complete equality in L016, J=H+sum_(j=1)^13 Z_3 q_j. The nonzero constant coefficients in its table are 16/9,1/3,7/3,1,1 at j=1,2,3,6,9. All other constant coefficients vanish, and c_0(H)=3Z_3. Consequently

\[
c_0(J)=3^{-2}\mathbb Z_3.
\]

Every q_j has c_26(q_j)=0, while c_26(H)=Z_3, since pi^26 belongs to H. Hence c_26(J)=Z_3 as well. These equalities use the full image, including the proved logarithm tails; they are not conclusions about a sampled sublattice.

Put a=c_0(y_1). Since M has zero constant coordinate and J=Z_3 y_1 direct-sum M, the first equality forces a Z_3=3^(-2)Z_3. Thus v_3(a)=-2. L016 bounds every element of J below by -2. The distinct fractional parts of the power-basis summand valuations imply v_3(y_1)<=v_3(a)=-2, so in fact v_3(y_1)=-2. This does not choose y_1 or its nonconstant coefficients.

The projection formula is P(x)=(c_0(x)/a)y_1. Every h in Gamma preserves trace and fixes y_1, hence Ph=P on all K. Since E is a Z_3-module, applying P to the finite orbit span therefore gives P(S_E)=P(E): each projected finite sum lies in P(E), and the identity group element gives the reverse inclusion.

Multiplication by pi changes the constant coordinate to 3c_26. Consequently c_0(A)=3Z_3 and c_0(B)=9c_26(J)=9Z_3. The fixed projections are exactly

\[
P(S_A)=(3/a)\mathbb Z_3y_1=27\mathbb Z_3y_1,
\qquad
P(S_B)=(9/a)\mathbb Z_3y_1=81\mathbb Z_3y_1.
\]

For these equalities, 9a is a Z_3-unit and multiplication by its inverse fixes Z_3 as a set. The minima of the two fixed projected lattices are therefore 3-2=1 and 4-2=2.

### Determine the other projections without interchanging section and projection

The whole ideal branch satisfies 3A=3pi O=pi^28 O subset H subset J, so A subset J/3. The whole logarithm branch satisfies

\[
9B=27\pi J\subseteq\pi^{82-54}O
=\pi^{28}O\subseteq H\subseteq J,
\]

using L016's J subset pi^(-54)O and pi^27=3. The exponent 28 exceeds the required 14. These are ideal bounds, with no O-module assumption on J. Because every reference action preserves J, they imply S_A subset J/3 and S_B subset J/9. Applying Q, whose action on J has image M, gives

\[
Q(S_A)\subseteq M/3,\qquad Q(S_B)\subseteq M/9.
\]

For the opposite inclusions, L027 gives the exact ordinary spans of the input sections A intersect V and B intersect V, namely M/3 and M/9. These sections are subsets of their respective full inputs, so their spans lie in S_A and S_B. They also lie in V, where Q is identity. Therefore M/3 subset Q(S_A) and M/9 subset Q(S_B). Both projected-span equalities follow. We have not identified E intersect V with Q(E), nor interchanged an intersection and an orbit-span operation. The two inclusions establish precisely what is needed.

Combining these equalities with the already justified integral splitting gives the two claimed full spans. All sums used here are finite; no closure or full-K irreducibility theorem has been applied.

### Exact radius and actual finite-word attainment

L026 proves min v_3(M)=-4/3, attained by m_0=3q_1-16q_2. Thus M/3 and M/9 have minima -7/3 and -10/3. Their respective fixed summands have minima 1 and 2, strictly larger. The nonarchimedean inequality therefore bounds every element of S_A below by -7/3 and every element of S_B below by -10/3; m_0/3 and m_0/9, respectively, show that each span bound is sharp. Cancellation between summands cannot lower these bounds.

For actual orbit attainment, reuse L027's finite-word witnesses on A intersect V and B intersect V. Each restricted word lifts to the same finite word in the full-K source generators, with exactly the same action on V. Those actual section inputs are also actual full inputs, so their image valuations -7/3 and -10/3 attain the full-input bounds just established. This argument does not replace an orbit input by a sum or a projector image, and does not equate an orbit with its span.

Group image distributes over A union B. Its attained minimum is consequently -10/3=-90/27, and its smallest centered field valuation disc is pi^(-90)O. The prescribed radius-81 disc is pi^(-108)O; -90>-108 proves strict containment of the former disc in the latter. The exact radius is 3^(10/3), not merely the previously known upper bound 81. The actual fixed line remains presentation-defined, but its computed valuation contribution proves the same sharp radius for every admitted presentation.
