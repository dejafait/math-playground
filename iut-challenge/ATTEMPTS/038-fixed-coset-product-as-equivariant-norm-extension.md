# Fixed-coset product as an equivariant algebraic-unit norm extension — stopped in proper degree

WHY IT FAILS: [L124](../lemmas/L124-fixed-coset-product-fails-natural-equivariance.md) proves that a product over a fixed transversal of G_p/G_v cannot be equivariant for the same natural G_v-actions on algebraic units modulo all roots of unity unless the transversal is {1}. Local Galois slimness forces a mismatch between the finite lists Th and hT, and the canonical proof turns that mismatch into a finite-normal-field, strict-disc unit whose discrepancy has nonzero logarithm. The quotient by all torsion therefore does not repair this formula. For k=Q_p the identity representative survives, so this is not a universal obstruction at every native place. Ordinary finite norms and other operations, including variable-degree proposals, are outside this stop. No essential IUT flaw or pilot-membership failure follows. Repeating the same fixed-product formula with a different transversal cannot reopen the proper-degree mechanism; a different operation or action is required and needs its own source assessment.
