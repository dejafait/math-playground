The exact smaller-input test at a = 1, beta = 1 tensor 2sqrt(2) and K = Q_2(sqrt(2)) is decided in [L009](../lemmas/L009-smaller-good-place-input-has-sharp-contained-orbit.md). The enlarged-lattice failure in L008 is preserved, but supplies no failure of the smaller-input estimate in this packet.

WHY IT FAILS: the proposed transfer of L008's radius-64 counterexample from beta^(-1) I to beta^(-1) T union J uses an enlargement in the wrong direction. L009 proves a genuine inclusion of the actual input in I/2, preserved by every permitted lattice automorphism, and its sharp whole-group hull remains inside hull(I/4). This excludes a smaller-input counterexample for the complete local group, not merely for selected matrices. Original initial theta data, full Ind3 and pilot/global normalization remain separate unestablished bridges. Stop this fixed-packet objection; the next potential mechanism is a general literal-input lattice inclusion, which needs its own literature scope assessment and is not proved here.
