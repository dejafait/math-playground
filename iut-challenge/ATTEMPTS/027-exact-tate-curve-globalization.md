# Exact L042 curve globalization — audited stop

WHY IT FAILS: [L043](../lemmas/L043-exact-tate-curve-excluded-over-number-fields.md)
applies the inspected p-adic Mahler–Manin theorem to the exact rational
q=3^30. Its standard Tate j-value is transcendental over Q, whereas
every embedded number-field curve has algebraic j. Geometric isomorphism
would identify these values. This rules out globalization of that exact
curve, preserving L042's audited local torsion/degree pass and
ATTEMPTS/022,025,026. It does not rule out a different curve with the
same local torsion field; such a target requires separate assessment.
The separate 2026-10-11 audit in L043 validates the exact application,
including the full product normalization and arbitrary embeddings.
No essential IUT flaw, native realization or original finite B>=A
failure follows. This stop is not reopened by a different-curve
finite-torsion realization target: that target changes the invariant
being retained and requires its own literature assessment.
