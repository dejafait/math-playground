# Local marking existence as a sufficient comparison test

Assessed on 2026-09-25 in the [marked transport review](../drafts/2026-09-25-marked-transport-review.md). The proposed intermediate test was to find structure poly-morphisms from a transported input to the local output determinant and infer the global degree comparison from those markings. The [source record](../foundations/02-comparison-claim-and-sources.md) now distinguishes the local markings from compatible morphisms of local-global collections.

WHY IT FAILS: [L004](../lemmas/L004-local-markings-and-global-degree.md) gives globally defined metrized lines with isomorphism markings at every place, retaining local unit actions and local scalar-extension compatibility, but with reversed degrees. The independent local multipliers have a nonzero total logarithmic defect and cannot be the localizations of a globally admissible morphism. Thus local markings and object localization do not suffice. This is a failure of the weakened test; the example omits the global morphism compatibility retained in the original source and does not demonstrate an IUT flaw. A formal degree comparison need not be realized by the ordinary global scalar used as a sufficient mechanism in the model.

Qualification added on 2026-09-26: omitting a global comparison arrow is different from violating a compatibility axiom imposed on existing arrows. [L005](../lemmas/L005-compatible-marked-categories-and-defect-components.md) puts the example inside a category that enforces that axiom and proves it lies in a different defect component from the identity-marked target. The stopped local-marking sufficiency claim stays stopped; the stronger categorical replacement is assessed in the [subsequent review](../drafts/2026-09-26-compatible-marked-category-test.md).
