WHY IT FAILS: The proposed exclusion required every full model MLF×-pair action, including arbitrary Galois components, to preserve the embedded J intersect Q_3. The inspected reconstruction sources give norm/trace compatibility, but distinguish embedded-line characteristicity and obstruct its universal reconstruction in explicit quadratic/even-degree cases; full-pair lifting does not impose an extra geometricity hypothesis. No degree-27 preservation theorem was found in the inspected sources. Thus this universal source premise cannot presently exclude L022. This is a stop on that inferred justification, not a proof that the degree-27 property is false, not a full-pair realization of the shear, and not a global ban on conditional line-preserving subgroups or genuinely new field-specific evidence. A source-realized reference action is a materially different test.
