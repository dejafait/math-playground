# An uncompensated determinant power as an obstruction

Assessed on 2026-09-25 within the [comparison degree audit](../drafts/2026-09-25-comparison-degree-audit.md).

The restricted proposed objection was that passage from packet volumes to determinant degrees might introduce an extra positive tensor-power factor on the output side alone. [L002](../lemmas/L002-comparison-composite-and-normalized-determinant.md) supplies the exact finite-packet normalization calculation. This is distinct from the non-valuation-preserving power map in ATTEMPTS/001.

WHY IT FAILS: a weighted relative determinant includes the inverse determinant of the reference lattice, and its common tensor exponent multiplies both compared degrees. Dividing by that positive exponent preserves the comparison exactly. Ignoring either correction creates an artificial discrepancy. This does not remove unequal scalings of different objects, prove that the source maps have a linear realization, or control the hull of their permitted images. The restricted shortcut is therefore stopped; the essential input-volume membership question remains open.
