# Identity-Galois full-pair lift of the compact involution

The approved necessary lift test is negative in [L018](../lemmas/L018-fixed-galois-full-pair-lift-is-impossible.md). This is a failure of a proposed realization route for L017's auxiliary witness, not a failure of its lattice automorphism or orbit computation.

WHY IT FAILS: Hoshi's full-pair kernel over identity G_K induces only Z_3-unit scalar actions on the actual logarithm image. The compact involution violates this necessary condition on the genuine logarithm q_1. Thus that exact lift cannot realize the witness. The result does not constrain nonidentity-Galois lifts through the same scalar-kernel argument, or actions on a structure after forgetting the full pair. Those questions require their own source-backed restrictions; original tensor/initial-data, ind3, pilot, essentiality and normalization remain unresolved. Preserve this stop evidence rather than retrying the identity kernel under another coordinate description.
