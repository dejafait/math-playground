Thus F_{Q_3} fails the retained H_E-indexed lattice condition in the fixed ordinary realization of IUT II Example 1.8(iv)/Definition 4.9(i), even though L125 is sound on the bare quotient. Its separate step522 critical audit validates the stop on this proposed same-index Ism use. It does not stop other norm operations, changes of source/target structure or a separately justified marking/index transport; those require their own source-compatible statement and coverage. No native initial-data instance, essential flaw in IUT or finite B>=A failure is proved. Reopening requires a materially different structured operation or explicitly specified transport, not another chosen root or another bare-group equivariance check.
