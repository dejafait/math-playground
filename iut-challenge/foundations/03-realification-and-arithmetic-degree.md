# Realification and arithmetic degree — cited inputs

These are supporting inputs for the marked-localization assessment. They do not settle the status of L005's defect or the IUT comparison claim.

## Hypotheses

For realification, let C be a Frobenioid with perf-factorial divisor monoid Φ, as in Mochizuki, Frobenioids I, Proposition 5.3.

For the arithmetic assertion, take a number field F, a possibly infinite Galois extension F̃/F, and the model Frobenioid of Example 6.3 over B(Gal(F̃/F))⁰, with arithmetic divisors and the multiplicative groups of the finite intermediate fields. Let T be a Frobenius-trivial reference object of C^rlf over such a field L. This includes the trivial extension of ℚ; it does not identify the marked category C_D with a Frobenioid.

## Conclusion

Proposition 5.3 (Realifications of Frobenioids) constructs C^rlf from Φ^rlf and the real span ℝ·Φ^birat of principal divisors. This is a span inside (Φ^rlf)^gp, not the whole divisor space by definition.

Theorem 6.4(i) (Arithmetic Frobenioids) identifies Pic_Φ(T) with ℝ by arithmetic degree. Here Pic_Φ(T) is the divisor quotient of Theorem 5.1(i) (Divisorial Descriptions), which also describes the corresponding object classes. Theorem 6.4(ii) states that equivalences between these global realifications act on the degree lines by a common positive real factor. It does not assert that this factor is always 1.
