# Minimal-field-line source review — saved reasoning

The approved question is whether full model MLF×-pair actions must preserve J intersect Q_3 in the unchanged degree-27 field. No shear evaluation or new lemma is undertaken.

New primary evidence: Hoshi–Nishio's June 2022 revised RIMS1931, Definition 2.1, Lemma 2.4 and Remark 2.5 distinguish preservation of the embedded minimal-field additive line from reconstructed norm/trace compatibility. Lemma 2.4 gives nonpreserving actions for odd residue characteristic and degree two; Theorem 2.7 extends the phenomenon to absolutely abelian even-degree fields. Neither is a degree-27 counterexample. Read the complete relevant proofs, including Lemma 2.6's restriction case. Hoshi's published Theorem 7.6 and proof give full-pair lifts over arbitrary Galois automorphisms and a unit-exponent torsor; the identity-kernel case does not impose geometricity on the other fibers.

The proposed unconditional inclusion test is unsupported by these sources and meets an explicit obstruction to universal functorial reconstruction of the embedded line. This does not settle the property for K=Q_3(pi), pi^27=3 or realize L022. Existing L019 norm/trace coverage is reused; the larger RIMS1882 PDF currently returns internal errors for targeted web accesses, which is recorded rather than treated as new theorem evidence.

A new search lead was Kondo's May 2026 RIMS1993 on odd-degree Galois fields. Theorem C and introductory qualifications have now been inspected: its finite-Galois-field/Lubin–Tate scope does not directly settle the prescribed-shear question; no applicability proof is supplied. This supplies a possible independently testable continuation, not an identification with L022 or a field-disc bound. Its separate assessment is now saved for supervisor approval.
