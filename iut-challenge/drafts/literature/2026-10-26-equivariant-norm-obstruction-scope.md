## Extracted source scope

In [IUT III, Remark 3.9.5(x)](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/Inter-universal%20Teichmuller%20Theory%20III.pdf#page=144), the proposed change replaces arithmetic line bundles over F_mod, or K_v at bad places, by ones over Q or Q_p, with a ramification adjustment. The required setting retains multiplicative structures and the faithful local Galois action; (Ob7) also retains auxiliary unit data. The displayed splitting is a direct-product assertion. It is not a stated theorem characterizing all equivariant maps, nor does the passage specify a general topology or functor axioms for the excluded operation. Its qualification about meaning cannot be replaced by a new universal quantifier over maps invented here.

In [IUT I, Definition 3.1](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/Inter-universal%20Teichmuller%20Theory%20I.pdf#page=61), F/F_mod is Galois and K/F is the finite Galois torsion extension. These clauses do not assert K/Q or K_v/Q_p is Galois. Clause (e) specifies the place section and G_v as a decomposition subgroup of G_K, up to conjugacy. Fixing a lift of the place gives the source inclusion into G_p. Work with the coset set G_p/G_v unless normality is independently supplied. The case k=Q_p must be separated rather than silently ruled out.

In [IUT II, Definition 4.9(i)](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/Inter-universal%20Teichmuller%20Theory%20II.pdf#page=153), the base is the category of connected finite etale covers of an MLF or CAF. Evaluation at a universal covering pro-object A gives units, torsion and their quotients with G=Aut(A)-action. A Kummer structure is an orbit of isomorphisms of ind-topological G-modules. The x-mu version is specified by submodules indexed by every open subgroup, using images of full-unit invariants before taking the torsion quotient; it is not merely the invariant subgroup of the quotient. Parts (ii)–(iv) carry this into split-Kummer Frobenioids. Parts (vii)–(viii), p.158, require constituent isomorphisms, and isomorphisms of the full collections, as prime-strip morphisms. The compatible-surjection description in (v) concerns archimedean places; do not substitute it for the nonarchimedean submodule data.

Thus an equivariant homomorphism alone would be only a necessary preliminary for the particular norm candidate. It would not identify the source prime-strips. Tests on algebraic finite-level units can refute a formula before completion; no identification of algebraic closure and completion, or weakening of ind-topological compatibility, is justified by that observation.

## Inspected known results and applicability limits

- [Stacks, Lemma 31.18.6, tag 0BD2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BD2): a finite locally free morphism of constant degree d>=1 has a canonical determinant norm commuting with arbitrary base change. The proof constructs it by multiplication matrices and checks its vanishing property. A finite extension of local fields meets the finite-free hypotheses, without normality. This imports a norm on the full finite base-change algebra, not a chosen connected factor. [Lemma 31.18.7, tag 0BD3](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BD3), also read, covers finite surjective integral schemes with normal target; it adds no closure extension.
- [Stacks, Lemmas 31.18.2–3, tag 0BCX](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BCX): for a finite morphism with a degree-d norm, there are norms of invertible sheaves and their morphisms; pullback line bundles acquire the d-th tensor power. Read the transition-function construction and local formula for morphisms. This is genuine known categorical support at finite level, but says nothing about the retained Kummer markings or arithmetic metrics.
- [Stacks, Lemma 9.13.4, tag 0CKK](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0CKK): for finite separable k/Q_p and algebraically closed Omega, k tensor_{Q_p} Omega is the product indexed by Q_p-embeddings k->Omega. Its proof uses embedding independence. This is the precise full-packet endpoint. Lemmas 9.13.1 and 9.13.3 give linear independence of distinct monoid characters and field embeddings. These are linear-independence statements, not an automatic multiplicative-independence assertion about products of automorphisms.
- [Mochizuki, The Absolute Anabelian Geometry of Hyperbolic Curves](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~motizuki/Absolute%20Anabelian%20Geometry.pdf#page=6), Definition 0.1(i), p.3, Theorem 1.1.1(ii), p.6: the decomposition group at a nonarchimedean place of a number field is slim, meaning every open subgroup has trivial centralizer in that group. Read the local-class-field-theory proof. Taking the number field Q and its p-place supplies the actual G_p input; no new slimness proof is needed. The garbled RIMS1363 web extraction was only a discovery lead; the readable 45-page author copy supplies the evidence.
- [Conrad, Infinite Series in p-Adic Fields](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/gradnumthy/infseriespadic.pdf#page=27), Theorem 8.5, pp.27–28, gives logarithmic multiplicativity on |u-1|<1. Theorem 8.7, pp.28–29, and Theorem 8.13, pp.30–33, give injectivity and inverse exponential/logarithm on the strict discs |x|<p^(-1/(p-1)), |u-1|<p^(-1/(p-1)), for a complete p-adic field with |p|=p^(-1). Read the proof, including the convergent-tail bound in Theorem 8.13. A finite normal extension used in a later test is complete and meets these hypotheses, including p=2. These statements do not supply an extension of a field norm.
