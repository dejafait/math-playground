## Primary theorem comparison

[Jannsen–Wingberg, *Die Struktur der absoluten Galoisgruppe p-adischer Zahlkörper*](https://epub.uni-regensburg.de/26689/1/jannsen17.pdf), Inventiones Mathematicae **70** (1982), 71–98, DOI 10.1007/BF01393199: read the introduction's presentation and construction method, printed pp. 71–72; §1.2's abstract operator-pro-p construction and parity-dependent relator, pp. 73–75; **Theorem 2 and its complete short proof**, p. 75; and §1.4(a–c), p. 76. Theorem 2 takes a finite p-adic field with p odd, a p-closed extension L, its maximal tame subextension T, the finite p-power roots in T, the associated tame cyclotomic character and its lift, and tame generators satisfying the residue-cardinality relation. It concludes an isomorphism with the abstract presentation constructed in §1.2. For odd absolute degree, that construction retains odd residue degree and its mod-p cyclotomic-character condition, together with the chosen parameters in the special x_1 word. The algebraic-closure case is explicitly §1.4(a). Its proof uses the classification of Demuškin formations with matching invariants; it does not assert compatibility with specified reciprocal images of wild generators. No reproof of that classification or construction is attempted. Only these presentation statements and relevant short proof cases were inspected, not the full technical proof of Theorem 1 in §§2–4.

[Hoshi–Nishio, revised June 2022 text](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~yuichiro/rims1931revised.pdf), §1, PDF pp. 3–5: Proposition 1.1 imposes four explicit generator/parameter conditions, including its nonzero abelian-relation coefficient. Lemma 1.3 proves that the reciprocal images y_1,...,y_d form a **Q_p-basis**, using the integral generating family z_0,...,z_d and a relation between z_0 and z_1. It does not state that y_1,...,y_d form an arbitrary integral logarithm basis. Theorem 1.5's shear fixes the other basis elements and takes y_d to y_d+y_(d-1), with integer powers also specified. L023 already transports precisely this known action; nonidentity and square-zero form alone do not specify its value on pi I. Retain the rational coefficient normalization, the x_0 relation, and the same reciprocal identification. A source basis cannot be replaced by q_1,q_2 or a power basis without a separate argument.

[Kondo, *Anabelian aspects of the outer automorphism groups of the absolute Galois groups of mixed-characteristic local fields*](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/preprint/file/RIMS1991.pdf), RIMS1991, **December 2025**, printed pp. 8–11 (PDF pp. 9–12): read Theorem 1.1's complete presentation statement and source reference, the reciprocal-coordinate setup, Lemma 1.2, **Theorem 1.3 and its proof**, including the degree-two, higher-even and higher-odd cases. Theorem 1.3 assumes only odd p and absolute degree d>=2; it states that the trace kernel is the rational span of y_1,y_3,...,y_d in even degree and of y_2,...,y_d in odd degree. This known rational description is imported as source evidence, without deriving an integral version or a radius consequence. Its proof explicitly exhibits paired generator shears and adjacent-pair automorphisms. Those are genuine group automorphisms in a fixed admitted presentation, not a converse realizing all trace-compatible lattice maps. The higher-odd proof refers to the analogous preceding even-case argument; both the displayed maps and the preceding coefficient-propagation cases were read. No missing integral argument is silently supplied here. The introduction, printed p. 4, explicitly notes that relating these noncanonical generators to ring structure is nontrivial. Its separate Hodge–Tate theorem has Galois/even-degree hypotheses and is not imported for this field. RIMS1991 is distinct from the May 2026 RIMS1993 lead screened earlier.
