Remark 1.10.1.

     (i) The “±-indeterminacy” of Theorem 1.10, (i), (iii), is reminiscent of, but
stronger than, the indeterminacy up to multiplication by a 12-th root of unity of
[Mzk13], Corollary 4.12. Also, we note that from the point of view of the technique
of the proof of loc. cit., applied in the present context of “Tate curves”, it is the
fact that there is a “special 2-torsion point”, i.e., the 2-torsion point whose image
in the special ﬁber lies in the same irreducible component as the origin, that allows
one to reduce the “12 = 2 · (3!)” of loc. cit. to “2”.

    (ii) We take this opportunity to remark that in [Mzk13], Corollary 4.12, the
author omitted the hypothesis that “K contain a primitive 12-th root of unity”.
The author apologizes for this omission.
