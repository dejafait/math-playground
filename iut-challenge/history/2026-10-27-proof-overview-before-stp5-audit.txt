## Known traps checked

- A model-category obstruction is not an IUT refutation; maintain realified/formal source structures and numerical realization.
- Mod-ell kernel equality or abstract generator selection does not prove integral graph, marking, quotient/completion or theta covariance.
- Do not assume downstairs splitting, a field of definition for U, global theta splitting, normality of K_C/Q, or any unproved essentiality/initial-data condition.
- A finite-level decomposition identity alone is insufficient; the separately audited L114 and integral application L121 supply cofinality before inverse limits and distinguish the algebraic closure from its completion.
