# Collatz conjecture

Apply ../GOAL.md as the shared research policy.

## Exact target and traps

For every positive integer n, iterating n/2 when even and 3n+1 when odd eventually reaches 1. Finite computations, density-one results, and probabilistic models do not settle the universal claim.
