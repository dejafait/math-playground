these two nonvanishing checks together cannot exclude every order even in

divisibility supplies no valuation comparison, full cancellation,
integrality, permuted real guards or actual cycle. The prior literal and
single-swapped exclusions and prime-five stops remain valid. The independent critical audit recorded in L060 found no defect in this
fixed-witness diagnostic stop. Any later arithmetic mechanism needs an
independently testable target and appropriate coverage.
