# Attempt 027 — Uniform prime-five exclusion of fixed-triple permutations

## WHY IT FAILS

[L057](../lemmas/L057-fixed-allocation-permutation-prime-five-stop.md)
gives an explicit intact-triple order, obtained by swapping L055's indices
0 and 6, whose unshifted integer affine numerator is 0 modulo 5. The
unchanged denominator is also 0 modulo 5. Thus this prime cannot give
uniform numerator nonvanishing over all orders of this one multiset.
This stops only that diagnostic: no valuation comparison, complete
cancellation, integrality, real-system guards after permutation or actual
cycle is proved. L056's literal-order obstruction, L055's audited real
witness and Attempt 026 remain valid. A different arithmetic diagnostic
would require its own independently testable target and prior assessment;
the present bounded step does not assess or run one.
