## WHY IT FAILS

For each proposed common clock bound, L025 gives an unbounded CRT family
in the surviving cylinder with no smaller residue-20 companion meeting
any allowed forward iterate. Thus no finite base exception or larger
fixed clock repairs this cover. The proof uses a slope threshold and a
partially actual anchor path; the finite screens are supporting checks
only. A clock growing with the root's initial odd-run length is a different,
unassessed mechanism because it can pass the first even exit. It is not
excluded by this obstruction. Neither stop proves nonconvergence.
