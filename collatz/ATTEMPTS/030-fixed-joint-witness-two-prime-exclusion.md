coverage. Integrality, permuted real guards and actual integer realization
remain unproved; the Collatz target and the earlier single-order exclusions
are unchanged. Both complete square evaluations are retained in L061 and
its certificate. The separate critical audit recorded in L061 found no
defect: both complete evaluations and both reduced-pair conclusions were
independently verified. The stopped two-prime route is not reopened.
