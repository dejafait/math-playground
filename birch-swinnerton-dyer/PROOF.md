## Assembly and unresolved gap

The [target scope](foundations/01-target-and-scope.md) is the Clay rank assertion for every elliptic curve E/Q, distinguished from refined BSD. Standard inputs include finite generation, analytic continuation and the analytic-rank-zero/one theorem. For arbitrary analytic order m(E)>=2, equality with rational rank remains unproved.

L001 separates rank from the Tate–Shafarevich defect in finite Selmer sizes. L002 and the cyclotomic inputs retain the additional augmentation defect in characteristic order. Conditional certificates remove specified defects, but uniform production and comparison with m(E), including rational point realization, are missing. Abstract module/tower examples are obstructions to inference, not elliptic-curve counterexamples.

- Midpoint enclosure is not exact-parameter enclosure or strict zero exclusion; independent signed replay and whole-source provenance remain required.
