## Known traps checked

- Finite Selmer depth, even parity, characteristic order and an abstract pairing do not establish the missing rational-rank comparison or full Sha statement.
- Separate conjugations on the tensor factors do not imply alternation; raw CM moments cannot be projected pointwise or treated as certified local classes.
- Midpoint enclosure is not exact-parameter enclosure or strict zero exclusion; independent signed replay and whole-source provenance remain required.
- Numerical success on one fixed curve neither handles arbitrary curves nor establishes refined BSD. Audit status and conditional premises remain local to each canonical result.
