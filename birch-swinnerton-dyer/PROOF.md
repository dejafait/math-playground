# Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture: argument overview
The target is Wiles's official Clay rank assertion for every E/Q. [The scope](foundations/01-target-and-scope.md) separates the stronger refinement. No candidate exists. The [standard inputs](foundations/02-standard-inputs.md) include finite generation, analytic continuation, the analytic-rank-zero/one theorem, and Selmer and Kummer theory.
## Unresolved gap
For arbitrary E/Q with analytic order m(E) >= 2, the missing claim is rank E(Q) = m(E). Finite Selmer sizes have a Tate–Shafarevich defect; cyclotomic characteristic order has an additional augmentation defect. The certificates remove defects conditionally; their uniform production and comparison with m(E) remain missing. Neither the checked generalised Kato constructions nor the [actual derived-class comparison](drafts/literature/2026-10-04-arithmetic-rank-two-exterior-certificate.md) supplies a nonzero rational certificate from m(E) = 2 alone; global point realization remains missing.
## Partial results
L001 gives the exact torsion-corrected size identity
\[\log_p|\operatorname{Sel}_{p^n}|-\log_p|E(\mathbf Q)_{\rm tors}[p^n]|=n\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbf Q)+\log_p|\Sha[p^n]|.\]
Finite-depth abstract towers match ranks zero and two, finite paired Sha, and zero pulled-back pairings. Even parity and r <= 2 do not force r = 2; no elliptic-curve realization or BSD counterexample is asserted.
Using the named theorems in [the cyclotomic foundations](foundations/03-cyclotomic-control.md), L002 proves, at an odd good-ordinary prime,
\[\operatorname{ord}_T f_X=\operatorname{rank}E(\mathbf Q)+\operatorname{corank}_{\mathbf Z_p}\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[p^\infty]+\operatorname{length}_{\Lambda_{(T)}}(T X_{(T)}).\]
The last term vanishes exactly when T X_(T) = 0. Control gives rank <= Selmer corank <= characteristic order. Abstract modules with ideal (T^4) have specialized dimensions two and four, both even; no arithmetic realization is asserted.
L003 applies [Schneider--Perrin-Riou](foundations/04-cyclotomic-height-criterion.md): finite p-primary Sha and a nonzero canonical cyclotomic regulator give ord_T f_X = rank E(Q), hence T X_(T) = 0. Equality with rank is equivalent to finite p-primary Sha and the natural isomorphism X_(T)[T] -> X_(T)/T X_(T). The converse retains finite Sha and non-CM E; no uniform hypotheses are supplied.
L004 uses [Kato divisibility](foundations/05-kato-divisibility.md) for non-CM E at good-ordinary p >= 5. Given n independent rational points and a rigorously nonzero T^n coefficient of the primitive ordinary L_p,
\[n\le r\le\operatorname{ord}_T f_X\le\operatorname{ord}_T L_p\le n.\]
Both defects vanish, p-primary Sha is finite, and the cyclotomic height is nondegenerate. With k < n points, only (r-k)+s+delta <= n-k follows. Neither n = m(E) nor uniform availability of this certificate is established.
