# L028 — Conditional prime-twist normalization and uniform symbol denominators

## Hypotheses

Let E/Q be non-CM, given by a global minimal Weierstrass equation,
with minimal discriminant Delta and conductor N. Let p>=5 be good
ordinary. Let f be the normalized weight-two newform associated to E.
Use the primitive untwisted trivial branch and kappa(gamma)=1+p of
[the ordinary conventions](../foundations/05-kato-divisibility.md).
Write Omega(E)=integral_(E(R)) |omega_E| for the **total** real Neron
period, where omega_E is the minimal differential.

Grant the following certified data; this result does not produce them.

1. A complete finite integral presentation of the entire torsion-free
   relative weight-two symbol module M=M_2(Gamma_0(N)). It specifies
   oriented integral Manin generators g_1,...,g_s and all relations:
   M=Z^s/R_sat, where R_sat={v in Z^s: kv in R for some k>=1} and R
   is generated by the full Manin relations. A symbol {a,b} is oriented
   from a to b. The identification includes arbitrary cusp paths,
   rather than only absolute cycles or an unspecified rational lattice.
2. A nonzero rational functional phi on M_Q=M tensor_Z Q, supplied by
   exact rational generator values x_i=phi(g_i) satisfying all relations.
   A certified rational line U=Q phi in the full relative dual has real
   extension containing the real integration functional
   I({a,b})=Re(2 pi i integral_a^b f(z) dz). Thus I belongs to U tensor_Q R.
   The certification identifies integration for this f on **all of M**;
   a software plus label, a finite list of matching Hecke eigenvalues,
   or identification only on absolute homology does not suffice.
   No scalar relating I and phi is supplied or assumed rational.
3. A prime ell congruent to 1 modulo 4, coprime to p Delta, and an exact
   rational q with an independent proof of
   q=L(E^ell,1)/Omega(E^ell) != 0. Here E^ell denotes the minimal
   quadratic twist and its period also integrates over the full real
   locus. Certification of q must not use the unknown scalar of phi.

All coordinate changes, relation witnesses and cusp orientations are part
of the presentation evidence. Direct values on its integral generators
are used below; rational-basis coordinates must be converted to those
values exactly, with every coordinate denominator retained. Independent
certification of the real line and of q remain substantial hypotheses.
Neither BSD, finite Sha, positive rational rank, Manin constant one,
optimality of E, nor nonvanishing of L(E,1) is assumed.

## Conclusion

Let chi be the quadratic character modulo ell, and compute the integral
symbol and the exact rational number

\[
G_\ell=\sum_{a=1}^{\ell-1}\chi(a)\{\infty,a/\ell\}\in M,
\qquad d=\phi(G_\ell).
\]

Then d!=0. The exact Neron-normalized real functional is

\[
\boxed{F=\frac qd\phi=\frac{I}{\Omega(E)}.}
\tag{1}
\]

In particular, the symbols in L027's convention are exactly
S(r)=F({infinity,r}) for every r in Q. Put y_i=(q/d)x_i, and, omitting
zero y_i from the second set, define the finite nonnegative integer

\[
\boxed{C=\max\bigl(\{0\}\cup\{-v_p(y_i):y_i\ne0,\ 1\le i\le s\}\bigr).}
\tag{2}
\]

Then F(M) is contained in p^(-C) Z_p, so
v_p(S(r))>=-C for **every** rational r. This certifies both the exact
symbol normalization and all-level symbol-denominator premises of L027
from the stated finite data and certification evidence.

No actual q, presentation, numerical C, quadratic coefficient or rank
bound is supplied. L027 still requires its finite working data and the
strict nonvanishing test min(n-1-C,M-C)>v_p(b); L026 retains all its
other hypotheses. Uniform availability, rational Kummer membership and
comparison with the complex vanishing order remain unresolved.

## Proof

**Imported inputs and specialization.** Use Stein, *Modular Forms, a
Computational Approach*, online General Modular Symbols, Definition 1.23,
[Proposition 1.24 and Theorem 1.25](https://www.wstein.org/books/modform/modform/modular_symbols.html).
These give integral generation and the complete Manin presentation after
torsion removal for a finite-index subgroup and integer weight k>=2.
Gamma_0(N) has finite index and k=2 has constant polynomial factor, so
the cited statements apply to the full relative M here. The presentation
and continued-fraction generation theorem are imported, not reproved.

Use Cremona, *Algorithms for Modular Elliptic Curves*, corrected second
edition, [Section 2.11, pp. 38--39, the identity preceding (2.11.4)](https://johncremona.github.io/book/fulltext/chapter2.pdf#page=32).
For odd ell not dividing N, with chi(-1)=1, it gives
the oriented period of sum chi(a){0,a/ell} as
sqrt(ell)L(f tensor chi,1). Our ell is odd; good reduction at ell
follows from ell not dividing Delta, and therefore ell does not divide
N. The associated quadratic twist has L(f tensor chi,s)=L(E^ell,s).
We use the supplied q, not the source's twist-search existence discussion.

Use Pal, *Periods of quadratic twists of elliptic curves*,
[arXiv:1012.0094v2, 21 January 2011, Corollary 2.6 and Theorem 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1012.0094v2).
For a minimal E and square-free positive d, the total periods satisfy
Omega(E^d)=u_tilde Omega(E)/sqrt(d). Corollary 2.6 gives u_tilde=1
when d is coprime to Delta and d=1 modulo 4. Thus in precisely our
prime case

\[
\Omega(E^\ell)=\frac{\Omega(E)}{\sqrt\ell}.
\tag{3}
\]

No negative-twist sign or unrestricted minimalization factor is imported.

**Path orientation and exact scale.** Since chi is nontrivial,
sum_(a=1)^(ell-1) chi(a)=0: multiplication by a nonsquare permutes the
nonzero residues and negates the sum. Path addition gives
{infinity,a/ell}={infinity,0}+{0,a/ell}. Consequently G_ell equals
Cremona's sum chi(a){0,a/ell} in M, with the same orientation.
Taking the real part of the cited period identity, using its positive
real sqrt(ell), gives

\[
I(G_\ell)=\sqrt\ell L(E^\ell,1),\qquad
\frac{I(G_\ell)}{\Omega(E)}
=\frac{L(E^\ell,1)}{\Omega(E^\ell)}=q.
\tag{4}
\]

The right side is nonzero, so I is nonzero. By the certified line
hypothesis, I=beta phi on M_R for some nonzero real beta. Evaluating
at G_ell proves beta d/Omega(E)=q. Hence d!=0 and
beta/Omega(E)=q/d, proving (1) on the entire relative module.
This also proves that the normalized scalar is rational; its rationality
was not an extra premise. All evaluations of d can be obtained from
integral continued-fraction expressions for the finitely many paths in
G_ell. Relation checks make the result independent of the chosen expression.

To match L027's plus convention explicitly, write
A(r)=2 pi i integral_infinity^r f(z) dz. The real Fourier coefficients
of f give f(-conjugate(z))=conjugate(f(z)); substitution in a cusp path
gives A(-r)=conjugate(A(r)). Cusp convergence justifies the endpoint
limits; the same identity holds for the truncated paths before passage
to those limits. Therefore

\[
\operatorname{Re} A(r)
=-\pi i\left(\int_r^{i\infty}f(z)dz
                  +\int_{-r}^{i\infty}f(z)dz\right)
=\lambda^+(r).
\tag{5}
\]

This is the definition in Stein--Wuthrich, *Algorithms for the arithmetic
of elliptic curves using Iwasawa theory*, Math. Comp. 82 (2013),
[Section 3, p. 1762](https://wstein.org/papers/mcom2649-iwasawa-alg.pdf#page=6).
Thus S(r)=lambda^+(r)/Omega(E)=F({infinity,r}), including the source's
[0]^+=L(E,1)/Omega(E). There is no sign reversal, least-positive-period
replacement, or half-projection applied to an integral generator.

**Full lattice and denominators.** Let z=(z_1,...,z_s) be an integral
vector representing any x in M. Its functional value is
phi(x)=sum_i z_i x_i. Rational relation checks suffice even for
torsion removal: if kv lies in R, then k phi(v)=0 in Q, so phi(v)=0.
Thus annihilating R annihilates R_sat, and the functional is well-defined
on Z^s/R_sat. Alternatively a supplied saturated relation matrix may
be checked directly. This argument does not weaken the separate requirement
that the presentation be complete and identify the whole M.

Each rational y_i has integral p-adic valuation when nonzero, so (2)
is a finite integer. For any integral z_i,
v_p(z_i y_i)>=v_p(y_i)>=-C, with zero terms assigned infinity.
The nonarchimedean inequality on the finite sum gives

\[
v_p(F(x))=v_p\left(\sum_i z_i y_i\right)\ge-C.
\tag{6}
\]

Every {infinity,r} is an integral element of M by the imported integral
generation statement; (6) applies at every rational argument, including
both symbol families at every level in L027. No bound depends on its
denominator, the length of a continued fraction, or the number of
generators in a particular expression. A rational basis alone would
not justify integral z_i; evaluating the complete integral generators
before (2) accounts for such coordinate denominators.

**Certificate scope and failure test.** Changing phi by any nonzero
rational scalar changes d by that same scalar, so F and the y_i remain
unchanged. Reversing the calibration path alone would change d's sign
and fail (4); the specified orientations are essential. No claim that
q can be certified by rescaling phi is allowed, since that would use
the desired conclusion. Identification only on cycles could leave an
uncontrolled cusp-boundary functional; the full relative line assumption
excludes that ambiguity without purporting to prove its certification.

The proof specializes known twist calibration and integral generation;
it is not a new nonvanishing theorem. The required finite evidence consists
of the complete presentation, exact row and relation checks, relative-line
identification, independent q, and the generator valuations in (2).
Without these, or if L027's later strict error threshold fails, no
coefficient certificate follows. No BSD candidate is asserted.
