## Conclusion

R is 31-maximal:

\[
 R\otimes_{\mathbf Z}\mathbf Z_{31}
   =\mathcal O_K\otimes_{\mathbf Z}\mathbf Z_{31}.
\]

More precisely, for I'=sqrt(31R), the complete finite calculation gives

\[
 I'=31R,\qquad
 N'=\ker\bigl(R\longrightarrow\operatorname{End}_{\mathbf F_{31}}
                      (I'/31I')\bigr)=31R,
 \qquad (I':I')=31^{-1}N'=R.
\]

Both Frobenius-squared and the entire action matrix have rank 48 over
F₃₁; both kernels have dimension zero. The independently verified global

matrix into 31⁻¹N' is 1₄₈, of exact determinant and index one.

## Proof

**Imported criterion and specialization.** Import Peter Stevenhagen,
*The arithmetic of number rings*, in *Algorithmic Number Theory*,
MSRI Publications **44** (2008), pp.209–266,
[Lemma 6.4 p.221 and Section 9 pp.233–235, especially Proposition 9.3
and its following action/kernel/lifting paragraph p.235](https://library.slmath.org/books/Book44/files/08psh.pdf#page=27).
For an order R in a number field and a rational prime p, the inverse
image of the kernel of an iterated Frobenius on R/pR, with p^k greater
than its F_p-dimension, is I=sqrt(pR). For the full action on I/pI,
the source identifies (I:I)=p⁻¹N, where N is the action kernel in R;
its integral preimage is generated by lifts of the entire kernel in
R/pR and all pR. The multiplier is strictly larger if R is not
p-maximal, giving the equality stopping criterion. These statements,
including their strictness and lifting cases, are imported, not
reproved from a dimension count.

L067 supplies the number field; audited L078 supplies a full Z-order,
so 31 is not invertible. The changed basis need not be monogenic or
contain 1 as a basis element. Here the dimension is 48 and 31²=961>48,
so two Frobenius iterates suffice. No old rank, kernel, gcd or index is
transferred to this changed input. The nonmonic first-correction theorem
is not reapplied to R. The assessed source coverage was saved before
this attempt in `drafts/literature/2026-10-18-enlarged-order-31-maximality.md`.
