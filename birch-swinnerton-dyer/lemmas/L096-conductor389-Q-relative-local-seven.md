## Conclusion

For every pair of integers a,b,

\[
aP+bQ\in7E(\mathbf Q_7)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a+4b\equiv0\pmod7.
\tag{1}
\]

In particular Q=4P in G/7G, so Q-4P is a rational point divisible
by seven in G. We do not establish that its class is nonzero in
E(Q)/7E(Q), that it generates the global localization kernel, or
