# L083 — Fixed all-depth split classical localization at seven

## Hypotheses

Let E/Q be the exact curve

\[
E:\ y^2+y=x^3+x^2-2x,\qquad [a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4,a_6]=[0,1,1,-2,0],
\]

and let P=(0,0). Use L030's minimal integral model, L033's good
reduction at seven with #E(F_7)=13, L034's published rational rank two,
L081's audited valuation v_7(t([13]P))=1 for t=-x/y, and L082's
published conclusion Sha(E/Q)[7^infinity]=0. Retain the qualifications
on the published computational group result and published Sha theorem:
their original computations have not been independently replayed here.
No seven-saturation assertion for the displayed pair in L034 is needed.

For every integer n>=1, put R_n=Z/7^nZ. Use the classical Selmer group
S_n=Sel_{7^n}(E/Q), imposing the Kummer condition at every place,
including bad primes and the real place. Write delta_n for the global
Kummer injection, delta_{n,7} for the local Kummer injection, and
G_n=E(Q_7)/7^n E(Q_7). Define

\[
\lambda_n:S_n\longrightarrow G_n,\qquad
\lambda_n(c)=\delta_{n,7}^{-1}(\operatorname{res}_7(c)),
\]

where the inverse is taken only on the image of delta_{n,7}.

## Conclusion

The map delta_n identifies S_n with E(Q)/7^nE(Q), a free R_n-module
of rank two. The module G_n is free of rank one, generated by the
image of P, and lambda_n is a split surjection. In particular there is
a split exact sequence of R_n-modules

\[
0\longrightarrow K_n\longrightarrow S_n
\xrightarrow{\lambda_n}G_n\longrightarrow0,
\qquad K_n\simeq R_n.
\]

Every element of K_n is a rational Kummer class at this quotient level.
If g_n denotes the image of P in G_n, one section is

\[
s_n(a g_n)=a\delta_n([P])\quad(a\in R_n).
\]

Thus |S_n|=7^(2n), |G_n|=|K_n|=7^n. No canonical or explicitly
computed kernel generator is supplied. A rational representative of a
kernel class is only asserted to lie in 7^nE(Q_7); it is not asserted
to have zero local logarithm. This is a fixed known-source specialization,
not a new general rank theorem or a BSD candidate. The separate critical
audit below passes, retaining the unreplayed published computations.

## Proof

**Imported results and why this specialization is needed.** Use Mordell's
finite-generation theorem and the following precise standard inputs.
J. S. Milne, *Elliptic Curves*, second edition (2021), Chapter IV,
Proposition 1.5, printed p.108, the localization discussion IV.1.9,
printed pp.110–111, and IV.2, equations (28)–(29), printed pp.112–113,
give the Kummer maps, their naturality under completion, and the finite
Selmer exact sequence. The
[global/local diagram and equation (29)](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/EC2.pdf#page=118)
do not assume finiteness of Sha. We apply them with the integer 7^n
over the characteristic-zero fields Q and Q_7. We do not apply the
prime-to-residue-characteristic statement IV.3.2 at seven.

For the local structure import J. H. Silverman, *The Arithmetic of
Elliptic Curves*, second edition, GTM 106 (2009), VII Propositions
2.1–2.2, printed pp.188–191, and IV Lemma 6.3(b) and Theorem 6.4(b),
printed pp.131–132: the
[reduction sequence](https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/~benoist/refs/Silverman.pdf#page=204),
[formal parameter](https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/~benoist/refs/Silverman.pdf#page=207)
and [strict-radius logarithm](https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/~benoist/refs/Silverman.pdf#page=150).
The [author's errata of 13 October 2022](https://www.math.brown.edu/johsilve/AEC/AECErrata.pdf),
entries for pp.130–133 and 185–191, are retained as in L081. Only the
strict radius r>v_7(7)/(7-1)=1/6 is used. The primary hypotheses and
relevant proof cases were inspected in the prior SPECIALIZE assessment
`drafts/literature/2026-10-18-fixed-seven-all-depth-localization.md`.
These named theorems and L034/L082's published computations are imported,
not reproved. The work below is the finite-level applicability argument,
not a repeat of L081's scalar computation or L005's rationalized line.

**The entire local quotient at every depth.** Put G=E(Q_7),
U=E_1(Q_7), and C=E(F_7). The model is minimal integral and good at
seven, so E_0(Q_7)=G and the cited reduction theorem gives

\[
0\longrightarrow U\longrightarrow G\xrightarrow{\rho}C
\longrightarrow0,\qquad |C|=13.
\tag{1}
\]

The field Q_7 is complete discretely valued, its ring is Z_7, and
v_7(7)=1. The formal parameter identifies U with the formal group on
7Z_7. Since 1>1/6, the cited logarithm is a group isomorphism

\[
L:U\xrightarrow{\sim}7\mathbf Z_7,
\tag{2}
\]

and its strict leading-term estimate gives v_7(L(A))=v_7(t(A)) for
nonidentity A in U. It is a homomorphism, hence for q=7^n it induces

\[
U/qU\xrightarrow{\sim}7\mathbf Z_7/7^{n+1}\mathbf Z_7
\xrightarrow{z\mapsto z/7}R_n.
\tag{3}
\]

All multiples and quotients in (3) use the same isomorphism (2); no
assertion of convergence at the boundary r=1/6 is involved.

Inclusion induces an isomorphism i_n:U/qU -> G/qG. For injectivity,
if u=q b belongs to U with b in G, then q rho(b)=0. Multiplication
by q is invertible on C, because gcd(7^n,13)=1; therefore b belongs
to U, and u belongs to qU. For surjectivity, given a in G choose
c in C with q c=rho(a), then lift c to b in G by (1). Now a-qb
belongs to U and represents the same class as a modulo qG. Thus both
directions hold for every n. In conjunction with (3), this proves
G_n is free of rank one over R_n.

Also G has no nonzero seven-primary torsion. Indeed if q a=0 then
q rho(a)=0 forces a into U, and (2) gives qL(a)=0 in the torsion-free
group 7Z_7. Thus L(a)=0 and a=0. Since E(Q) injects into E(Q_7),
the same holds for rational seven-primary torsion.

**The actual point generates at every depth.** Let W=[13]P. L081
proves W belongs to U and v_7(t(W))=1 by exact rational arithmetic;
the audited value, not the parameter at P, is used here. By (2),
v_7(L(W))=1. Write L(W)=7u with u a unit in Z_7. Its image under
(3) is the unit u modulo 7^n, and therefore generates R_n as an
R_n-module. Consequently [W] generates G_n through i_n. But
[W]=13[P] in G_n and 13 is a unit in R_n, so g_n=[P] generates G_n
as well. Its order is exactly 7^n. This proves all-depth generation
from the fixed valuation-one input; nonzeroness modulo seven alone
would not have implied it without the full quotient comparison above.

**Global Kummer identification and matching localization.** By Mordell's
theorem and L034's rank-two input, E(Q) is isomorphic to Z^2 direct-sum
a finite torsion group T. The torsion exclusion just proved shows that
7 does not divide |T|: otherwise the structure theorem for a finite
abelian group gives nonzero seven-torsion. Hence multiplication by q is
an automorphism of T. Therefore

\[
A_n:=E(\mathbf Q)/qE(\mathbf Q)\simeq R_n^2.
\tag{4}
\]

This uses an abstract basis of the free quotient and does not need the
displayed pair of L034 to be seven-saturated. The rank input retains
its stated published computational provenance.

Milne's finite Kummer sequence, with all classical local conditions, is

\[
0\longrightarrow A_n\xrightarrow{\delta_n}S_n
\longrightarrow\Sha(E/\mathbf Q)[q]\longrightarrow0.
\tag{5}
\]

By L082, the rightmost group is zero for every n, so delta_n is an
R_n-linear isomorphism. At every place v the Kummer image of a rational
point lies in the local Kummer image of E(Q_v)/qE(Q_v). Thus no local
condition in (5), including a bad prime or the real place, is dropped.
Conversely, by the definition of the classical group S_n, restriction
of any c in S_n to seven lies in the image of the injective map
delta_{n,7}. Hence lambda_n in the statement is well-defined and
R_n-linear. Naturality of the connecting cocycle under Q -> Q_7 gives

\[
\operatorname{res}_7(\delta_n([A]))
=\delta_{n,7}([A]_7),\qquad
\lambda_n\delta_n([A])=[A]_7
\quad(A\in E(\mathbf Q)).
\tag{6}
\]

The inverse in the definition of lambda_n is only on the local Kummer
image, never on all of H^1(Q_7,E[q]). In particular (6) sends
delta_n([P]) to g_n, proving surjectivity.

**Section and cyclic kernel over the finite ring.** Since g_n is a
free generator, a in the formula for s_n is unique in R_n. The element
delta_n([P]) is killed by q, so the formula is independent of integer
representatives and defines an R_n-linear map. Equation (6) gives
lambda_n s_n=identity. Its image and the kernel have zero intersection;
each c in S_n decomposes as
c=(c-s_n(lambda_n(c)))+s_n(lambda_n(c)). This is the asserted splitting.

For completeness, the rank of the kernel can be established by a
unit-coordinate argument, without treating R_n as a field. Choose an
R_n-basis e_1,e_2 of S_n using (4)–(5), and write
lambda_n(e_1)=a g_n, lambda_n(e_2)=b g_n. Surjectivity implies the
ideal (a,b) equals R_n. The ring R_n is local with maximal ideal
7R_n, so at least one of a,b is a unit. After interchanging the basis
if necessary assume a is a unit. A vector x e_1+y e_2 is in the kernel
exactly when ax+by=0, equivalently x=-a^(-1)by. Hence

\[
R_n\longrightarrow K_n,\qquad
y\longmapsto -a^{-1}b y e_1+y e_2
\]

is an R_n-linear bijection: the second coordinate proves injectivity,
and the displayed equation proves surjectivity. This gives a cyclic
kernel of order 7^n, including for n>1. This elementary applicability
check supplies the rank information needed in addition to the general
free-module splitting result of the
[Stacks Project, Lemma 10.77.2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/05CD);
no inference from vector-space dimension over F_7 is used at higher n.

Finally delta_n is onto, so every kernel class has the form delta_n([A])
for a rational point A. By (6), membership in the kernel says exactly
A in qE(Q_7). It does not say that A itself is a rational point with
zero local logarithm; the rational representative may depend on n,
and no canonical generator or choices across depths are claimed.

**Relevance, test and limitations.** All four applicability tests pass:
the torsion exclusion, complete local-quotient comparison and primitive
generation, Kummer identification/naturality, and finite-ring splitting
with cyclic kernel. This completes the exact approved fixed application.
The general ordinary-semisimplicity import stopped in ATTEMPTS/031 and the

fixed inputs. No global degree-48 arithmetic calculation is used.
This result neither supplies those inputs uniformly nor proves any
L026 premise package, new rational rank lower bound, complex-order
comparison, full Sha finiteness or refined leading-coefficient formula.

**Separate critical audit, 2026-10-18 (step 319).** The approved audit
passes for precisely the stated fixed curve, prime and every finite
depth. It reuses the primary-source coverage in
`drafts/literature/2026-10-18-fixed-seven-all-depth-localization-critical-audit.md`
and the completed upstream audits; it does not replay Cremona's group
computation, Stein--Wuthrich's computation or L081's point arithmetic.
The following checks concern the new assembly rather than those inputs.

First, L030 supplies a minimal integral equation over Z_7 and L033
supplies good reduction with order 13. Thus the reduction sequence is
for the full group G, not merely a subgroup of points with nonsingular
reduction. The integer radius is 1, strictly greater than 1/6, regardless
of n. The same injective and surjective logarithm on U identifies qU
with 7^(n+1)Z_7: surjectivity of the logarithm supplies every preimage,
and injectivity supplies the reverse containment. There is no change of
formal logarithm with coefficient depth.

Both quotient containments in the proof survive inspection. A putative
extra division point b outside U would have q rho(b)=0 in the order-13
group, impossible unless rho(b)=0. Conversely the lift of the unique
q-division of rho(a) subtracts an actual q-multiple in G and moves a
into U. This proves an R_n-linear isomorphism of the complete quotients;
neither a cardinality guess nor a splitting of the reduction sequence
is assumed. The torsion argument uses this same invertibility on C
and the torsion-free additive group 7Z_7, so excludes every rational
seven-primary torsion element as well.

L081 supplies a nonidentity W=[13]P in U with parameter valuation
exactly one. The strict leading-term estimate therefore makes L(W)/7
a unit, not just a nonzero residue at some unspecified depth. Its
reduction is a unit for each n, and multiplication by 13 is invertible
in each R_n. This proves that P has exact quotient order 7^n. No
logarithm at the affine point (0,0), saturation of the published pair,
or ordinary-reduction theorem beyond the stated local structure is used.

Next, finite generation, rank two and the absence of rational
seven-primary torsion give the full quotient R_n^2. L082 removes the
entire right term of (5), including every possible finite-depth class;
finiteness alone would not do so. Milne's all-place sequence applies
to q=7^n in characteristic zero. Its connecting cocycle commutes with
completion, yielding (6) with the same q and the same point. This
checks both that rational classes satisfy every classical local
condition and that the inverse local Kummer map is defined for every
Selmer class. No ambient cohomology group is identified with G_n.

For the splitting, g_n has exact order q and delta_n([P]) is killed
by q; its image of exact order q also proves its own order is q.
Thus the proposed section is well-defined and injective. Independently,
the operator c -> c-s_n(lambda_n(c)) has image K_n, is the identity
on K_n and vanishes on the section. This checks the claimed direct
sum without an inverse-limit exactness or projectivity assumption.
For the kernel rank, the equation (a,b)=R_n forces a unit coordinate:
if neither were a unit, the image would be contained in 7R_n, also
when n=1 where that ideal is zero. With a a unit, the two vectors
e_1 and -a^(-1)b e_1+e_2 have determinant one in the original basis.
Localization is an isomorphism on the first summand and zero on the
second. This independently confirms the displayed kernel bijection
over the finite ring, its exact order and all stated cardinalities.

Finally, delta_n being onto supplies a rational representative of each
kernel class, and (6) gives precisely divisibility by q in E(Q_7).
Neither coherent rational representatives across depths nor an actual
rational point with zero logarithm follows. Every essential input is
already among L030, L033, L034, L081 and L082 in the existing DAG row;
the audit adds no mathematical dependency. The source qualifications
and all general BSD gaps remain. No mathematical script is needed for
these symbolic finite-depth checks. This validates a known-source
application, not a new rank or Sha result beyond the checked sources.
