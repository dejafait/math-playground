[monotonic](https://docs.python.org/3/library/time.html#time.monotonic) supports elapsed differences shared across modern macOS processes. [setitimer](https://docs.python.org/3/library/signal.html#signal.setitimer) clears on zero; [handler dispatch](https://docs.python.org/3/library/signal.html#execution-of-python-signal-handlers) may be delayed by C work.
