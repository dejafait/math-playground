and the modular degree*, author PDF dated 28 January 2022, Theorems
1.2/7.2, pp.3 and 45--46. Its unconditional conclusion is
c_phi | 6 deg(phi), for every parametrization at exact conductor; the
factor six covers the exceptional 2/3 cases. The complete final proof

is relevant source coverage, not an unconditional import of the saved
target. Both real-lattice types occur explicitly in the proof.
