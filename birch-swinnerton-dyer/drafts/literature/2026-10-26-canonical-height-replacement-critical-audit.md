## Primary coverage and source limits

**MST06.** Barry Mazur, William Stein and John Tate, *Computation of
p-Adic Heights and Log Convergence*, Documenta Mathematica, Extra Volume
Coates (2006), 577--614, [publisher PDF](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/27479).
The cited introduction supplies the cyclotomic functional, admissible-point
formula, quadratic extension, polarization and regulator definition.
Theorem 1.3 and (1.7)--(1.9) retain the model and differential conventions
for arbitrary b_2. Section 2.1 defines the functional by composition with
the universal pairing; Section 2.6 explicitly removes admissibility by
multiples; Sections 2.7--2.8 identify the cyclotomic specialization.
Import these statements; do not reprove the construction or assume
Conjecture 1.1. The relevant passages were read as extracted PDF text.

**Harvey.** David Harvey, *Efficient computation of p-adic heights*,
[arXiv:0708.3404v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0708.3404v1), 24 August 2007.
The p.2 normalization explicitly gives the factor 2p relative to MST.
Theorems 1--3 and Sections 2.1--2.2 cover the stated integral model,
differential and good ordinary p>=5 scope. Section 5 accounts for the
final absolute precision after division by the admissible multiplier;
Lemma 8 treats unit logarithms. Proposition 9 requires bad-prime
admissibility, odd modulus and m>=2; its application handles the paired
sign ambiguity. These imported results do not certify an implementation
or license applying an affine formula to zero.

**Characteristic order.** Anwesh Ray, *Remarks on Hilbert's tenth problem
and the Iwasawa theory of elliptic curves*, Bull. Aust. Math. Soc. 107
(2023), 440--450, [Theorem 3.4 and preceding definitions, p.445](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/32000D6BB2B3EC97CAD0B6F932B84935/S000497272200082Xa.pdf).
The statement requires odd ordinary p, finite p-primary Sha, nonzero
regulator and a finitely generated torsion classical Selmer dual. Its
regulator is a Mordell--Weil height determinant; the normalized regulator
is separately defined there. Only its order conclusion at K=Q is needed.
Theorem 3.4 has no non-CM restriction. Its attribution to Schneider,
Theorem 2', p.342, is inspected through Ray; Schneider's original proof
remains unread. This reused canonical-height input concerns nonvanishing,
not an exact refined leading coefficient.
