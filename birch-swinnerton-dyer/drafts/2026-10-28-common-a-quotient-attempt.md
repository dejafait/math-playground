preserves the tower. The Lambda-algebra generated by the physical Hecke
algebra and W is finite inside End_Lambda(H). A relation in this algebra
acts as zero on the ordinary coefficient completion if it acts as zero
on H: ordinary control reduces it to each finite-level homology, where
Jacobian endomorphisms are faithfully detected. This is the crucial

Complete the diamond weight ring at augmentation after localizing there;
the resulting base is Q_p[[t]]. The finite etale Hecke algebra splits
into its completed classical branches. The A branch is distinguished
from all other branches by good Hecke operators because the original
level is exactly the conductor and the A newvector multiplicity is one.
