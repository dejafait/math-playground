# Rational Kummer correction of the auxiliary-prime Kato family

Date: 2026-10-03. Outcome: conditional extraction mechanism rejected
by an application of known arithmetic results; no BSD counterexample.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L014](../lemmas/L014-rational-kummer-corrections-of-kato-systems.md)
shows that rational Kummer subtraction leaves every p-singular
localization unchanged. Under the retained zero local-torsion and
nonzero mod-p Kurihara premises, the chosen two-prime component remains
nonfinite at p even if one drops all family compatibility demands.
Preserving the original transverse conditions, coefficient reductions
and finite-singular relations would also make the complete rational
correction a classical ordinary Kolyvagin system; Kim's Theorem 2.5(1)
forces it to be zero. The failed mechanism changes neither the
singular value nor the system's core rank. Any further construction
must change the arithmetic input or its local conditions and relations.
This result does not decide rationality of a single determinant,
extend L013 to an arithmetic system, or rule out Sakamoto's different
rank-zero objects. The precise hypotheses and proof are retained in
L014 rather than repeated here.
