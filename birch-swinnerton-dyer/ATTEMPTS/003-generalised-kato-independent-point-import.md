# Direct import of two rational points from generalised Kato classes

Date: 2026-09-25. Outcome: informative negative for the direct use of
the 2016 construction as L004's analytic-rank-two point input.

## WHY IT FAILS

The [primary-source audit](../foundations/06-generalised-kato-scope.md)
separates Theorem 3.1's Selmer membership and Corollary 3.6's conditional
twisted-Selmer independence from Conjectures 3.2 and 3.12. Importing
the latter as proved would assume the missing rank comparison or
Mordell--Weil membership. In the relevant adjoint rank-(2,0) formulas,
even the predicted four stabilisations lie on at most one line. Thus
they do not provide the proposed two independent rational points.
[L005](../lemmas/L005-strict-kummer-rank-two-threshold.md) preserves a
useful alternative: one nonzero strict class suffices if its rational
Kummer membership is proved. The quotient in that lemma identifies
the missing condition; neither mere Selmer membership nor the four
stabilisation labels verifies it. The triple-product p-adic value in
Corollary 3.6 is also not L004's cyclotomic coefficient.

This stops only the direct point-import inference and the proposed
independence mechanism. It does not stop investigation of strict
classes, rationality results, auxiliary choices, or later theorems.
