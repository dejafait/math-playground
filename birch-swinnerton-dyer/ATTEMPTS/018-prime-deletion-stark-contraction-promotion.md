# Automatic p^2 promotion from Stark determinant contraction

Date: 2026-10-04. Outcome: informative negative for the tested top-index
and divisor-contraction mechanism; the actual arithmetic lifting question
remains open.

The attempt adds Section 2.5's p-local canonical extension and Stark
determinant transitions to the previous self-duality package. It uses the
unchanged saved fixed-prime p^2 target and its ready SPECIALIZE coverage.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L017](../lemmas/L017-prime-deletion-stark-contraction-retains-p2-error.md)
shows that the actual contraction at N is an isomorphism of determinant
lines, so it places no additional restriction on tau at that index.
The proper-divisor Stark vectors can be nonzero of order p while their
finite scalar regulators vanish; the final singular contraction multiplies
the transverse errors to p^2BC = 0 in Z/p^2Z. A tau = 1 model extends
the p-local sequence and all divisor contraction diagrams at N. Thus
these data do not force automatic classical lifting. No full auxiliary
family or arithmetic counterexample is constructed. A stronger coefficient
test with an analytic initial-value constraint is a different mechanism;
its changed hypotheses and required identities must be assessed separately.
