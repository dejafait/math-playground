# Primitivity as elimination of the finite residual defect

Date: 2026-10-05. Outcome: bounded structural audit, not an arithmetic
counterexample to a stronger primitivity implication.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L021](../lemmas/L021-finite-paired-residual-index-defect.md) specializes
Kim's structure and Sakamoto's residual index theorems: under granted
corank two, t positive finite cyclic pairs raise the minimal residual
prime count to 2+2t. Primitivity gives a unit somewhere in the collection,
not at the first integral index; the extra condition is zero finite
Selmer quotient. The formal Fitting check retains one positive finite
pair with eventual unit valuation, but constructs no arithmetic family.
Stop inferring the two-prime threshold from corank and primitivity through
these constraints alone. Preserve the possibility of a separate stronger
arithmetic input. The proposed next review tests the different prime-set
eligibility issue after explicitly granting zero finite quotient.
