## WHY IT FAILS

Consequently the forgetting pushdown from the covariant ordinary
summand has image in p times integral target homology; even after
the ordinary and finite-diamond projection it is zero modulo p.
It cannot be the surjective control map at a nonzero target. This
blocks identifying the original-label covariant tower with the
controlled tower using the unchanged transitions. It does not refute
the source's theorem in its own conventions, disprove L128 (2)/(3),
or prove a relation fails on N_0. Stop before faithful detection and
completion. A future repair needs a genuinely different comparison
or transition with its own source gate and bounded test; repeating
the unchanged pushdown argument is not justified. Preserve /061/062.
