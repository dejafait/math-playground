# Importing Kummer membership through an ordinary dual-family trace

Date: 2026-09-25. Outcome: informative negative for continuing the
usual classical tame-dual contraction along the diagonal branch.

## WHY IT FAILS

The [construction audit](../foundations/08-diagonal-cycle-specialization.md)
identifies the available cohomological maps and the missing comparison
with a cycle-valued map to E. The proposed shortcut was to keep the
auxiliary forms related by a classical tame twist, use their dual
pairing to contract the cycle, and then specialize. The full root
calculation in [L007](../lemmas/L007-ordinary-dual-family-stabilization-obstruction.md)
shows that this continuation selects (alpha,beta^(-1)), not the
(alpha,alpha^(-1)) diagonal pair. Distinct roots are essential. In
the restricted setting already checked in the Castella--Hsieh
foundations, the crossed classes vanish. Thus this particular
continuation cannot certify the nonzero strict Kummer class required
by L006. A trace exists on the weight-one Artin fiber; its compatibility
with a geometric class is precisely what this failed family argument
does not establish. This does not rule out other cycle constructions,
infinitesimal comparisons, or a different motivic lift.
