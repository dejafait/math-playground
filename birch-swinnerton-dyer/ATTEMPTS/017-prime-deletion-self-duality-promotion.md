# Automatic p^2 promotion from the two-prime self-duality package

Date: 2026-10-04. Outcome: informative negative for automatic promotion
from the tested constraints. The actual fixed-prime arithmetic lifting
question remains open.

The approved target was classical lifting of Sakamoto's two prescribed
prime-deletion classes, at the same primes eligible for P_(2,0), with
non-anomalous ordinary hypotheses and a minimal nonzero mod-p two-prime
Kurihara premise. This step tested whether self-duality and the tracked
finite-singular equations supply the vanishing still missing in L015.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L016](../lemmas/L016-prime-deletion-alternating-p2-obstruction.md)
shows that reciprocity removes the one-prime scalar errors but leaves
one alternating transverse coefficient tau. Its explicit maximal-isotropic
localization model satisfies the coefficient maps and every tested
two-prime equation with tau = 1. Thus the proposed vanishing deduction
from that package fails. The model does not extend the complete Kato
family or realize an elliptic curve, so this is not an arithmetic
counterexample to the original target. A further constraint from the
actual global construction, rather than another use of the same
self-pairing equations, would change the test. Even tau = 0 gives a
Selmer basis and leaves the rational-rank lower bound unresolved.
