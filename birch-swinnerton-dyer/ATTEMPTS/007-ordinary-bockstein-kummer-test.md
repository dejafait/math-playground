# Ordinary first-order lifting as a Kummer-membership test

Date: 2026-09-25. Outcome: informative negative for this test in the
checked anticyclotomic rank-(2,0) Selmer setting.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L009](../lemmas/L009-class-bockstein-and-strict-local-derivative.md)
proves that the ordinary first Bockstein vanishes on the entire
Selmer space: the anticyclotomic tangent reverses conjugation sign,
while the target's minus part vanishes by duality and the auxiliary
analytic-rank-zero twist. Thus ordinary first-order liftability adds
no constraint to the existing Selmer membership and cannot supply
the missing inference q(kappa) = 0 by itself. Requiring zero local
conditions at both primes leaves a separate derivative in D^-;
ordinary vanishing does not settle its value. No Kummer interpretation
of that stricter condition, no elliptic curve with infinite Sha, and
no BSD counterexample has been established. The prior obstruction to
a universal trace and this class-level vanishing are compatible.
