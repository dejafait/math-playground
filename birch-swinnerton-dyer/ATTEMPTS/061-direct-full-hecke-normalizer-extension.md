# Direct full-Hecke-linear normalizer extension

2026-10-27, step 528. Informative negative for the formal commuting-
operator shortcut; not a disproof of scalar action after specialization.

## WHY IT FAILS

At a split conductor prime of exponent one or two, the actual degree-one
normalizer conjugates U_q to the distinct opposite abstract double coset.
The [canonical calculation and complete-moment identity](../lemmas/L127-tame-normalizer-localization-defect.md)
therefore prevent inferring full-Hecke-linearity merely from level
normalization. Before localization the geometric action is well defined,
but the original residual projection leaves the exact possible term
P W (1-P) tilde-J; extending P W P through tensoring with I additionally
requires the balanced commutators to vanish there. Neither their
vanishing nor nonvanishing on the actual specialized module is proved.
Local newvector scalar theorems cannot by themselves justify these maps.
No individual defect term has been projected by pr_A. Park only this
automatic extension argument, retain L126, and critically review L127
before selecting a quotient-based repair or a case without split tame
conductor. Neither alternative is attempted or approved here.
