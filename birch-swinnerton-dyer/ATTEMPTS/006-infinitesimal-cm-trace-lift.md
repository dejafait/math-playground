# Lifting the CM trace through a first-order diagonal deformation

Date: 2026-09-25. Outcome: informative negative for a regular
Galois-equivariant contraction extending the auxiliary fiber trace.

## WHY IT FAILS

The actual normalized CM family has an anticyclotomic summand,
as pinned in [the source normalization](../foundations/09-cm-diagonal-deformation.md).
[L008](../lemmas/L008-infinitesimal-cm-trace-obstruction.md) proves
that its nonzero first derivative obstructs the trace already over
Q_p[epsilon]/(epsilon^2). All equivariant auxiliary functionals reduce
to zero, and arbitrary corrections involving the constant elliptic-curve
representation cannot cancel the obstruction. Thus weakening classical
duality to a regular first-order trace lift does not repair the earlier
contraction route. This calculation uses the critical twist and the
actual weight-one coordinate; it does not assume L007's accumulating
classical points. It says nothing against the trace at the isolated
fiber, specialization of an individual class, or a derived geometric
construction. In particular it does not prove that kappa is non-Kummer.
