# Deducing strict first-order lifting from the Coleman double zero

Date: 2026-09-25. Outcome: informative negative for the inference
from the checked scalar reciprocity data alone.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L010](../lemmas/L010-diagonal-local-derivative-and-coleman-kernel.md)
shows that the actual diagonal family's first-order ordinary lift
has localizations (epsilon u,0) and strict obstruction
(u,-tau u)/2. The source's double zero places u in the finite local
line, which is exactly the kernel of the augmentation Coleman map;
it does not force u = 0. Even a fixed full scalar Coleman series
allows arbitrary u in the local module after extending coefficients.
Strict lifting requires the full localization, not only its scalar
image, to vanish modulo T^2. This does not prove a nonzero obstruction
for the actual global class, exclude additional global constraints,
or establish any implication between strict lifting and rational
Kummer membership. Further scalar-order calculations alone do not
address the missing component; a different input would be needed.
