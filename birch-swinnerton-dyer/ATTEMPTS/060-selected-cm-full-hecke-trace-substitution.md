# Replacing selected CM branches by a normalized full Hecke trace

2026-10-27, step 522. Informative negative for an automatic branch/trace
substitution, not a disproof of a scalar action on the projected class.

## WHY IT FAILS

The [canonical local calculation](../lemmas/L125-selected-cm-branch-full-trace-obstruction.md)
tests an allowed choice of L124's rational normalizer at a split good
prime q. Its full fiber has q+1 branches, of which q-1 have CM conductor
q. The selected branch has conductor one, and multiplication by every
coordinate in the complete original CM list preserves that order.
Consequently the full divisor trace is not proportional to the selected
divisor, with all stabilizers and positive multiplicities retained.
A degree normalization cannot repair the missing support. In the actual
complete weighted measure the omitted branches give the exact additional
term J_miss; its vanishing after the coefficient maps is unproved.
Divisor inequality cannot be promoted to cohomological nonscalarity.

Park the automatic full-fiber substitution. This does not stop the scalar
question or all normalizer choices: a degree-one tame normalizer would
avoid this particular fiber obstruction. Replacing w by one through a
CM-torus translation is a distinct proposed mechanism and needs separate
coverage for the complete projected moment. The kernel-sign, rationality
and shallow-quotient stops in ATTEMPTS/003/004/030/058/059 remain intact.
