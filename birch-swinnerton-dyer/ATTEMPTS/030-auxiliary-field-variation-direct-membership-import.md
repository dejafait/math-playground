## WHY IT FAILS

Castella's Theorem 5.2.3 makes generalized Kato nonvanishing at classical
Selmer dimension two equivalent to nonzero localization on the rational
Selmer space, and places the nonzero class in its strict kernel. This
does not supply rational Kummer membership; varying admissible auxiliary
fields does not remove the localization condition in that theorem.
Castella--Hsieh's point formula still adds finite p-primary Sha. L026
supplies neither localization nor that finiteness. Park the direct import,
preserve the fixed-field failures and review a separate cyclotomic
local-condition criterion. This is no nonexistence theorem for geometric
membership comparisons or a global ban on auxiliary variation.
