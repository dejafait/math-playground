Date: 2026-10-03. Outcome: direct theorem import excluded by scope;
no nonexistence theorem for the arithmetic certificate.

## WHY IT FAILS

The [completed source assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-10-03-cyclotomic-kato-derivative.md)
locates finite p-primary Sha in BKS's rational identification and
rank-indexed derivative construction. Applying that construction with
r_alg = 2 would assume the missing lower bound. Sano's broader
determinant descent is available with cohomological hypotheses, but
its source is a cohomology determinant with a dual H^2 factor, its
degree depends on Bocksteins, and its theorem does not identify the
preimage with an exterior square of rational points. The inspected
2026 determinant/Stark-system and height results still concern Selmer
data or nonzero character specializations. Therefore they cannot be
cited as the requested rational certificate without an additional
arithmetic argument. This excludes the proposed direct import, not
the derivative mechanism as a whole. The covered model test isolates
whether degree-one descent supplies any new formal constraint on the
Kummer/Sha quotient; it has not been performed. The earlier Eisenstein
stop and all existing mathematical artifacts remain intact.
