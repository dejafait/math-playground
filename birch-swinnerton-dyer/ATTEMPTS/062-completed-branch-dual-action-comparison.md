# Completed-branch dual-action comparison

2026-10-28, step 534. Informative negative for the written Step 1
handoff in L128, not a disproof of its scalar identity.

## WHY IT FAILS

The [canonical audit in L128](../lemmas/L128-completed-a-branch-normalizer-scalar.md)
checks the natural evaluation pairing: dualizing a covariant diamond
endomorphism produces the inverse diamond on covariant homology.
Commutativity of the Hecke algebra handles opposite multiplication
but does not identify these labeled representations or their ordinary
summands. Defining a transported action instead requires comparing
that tower with the one controlled by the imported theorems. The
written proof supplies neither comparison before transferring relations
to N_0. Faithful Tate detection and flat completion apply only after
this identification and do not replace it. Equations (2)/(3) are held;
no physical nonscalarity is proved. A future covariant Betti--Tate
relation test would be a refined mechanism requiring its own source
gate, not a repetition of the unsupported opposite-action assertion.
