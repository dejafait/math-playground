# Equality in the degree threshold

The bounded audit tested whether finite Kummer naturality and compatible
coefficient rows could strengthen L020's threshold from m > d to m >= d,
where d is the common extension degree's p-valuation. The formal test
is written in the canonical [L020 proof](../lemmas/L020-finite-extension-global-kummer-certificate.md).

**WHY IT FAILS.** At equality, a nonzero divisible quotient admits the
tested restriction/corestriction composite and exact point-kernel rows
while the whole prescribed basis restricts into the formal point kernel.
Coefficient compatibility adds no contradiction in this model. Therefore
these algebraic data do not imply rank two at equality. This stops only
that weakening; the strict certificate survives, and actual arithmetic
maps or realizing fields are not constructed or refuted. Reopening would
require a specific arithmetic constraint absent from the tested rows,
not another annihilator or coefficient-diagram calculation.
