# Automatic strict Kummer lifting from formal duality

Date: 2026-09-26. Outcome: informative negative for the inference
from the checked duality, conjugation, and rational-logarithm data.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L011](../lemmas/L011-strict-relaxed-pairing-on-kummer-vectors.md)
shows that the dual of zero local conditions is relaxed. Its minus
space is one-dimensional, although the ordinary minus space vanishes.
The strict rational-point vector pairs with this surviving space by
a choice-independent determinant, which is not killed by conjugation.
The displayed dual-complex model retains full formal Kummer membership
and an injective logarithm on the rational lattice, yet permits a
nonzero determinant. Thus these data cannot prove automatic strict
lifting. The model is not an elliptic curve: necessity for actual
rational points, sufficiency for Kummer membership, and the actual
value for kappa remain undecided. An arithmetic calculation of the
mixed obstruction would be a materially different input; this stop
does not exclude it or assert a BSD counterexample.
