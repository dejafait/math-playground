# Rational rank inference from degree-one cyclotomic descent

Date: 2026-10-03. Outcome: formal implication refuted by a compatible
model; no assertion of elliptic-curve realization or BSD failure.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L013](../lemmas/L013-cyclotomic-determinant-descent-with-sha-direction.md)
retains the first Bockstein and its exact degree-one determinant descent,
the dual H^2 factor, characteristic divisibility with equality, scalar
order two, nonzero p-localization, inverse-parameter duality and the
actual local-condition mapping fibers. Its marked rational and Sha
dimensions are nevertheless (1,1). Even the contracted leading vector
is rational: the failed inference concerns its determinant preimage,
which still uses a nonzero Sha direction. Thus these formal data alone
cannot give two rational Kummer directions. The known formalism is
reproduced, not claimed as a new arithmetic theorem. A further input
must exclude the mixed component for the actual Euler-system class;
auxiliary-prime relations are a proposed separate arithmetic test,
not a premise silently added to this model. The exhausted earlier
routes and their evidence are preserved.
