# Obtaining the anticyclotomic lift from rank-zero reciprocity alone

Date: 2026-09-26. Outcome: EXPLORATION; no arithmetic nonliftability
result is claimed.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L012](../lemmas/L012-rank-zero-kato-obstruction-descent.md) identifies
the actual Kato representative and its nonzero normalization. Its
mixed pairing is nevertheless the same unknown t_d from L011,
multiplied by a known nonzero scalar. The cyclotomic lift constrains
a different conjugation component. Descending the desired lift
specifies an extension W_d over Q but supplies no cohomology class
in it. Thus the reciprocity and cyclotomic data do not complete this
attempt; no obstruction value has been found. This closes the
attempt to deduce a lift from its normalization. The existence of an
arithmetic lift and the BSD problem remain open. Constructing a class in the specified
extension would be new input and requires its own source assessment.
