## Assembly and unresolved gap

The current branch studies translations and reception of supplied algebraic/propositional certificates. Hardness of one representation, chosen order, extension ancestry or a particular extraction algorithm is not hardness of every proof or an unrestricted decision problem. Existing simulation and width results retain their exact certificate/model promises and resource measures. No strict proposed-transport receiver exists; generated-object invariants exclude particular malformed metadata accepted by isolated postchecks. Actual ranks, fitting coordinate evidence and finite equality receipts remain unreceived; full-rank failure says nothing about unknown actual ranks. ## Known traps checked

- Formal polynomial identities are not arbitrary propositional tree equalities. Account for Boolean/commutator axioms, ordering, all copied occurrences and whole encoding length.
- A valid supplied proof/certificate is an input, not an existence theorem. Fixed-template preparation has no automatic uniform polynomial bound.
- Cache, serializer, wrapper and trusted-path checks retain their stated byte/time/authorization boundaries; a hash or sample replay does not establish full soundness or total resources.
