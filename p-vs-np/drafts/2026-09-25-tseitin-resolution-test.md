An elementary existence route is a fixed Hamilton cycle plus the simple undirected edges of twelve independent uniform permutations: for disjoint sets A,B with |A|>=n/4 and |B|>=n/3, the probability of no permutation edge from A to B is at most (2/3)^(3n), and there are at most 3^n disjoint pairs. The resulting failure bound is (8/9)^n<1; deleting the S-side endpoints of a cut smaller than n/12 would contradict the pair property. Maximum degree is at most 26.
