# Explicit extended-resolution certification of parity cancellation

Saved 2026-09-25 before the detailed construction; completed in [L012](../lemmas/L012-tseitin-er-row-addition.md). The text below preserves the initial plan, not a candidate resolution or the current checkpoint. The final construction uses a ternary XOR invariant with at most six wires per coordinate; zero coefficients alias the preceding prefix, so no separate sparse-to-dense equivalence proof is needed.

**Gap and intermediate target.** L010 separates resolution proof size from unrestricted decision on Tseitin CNFs. L011 leaves the passage from arbitrary rejecting computations to ER unproved. The bounded target here is an explicit polynomial-size ER refutation of L010's odd-charge formulas, using extension definitions and certified F₂ row additions. Its downstream use is to identify a concrete correctness invariant that ER can express, and determine whether the existing hard family remains useful for an ER lower-bound route. It cannot itself prove a universal computation-to-ER simulation.

**Prior work and discriminating test.** The previous resolution obstruction and automatic-transfer failure are preserved. No earlier lemma supplies ER proofs for this family. Continue this construction if local XOR identities compile to legal resolution/extension inferences and the complete binary proof has one polynomial bound in the original CNF length. Abandon the claimed polynomial construction if a step needs an unproved semantic inference or exponentially many lines in the graph size. The ultimate separation would still need a different family with superpolynomial ER lower bounds and a justified implication from SAT in P to polynomial boundedness of ER, or a different route altogether.

**Working mechanism.** Use a fresh constant-false extension, implement each two-input XOR by three legal AND extensions, and prove constant-size Boolean consequences by an explicit truth-table resolution construction. Give each support vector a canonical prefix XOR representation. During a row addition, certify equality of the new prefixes with the XOR of the old prefixes using a constant-size identity at each coordinate. Initial vertex equations have width at most 26; derive their output units from their explicit parity blocks and a sparse XOR chain before relating that chain to the canonical prefixes. Add all vertex rows: each edge occurs twice, leaving the zero support and odd right-hand side. Charge extensions, wide initial clauses, inference references, and variable indices.

**Checks still required.** Spell out the local consequence compiler rather than silently using semantic consequence as an ER rule; avoid exponential enumeration over the full dense prefix circuit; handle the zero vector and repeated operands; bound the initial parity-block derivation; and verify at least the local macros or small generated proofs with an independent inference checker.
