**Gap and intermediate target.** The diagonal route has D_full outside P but lacks a sound, complete certificate scheme with one fixed polynomial bound. Determine whether this exact language is EXPTIME-complete under polynomial-time many-one reductions. A positive classification would identify a consequence of every possible NP certificate scheme for the full language, beyond the format-specific failures. It would not prove D_full outside NP or settle P versus NP.

**Discriminating test.** Prove a deterministic 2^(O(N log(N+1))) simulation upper bound using unary k ≤ N. For hardness, start with an arbitrary fixed exponential-time decider A and compile its input y into a polynomial-size machine e_y that rejects exactly when A(y) accepts. Choose a unary exponent k polynomial in |y| that dominates the exponential simulation time and the cost of clearing the full reduced input. Produce the unpadded core z(e_y,k). Completeness would justify reassessing full-language certificate compression as the stronger objective NP = EXPTIME; failure of the compiler or clock bound would require identifying that exact obstruction before drawing this conclusion.
