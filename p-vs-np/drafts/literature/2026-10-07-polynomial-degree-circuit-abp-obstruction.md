## Inspected primary results and qualifications

**Nisan.** Noam Nisan, *Lower Bounds for Non-Commutative Computation (Extended Abstract)*, STOC 1991, pp.410–418, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/103418.103462); [accessible primary-paper mirror](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in/~chandan/courses/arithmetic_circuits/notes/noncomm.pdf), nine pages, retrieved 2026-10-07. Read §§2–3 and §4.2, PDF pp.4–6. Section 2 works over the reals. Definition 1 requires consecutive layers and homogeneous linear labels, measuring vertices. Theorem 1 equates minimum ABP size with the sum of the prefix/suffix coefficient-matrix ranks. Its lower-bound proof factors each matrix through its cut-layer vertices; its upper-bound proof eliminates dependent columns or rows. Theorem 4 states the exact two-variable palindrome family, linear circuit complexity, exponential ABP/formula complexity and linear minimum circuit depth. Its displayed circuit recurrence matches the TARGET, and its middle-cut proof uses a full-rank matrix indexed by length-k words (reverse the column ordering when comparing with a diagonal matrix). Lemma 1(1) relates homogeneous ABPs to formulas. No real-field theorem is silently asserted over F₂; the subsequent application must justify that change using the inspected lower-bound proof. The upper-bound characterization and circuit-depth conclusion need no reproof for this target.

**Raz–Shpilka.** Ran Raz and Amir Shpilka, *Deterministic Polynomial Identity Testing in Non Commutative Models*, [author-hosted 16-page manuscript](https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~shpilka/publications/RazShpilka_PIT.pdf), retrieved 2026-10-07, no revision date printed on the title page. Read Definition 1 and Lemma 2, printed pp.4–6, including all five construction steps and runtime discussion. This source works over a field F without a characteristic restriction. Its lemma starts from a size-s formula with zero free term and gives homogeneous-component ABPs with O(s²) vertices each and O(s³) total construction time. Its intermediate graph is acyclic and can have variable or scalar edges without consecutive layers. Steps 2–5 use degree copies, same-degree scalar edges, pruning and scalar-edge elimination in acyclic order before selecting an output component. The zero-free-term condition applies to the positive-degree receiving component, not P_0. A later application must explain why the inspected normalization stages apply to an explicitly supplied affine-edge DAG after label splitting, and bound the resulting graph and binary labels. The formula-specific O(s³) runtime is not imported unchanged for arbitrary DAGs; no formula unfolding is authorized.
