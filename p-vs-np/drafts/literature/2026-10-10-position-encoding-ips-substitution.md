Construction 4.1 defines an F-linear map with phi(1)=1 and phi(z_i1...z_id)=z_i1,1...z_id,d, and matrices Z_i,D of dimension D+1 whose only nonzero entries are (k,k+1)=z_i,k+1. Lemma 4.2 identifies (f(Z_D))_0,l with phi(H_l(f)) for 0≤l≤D; its proof treats the constant word separately and longer words by strictly increasing matrix indices. Lemma 4.3 requires deg(f)≤D and tests every homogeneous component, including zero. Lemma 4.4 gives component width r(D+1); Lemma 4.5 relabels homogeneous linear edges by layer, with a path-expansion proof. Theorem 4.6 handles the constant component separately. Corollary 4.7 additionally requires |F|≥(2(D+1)^5 n r^4)^2 for its hitting set; this is not a hypothesis of Construction 4.1.

Definition 1.1 uses consecutive layers, unique source/sink and affine labels. L048 permits more general designated endpoints and unequal path lengths.
