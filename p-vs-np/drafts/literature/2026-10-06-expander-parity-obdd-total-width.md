## Inspected sources

**Graph input.** Simone Bova and Friedrich Slivovsky, *On Compiling Structured CNFs to OBDDs*, Theory of Computing Systems **61** (2017), 637–655, published online 26 October 2016, [DOI/full text](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00224-016-9715-z), [journal PDF](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s00224-016-9715-z.pdf), §2 and §4.1–4.2, Definition 16 and Lemma 18, printed pp. 650, 652–653. For positive graph clauses (u OR v), maximum degree d and external expansion epsilon, the proof's half-prefix claim supplies at least epsilon*n/(16d) crossing pairs in every order. Their prefix endpoints are independent and cross adjacencies are diagonal; suffix endpoints may be adjacent. Read the complete greedy construction and its empty-A and empty-B cases. Theorem 17's distinguishing suffix assignment selects only one endpoint, so its proof establishes distinct residuals, not F₂ linear independence. Theorem 19 is a deterministic OBDD size consequence. Thus neither a fully induced matching nor parity total width may be quoted from its conclusion.

**Parity input.** Henrik Brosenne, Matthias Homeister and Stephan Waack, *Characterizing the complexity of boolean functions represented by well-structured graph-driven parity-FBDDs*, RAIRO Theoretical Informatics and Applications **36(3)** (2002), 229–247, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1051/ita:2002011), [primary scan](https://www.numdam.org/article/ITA_2002__36_3_229_0.pdf), definitions pp. 230–233, equations (1)–(11) and Lemmas 1.5–1.6 pp. 235–239, Theorem 1.7 pp. 240. Boolean functions form an F₂ vector space; acceptance counts paths modulo two. OBDDs use line orderings. Lemma 1.5's proof expresses restrictions through reached-node resulting functions. Theorem 1.7 gives minimal level-node counts as differences of dimensions of nested spaces including later levels. Read the containment, kernel and basis-construction proofs. This is supporting machinery, not an equality of raw cut rank with sparse-level width. The approved dense model needs its own explicit cut factorization and sum accounting. No strongly-mixed or code hypothesis is assumed for independent sets.

**Rank input.** Stasys Jukna, *Boolean Function Complexity: Advances and Frontiers*, [author-hosted bool-V7 draft](https://web.vu.lt/mif/s.jukna/boolean/bool-V7.pdf), title marked “Draft”, §4.3, Lemma 4.11, printed pp. 100–101 (PDF pages 107–108). Rows and columns of D_(r,k) are subsets of [r] of size at most k; the entry is 1 exactly for disjoint sets. For 1<=k<=r the lemma asserts full rank over GF(2), sum_(i=0)^k binom(r,i). Read the complete proof, including maximal nonzero support, Boolean substitution, and a nonzero column coordinate. Future use with k=r must identify the exact restricted matrix and cite this lemma; no reproof or real-rank transfer is needed. Numbering and pagination refer to this inspected draft, not an uninspected published edition. This author book proof is adequate for the standard rank input.

**Scope check.** Stephen Cook's [official Clay description](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/pvsnp.pdf), §1, pp. 1–2, remains the version linked by the [current Clay page](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/p-vs-np/), inspected 2026-10-06.
