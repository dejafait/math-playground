# Noncommutative formula-IPS: inspected simulation statements

Source review dated 2026-10-03. This records citations and their scope; it contains no new simulation proof or bit-length bound.

## Hypotheses

Use a supplied, division-free, fan-in-two **tree formula** C(x,y), with ordered multiplication, for the clause polynomials of a fixed CNF and the Boolean and commutator axioms. The two certificate requirements are formal noncommutative identities C(x,0)=0 and C(x,F(x))=1, rather than equality merely on Boolean assignments. In the selected specialization, the field is F₂ and the CNF has width at most three. Arithmetic circuit sharing, division gates, other fields and unrestricted axiom representations are outside that specialization.

The source's gate-count parameter s and the notebook's full binary length must be distinguished. The latter includes the CNF, its variable labels, all axiom descriptions and placeholder labels, the supplied formula, and the eventual proof's indices and inference references. Count distinct variables after dense relabeling, not the largest binary identifier. A low-degree hypothesis must control the supplied syntax and its substitutions; cancellation in the output polynomial alone is insufficient for the selected bookkeeping test.

## Conclusion

The following are imports by citation, with no reproof here:

- **Li–Tzameret–Wang, Theorem 1.7 and its following note:** over F₂, noncommutative formula-IPS certificates for CNFs have Frege simulations of size s^{O(log s)}. The note gives poly(s·binom(d+r+1,r)) for degree r and depth d, and states polynomial simulation for logarithmic-degree certificates. **Theorem 4.1**, the detailed restatement, assumes a 3CNF. **Theorem 4.11** concerns syntactically homogeneous *zero-identity formulas*, not arbitrary homogeneous IPS certificates after axiom substitution.
- **Grochow–Pitassi, Theorem 4.1:** the general commutative circuit-IPS comparison with EF assumes polynomial-size EF proofs of the PIT axioms for a correct polynomial-size PIT circuit family. It does not remove that premise using external verification alone.
- **Chatterjee–Chatterjee–Ghosal–Mukhopadhyay, Theorems 1.2 and 3.3:** the September 2026 manuscript states a quasipolynomial Frege simulation over F₂ for two-bucket partially commutative formula-IPS. This enlarges the identity model but supplies no stated polynomial general-degree replacement.
