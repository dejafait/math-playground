The target was a fixed polynomial original-Q ledger for the specified
literal implementation of corrected axiom/MP extraction with renaming
inferences. The existing source assessment supports this diagnostic;
it does not supply a general SF/EF-to-affine-ABP theorem.

**WHY IT FAILS.** [L099](../lemmas/L099-renaming-literal-context-copy-obstruction.md)
gives explicit assumption-free proofs with n swap diamonds and complete
input Q≤C(n+2)³. Backward requests force 2^n whole copies of one axiom
seed under distinct actions on used labels. Every copy reaches the
actual final output through nonzero individual multipliers. The count
ignores unused dictionary entries and survives injective occurrence
representatives and complementation. Noninjective placeholder reception
is sound, with a collapsed commutator mapped to zero; that is not the
failure. The independent step-388 all-size audit in L099 finds no
substantive defect and justifies the same count for newly generated
complemented seeds without assuming equivariance of seed generation.

Stop the universal polynomial ledger for this whole-seed-copy
implementation. The source CNFs have immediate alternative proofs;
this is not optimal-witness, SF/EF or SAT hardness. Partial block sharing,
factoring collections of contexts, changing the seed or output, and
different proof normalization are outside the tested implementation.
Any new mechanism needs its own exact target and source assessment.
