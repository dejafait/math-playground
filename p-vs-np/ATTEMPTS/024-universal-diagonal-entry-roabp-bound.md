**WHY IT FAILS.** [L051](../lemmas/L051-valid-certificate-diagonal-entry-roabp-obstruction.md) realizes FSTW Corollary 6.22's hard scalar product inside valid, polynomial-bit certificate packages. Its 2^floor(n/2) width threshold is superpolynomial in Q, even after paying the full explicit matrix dictionary. Stop this universal supplied-output representation claim, subject to a separate critical audit of L051. The same formulas admit an easy certificate, so certificate selection, narrower certificate classes and changed receivers are not excluded. Matrix satisfiability and receiving identities remain independently unresolved; this is no every-certificate or ER lower bound and no P-versus-NP conclusion.
