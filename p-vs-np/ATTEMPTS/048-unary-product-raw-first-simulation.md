# Attempt 048 — universal raw-first unary-product simulations

Step 527, 2026-10-27. The full equations, counterexample, constant-free
subfamily and exact right-closed comparison are canonical in
[L113](../lemmas/L113-unary-product-raw-simulation-obstruction.md).

**WHY IT FAILS.** Requiring separate scalar-one and x intertwiners on every
arrow preserves a series in which the positions of constant edges are
marked. Independent FG/GF product wrappers need not preserve that series,
even when their actual unary polynomials agree. L113 exhibits an exact
coefficient mismatch, so increasing support, adding intermediates or
merely forbidding constants inside operands cannot repair the universal
raw-first proposal. This is a failure of that sufficient contract, not
of the intended right-closed equality or of all sparse chains. The
closed-only contract remains an independently testable continuation under
the existing unary-product assessment; L112's cut-table failure is also
retained. No historical receiver or accounting route is reopened.
