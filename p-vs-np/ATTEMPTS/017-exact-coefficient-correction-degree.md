# Attempt 017 — Reduce coefficients while retaining exact corrections

The proposal reduced each shared x-only coefficient P to a low-degree representative r and carried an original-Boolean/commutator witness E with E(x,0)=0 and E(x,B)=P+r. A local multiplication update and coefficient transport would preserve both IPS identities, permitting a changed output certificate and nonlinear correction placeholders.

**WHY IT FAILS.** [L023](../lemmas/L023-exact-coefficient-correction-degree-obstruction.md) proves that the ordered update is valid and preserves small shared size, but every exact correction has degree at least half the degree of P+r. Applied to L022's actual coefficient and any polynomial-degree representative, this is superpolynomial in supplied ER length. Repeated squaring makes the issue sharp: a constant-degree reduced representative has linear-size correction circuits of exponential formal degree, forced by the nonlinear correction product. Stop the requirement of independently polynomial-degree exact lifts. This does not bound the degree after cancellation across a full certificate and does not refute direct reduced per-clause invariants or alternative certificates. No proof-system or decision lower bound follows.
