# Attempt 020 — Use degree control for general same-output circuit-to-ABP conversion

The proposed intermediate route was to convert arbitrary polynomial-size, polynomial-syntactic-degree shared noncommutative circuits into explicit polynomial-size affine-edge ABPs computing the identical formal polynomial, as a representation step toward the existing forward proof simulation. This was an independent test of degree control, rather than Attempt 016's exponential-degree obstruction.

**WHY IT FAILS.** [L033](../lemmas/L033-polynomial-degree-palindrome-affine-abp-obstruction.md) reproduces Nisan's palindrome separation over F₂ with the broader receiving model and full binary accounting: the degree-2k, linear-gate recurrence forces at least 2^k vertices in every identical-output affine-edge DAG. Constant edges admit polynomial normalization and do not remove its cut-rank lower bound. Stop the blanket degree-only same-output conversion premise. The family is not shown to be an IPS certificate or an unavoidable L025 output, and changing certificates or using further structure remains open. This is neither an ER nor a SAT lower bound; the known result and all earlier stop evidence are preserved.
