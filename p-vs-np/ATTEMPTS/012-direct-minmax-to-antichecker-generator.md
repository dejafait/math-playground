# Direct minmax import into the feasible antichecker transfer

Reviewed 2026-09-27. The proposal was to obtain Pich–Santhanam, arXiv:2312.08163v1, Theorem 7's generator premise by citing Lipton–Young's small-support theorem and constructive variants.

**WHY IT FAILS.** The [source comparison](../foundations/09-antichecker-existence-and-generation.md) separates a short antichecker, an existential sampling circuit and a uniform generator with certified assignment data. Lipton–Young's size theorem covers the first; its uniform constructions use Σ₂ resources. The inspected greedy alternative requires optimization and weight updates not implemented for the succinct SAT/circuit game. None supplies the required polynomial-time function or S^1_2 proof of its solver-or-antichecker guarantee. Pich–Santhanam explicitly warns that ordinary existential provability yields interactive witnesses whose later outputs depend on earlier assignments, so its direct transfer proof does not follow that way either. This stops the direct citation-based import, not all feasible antichecker constructions or the weaker uniform transfer. It proves no impossibility or unprovability. A superpolynomial EF/ER lower bound remains independently missing.
