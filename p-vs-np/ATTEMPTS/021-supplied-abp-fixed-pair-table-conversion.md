# Attempt 021 — Convert L025's prescribed pair table from affine-ABP inputs

The proposal was to bound the aggregate identical-output affine-ABP size of L025's ordered commutator-pair table by a polynomial in T+S+E_ABP+d+h, assuming every supplied representative and relation already has an explicit affine ABP satisfying every formal promise.

**WHY IT FAILS.** [L034](../lemmas/L034-supplied-abp-pair-table-obstruction.md) supplies a promise-valid used-extension ER family with d=1, h=3 and polynomial input encoding. Projecting one prescribed pair output to one independent commutator placeholder gives an iterated two-sided commutator with a binomial-size identity coefficient submatrix. Nisan's cut-rank mechanism, with finite constant-path closure, forces at least 2^k/(k+1) affine-ABP vertices. Thus small supplied graphs and degree bounds do not control this exact recurrence. Its separate critical audit on 2026-10-07 found no substantive error; the full checks are in canonical L034. The relation witnesses use an easy original-clause contradiction, and the extension representatives are not faithful on every original assignment; those stronger conditions were not part of the tested promises. Alternative witnesses, certificates, representations and refutations remain open. No ER or SAT lower bound follows. Preserve Attempts 016/020, which concern different conversion claims.
