# Normalizing constructive fixed-exponent witnesses by padding

Tested 2026-09-25. The proposal was to turn the constructive Kannan witnesses into one polynomially bounded verifier by increasing their input lengths, while retaining enough of their individual circuit lower bounds to defeat every polynomial bound on the combined language.

**WHY IT FAILS.** [L009](../lemmas/L009-padded-join-exponent-tradeoff.md) gives a clocked join with one common Sigma_2^P bound, explicitly charging parameter-dependent setup. The source gives hardness exponent j but an alternating resource budget with exponent j^2. Padding to length n^(a_j) up to logarithmic factors and covering this budget by N^C forces a_j C>=j^2. Fixed-tag restrictions therefore offer only the bounded threshold j/a_j<=C/j; the explicit clock construction gives 1/j. The universal stage also remains unless a separate collapse is assumed. Under P=NP the common padded language would be in P, with core circuit bounds consistent with all the supplied lower bounds. This stops this resource-normalization and restriction mechanism. It does not prove that the padded join has small circuits unconditionally, that every algorithm needs the displayed budget, or that improved constructions and other uses of Kannan's theorem are impossible.
