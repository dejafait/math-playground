# Dropping the supplied covers from the successful two-round transfer

Assessed 2026-10-03. [L015](../lemmas/L015-supplied-kpt-output-cover-transfer.md) proves that separately supplied complete-output covers and short coverage proofs suffice. It does not construct those premises from an adaptive KPT strategy. This records the remaining obstruction when one tries to reuse its finite-case proof without them; the conditional transfer itself is successful.

**WHY IT FAILS.** Concrete antichecker challenges depend on the complete fixed tuples. Polynomial output length or a per-history candidate count does not provide a polynomial list covering the tuples over all free first assignments, or a short proof of that cover. Without the supplied coverage implication, separate case implications cannot exclude an unlisted output. The [finite Boolean check](../scripts/kpt-cover/case-inference.txt) includes a valuation witnessing that failure of the proof inference; it is not a counterexample to a concrete KPT strategy. Even on an unsatisfiable F, semantic vacuity of the cover does not grant its short EF proof. Thus the cover-construction and proof-bound premises are parked, not discharged by L015, and no additional exploration budget is allocated to repeated source recovery or to dropping them. The earlier canonical-recovery obstruction in ATTEMPTS/013 is preserved. This is no impossibility theorem for cover construction, no proof-size lower bound, and no separation of P from NP.
