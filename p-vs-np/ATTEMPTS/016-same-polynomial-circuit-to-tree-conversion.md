The imported noncommutative formula degree/size bound excludes every polynomial-size arithmetic tree for that identical polynomial, despite L021's polynomial-size shared circuit. This strengthens the earlier syntactic unfolding obstruction to a same-output representation obstruction. It does not exclude a translation producing a different certificate: the very same original CNF already has L019's constant-size certificate. No all-certificate hardness, ER lower bound or P-versus-NP conclusion follows. Preserve the circuit construction and earlier stops; any continuation must allow changing the output polynomial or choose another mechanism.
