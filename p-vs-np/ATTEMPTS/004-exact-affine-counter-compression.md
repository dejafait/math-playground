# Exact F₂-affine lifting as counter-trace compression

Tested 2026-09-25. The proposal was to encode configurations in a vector space of dimension polynomial in their bit width, represent every step by one affine map, and recover configuration bits affinely. Explicit matrices and vectors could then evaluate long blocks by repeated squaring. The intended use was structured computation certificates for a diagonal language; efficient construction, validation, and coverage of the needed computations would still require proofs.

**WHY IT FAILS.** [L004](../lemmas/L004-exact-affine-counter-dimension.md) proves that even the cyclic w-bit counter has minimum exact lift dimension 2^(w−1) over F₂ with affine bit decoding. The lower bound allows arbitrary encodings and uses just the top-bit decoder; a matching construction shows the threshold is exact. It excludes polynomial-dimensional explicit lifted states for this proposed universal representation. It does not exclude compact implicit descriptions, modular arithmetic over other rings, nonlinear decoders, or other certificate families. Counting itself remains easy. No lower bound on every NP verifier or on unrestricted computation follows.
