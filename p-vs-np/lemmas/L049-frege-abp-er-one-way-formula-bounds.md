**Eliminate extra Frege variables and match the polynomial-system premise.** Assume B_Frege,3, choose a fixed polynomial majorant bL^d for its bound, with b>=1 and integer d>=0, and fix any UNSAT F without an empty clause. Select one promised ordinary Frege proof pi of NOT Phi_F, with full bit length S<=bL^d. Proof variables that do not occur in F are not allowed in the final certificate alphabet. Simultaneously substitute false for every such variable in every line of pi. Frege axiom instances and inference rules are closed under uniform formula substitution: each transformed axiom remains an instance of its fixed schema, and each transformed rule retains its substituted premises and conclusion. If constants are not primitives of the chosen Frege system, use D=(x_* AND NOT x_*) for false, where x_* is the first occurring original variable, and its negation for true. Every old formula tree acquires at most a constant-factor number of nodes per substituted leaf. There are at most S written variable occurrences and at most S records, and line-reference bounds remain polynomial. This argument does not substitute extension definitions or unfold a shared circuit.

For clarity about FSTW's receiving premise, interpret each clause equation c_j=0 propositionally: its factors are literal falsity bits, ordered multiplication is AND, and F₂ addition is XOR. For a clause of width at most three, the formula expressing c_j=0 is equivalent to that actual clause. Each equivalence is a substitution into one of finitely many fixed Boolean tautologies on at most three argument bits. Repeated and complementary literals are handled by substituting their actual formulas into those tautologies; distinctness of their values is unnecessary. Different arithmetic tree parenthesizations have fixed associativity proofs. Every Boolean equation x_i²+x_i=0 expresses a fixed tautology. Constants can again be represented using x_*.

Assemble these clause equivalences and Boolean tautologies under the explicitly parenthesized conjunction by successive connective congruences. This identifies the conjunction of the equation predicates with Phi_F. Apply the fixed propositional implication carrying NOT Phi_F to the negation of that equation conjunction. The result is a Frege refutation of the polynomial system, in the sense of FSTW's premise. Equivalently it derives contradiction from those equation predicates as premises. This finite assembly uses polynomially many fixed-schema instances: each clause has bounded width, the conjunction has O(m+n) entries, and moving or adjoining an entry through a conjunction requires at most polynomially many congruences on trees of polynomial size. Every written name and context is bounded by a polynomial in L. Thus fixed constants c_0 and integer k_0>=3 can be chosen, independently of pi and F, so the receiving Frege **symbol count** satisfies

    s <= c_0 [ L(S+1) + L^k_0 ].                         (1)

No algebraic reasoning is assumed in the Frege proof: the displayed equations here stand for the explicit Boolean formulas just described. The arithmetic clause encodings themselves have at most three literal factors. The construction uses only the original occurring alphabet, and does not infer short Frege proofs of arbitrary semantic circuit equalities.
