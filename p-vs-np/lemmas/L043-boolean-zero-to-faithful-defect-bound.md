# L043 — Boolean-zero witness bounds imply aggregate faithful AND-defect bounds

## Hypotheses

Work in the free associative algebra F₂⟨x₁,…,x_n⟩, with precisely L041's universal premise W: one fixed polynomial p bounds the full encoding of some explicit acyclic affine-F₂ ABP H for every explicitly encoded x-only Boolean-zero graph G of full length E, with

    |H|≤p(E+2),       H(x,0)=0,       H(x,B_x)=f_G(x).

B_x consists only of x_i²+x_i and x_i x_j+x_j x_i for i<j, with independent Boolean and pair-once commutator placeholders. Complete original-name, axiom and placeholder dictionaries are charged to H. The premise permits constants, n=0, arbitrary designated endpoints, empty paths, cancellation and nonlinear placeholder use. It is unproved and supplies neither a constructor nor a promise tester.

Use exactly L042's V domain: every valid supplied used-extension signed-AND ER refutation of a width-at-most-three host CNF, and every complete supplied x-only representative collection faithful to the recursively defined values on **all** original Boolean assignments. Original and constant values are exact, and every signed complement is formally 1+r_v. All graphs, labels, original identifiers, root associations and complete input dictionaries are explicit and charged. Set

    M=2+T+S+E_val,       T=2+|K|+|π|.

S counts the abstract value nodes. E_val includes no sought witness graph. Neither a sought witness degree h nor a witness-size bound is included in M. Actual signed operands u,v of an AND node i retain their given order. There are no implicit graphs, circuit-valued edge labels or formula-size promises. The used-extension/host restrictions are retained, although the proof below needs only the corresponding faithful-value identities and explicit framing.

## Conclusion

W implies V. There is one polynomial Q, independent of the eligible host, supplied proof and supplied faithful collection, bounding the **aggregate full-bit encoding** of some witnesses H_i for all its AND nodes, with

    H_i(x,0)=0,       H_i(x,B_x)=r_i+r_u r_v.

Only Boolean and pair-once commutator placeholders occur; original-clause and extension-axiom placeholders are forbidden. All separately written graphs, dictionaries, endpoints and associations to their AND nodes are charged to Q(M). This is a conditional applicability result, not a proof of W or V, representative existence, construction, NP=coNP or either answer to P versus NP. The separate critical audit on 2026-10-09 found no substantive error within these hypotheses; the proof remains informal.

**Eligibility for every supplied collection.** Fix an arbitrary eligible instance and its entire supplied faithful collection. Let I be its AND nodes, s=|I|≤S. For each i∈I with its actual ordered signed operands u,v define, formally,

    d_i=r_i+r_u r_v.                                      (1)

For any assignment a∈{0,1}^n, all-assignment fidelity at the gate and its operands gives r_i(a)=r_u(a)r_v(a): the recursive AND function is scalar multiplication in F₂. Signed operands have their actual recursive complemented values because r_NOT_w=1+r_w formally, and originals/constants have exact values. Therefore d_i(a)=0. This proves Boolean-zero eligibility independently of whether a satisfies K. No host clause, earlier relation witness, formal commutativity or Boolean reduction in the free algebra is used to obtain (1). Repeated operands cause no change: two scalar factors are still the specified factors.

**Explicit ordered defect graphs and endpoints.** Make three separate copies of the supplied representative graphs for r_i,r_u,r_v, even if some roots or operands coincide or the input storage shares vertices. Use one branch for the r_i copy. On the other branch connect the designated exit of the r_u copy to the designated entrance of the r_v copy with a constant-1 edge. Add a fresh entrance with 1-edges to the entrances of the r_i and r_u copies, and a fresh exit with 1-edges from the exits of the r_i and r_v copies. Keep every original vertex, edge and affine label within each copy. No inter-copy edge other than those five connectors is added. The resulting G_i is an explicit x-only affine graph.

It is acyclic: each copy is a DAG; all connectors go from the new entrance, then along one branch (u before v on the product branch), then to the new exit. There is no connector back to an earlier copy. An accepting path must choose either the i branch or the ordered u,v branch. Within a copy it follows a path between the designated endpoints. If it leaves a designated exit along an internal edge, it cannot return to that exit to take its sole outgoing connector without a cycle. Incoming edges to a designated entrance likewise do not create extra accepting paths. Thus nonextremal endpoints cause no additional contributions. If a copy's endpoints coincide, its only designated internal path is empty, with weight 1; any nonempty return would be a cycle. The connectors preserve this contribution. If there is no designated path, that branch contributes zero. All these statements concern actual paths, including ones whose labels vanish or whose contributions cancel.

Finite distributivity over these finite path sets now gives f_G_i=r_i+r_u r_v with exactly the order in (1). No path enumeration is required to construct the graph. Zero/parallel edges, unequal lengths, dead vertices, constant outputs, repeated signed operands and coincident endpoints are retained. A constant-zero summand can have disconnected endpoints; a constant-one summand or empty path contributes exactly 1. No formal identity between the supplied root and a tree expression is assumed.

**One complete graph-input bound.** Fix the explicit list encodings used in L041/L042. Constants below depend only on those encodings. Record and affine-entry counts are at most a constant times their full written length. Since E_val≤M, each supplied representative and all dictionary payloads have length at most M; each identifier's written length is at most M, and the number of explicitly listed originals is at most a constant times M. Graph sharing, if any, is charged by its explicit stored records and root associations; extracting three copies of the underlying recorded graphs costs at most three copies of that storage, not an unfolding of paths.

For a G_i, the three copies have O(M) vertex, edge and label-entry records. Renumber graph vertices densely, rewrite their edge/endpoints references, and retain the complete original dictionary, including originals unused by the defect. The number of new vertices is O(M), so dense vertex references have O(log(M+2)) bits. Original-symbol references and their full name map can be retained, or densely renamed with the map retained. A deliberately loose charge of O(M) bits per rewritten record, label occurrence and retained identifier gives O((M+1)²) bits for the graph and its ordinary framing. Five connectors and its two new root records fit that bound.

Use the same original ordering and fixed canonical Boolean/pair-once placeholder naming for every application of W. The full B_x list has O((M+1)²) entries and bounded word length per axiom; even repeating O(M)-bit original names in each entry gives O((M+1)³) bits. The dictionary construction is therefore polynomial and introduces no symbols outside W. Host clause dictionaries may remain part of the supplied E_val; they are not symbols of G_i or H_i. The graph input itself needs only its complete original alphabet and graph framing. Charge any accompanying B_x preparation as well in this conservative estimate; do not insert foreign placeholder symbols into the x-only graph alphabet. Consequently one absolute C≥1 works simultaneously for every i and every collection:

    E_i=|G_i|≤C(M+1)³.                                   (2)

This bounds actual allowed graph encodings, without padding, a formula conversion, a fidelity certificate, any H or any h. The described input-graph and dictionary preparation takes polynomial bit time in M; no witness-finding time is claimed.

**Existential witnesses and one aggregate polynomial.** By eligibility and (2), W supplies some H_i for each i∈I satisfying the two required formal identities for the complete original dictionary. Choose a witness for each of the finitely many nodes. The choice is existential; it does not invoke a procedure or impose pathwise placeholder linearity. All identities include the entire nonlinear placeholder sectors and their possible cancellations. W forbids clause/extension placeholders, so none enter these witnesses.

Do not assume p is monotone. Choose fixed integers A≥1 and d≥0 with p(t)≤A(t+1)^d for every integer t≥0, for example by rounding up a bound on the sum of absolute coefficients and taking d at least its degree. With

    P(M)=A(C(M+1)³+3)^d,

each complete witness has |H_i|≤p(E_i+2)≤P(M). A witness includes its complete original, B_x and placeholder dictionaries, even for unused symbols. Retain these full dictionaries in every separately written output; no implicit sharing or omission is needed. Keep each witness in a separate local graph frame, so vertex names need not be globally merged. The common original names, axiom order and placeholder map agree by construction with those fixed for its G_i. Include the association to i and that witness's designated root/endpoints explicitly.

If the host interface requires its complete original-axiom registry, copy the supplied registry as additional framing data, at cost at most M per block. Its clause entries are not symbols in H_i's free alphabet and cannot occur in witness edge labels. Thus full host framing, if retained, does not add forbidden clause placeholders to the polynomial.

For one witness, its supplied full graph/dictionary encoding has at most P(M) bits; an AND-node identifier or association copied from the input has at most M bits. Separators, local-frame tags, record counts and their binary lengths add at most a fixed polynomial in these actual written quantities. More explicitly, there are O(P(M)+M+1) records/bits to frame, each identifier or length can be written in O(P(M)+M+1) bits, so D(P(M)+M+1)² is a conservative full-bit bound for that block. This also covers dense reserialization if required; it cannot introduce superpolynomial name lengths or additional mathematical symbols. Include an empty-list header and one common original/axiom dictionary if s=0, at cost O((M+1)³). For a sufficiently large fixed D the entire aggregate is bounded by the single polynomial

    Q(M)=D(M+1)³(P(M)+M+1)².                              (3)

Indeed s≤S≤M bounds the number of separately written blocks, and (3) dominates their sum and the common framing. All constants and exponents are independent of the host, proof, collection, node, variable count and chosen witnesses. The achieved threshold is polynomial **aggregate** full length in exactly M, as required by V; an individual graph bound alone would not meet that threshold.

**Boundary cases and logical limits.** If I is empty, there are no identities to discharge, and the empty aggregate with its complete required framing satisfies (3); W need not be invoked. If n=0, there is one scalar assignment. Fidelity makes every eligible defect zero on it, so W applies to its constant-only graph with an empty B_x/placeholder dictionary. Such a free-algebra polynomial is its scalar constant, hence zero; the argument does not require guessing a Boolean promise or adding a variable. Any allowed AND nodes with constant values are covered, and the empty-node case remains vacuous. All original variables are retained when n>0 even if no defect uses them. Arbitrary long original identifiers are already included in E_val and the above bounds.

W's existential witnesses do not test the supplied collection's fidelity; fidelity is a hypothesis used only for invocation. The proof is universal over the given supplied collection and never replaces its representatives by more convenient ones. Finite graph witnesses also admit finite formal-degree bounds: their path lengths are at most their vertex counts minus one, so one can choose h after their existence. This is not an input to (2) or (3). No witness constructor, earlier H, class equality, U, original-clause placeholder or representation theorem is smuggled into M.

Thus the witnesses and bound (3) satisfy precisely V. This conditional bridge can supply the missing local AND witnesses when faithful representatives and W are assumed. Independent existence/construction gaps, alternative nonfaithful data, every-certificate hardness and unrestricted SAT transfer remain unresolved. Neither W nor V is proved or refuted by this implication. No main-problem candidate is claimed.

**Separate critical audit — 2026-10-09.** This audit uses the pre-existing EXPLORE scope in `drafts/literature/2026-10-08-boolean-zero-to-faithful-defect-critical-audit.md`. It checks this proof, rather than establishing W or importing either complexity consequence of L041/L042. The supporting source remains the precisely cited Raz–Shpilka graph-composition passage above; the arbitrary-endpoint and full-encoding obligations are checked here. No further representation theorem is needed.

For eligibility, scalar evaluation is a homomorphism from the free algebra to F₂ for each assignment. All-assignment fidelity therefore identifies the three evaluated roots with the gate's recursive value and its actual two signed inputs, giving zero in (1). A contradictory host does not make this condition vacuous: the quantifier ranges over all original assignments, not satisfying assignments. Exact base values and complements supply the signed cases. Repetition of an operand does not authorize free-ring idempotence; the construction retains two ordered copies and uses equality only after scalar evaluation.

For path correspondence, write a path from the fresh entrance to the fresh exit as its successive visits to the disjoint copies. The only possible copy sequences are i and u,v. Within each visited copy, the entry and departure connectors force the segment to start and end at that copy's designated endpoints. A directed path in a DAG cannot revisit a vertex. In particular, it cannot pass through its designated exit, continue internally and later return to that exit to depart, nor return to its entrance through an incoming edge. Conversely, each designated i path, or each pair of designated u and v paths, extends uniquely by the indicated connectors to an accepting path. Disjoint copies make this inverse valid even when operands, roots or stored underlying graphs coincide. The path weights are respectively the i weight and the u weight followed by the v weight; all connectors contribute 1. If endpoints coincide the segment is exactly the empty path, while a missing designated path removes the branch. Thus the correspondence includes constants and arbitrary nonextremal endpoints before any summation or cancellation. Dead edges, parallel edges and zero labels do not invalidate it. Finite distributivity gives precisely (1), without a path expansion or a formula-size assumption.

For (2), actual list records and original-name entries are paid for by E_val, so their number and each written identifier length are O(M). Three copies use O(M) records even if a whole shared graph must be copied for each selected root; they do not copy one graph per accepting path. Dense graph renaming gives O(log(M+2))-bit vertex references. Retaining long symbol names and charging O(M) bits per occurrence still costs O((M+1)²) for graph data. At most O(M) explicitly named originals give O((M+1)²) Boolean/commutator axioms, each with O(M)-bit name payload, so the optional accompanying complete axiom list fits O((M+1)³). The actual x-only G_i input contains no host-clause symbol. Charging accompanying dictionary preparation only overestimates its length and is not an invocation of W on a different alphabet. Unused originals remain explicit, so neither an implicit large variable universe nor a free name dictionary escapes the bound.

For (3), apply the universal W from L041, not its weaker sufficient restriction to 3-CNF challenge graphs. For every i, put t=E_i+2. Then p(t)≤A(t+1)^d≤A(C(M+1)³+3)^d=P(M); both inequalities hold without assuming p itself increasing. The constants A,d come from the single fixed p, and C is encoding-dependent only. Each selected witness already includes its complete dictionaries. A separate local frame avoids any global vertex-identification cost; its node association and any copied host registry have O(M) bits, and a registry is metadata, not permission to use its forbidden symbols in edge labels. The conservative per-block bound D(P(M)+M+1)² includes all serialization overhead. Multiplying by s≤M and adding O((M+1)³) common framing is dominated by (3) after increasing the fixed D. Hence this is one polynomial for the entire collection, not an instance-dependent exponent or merely an individual-witness bound. Finite existential choices require neither a constructor nor a fidelity tester. Both identities remain formal in the full placeholder algebra, with nonlinear sectors allowed.

For zero AND nodes, only the charged empty aggregate framing remains. For n=0, the free algebra has only constants and its unique scalar evaluation is the identity; eligible defects are formally zero, so W's stated constant case applies with an empty axiom list. The empty-path convention causes no exceptional output: a coincident-endpoint representative is 1, whereas an H satisfying H(x,0)=0 cannot itself have coincident endpoints in a DAG. W asks for some H and imposes no requirement to preserve input endpoints. A finite degree can be assigned after witness selection and never enters M or the invocation bound.
