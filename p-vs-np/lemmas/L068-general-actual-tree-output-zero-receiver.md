# L068 — General actual-tree native/literal-image output-zero reception

## Hypotheses

Fix a positive integer admission constant a and all encodings. Supply L067's
explicit ordered division-free F₂ occurrence tree f, with U nodes, depth d,
distinct ordered dictionary names x_0,...,x_(n-1), one name q,
and either L066 zero-edge convention. Require
d ≤ a·bit_length(U+1). Repeated letter/subtree occurrences, unused dictionary
entries, constants, mixed degree, n=0 and q/name collisions are allowed.
As in L067/L066, every name is a nonempty ASCII alphanumeric/underscore
payload; arbitrary ASCII punctuation is not admitted.
The polynomial of f is zero in the **free noncommutative F₂ algebra** on
the dictionary. Boolean-only or commutative vanishing is insufficient.

Also receive any explicit finite list of complete assumption-free native
proof occurrences admitted by L066. They are validated and retained, including
every unused/deep record, complete substitution field, header and reference;
they supply no additional premise. Repeated paths/contents are distinct
occurrences. Charge all original source, proof, path and payload bytes in
Q_0, with Q_0≥2. The fixed checked libraries of L056 and L064–L066 are
paid constants, not caller-selected proofs. No external cache is received.
Any separately received source graph, tables or suffixes must equal the
actual L066 reconstruction. Such copies count toward Q_0.

Use fully expanded native X,E,P,Y,Z and L056's literal translation τ.
F_native is the direct source translation, not a Boolean simplification.
Only native proof inputs and their constructed literal images are covered;
independently supplied ordinary NOT/OR proofs are outside the contract.

## Conclusion

There is a deterministic abstract complete-record construction ending at
the actual last native record

    E(F_native,Z),    Y=(q→q), Z=NOT Y,

and at its exact literal-image last record. It constructs the complete
local and closed-coordinate packages, exact-length tables, indexed L064
chain, source bridge and final composition. It retains every complete
coordinate prefix and its nested local/optional occurrences when importing
the chain, and both entire bridge/chain occurrences in the final proof.
The final MPs use the transported **(level 0,row 0)** root of that chain.
No equal-endpoint replacement, ancestor pruning or recursively enlarged
suffix is used. All term abbreviations are expanded in serialization.

One fixed polynomial in Q_0, for fixed a/encodings, bounds construction and
checking bit time and the aggregate full binary output, including both
formats, all intermediate exports, original inputs, fixed libraries,
locations and the finite metadata specified below. A source-only recursive
majorant is given in §3; it contains no measured intermediate parameter.
This proves an informal all-size conditional composition by reproduction
of existing machinery. A generic executable wrapper and a nonempty-letter
joined stream are **not** implemented or verified here. The separate step-247
informal audit below finds no substantive defect and makes the singleton-row
slack in the locations bound explicit; it verifies no generic executable.

This is the zero-identity component for already supplied source syntax.
It does not complete the two-identity/original-CNF receiver, ordinary-input
completeness, certificate existence, arbitrary ER representation, W/V/U,
or unrestricted SAT decision/separation. It is not a main-problem candidate
or a claim of originality beyond the sources checked.
