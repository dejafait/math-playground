# L048 — Formula-length equivalence of two affine-ABP certificate bounds

## Hypotheses

Work in the free associative algebra over F₂ and fix the ordinary explicit binary list encoding used in C046a and L047. A formula package F is a width-at-most-three CNF with its written clause/literal order and a complete dictionary of exactly its distinct **occurring** original variable identifiers, in deterministic lexicographic order. The dictionary stores their actual name payloads. Every clause/literal and dictionary entry is written. Repetitions and complementary literals are retained. No external alphabet, maximum-index variable range or unused declared original variable is part of this convention. Write N=|F| and L=N+2 for the complete formula-package bit length.

Use L041's unconditional ordered definitions: for clause j, c_j is the product of literal falsity factors v(x_i)=1+x_i and v(¬x_i)=x_i, in written order; s_j=1+c_j and f_F=s₁…s_m. Empty products are 1. Order B_x first by the Boolean axioms x_i²+x_i, then by the commutators x_i x_k+x_k x_i for i<k in lexicographic pair order. Let A=(c₁,…,c_m,B_x). Equal clause polynomials have separate positions.

Generate independent placeholders deterministically from positions of A: its a-th placeholder has name t_a, encoded as a fixed namespace tag and the dense binary positive integer a. The original and placeholder namespaces are distinct. The B_x-only placeholder dictionary is the restriction to positions m+1,…,|A|_positions; σ_i and τ_ik are role notation for those same names. Thus both formats use the same B_x symbols, without requiring a second naming convention starting at 1. All these dictionaries and the ordered axiom words are explicit. No arbitrarily chosen fresh name payload can be hidden in N.

In either format, an ABP is an explicitly encoded finite acyclic affine-F₂ edge-labelled graph with designated entrance/exit and the sum of ordered label products over their paths. The empty path contributes 1. Affine labels list constant bits and individual symbol coefficients/occurrences. Allow parallel/zero edges, dead vertices, unequal path lengths, nonextremal or coincident endpoints, mixed labels, nonlinear placeholder paths and cancellation. Exclude implicit graphs and circuit-valued labels. The complete bit length |C| or |H| includes graph records, all label entries, endpoints, complete permitted original/placeholder dictionaries, axiom lists (A for C and B_x for H), unused permitted graph symbols, raw names and framing. Charge repeated dictionaries again when forming the component input parameter Q. An original variable or permitted placeholder may be unused in a graph; it must still be listed in its complete dictionary. Only the formula's original alphabet is permitted.

Consider the following two unproved assertions for this common convention:

* **P_A:** There exists one fixed polynomial p such that, for every unsatisfiable F, there exists some C over x and the placeholders of A with |C|≤p(N+2) and formal identities C(x,0)=0 and C(x,A(x))=1.
* **P_product:** There exists one fixed polynomial q such that, for every unsatisfiable F, there exists some H over x and only the placeholders of B_x with |H|≤q(N+2) and formal identities H(x,0)=0 and H(x,B_x(x))=f_F(x).

The polynomial in each assertion is independent of F and of certificate choice. These are existence assertions, with no finder, all-assignment-faithful representative, supplied ER proof or pathwise-placeholder promise. Use the already audited local interfaces C046a and L047, not any universal W premise.

## Conclusion

For the stated occurring-variable formula convention,

    P_A ⇔ P_product.

More precisely, there is an encoding-dependent constant a≥1 with the complete formula-derived A, B_x and naming packages bounded by aL³. If p(z)≤b z^d for integers z≥2, with b≥1 and integer d≥0, set e=max(3,d). C046a gives P_product with the fixed majorant

    q_new(z)=K_1(1+a+b)^80 z^(80e).

If q(z)≤b z^d for z≥2, L047 gives P_A with the fixed majorant

    p_new(z)=K_2(1+a+b)^6 z^(6e).

Here K_1,K_2≥1 are the fixed complete output constants of C046a and L047. Both conversions take deterministic polynomial bit time in the full supplied input; this does not provide a finder from F. The bounds are conservative and are not claimed optimal.

This is a local application of existing interfaces and complete encoding accounting, classified REPRODUCTION rather than certified originality. It establishes neither assertion, extends neither to all Boolean-zero graphs, and supplies no general ER-to-ABP representation, W/V/U, fidelity, ER boundedness, NP=coNP or unrestricted SAT decision/separation. The separate critical audit on 2026-10-10 (step 139), recorded below, found no substantive error under the stated occurring-variable convention.

**Complete formula-only ledger.** Fix constants c_r≥1 depending only on the encoding. Because every record is written and consumes bits, n,m and the number of literals are at most c_1L. Each raw original name has at most L bits. This counts distinct actual names, not all integers up to the largest written binary index. The original-name dictionary has n written entries; its names and framing are already paid for in N. Repeating it a fixed number of times in the derived packages costs at most c_2L² even if references are stored redundantly.

Expand c_j only within its at-most-three factors. It has at most 2³=8 word occurrences, each of length at most three. Keep multiplicities, so this representation remains exact under F₂ cancellation. An empty clause has the one empty word c_j=1. The s_j expression has at most nine word occurrences. No expansion of s₁…s_m is used. Each Boolean axiom has two words of lengths two and one; each commutator has two words of length two. Hence B_x has n+n(n−1)/2 positions and A has

    M_A=m+n+n(n−1)/2 ≤ c_3L²

positions. Their total word, coefficient, symbol-occurrence and list records are at most c_4L². Writing a raw original name at each occurrence costs at most L bits per name; using dictionary references can only reduce that charge. Dense A-position placeholder names and their reference fields have at most c_5 log₂(L+2) bits, since M_A≤c_3L². Tags prevent collisions with original names even when the raw payloads look alike. Every axiom position, including a repeated clause, has its own written placeholder record; the B_x restriction keeps the corresponding A-position numbers. No additional symbols are introduced.

Counts, word-length fields, ordered position/role entries, namespace tags and framing have O(log(L+2)) bits per record in this fixed binary list encoding. The full original dictionary, complete A and B_x word lists, and complete placeholder/name/role dictionaries therefore have a combined bound aL³ after fixing a≥1 large enough for all cases L≥2. In particular each package separately obeys

    |A|≤aL³,              |B_x|_full≤aL³.             (1)

This includes every permitted placeholder, even one unused by a graph. Formula variables unused by a particular graph are also retained. Variables unused by the formula are excluded from *both* assertions; no claim about an unpaid or paid-for larger declared universe is needed. No full dictionary is copied once per axiom: the fixed package is one explicit list with its complete dictionaries, with raw name occurrences charged above. If several such packages are supplied to a component, each is charged in Q anew.

The ledger is constructive at the level needed for applicability: parse written records, sort/compare written names to obtain the deterministic original order, generate the at-most-eight clause words, loop over actually listed i and pairs i<k, and assign the dense position names. These loops create O(L²) bounded-length records and use only polynomial-size strings and actual written counts. Ordinary scans and string comparisons give deterministic polynomial bit time. This is the shared finite dictionary preparation; it does not construct a missing certificate or enumerate the product's words.

**P_A implies P_product.** Assume P_A and fix any unsatisfiable F. Choose one of its existentially supplied C. Its complete alphabet, ordered original A, independent placeholder positions, explicit affine-DAG model and both formal identities meet C046a's hypotheses. The occurring-only convention meets its phrase “exactly its original variable identifiers.” Its parameter is

    Q_C=2+|F|+|C|+|A|=L+|C|+|A|.

C046a outputs H with H(x,0)=0 and H(x,B_x)=f_F in the free algebra and |H|≤K_1Q_C^80. Its output preserves the B_x placeholder names by their unchanged A positions, which is exactly the common dictionary specified above; the role notation σ,τ does not rename or identify symbols. Its complete output includes all original names, B_x, unused permitted graph symbols and framing. Its stronger exactly-one-placeholder-edge output property is allowed by P_product but not required there.

Any fixed polynomial bound p has a majorant b z^d on integers z≥2: take d at least its degree and b large enough to dominate the sum of absolute coefficients and all fixed constants. Thus (1), |C|≤p(L) and e=max(3,d) give

    Q_C≤L+bL^d+aL³≤(1+a+b)L^e,
    |H|≤K_1(1+a+b)^80 L^(80e).                       (2)

The constants a,b,d,e,K_1 are fixed before F is chosen. For every unsatisfiable F the chosen C yields some H obeying the same (2). This establishes P_product with q_new; no common choice rule, uniform algorithm finding C, or monotonicity of the original p is assumed. The new bound is an increasing polynomial majorant. Component polynomial bit time is in Q_C for a *supplied* C, not a means of discovering C from F.

**P_product implies P_A.** Assume P_product and fix any unsatisfiable F. Choose an existentially supplied H. Its complete B_x dictionary, the corresponding common placeholder names and its two formal identities are exactly L047's supplied-H hypotheses. Its arbitrary nonlinear paths and cancellation are permitted; no pathwise restriction is needed. Supply also the formula-derived full A from (1). L047's enlargement from B_x positions to A positions keeps the same independent symbols under the present convention. It gives C(x,0)=0 and C(x,A)=1 with complete |C|≤K_2Q_H^6, where

    Q_H=2+|F|+|H|+|A|=L+|H|+|A|.

Choose fixed b,d dominating q on z≥2 and set e=max(3,d). Then

    Q_H≤L+bL^d+aL³≤(1+a+b)L^e,
    |C|≤K_2(1+a+b)^6 L^(6e).                        (3)

Every cost of the enlarged alphabet and full axiom list is included in L047's output. The repeated A charge in Q_H is deliberate, even though the input H already carries B_x. The constants in (3) are independent of F and the selected H, so p_new establishes P_A. Again no finder follows. The sharper K_0Q_H^4 size bound in L047 is unnecessary for the claimed common exponent-six bound.

**Boundary qualifications and continuation test.** The proof applies the two component conclusions without any degree, path-count or fidelity assumption. Nonextremal endpoints, dead records, zero edges, mixed labels and nonlinear cancellation remain allowed. Coincident endpoints have polynomial 1 by the acyclic empty-path convention and cannot satisfy the first identity in either premise. A no-path C has polynomial 0 and cannot satisfy its second identity; a no-path H can satisfy its second identity when f_F is formally zero and is included by L047. These inconsistent input cases do not create a missing existential case.

Empty clauses give c_j=1, s_j=0 and f_F=0. Both components explicitly handle them and n=0. In the occurring-only convention n=0 means every written clause is empty; a nonempty list of them is UNSAT and the zero H is permitted. With no clauses, n=0 and f_F=1; F is satisfiable and falls outside both all-UNSAT quantifiers. Repetitions, complementary literals and equal clause polynomials retain their written positions and distinct placeholders throughout. Scalar satisfaction is never substituted for either formal certificate identity.

The approved test is passed: both implications require only a supplied certificate, the complete formula-derived ledger (1) and the component formal identities/full-bit bounds. Attempts 020/021 concern prescribed-output conversion from other representations; no such conversion is invoked. Attempt 018's repeated approval-only route is not reopened. The result shows that these two restricted formula-length existence obligations are the same up to polynomial overhead, rather than removing either obligation. General ER-to-original-axiom ABP representation, either premise itself, W/V/U, faithful representatives, ER boundedness and unrestricted SAT transfer remain unresolved. No complete main-problem candidate is proposed.

**Independent critical audit, 2026-10-10 (step 139).** The prior SPECIALIZE assessment `drafts/literature/2026-10-10-formula-length-equivalence-critical-audit.md` covers precisely this audit. The mathematical inputs are the stated C046a and L047 interfaces, their canonical step-131/135 audits, and L041's unconditional ordered definitions. The primary-source comparisons above are reused as comparisons, not imported full matching theorems. This audit checks the application independently; it neither reproves the components nor tests their implementations.

For the ledger, count positions, word occurrences and symbol occurrences separately. The clause list has m positions, at most 8m word occurrences and at most 24m original-symbol occurrences. The Boolean part has n positions, 2n word occurrences and 3n original-symbol occurrences. The pair-once commutator part has n(n−1)/2 positions, n(n−1) word occurrences and 2n(n−1) original-symbol occurrences. These are upper bounds on the written lists with multiplicity, so no cancellation or distinct-word assumption is hidden. Empty words require a length/coefficient record even though they have no symbols; the word count pays for them. Consequently all these record counts are O(L²). Keeping both A and B_x repeats the latter two parts only a fixed number of times.

Every original payload has at most L bits, so even raw names at all O(L²) symbol occurrences cost O(L³). The original dictionary and any fixed repeated copies cost at most O(L²) under this deliberately redundant estimate. There are M_A=m+n+n(n−1)/2 placeholder records, and at most that many B-only records. Each contains a fixed namespace tag, an A-position index of O(log(L+2)) bits and boundedly many role/reference fields. Boolean roles can store one original identifier and commutator roles two; even using their raw payloads costs O(L) per such record, already within O(L³). Dense names depend on positions, never on the numeric value of an original name or on externally chosen fresh payloads. Word/list counts, lengths, coefficients, signs, order fields and namespace/record framing cost O(L² log(L+2)); global length fields have O(log(L+2)) bits because the total just counted is polynomial. For L≥2, log(L+2)≤L. Thus one encoding-dependent a bounds the combined packages by aL³, including n=0 and empty lists. This is a bound on complete formula-derived data, not a bound on an unknown certificate graph. Each graph's vertices, labels, roots and repeated dictionaries remain separately paid in |C| or |H|. No dictionary is replicated once per axiom in the fixed encoding, and no expanded word list for f_F is requested.

For alphabet alignment, index B_x by r=1,…,n+n(n−1)/2. Its r-th placeholder is literally the symbol t_(m+r) in both premises. It substitutes to zero in the first identity and to the same ordered B_x polynomial in the second. The clause placeholders t_1,…,t_m are distinct from these and from all original variables. C046a interprets roles by unchanged A positions and returns precisely the B-only restriction; its output has no additional originals or clause placeholders. L047 embeds each B role at A position m+r. Under the present convention this changes its dictionary position when needed, not its raw symbol or algebraic meaning. The embedding is injective even when two clause polynomials coincide, and commutes with both substitutions. Complete unused permitted entries are retained by both conclusions. There is no second indexing origin that silently turns t_(m+r) into t_r.

For the first receiving call, a chosen C has the explicit affine-DAG model, occurring original alphabet, ordered A and both formal identities required by C046a. Its conclusion is exactly the two identities and full H package required by P_product, for the identical written-order f_F. The additional pathwise output promise is harmless because P_product does not prohibit it. For the reverse call, a chosen H has exactly L047's complete B-only alphabet and both formal identities; L047 receives the separately supplied full A and returns both P_A identities with the full A dictionary. Arbitrary nonlinear placeholder paths and cancellation are expressly admitted in both receiving statements. L041's W premise, fidelity, degree bounds and ER data enter neither call. Neither call infers a formal identity from scalar unsatisfiability.

For the all-formula quantifiers, write the first assumption as ∃p ∀F∈UNSAT ∃C satisfying its two identities and |C|≤p(L). Fix that p first, and then fixed b≥1 and integer d≥0 with p(z)≤bz^d on integers z≥2. The encoding constant a and component constant K_1 were fixed independently of p and F. For every such F and any C satisfying the assumed bound, Q_C=L+|C|+|A|≤(1+a+b)L^e, e=max(3,d). C046a therefore supplies an H of length at most K_1(1+a+b)^80 L^(80e), with the same polynomial for every F. This proves ∃q_new ∀F∈UNSAT ∃H in the required order, without selecting certificates by an algorithm. Fixing q first and using L047 gives identically Q_H≤(1+a+b)L^e and K_2(1+a+b)^6 L^(6e), proving the reverse quantified assertion. The e≥3 choice absorbs the dictionary ledger even for d=0; no exponent depends on F. Repeated dictionaries stay in the component Q parameters and are never subtracted. C046a's exponent 80 is only a size exponent. Its fixed polynomial bit-time conclusion and L047's time bound concern supplied certificates; neither is a finder or a polynomial-time SAT decision procedure.

For boundaries, in a DAG coincident designated endpoints have only the empty path, yielding polynomial 1 before and after zero substitution. They cannot witness either first identity. A no-path C has polynomial 0 and fails its second identity. A no-path H is instead legitimate precisely when the required f_F is formally zero; L047 permits it. An empty clause gives c_j=1 and s_j=0, hence f_F=0. With n=0 and at least one clause, every clause is empty and zero H with empty B_x is valid; A still has its written clause positions and names. With no clauses, occurring-only means n=0, f_F=1 and the formula is satisfiable, so neither all-UNSAT premise owes a certificate. More generally, the scalar-evaluation inconsistency checks in both components cover a no-clause input even if presented outside this convention. Nonextremal endpoints, dead records, zero/mixed labels and cancellation remain inside the existing component hypotheses. Repeated and complementary literals are retained in order, and equal clauses retain distinct positions. None of these cases forces an extra axiom, variable or certificate-size premise.

All three discriminating tests pass: the full formula-derived package is O(L³), both calls receive exactly their stated inputs, and the two fixed majorants have the advertised exponents and quantifiers. No load-bearing error or bound repair was found. The equivalence is retained as an informal conditional application; it establishes neither premise and provides no new progress beyond the checked source comparisons.
