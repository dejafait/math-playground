# L057 — Checked single-factor distribution strings over a balanced XOR tree

## Hypotheses

Supply M=2^h fully written native NOT/implication formulas T_0,...,T_(M−1), h≥1, and one fully written native factor A. Use ASCII alphanumeric/underscore variable payloads; arbitrary finite binary identifiers may be injectively encoded first, paying their encoded lengths. All occurrences are copied formula trees, not formula-node references. Define literal abbreviations

    X(U,V)=((U→V)→NOT(V→U)), E(U,V)=NOT X(U,V),
    P(U,V)=NOT(U→NOT V).

Let B_M be the perfect ordered binary X tree on its M slots. Native proofs use ax-1/2/3 and MP as specified in L056. The literal image τ preserves NOT and replaces U→V with NOT τ(U) OR τ(V), retaining every resulting double negation. For j=native,image let t_j≥1 and d_j bound nodes and depth of A and every T_i in that format. Let B≥1 bound every written variable payload in bytes, including fixed schematic names.

No IPS identity, certificate-existence promise, extension variable or hypothesis on the truth of A/T_i is used. “Shallow” means the actual d_j is charged; the construction also applies to deeper supplied terms, with that depth retained in the bound. This is exactly the start-of-turn target in `drafts/literature/2026-10-11-balanced-distribution-proof-strings.md`, not an arbitrary ordinary-input normalization target.

## Conclusion

There is an explicitly constructed native Frege proof of

    E(P(A,B_M(T)), B_M(P(A,T_0),...,P(A,T_(M−1)))),          (1)

and a checked line-by-line NOT/OR image in the translated axiom/MP calculus. No completeness for arbitrary NOT/OR inputs or every ordinary Frege presentation is claimed. Set

    H=h+1, L=2706,
    (D_native,S_native)=(18,2040), (D_image,S_image)=(24,2712),
    J=L+(M−1)(h+1)(L+2),
    P_j=8·4^(H+1)(t_j+1)=128 M²(t_j+1).

The construction uses exactly M−1 distribution events, at most h−1 XOR context lifts per event, and at most J primitive lines. Every primitive line has depth at most D_j+c_j H+d_j, with c_native=3 and c_image=6. Every record, including all explicitly written axiom-substitution formulas, has at most S_j P_j nodes. The complete serialized uncompressed proof has at most

    (J+2)[(B+4)S_j P_j+256+64 ceil(log₂(J+2))] bytes.      (2)

Its full binary length is eight times (2). This is one fixed polynomial in M,t_j,B, and logarithmic depth when the supplied factor and slots have logarithmic depth. These are informal all-size accounting claims plus bounded complete string evidence; the separate step-174 critical audit below verifies this component, with a factor-path prose correction already covered by H. It reproduces known logical laws, without a claim of mathematical originality, a complete receiver or a P-versus-NP resolution.

The two literal templates express those known Boolean laws; synthesis handles their double-negation/definition differences. ### Mixed-tree procedure and endpoint

For syntax bookkeeping use a perfect binary slot skeleton whose internal nodes are X, with one pending P(A,subtree) at each active boundary. Start at P(A,B_M(T)). At a pending product whose second child has a binary slot split X(U,V), apply dist to replace it by X(P(A,U),P(A,V)). Lift this local E equality once through each already distributed XOR ancestor using congL/congR followed by MP. Compose the resulting whole-expression equality with the preceding whole-expression equality using trans and two MPs. Process its left and right pending children in order; a one-slot child needs no event.

Inductively a pending node covers a contiguous perfect slot subtree; distributing it retains its exact ordered binary split and puts pending products on its two children. Outer ancestors have already been distributed and are therefore all X. Each original internal slot node is processed once, so there are exactly M−1 events. All original leaves end as P(A,T_i), in their original perfect order. The composed endpoint is therefore exactly (1). The independently reconstructed request endpoint uses only the written factor/slots and the mathematical definitions, with no traversal or event trace from the emitter.

Every intermediate branch from the root to a slot follows its original X path with at most one P on it. A path that ends in a copied factor has at most h+1 X/P positions (h XOR ancestors and its product at a final leaf). The original step-172 prose said h here; step 174 corrects that off-by-one without changing H or any conclusion bound. Treat actual A,T_i as opaque terms only for this structural count: their fully written sizes/depths remain charged below. Consequently every intermediate expression has structural height at most H=h+1 above these terms. A distribution event at original slot depth k has k≤h−1 XOR ancestors. Its dist arguments are A and its two actual pending slot subtrees; its lift arguments are old/new children and the unchanged sibling. Trans receives the initial, previous and next whole expressions. All are actual mixed expressions/subtrees of height ≤H. No earlier equivalence formula or proof is inserted as a new leaf term, and no chain of products indexed by the number of events is formed.

`scripts/balanced-distribution/run_suite.py` implements this procedure. Each request creates a fresh `Proof` object without synthesizer helpers or earlier requests. `Templates.use` iterates over every record of a previously checked native archive, substitutes its formula/substitution fields, and relocates MP references through the copied record list. The proof object can deduplicate equal records within this charged construction and output only reachable records. Every retained derivation comes from one of those copies or the stated MPs. Unlike an arbitrary externally populated mutable cache, this state cannot supply an unrelated uncharged derivation. Synthesizer hidden rows are never loaded into growing request state. Separate old archives/code are preserved.

### All-size line, depth and full-bit ledger

Initial reflexivity costs at most L lines. An event with k XOR ancestors uses at most L for dist, k(L+1) for lifting and L+2 for composition, totaling (k+2)L+k+2. Since k≤h−1, this is at most (h+1)(L+2). Summing M−1 events and initial reflexivity gives J. Copying/pruning within the specified fresh construction can only reduce it; complete proof text is written at every retained record.

Native X and P add at most three depth levels along an argument path; their images add at most six (P alone needs at most three). Substitution into a checked fixed record adds at most c_j H+d_j to its D_j depth. Additional MP conclusions are subformulas of the copied implication's conclusion and satisfy that same bound. Explicit axiom substitutions occur in their axiom formulas, so they need no deeper bound. The estimate is independent of the number of composed equivalences.

For native nodes, |X(U,V)|=4+2|U|+2|V| and |P(U,V)|=3+|U|+|V|. For image nodes, the corresponding counts are 7+2|τ(U)|+2|τ(V)| and 4+|τ(U)|+|τ(V)|. Thus a height-i expression is bounded by p_0=t_j and p_(i+1)≤8+4p_i. Solving gives

    p_i≤4^i t_j+8(4^i−1)/3≤8·4^(H+1)(t_j+1)=P_j  (i≤H).

This charges every copied factor and every repeated XOR child. If a fixed record has s operator nodes and v variable occurrences, *including all axiom-substitution fields*, the copied record has at most s+vP_j≤S_jP_j nodes. A non-template MP record's sole formula is a subformula of a copied law, so the same node bound applies. Fixed uninstantiated auxiliary variables are single leaves and their payloads are included in B. The header repeats the final formula and obeys the same bound.

ASCII variable text costs at most B bytes per occurrence and fully parenthesized operators cost at most three punctuation bytes per node, safely bounded by B+4 per node. A record has only rule tags, at most three substitution keys or two earlier decimal references, JSON framing and newline; these cost less than 256+64 ceil(log₂(J+2)) bytes outside the formula strings. Decimal reference lengths are no larger than the binary logarithmic allowance. J+2 records cover lines, header and slack. This proves (2), explicitly paying all formula/substitution copies, identifiers, rule/reference fields and framing; gzip storage is not the Frege length. Since 4^(H+1)=16M² and h=log₂M, the displayed full-bit/time accounting is polynomial in M,t_j,B. The template-copying, traversal, reference relocation and serialization admit polynomial construction time; bounded Python execution is not a verified all-interpreter resource guarantee.
