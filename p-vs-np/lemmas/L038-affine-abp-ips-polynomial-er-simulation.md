# L038 — Supplied affine-ABP IPS certificates have polynomial binary ER simulations

## Hypotheses

Fix the notebook's standard circuit Frege (CF), extended Frege (EF) and ER systems and full binary encodings, with fresh AND extensions in ER. Let F be an explicitly encoded CNF of width at most three over its distinct original variables x₁,…,x_n. Write Phi for its ordinary propositional conjunction. Its explicit original axiom list A consists of every clause's ordered falsity product, all x_i²+x_i, and all x_i x_j+x_j x_i for i<j, over F₂. A positive literal contributes 1+x_i and a negative literal contributes x_i; an empty clause contributes 1. Distinct independent placeholders t_j index this entire list. Clause repetitions, repeated literals and complementary literals are allowed. Original variable identifiers, ordering and dictionary framing are charged.

Let C be an explicit finite acyclic affine-edge noncommutative ABP over F₂ in x,t, with specified vertices s,t_out. Each label is an explicit finite affine sum of constants and individual alphabet symbols. Parallel edges, zero labels, dead vertices, unequal path lengths, nonextremal endpoints and coincident endpoints are permitted. A path's label product follows its edge order; an empty path contributes 1. The output is the sum of all s-to-t_out paths in the free associative algebra. No implicit graphs or circuit-valued labels occur. Assume the formal identities

    C(x,0)=0,                     C(x,A(x))=1.                 (1)

There is no placeholder-linearity, pathwise-zero, formula-unfolding, homogeneous-input or degree promise. Define

    Q=2+|F|+|A|+|C|,

including every graph vertex/edge record, affine-label entry, root, alphabet/axiom dictionary and binary identifier. If dictionaries are absent, construct and charge them first. The specified encoding records vertices and label entries explicitly; record counts are bounded by full input bits.

## Conclusion

For fixed constants K,e there is a deterministic construction of an EF proof sigma of the **formula** NOT Phi and an ER refutation rho whose original premises are exactly clauses of F, with

    |sigma|+|rho|+|F| <= K(Q+2)^e.

Running time is bounded by a fixed polynomial in Q, also on invalid descriptions: the construction checks syntax and both formal identities and rejects if they fail. Proof existence therefore does not require externally supplied identity-proof strings or PIT/soundness axioms. Nonlinear placeholder uses and cancellation-based zero identities are included.

This is an informal conditional upper-bound simulation; its separate critical audit on 2026-10-08 found no substantive error, with the implementation qualifications below. It receives supplied certificates, including those conditionally produced by L037; it neither supplies arbitrary reduced data nor proves certificate hardness, ER lower bounds, or a SAT decision-time conclusion. No certified originality or P-versus-NP candidate is claimed.

**Topological evaluator and endpoint conventions.** Topologically number the original vertices. For any supplied Boolean values for its alphabet, define the shared circuit

    p_v=delta_(v,s) XOR XOR_(edges e:u->v) (p_u AND ell_e),    (2)

where ell_e is the fixed-parenthesized XOR of the explicit affine label's symbol values and constant bit. Empty sums are 0. This is a circuit definition, not an axiom about unconstrained p variables. It uses references to earlier p_u circuits and has size linear in vertex, edge and label-entry counts, with a fixed implementation of XOR/AND. Topological induction in the free algebra proves (2) computes all paths from s to v, with the injection giving the empty path. The induction is the usual last-edge partition; it does not enumerate paths algorithmically. The designated output is p_(t_out).

Add fresh α,ω and constant-1 edges α->s and t_out->ω, with α first and ω last in a topological order. Existing edges into s or out of t_out may be retained. No s-to-t_out accepting path can leave t_out and return, or leave s and return, in a DAG. Thus paths α-to-ω are precisely the old accepting paths with connectors, including the empty old path if s=t_out. The wrapper evaluator equals the old one: its α-value is 1; at each original vertex its recurrence differs from (2) only by replacing the injection at s with the α-connector, and coordinate induction proves equality; its ω-value is the old output. Predecessors of s unreachable from s have value zero by the same induction. Unrelated sources have zero injection. These arguments do not presume s is minimal or t_out maximal. In particular a coincident-endpoint DAG has output 1 and cannot satisfy the first identity (1); the algorithm still handles it and rejects that invalid certificate.

**Edge replacement and its circuit proof.** Split each affine label into parallel single-symbol and constant-1 edges, combining repeated entries modulo two or retaining pairs until cancellation. Distributivity proves that the new incoming sum at each vertex equals the old one. Every local expanded list has at most the total number of supplied label entries, so sorting/cancellation and congruence give a polynomial CF proof of equality of the two topological evaluators. A zero coefficient contributes no edge.

For any original axiom, expand its bounded arithmetic tree into a list of words. A width-three clause gives at most 8 words of length at most 3; a Boolean or commutator axiom gives at most 2 words of length at most 2. Repeated words may be kept with multiplicity and later canceled. The empty clause gives the empty word. For a placeholder edge u->v, replace it by independently copied word paths from u to v, with fresh internal vertices; an empty word is a direct 1-edge and a zero list adds nothing. The largest gadget has at most 16 internal vertices and 24 edges. Base-symbol and constant edges are retained. Its formal polynomial is A_j, by finite distributivity of the bounded axiom expression. All gadgets are disjoint internally and lie between their old endpoints in a topological order.

For the zero substitution, instead delete placeholder singleton edges and retain **all** x-only and constant edges. This computes C(x,0). It does not assert that any surviving path vanishes individually. For the axiom substitution, replace every placeholder occurrence by its entire gadget. If a path uses several placeholders, its ordered product contains all their substituted factors, so nonlinear placeholder dependence is preserved. Neither construction enumerates complete paths of C. Append one additional α-to-ω 1-edge for the axiom-substituted graph; its output is C(x,A)+1. Call these x-only normalized graphs H₀,H₁. Both have O(Q) records and at most O(Q) vertices after dense naming.

The essential Boolean identification is also local. For a word a₁…a_l in a gadget, an internal prefix value is the entering old-vertex value times a₁…a_j, by the internal recurrence and AND associativity. There are at most three factors. Its final incoming contribution is p_u AND that word's Boolean value. Summing over the bounded word list and distributing shows the total equals p_u AND Eval(A_j). A disjoint constant-size gadget has no other incoming edges at its internal vertices. The same proof covers an empty word, repeated words and zero lists. Induction at the old vertices, using these contributions and previously proved old/new values, identifies the gadget evaluator with (2) after the stated substitution. Only preceding circuits, not unfolded ancestors, are substituted. Affine splitting has the preceding distributivity proof. Endpoint wrappers use their already proved induction. We therefore obtain polynomial CF proofs, with x free, that

    Eval(H₀)=Q₀,             Eval(H₁)=Q_A XOR 1,              (3)

where Q₀,Q_A are precisely the original topological circuit (2) evaluated with placeholders 0 or the shared axiom-value circuits. Structural congruence identifies copied axiom circuits with those shared values; the bounded distributivity proof identifies an expanded word gadget with the original axiom tree. These are distinct proof operations. Semantic graph equality is not substituted for this circuit derivation.

**Homogeneous inputs and finite constant closure.** Fix either H, with N vertices, source 0 and sink N−1. After splitting, its only labels are 1 or original x_a. Let E₀ and E_a be their coefficient matrices, combining parallel entries modulo two. These are strictly upper triangular. Compute over F₂

    K=I+E₀+…+E₀^(N−1),     G_a=E_a K,
    h₀=e₀ K,               b_a=e₀ K E_a K.                  (4)

The tables satisfy K E₀=E₀ K=K+I; K is upper triangular and every G_a strictly upper triangular. Compute and check all these constant bits by polynomial finite linear algebra. F₂ operations are bit operations; no field elements or long indices are unit-cost objects.

For 1<=k<=D=N−1 construct B_k with one source in layer 0, N vertices in each of layers 1,…,k, first x_a coefficient row b_a and all later coefficient matrices G_a. Its designated sink is coordinate N−1 in layer k. This is a nonempty explicitly layered homogeneous linear-edge ABP of L017's exact type. Its coefficients are the degree-k component of H: each original path decomposes uniquely into a constant segment before the first variable edge and after each variable edge; K records each segment including the empty one. The degree-zero bit is h₀(N−1). No DAG path uses more than N−1 edges, hence no word has degree above D. This decomposition is a semantic applicability proof, not an invocation of the formula-generated O(s³) normalization bound. If H is formally zero, the ground bit and every B_k output are zero. Boolean-zero alone does not suffice.

**Proving graph/component identification.** Use shared factored row circuits

    h_i=sum_a (h_(i−1) G_a) x_a,  1<=i<=D,
    r=sum_(i=0)^D h_i,          X=sum_a E_a x_a.              (5)

At i=1 the checked identity h₀G_a=b_a identifies this with the first-layer row. Multiplication by x_a is AND on Boolean values and appends the variable on the right in the formal interpretation. Strict triangularity gives CF proofs h_i(v)=0 for v<i: every contribution at coordinate v comes from u<v and hence is zero by the preceding support equations. Initial h₀ has arbitrary upper-triangular constant support. Consequently h_D X K=0, since a nonzero last-row coordinate can only be N−1 and every last row of G_a is zero. The row h_D itself need **not** be zero.

Summing the finitely many recurrences (5), using that boundary, gives

    r=e₀ K+r X K.

Right multiplication by E₀ and the checked K E₀=K+I give

    r E₀=e₀(K+I)+r X(K+I)=r+e₀+r X,
    r=e₀+r E₀+r X.                                         (6)

Coordinate v of (6) is precisely the normalized graph evaluator (2), with injection at 0. Induction in v proves r_v equals its actual shared graph-evaluation circuit. Thus Eval(H)=r_(N−1).

All equalities in this paragraph have polynomial CF proofs, rather than just semantic correctness. Expand one coordinate at a time, retaining earlier h-circuits as references. Distribute into terms h_i(u) AND x_a or h_i(u). At most O((D+1)(n+1)N³) term occurrences per coordinate cover the matrix products in (6). Their parities are checked bits of (4). Sort by their finite indices using XOR associativity/commutativity and cancel equal pairs. Apply fixed AND-distributivity and congruence proofs, with constant reductions for the support equations. Each fixed law has a fixed Frege proof, and Pudlák's circuit substitution permits the actual rooted circuits in it. An H-term list takes O((H+1)³) local steps even charging enclosing XOR contexts: at most H² swaps with O(H+1) congruences each, plus polynomial distribution and cancellation. Summation uses each row recurrence once as an equality; it never expands a row into words. There are polynomially many coordinate and support equations. This reproduces L018's finite-closure operations with their hypotheses checked for the present arbitrary graph, without its tree/subtree interface.

**Specified flat evaluator, zero proofs and discharge.** For B_k use the exact flat recurrence of L017, including its scalar-1 gates at layer zero:

    a₁(v)=XOR_(a:b_a(v)=1) (1 AND x_a),
    a_i(v)=XOR_(a,u:G_a(u,v)=1) (a_(i−1)(u) AND x_a).         (7)

Use L017's fixed Boolean gate implementation and parenthesization. Its prefix evaluator through layer k is (7) and its output a_k(N−1). By induction in i prove a_i(v) iff h_i(v). The base reduces 1 AND x_a and uses b_a. Later steps replace the previous a values by h, distribute each factored row sum against x_a, and reassociate to the flat order. Each coordinate contains at most nN terms, with earlier circuits shared. Empty lists and an empty alphabet use 0 AND x=0 and constant reduction. This polynomial derivation is essential: differently factored circuits are not identified by parenthesization or reflexivity alone.

Apply the imported homogeneous coefficient-space algorithm to each B_k. Formal zero gives R/T witnesses of L017's transition and terminal equations. Construct L017's EF proof Delta_k -> NOT o_k, convert it to CF by Jeřábek Lemma 2.4, and substitute the **actual rooted flat circuits** for every evaluation-gate variable in Delta_k and o_k. Evaluation names and all extension names are first freshened away from x; EF-to-CF has eliminated the proof's extension variables. For every evaluation gate g its substituted defining equivalence is P_g iff op(P_inputs), with identical rooted circuits on both sides. Reflexivity and the constant laws prove all these equivalences; conjunction proves the substituted Delta_k. Modus ponens gives NOT a_k(N−1), and (7)'s separately proved identification gives NOT h_k(N−1). No evaluation-definition hypothesis remains. No x assignment is fixed.

Degree zero is the checked ground bit h₀(N−1)=0. Combining all D positive-degree proofs with it and (6) proves Eval(H)=0 in CF. Applying this construction to H₀,H₁ and then (3) yields free-assignment CF proofs

    Q₀=0,                         Q_A=1.                   (8)

These are circuit conclusions inside CF; no CF-to-EF conversion has yet been invoked on them. The algorithm checks both identities by the same homogeneous coefficient-space calculation before generating these proofs. A nonzero ground bit or terminal coefficient space causes rejection. The checked numeric witnesses justify each zero proof, not a propositional PIT-correctness axiom.

**Shared reflection and original clauses.** Under Phi, each clause's falsity product evaluates to zero, by a fixed tautology on at most three variable values and projection of the clause from Phi. Repeated or complementary occurrences require no extra variable enumeration; substitute their actual literals in that fixed proof. Boolean axioms and commutators have fixed proofs (x AND x) XOR x=0 and (x AND z) XOR (z AND x)=0. Thus Phi implies Eval(A_j)=0 for every placeholder j, with polynomial proof cost in the full axiom list.

In the original circuit (2), the affine label evaluated using A is, under Phi, equal to that label with placeholders zero. This follows by congruence for each explicit XOR entry using the preceding axiom-value equalities; constants and x agree. Topological induction on the original vertices then gives

    Phi -> (Q_A iff Q₀).                                   (9)

The vertex step uses only its polynomial incoming list, earlier vertex equalities and AND/XOR congruence. It applies even when many paths contain several placeholders and when zero at t=0 relies on cancellations: it compares whole circuit values, not individual paths. Equations (8) and (9) give a CF proof of the ordinary **formula** NOT Phi. The proof no longer has evaluation-definition guards or extra original axiom premises. This is the graph specialization of the cited reflection step, proved here without a formula-tree assumption.

**One polynomial for all costs.** Write c for fixed encoding constants. Original affine splitting and the bounded edge gadgets give N<=c(Q+2) for each H and polynomial full binary size, including sparse original identifiers of at most Q bits each. No degree-dependent edge gadget occurs. There are D=O(Q) component ABPs. Their total dense coefficient records are

    O(sum_(k=1)^D n k N²)=O((Q+2)^5).

Vertex, layer, label and root framing and sparse names give a conservative O((Q+2)^6) bound on total component bits. R_i has at most N rows; each transition table has at most nN² bits per layer. The total witness records and polynomial-time field-bit construction/checking have a fixed polynomial bound as well. An empty space reduces these counts and is already permitted by L017.

The total size of all flat/factored evaluators, even copying the complete prefix for each B_k separately, is O((Q+2)^5). If M_k is one component's complete evaluation gate count plus n+2, then every rooted gate-substitution DAG has at most M_k nodes. Writing all these roots independently therefore costs at most sum_k M_k² <= (sum_k M_k)²=O((Q+2)^10) node occurrences, and O((Q+2)^10(Q+log(Q+2))) bits. The same principle charges every circuit used in a local proof line as its whole DAG; no cross-line sharing is needed for the bound. Rooted substitution does not mean unfolding into a tree.

For identification (4)–(7), the largest expanded coordinate list has O((Q+2)^5) terms; at most O((Q+2)^2) coordinate/support/induction equations are needed. Each term keeps earlier circuit roots and each intermediate expression has polynomial DAG size, bounded conservatively by O((Q+2)^7) with finite index dictionaries. The O((H+1)³) procedure above hence gives at most O((Q+2)^17) local steps and O((Q+2)^24) circuit-symbol occurrences; O((Q+2)^30) safely absorbs all identifier bits, endpoint/gadget equations and the reflection induction. These loose exponents are fixed and do not depend on C's degree or number of complete paths.

If K₀(L+2)^e₀ bounds L017's construction, all D applications have at most O((Q+2)^(6e₀+1)) total proof bits and polynomial running time, since each full component has length O((Q+2)^6). The inspected EF-to-CF and circuit-substitution operations are fixed polynomial-time algorithms in supplied proof length plus complete substitution-circuit descriptions. Composing their fixed polynomials with these estimates and the local identification bounds gives one fixed polynomial P(Q) for all CF proof symbols, circuits, references and work. Restoring original names costs at most c(P(Q)+1)(Q+log(P(Q)+Q+2)+1), still polynomial. There is no unit-cost unbounded identifier, matrix or circuit.

Finally convert the CF proof whose conclusion is the **formula** NOT Phi to EF by Jeřábek Lemma 2.5, then use the cited EF/ER comparison in its original-CNF refutation convention to get rho. All these algorithms have fixed polynomial overhead. Fixed Boolean connectives translate to a constant number of fresh AND extensions; negation uses opposite literals and OR uses De Morgan. No algebraic axiom or PIT premise is added as an original clause. If F has an empty clause, directly return its immediate refutation. If it has no empty clause and no variables, valid identities (1) cannot exist because F is satisfiable. Otherwise false constants, when needed in the ER encoding, can be defined by z iff x AND NOT x and NOT z derived from that definition, without an assumed unit clause. All fresh indices and references are charged. Composition yields K,e in the conclusion, with polynomial failure behavior on malformed or nonzero-identity inputs.

The following checks make explicit the applicability, accounting conventions and algorithm order already required by the construction.

For arbitrary endpoints, the wrapper has no incoming edge at α and no outgoing edge at ω. Old incoming edges at s have zero prefix values unless their tails are reachable from s; such reachability would create a directed cycle, so their values are zero. An old outgoing edge from t_out cannot return to t_out. This verifies both the path correspondence and the injection in (2), without deleting a potentially relevant prefix. When endpoints coincide only the empty old path is possible, giving formal output 1; its nonzero ground bit causes rejection. Splitting an affine sum permits duplicate singleton edges: combining their coefficient bits is justified propositionally by distributivity and XOR cancellation. For an axiom word of length l<=3, the gadget uses l edges and max(0,l-1) new vertices, except an empty word uses one 1-edge. Thus at most 8 words use at most 24 edges and 16 internal vertices; Boolean and commutator gadgets meet the same bounds. Fresh internal vertices have only their specified word-prefix inputs. This is why their contribution proof is a constant-size derivation with the entering p_u kept as a circuit, even when that circuit represents exponentially many paths. Different occurrences, including several placeholders on one path, receive independent gadgets. Placeholder-free paths survive the zero substitution; neither identity is justified by pathwise vanishing.

The component construction retains a K before the first variable and after every variable, including the last. Constant-path multiplicities are counted modulo two, not by a reachability test. For the support induction, v<i implies every contributing u satisfies u<v<i and hence u<i-1. At i=1 and v=0 there is no predecessor. The coefficient-space witnesses are applicable because each B_k has one source, explicit nonempty layers, homogeneous linear labels and a formally zero output. Width-zero coefficient *spaces* do not require width-zero graph layers. The first flat layer retains 1 AND x_a. For the cost audit, all B_k use prefixes of the same coefficient/evaluation sequence: the first row is b_a and every subsequent matrix is G_a.
