# L047 — Original-axiom affine ABP from a supplied clause-product witness

## Hypotheses

Work in the free associative algebra over F₂. Let F be an explicitly encoded width-at-most-three CNF with its written clause and literal order. Its complete original alphabet is x₁,…,x_n with actual name payloads. Retain repeated clauses, repeated literals and complementary literals. For written clause D_j define, as in L041,

    c_j = ∏_(ℓ in D_j, in written order) v(ℓ),
    v(x_i)=1+x_i,                 v(¬x_i)=x_i,
    s_j=1+c_j,                    f_F=s₁…s_m.

An empty product is 1. Let B_x be the ordered list of b_i=x_i²+x_i and b_ik=x_i x_k+x_k x_i for i<k, and let A be the written clause list c₁,…,c_m followed by B_x. Each position of A has its own independent free placeholder. Write these as y_j,σ_i,τ_ik by their roles; this notation interprets the stored placeholder identifiers by position and does not merge equal clause polynomials.

Supply an explicit finite acyclic affine-F₂ ABP H over x and only the Boolean/commutator placeholders σ,τ. Its designated entrance and exit may be nonextremal. The polynomial is the finite sum of ordered edge-label products over designated paths; an empty path contributes 1. Affine labels explicitly list constant bits and individual symbol coefficients/occurrences. Parallel edges, zero labels, dead vertices, unequal path lengths, mixed labels, nonlinear placeholder paths and cancellation are allowed. No implicit graph or circuit-valued label is allowed. Assume the formal free-algebra identities

    H(x,0)=0,                    H(x,B_x(x))=f_F(x).       (1)

Use one fixed ordinary explicit binary list encoding for F,H,A and their complete alphabets, names, axiom/placeholder dictionaries, endpoints and framing. Every declared item is actually written, including unused permitted symbols. Set Q=2+|F|+|H|+|A|, charging the complete input packages even when they repeat a dictionary. L041 supplies the unconditional ordered translation and bounded clause gadgets only; its universal W premise is not assumed.

## Conclusion

There is a deterministic construction of an explicit acyclic affine-F₂ ABP C over x and all placeholders for A satisfying both formal IPS identities

    C(x,0)=0,                    C(x,A(x))=1.             (2)

For one absolute encoding-dependent constant K, the complete output length and deterministic bit time are both at most KQ⁶. In fact the output length is at most K₀Q⁴ for an absolute K₀. The output includes complete A and original/placeholder dictionaries with unused permitted symbols and long raw names. The constructor does not test (1), impose a pathwise promise on H, or convert H to another representation. C need not be pathwise linear in its placeholders.

This establishes a local supplied-witness interface. It constructs neither H from F nor an original-axiom ABP from arbitrary ER data, and establishes no bound solely in |F|, universal W/V/U premise, faithful representatives, ER polynomial boundedness or unrestricted SAT conclusion. POTENTIALLY_NEW means the saved search did not establish full coverage, not certified originality. The separate critical audit on 2026-10-10 found no load-bearing error; it clarified the inter-gadget connector charge without changing the construction or bounds. This remains an informal proof without a verified implementation.

## Proof

Those sources support the comparison and graph convention, without a full matching converse import.

The difference to verify is the ordered free-ring correction, arbitrary-endpoint graph implementation and complete bit/time ledger. These are proved below rather than obtained by a commutative specialization. L041 supplies the ordered c_j,s_j,f_F definitions and the unconditional bounded gadgets. Neither its W premise nor its NP=coNP implication or PIT construction is used. C046a is context for a possible later comparison, not an input to this proof. No reproof of a cited PIT or formula-conversion theorem is needed.

**Ordered correction.** Define x-only prefixes P₀=1 and P_j=s₁…s_j. In the free algebra, keeping c_j on the right of its entire prefix, distributivity and characteristic two give

    P_j=P_(j−1)(1+c_j)=P_(j−1)+P_(j−1)c_j,
    P_(j−1)c_j=P_(j−1)+P_j.

Thus, for m≥1, cancellation in the finite sum gives

    Σ_(j=1)^m P_(j−1)c_j
      = (P₀+P₁)+(P₁+P₂)+…+(P_(m−1)+P_m)
      = 1+f_F.                                         (3)

For m=0 the sum is 0 and 1+f_F=1+1=0, so (3) remains valid. No product factors commute, and no Boolean evaluation or reduced-polynomial identification enters this identity. Define in the enlarged free algebra

    T(x,y)=Σ_(j=1)^m P_(j−1)y_j.

Each summand vanishes at y=0, while (3) gives T(x,c)=1+f_F formally.

Interpret H's original symbols and Boolean/commutator placeholders in C's larger dictionary using their positions in B_x and A. This is an injective relabelling of independent symbols; in A the position of a B_x entry is shifted by m. Its two substitutions commute with this relabelling: the corresponding placeholders are sent respectively to 0 and to exactly the same ordered Boolean/commutator polynomials. It introduces no clause placeholders in H and preserves its complete nonlinear polynomial. Denote this embedded polynomial by Ĥ. Set

    C(x,y,σ,τ)=T(x,y)+Ĥ(x,σ,τ).                         (4)

From (1), (3) and characteristic two,

    C(x,0)=0+H(x,0)=0,
    C(x,A)=1+f_F+H(x,B_x)=1+f_F+f_F=1.

This proves (2) as identities in the free algebra. The first identity allows cancellation among placeholder-free paths of H; no individual path is assumed zero. The second retains every nonlinear placeholder sector of H. No equality between C and H before substitution is asserted.

**Explicit graphs for the correction.** For each s_i, use L041's bounded parallel word-path gadget: expand only that clause's at-most-three literal-falsity factors and add the extra constant 1. There are at most nine word occurrences, each of length at most three. Retain duplicate words as distinct paths if they arise. An empty word is a constant-1 edge between distinct terminals, never identification of those terminals. Use fresh internal vertices; a zero polynomial may be represented by cancelling paths or no connecting path. These gadgets have at most 18 internal vertices and 27 edges. Finite distributivity proves their output is exactly s_i in its written order, even for repetitions or complementary literals.

For each j separately, concatenate independent copies of the gadgets s₁,…,s_(j−1) with constant-1 connector edges in that order. Use fresh outer terminals. For j=1 the prefix is a single constant-1 edge between distinct terminals. The resulting prefix graph computes P_(j−1), since an accepting path traverses one gadget path in each successive copy in order. Append a new y_j edge from its designated exit to a new branch exit. The branch therefore computes P_(j−1)y_j. All copies for different j are disjoint. Sum these branches using fresh global entrance/exit and constant-1 connectors. If m=0 take two disconnected terminals. This is an explicit graph for T. Every accepted correction path contains exactly its branch's one clause-placeholder edge. The construction copies only clause gadgets, never complete accepting paths, and has O(m²+1) records.

**Embedding arbitrary H and summing safely.** Copy the entire supplied H once, relabelling only its symbols by the dictionary map just described and its vertices by fresh dense identifiers. Keep its affine labels, including zero labels and duplicate symbol occurrences; coefficient collection is unnecessary. Add fresh global terminals for C. Connect the entrance of C to the entrance of T and to the designated entrance of the H copy by 1-edges, and connect their designated exits to the exit of C by 1-edges. All operand copies and global terminals are disjoint; there are no other connections between branches.

The correction graph has extremal outer terminals. For H, an accepting route in C must enter at H's designated entrance and leave at its designated exit. If it reaches that exit but continues internally, it cannot return there to take the connector: returning would create a nonempty directed closed walk in a DAG. Incoming edges to the designated entrance give no extra external entry. Thus the internal segments contributing to this branch are exactly H's designated paths, including its empty path if the endpoints coincide. Dead vertices and nonextremal endpoints require no deletion. Placing C's fresh entrance before both operand DAGs and its fresh exit after both supplies a topological ordering of C. Even coincident H endpoints are one vertex within one operand and do not identify outer terminals or create a cycle. Therefore finite path semantics gives exactly (4), without a pathwise hypothesis or path unfolding.

**One complete binary ledger.** Fix encoding constants a_r≥1. Every input vertex, edge, label entry, clause, literal, symbol and axiom record consumes at least a fixed positive number of bits in the explicit list convention. Consequently their total actual count is at most a₁Q. Each raw input identifier/name has length at most Q. These are actual written counts, not values of unfulfilled binary declarations. In particular m≤a₁Q, and the complete quadratic commutator dictionary is already charged in |A|.

Each copied clause gadget has a constant number of records with constant or singleton labels. There are Σ_(j=1)^m(j−1)=m(m−1)/2 gadget copies. Charge each connector between successive gadgets with its adjacent copy, adding O(m²) records in total; the remaining fresh prefix/branch/outer attachments add O(m+1) records. Hence T has at most a₂Q² graph/label records, including disconnected and cancelling branches. The one copy of H adds at most a₃Q records, counting each affine label entry separately. Together C has at most a₄Q² records, regardless of path count, formal degree or cancellations. New graph IDs can be dense, with at most a₅log₂(Q+2) bits. Symbol references range over the complete input dictionaries and have that same order of bit length. Raw original and placeholder names are retained as charged string payloads. Even redundantly writing a raw name in every output label field costs at most a₆Q³. Retaining complete A, names, unused permitted symbols and the placeholder-position dictionary adds O(Q) supplied bits, plus at most O(Q²) if those input names are redundantly written in the position table. There is no new original symbol or new axiom. Framing, length fields, counts, endpoints and references cost at most a₇(Q³+Q²log₂(Q+2)+Q). For Q≥2 this gives

    |C|_full ≤ K₀Q⁴.                                  (5)

This bound also allows retention of F with the output. No sparse raw name is treated as a unit-cost integer, and an unused permitted symbol is not silently dropped.

Here is a fixed deterministic bit-time implementation, to make the common polynomial bound explicit. Parse actual list records and verify syntax/dictionary positions using scans of the written tables. Map H's at most a₁Q vertices and symbols by those scans; validate its DAG by elementary topological sorting on these actual records. Construct each constant-size s_i template in written literal order, then run the two finite loops j=1,…,m and i=1,…,j−1 to copy and connect the templates. Allocate dense fresh vertex IDs using binary counters and directly store the old-to-new map for the single H copy. Write its labels and dictionary positions without expanding its affine labels or paths. Finally write the full A/dictionaries and the outer connectors. Identity checking is absent.

These loops create at most a₄Q² records. No output-table sorting or lookup over an implicit universe is required. A deliberately redundant implementation may use at most a₈Q² string/table comparisons and elementary counter/serialization operations per record creation; table sizes are at most O(Q) and input names at most Q, so this is a generous majorant of linear table lookup and constant-size template copying. Each such operation on names, dense counters or length fields costs at most a₉Q bit steps (all new dense counters/lengths have O(log(Q+2)) bits, and no new raw names are longer than input names). Initial parsing, dictionary verification and a naive topological sort on O(Q) records are also bounded by this O(Q⁵) majorant. Writing the at-most-K₀Q⁴ output bits is included after increasing the fixed constant. Thus for an absolute a₁₀ the entire construction costs at most a₁₀Q⁵+a₁₀Q⁴ bit steps. Enlarging K bounds this time and (5) by the single polynomial KQ⁶. Constants depend only on the fixed encoding and implementation, not on F,H,n,m, degree, names or path count. The implementation runs on all syntactically valid input packages; correctness under (1) does not require a solver for the supplied identities.

**Boundary and failure checks.** If D_j is empty, c_j=1 and s_j=0. Its gadget is the sum of two constant-1 word paths, or a disconnected zero gadget. Prefixes after it are formally zero, while the telescoping proof still accounts for the earlier prefixes and that clause. The input H may be the disconnected zero graph when f_F=0; no accepting H path is not an invalid witness in this case, and its correction branch still supplies (2). More generally, no accepting H path gives H=0, so (1) demands formal f_F=0; the construction remains correct. Zero labels and nonlinear cancellations in H are retained verbatim.

For m=0, f_F=1. No H can satisfy (1): choose any scalar Boolean assignment (including the unique empty assignment when n=0). Every B_x axiom then evaluates to zero, and applying this evaluation to both identities would give H(a,0)=0 and H(a,0)=1. Nevertheless T=0 and the graph operations remain defined, and the formal deduction from inconsistent (1) has no missing constructive case. When n=0, every clause is empty. If there is at least one clause, f_F=0 and H=0 is a valid input; the clause-placeholder correction and an empty B_x dictionary work as above. Coincident H endpoints in an acyclic graph give precisely its empty path and polynomial 1, contradicting H(x,0)=0. This syntax case remains defined but cannot satisfy (1). Constant-only graphs, unused dictionary entries, nonextremal roots and empty correction branches are covered by the explicit construction and ledger. Repeated clause occurrences have separate y_j edges even when their c_j agree. Repeated/complementary literals retain their original ordered factors; neither Boolean simplification nor commutation is used.

Its EXPLORE scope covers the supplied-H theorem exactly. This audit reuses the inspected sources cited above and L041's unconditional definitions/gadgets, not W, PIT, C046a or a homogeneous-ABP receiving theorem. The following checks reproduce the existing proof obligations; they establish no stronger theorem or certified originality.

For the algebra check, let substitution at zero and at A be homomorphisms out of the free algebra with the enlarged independent alphabet. The map of H's symbols fixes each original x and sends the placeholder of B_x position r to the placeholder of A position m+r. These images are distinct from one another and from every clause placeholder. Composing this map with either substitution is exactly H's original zero or B_x substitution. Thus cancellation and every word containing several placeholders survive the embedding. Separately, the j-th correction term substitutes to P_(j−1)c_j=P_(j−1)+P_j with c_j on the right. In the finite sum each interior P_j occurs twice, leaving P₀+P_m; no reordering of factors is involved. The two identities in (2) therefore follow under precisely (1), including cancellation-based zero rather than merely pathwise vanishing. Equal clause polynomials still have different y_j positions.

For the graph check, each gadget's distinct terminals and disjoint internal paths represent the at-most-nine word occurrences exactly, including the length-zero word as an edge. Serial gadget connectors contribute scalar 1 between factors and cannot change their order. Distinct correction branches have no cross edges. In the H branch, any accepting path must use the designated entry connector and designated exit connector. Its internal segment is a designated H path. Conversely, any such H path extends uniquely using these two connectors. If the route continues internally beyond H's exit, it cannot return there in a DAG and cannot reach another branch or the global exit elsewhere. This proves the path correspondence for nonextremal endpoints without pruning or enumerating paths. Adding a new source and sink outside disjoint DAGs preserves acyclicity. Coincident designated endpoints contribute only the empty internal path; the resulting H polynomial is 1 and cannot satisfy the first input identity. The wrapper is still syntactically defined on that case.

The ledger check counts both vertices and every edge-label entry. There are m(m−1)/2 clause-gadget copies. For m≥2 there are (m−1)(m−2)/2 connectors between successive gadgets, in addition to O(m+1) branch attachments. The earlier wording about O(m+1) extra connectors did not explicitly allocate these inter-gadget connectors; the paragraph above now allocates them to their adjacent copies. For m=0 or 1 the same O(m²+1) bound holds directly. Copying H once adds O(Q) records, even for mixed labels. Thus all records are O(Q²); the connector clarification changes neither this bound nor either displayed polynomial bound. Every raw name is already a string of length at most Q, and no new raw symbol name is introduced. O(Q²) possible repeated payloads therefore cost O(Q³); complete A and all unused dictionary entries remain charged. Dense IDs and their framing have logarithmic length because their actual counts are polynomial, irrespective of input identifiers' numeric values. The full quadratic commutator list is written in A and never treated as an implicit constant-size range. These facts justify the deliberately loose K₀Q⁴ output bound.

For deterministic bit time, a sequential implementation scans only explicit input tables for original vertex/symbol lookups and keeps the fresh vertex counter separately. It never searches through all generated paths or repeatedly sorts the output. Each generated record can be appended sequentially; if the encoding puts lengths first, one count-only pass followed by the same emitting pass supplies them, multiplying work by a constant. Constant-size templates are read from input-sized tables, and comparisons of raw names cost O(Q), not unit time. At most O(Q²) such comparisons/counter operations per one of O(Q²) output records, with O(Q) bit cost each, gives the stated O(Q⁵) majorant. Input dictionary checks and a naive topological sort use only O(Q) actual records and fit this majorant; one sequential output pass costs at most O(Q⁴). Hence one fixed encoding/implementation-dependent K covers both complete output and bit time by KQ⁶. No identity verification, arbitrary arithmetic-circuit operation, giant declared universe or uncharged random access is necessary. This is an informal bit-algorithm audit, not an executable performance certificate.

Finally, an empty clause gives c_j=1 and s_j=0 formally; the gadget's two constant paths cancel and the telescoping identity remains valid on either side of that zero factor. A no-path H is zero, as is any cancellation-based zero H, but a graph with paths need not be zero; correctness always uses its supplied formal identities. Zero labels and dead components add records without changing path semantics. With m=0, f_F=1 and evaluation at a scalar Boolean assignment contradicts (1), also for the unique assignment when n=0. With n=0 and m≥1 all clauses are empty, f_F=0 and the zero H is permissible. Repeated/complementary literals remain ordered factors and require no special simplification. These checks exhaust the listed boundary obligations without a Boolean-to-formal inference. No load-bearing error was found.
