# L056 — Checked primitive Frege proofs for balanced slot exchange and cancellation

## Hypotheses

Let M=2^h with h≥1. Supply M fully written formulas T_0,...,T_(M−1) in NOT/implication syntax, and an index 0≤r<M−1. Variable names are written ASCII alphanumeric/underscore payloads. Other finite binary names can first be injectively encoded as ASCII payloads, charging their complete encoding. Fix a free variable q; it is neither an extension variable nor a hypothesis. Define literal formula abbreviations

    X(A,B) = ((A→B)→¬(B→A)),   E(A,B)=¬X(A,B),
    Z=¬(q→q).

These abbreviations are always expanded before checking. X is Boolean XOR and E is Boolean equivalence. Let B_M(T) be the perfect binary X tree on the listed slots. Exchange replaces T_r,T_(r+1) by T_(r+1),T_r. Cancellation requires literal equality T_r=T_(r+1) and replaces both slots by Z,Z. No semantic test for equality of different terms is permitted.

Use the three classical axiom schemas ax-1, ax-2, ax-3 and MP stated below. The ordinary image τ sends ¬A to ¬τ(A) and A→B to ¬τ(A) OR τ(B), without simplifying double negations. For each format j∈{native,image}, let t_j and d_j bound the nodes and depth of all input terms and Z in that format. Let B≥1 bound every raw variable payload in bytes, including q and the fixed library's one-character schematic names. Trees are fully copied at every occurrence; no formula is represented by a hash, name or reference to a shared formula node.

This statement tests the slot operations proposed in L055 §5. That proposal and the receiver theorem are motivation, not mathematical premises. Neither supplied IPS identities nor any existential certificate-size or representation assumption is used here.

## Conclusion

There is an explicit native Frege proof of E(B_M(T),B_M(T')) for either operation, and a line-by-line NOT/OR translation checked against the translated axiom schemas and translated MP. The translated image is **not claimed to be a separately established complete system for arbitrary NOT/OR inputs**, or a checked proof in every chosen ordinary Frege presentation. The inputs here are native formulas and their literal translated images; arbitrary ordinary-input normalization remains outside the component.

The construction restores the perfect M-slot shape after each operation. Set

    H=3h+2,  L=3470,
    (D_native,S_native)=(18,2456),  (D_image,S_image)=(25,3492),
    J={1+(4h+3)(3h+8)}(L+2),
    P_j=8·4^(H+1)(t_j+1).

Every temporary slot tree has height at most H above its terms. Native emitted depth is at most D_native+3H+d_native; image emitted depth is at most D_image+6H+d_image. Each proof has at most J lines, at most S_j P_j nodes per record including explicitly written axiom substitutions, and at most

    (J+2)[(B+4)S_j P_j +256+64 ceil(log₂(J+2))] bytes.       (1)

Full binary length is eight times this byte bound. Since 4^(H+1)=64 M^6 and J=O((h+1)^2), this is a fixed polynomial in M,t_j,B. Logarithmic-depth input terms give logarithmic emitted depth. This is an informal all-size proof of this component and a bounded implementation reproduction. The separate step-170 audit below checks the mathematical construction and selected complete archives; finite checks alone are not the all-size argument. The bounds describe copying the fixed checked templates for the current request, not importing arbitrary proofs from an externally populated cache.

### Exposure, local rewrite, restoration

Find the lowest common ancestor of the two consecutive slots. Below it the selected left term is the final leaf of the left child, and the selected right term is the first leaf of the right child. Isolate the former as the right child of L XOR a by repeatedly rotating U XOR (V XOR W) to (U XOR V) XOR W. Each rotation advances one edge down the original right boundary path. Isolate the latter as the left child of b XOR R by the reverse rotation along the original left boundary path. Empty surrounding blocks mean an already isolated leaf; no zero block is inserted for an absent block.

When both surrounding blocks are present, rotate

    (L XOR a) XOR (b XOR R)
      -> L XOR (a XOR (b XOR R))
      -> L XOR ((a XOR b) XOR R).

If just L is present use the first rotation with right operand b. If just R is present use the second rotation at the ancestor itself. If neither is present the pair is already exposed. Thus the selected terms become children of one X node without changing any untouched subtree. Exchange uses the checked commutativity instance there. Cancellation first proves E(X(a,a),Z), then E(Z,X(Z,Z)), and combines them by the checked transitivity instance. It therefore retains two slots, without retaining the removed a occurrences as a shared abbreviation.

Lift each local equivalence through its actual ancestors using one left/right congruence instance and MP per node. Reverse the saved rotations in reverse order after the local rewrite, using associativity and symmetry when required. The pair's shape remains a two-leaf binary node, so all inverse rotations are syntactically defined. Induction over the rotation list shows that the final shape is the original perfect slot tree, with exactly the requested two leaf replacements. Combine consecutive whole-tree equivalences using transitivity with arguments original-tree, current-tree, next-tree. Previously proved equivalences occur as earlier proof lines; no equivalence formula or previous proof is recursively inserted into a term.

### All-size depth, lines and bytes

Let the ancestor be at depth d and its original subtree have height k=h−d. Each boundary isolation has at most k−1 rotations, and the junction has at most two; there are at most 2h forward and 2h inverse rotations. Including the local rewrite gives at most 4h+3 events. This intentionally allows slack for cancellation's two fixed laws and combination.

For the height bound, remove the two original boundary paths and view every off-path subtree as an opaque block. There are at most 2k boundary-path nodes; the junction introduces at most two further spine nodes. Rotations only reparent these spine nodes and blocks, and do not alter any block's internal tree. A path in any exposed/intermediate shape visits at most 2k+2 spine nodes and one off-path subtree of height at most k, plus the d unchanged outer ancestors. Its height is consequently at most d+3k+2≤3h+2. Reverse rotations visit these same shapes with changed leaf terms. Original and final balanced trees also obey H. This argument handles a pair crossing the root and every smaller ancestor cut; no flattening into an M-long chain is used.

One local equality takes at most 3L+2 primitive lines: a reversed rotation takes assoc and symm plus MP; cancellation takes cancel, zero_slots and trans plus two MPs. Each of at most H context lifts takes L+1 lines, and cumulative transitivity takes L+2. The total per event is at most (H+6)(L+2)=(3h+8)(L+2). Initial reflexivity takes at most L. This proves J. Here form the proof by appending substituted copies of the checked fixed strings, relocating references within each copy, and appending the stated MPs. Reusing earlier equal formulas within this assembled proof, or pruning its unused lines, only decreases the bound; references never name an implicit formula. This observation does not justify importing an arbitrary cached derivation from outside the assembly. In a degenerate Z,Z cancellation, the final endpoint equals the initial endpoint and the output may prune to reflexivity. That case does not test the nontrivial cancellation chain; the M=2 and M=4 repeated-term cases do.

Native X adds at most three levels to an argument path, and its ordinary image adds at most six. Replacing a schematic variable in a fixed proof line adds only the substituted argument depth to that line's depth. All actual arguments are subtrees or whole trees of height at most H above the input terms, including cumulative transitivity arguments. Thus emitted depths obey the two stated bounds independently of the number of steps.

Native expanded X has 4+2|A|+2|B| nodes, and its image has 7+2|τ(A)|+2|τ(B)|. The recurrence p_(i+1)≤8+4p_i, p_0≤t_j, is bounded by P_j for i≤H. Substitution into a fixed primitive record of at most S_j nodes, counting its substitution fields too, gives at most S_j P_j nodes. Raw name occurrences cost at most B bytes; ordinary punctuation costs at most three bytes per operator node. Formula text therefore costs at most (B+4) times this node bound. JSON rule tags, at most three substitution keys or two line references, quotes, separators and framing cost less than 256+64 ceil(log₂(J+2)) bytes per record. Decimal reference fields are no longer than binary-length bounds. The header's fully written goal is bounded by the same node estimate; J+2 records allow it and framing. This proves (1), paying every formula occurrence, raw name, axiom substitution, rule and reference. For ASCII payloads no variable-string escape expansion is hidden. Runtime to copy, serialize and emit these bounded strings is polynomial in the same parameters; the bounded Python implementation is not claimed to be verified for every interpreter/resource limit.

In L055's intended use M≤cR^5, fully expanded shallow suffix terms have polynomial t_j,B and d_j=O(log R). The component's bounds then give polynomial full-bit cost and logarithmic depth in R. This supplies only the exchange/cancellation component. Distribution, coordinate combinations, suffix proofs, both zero-identity receivers and final original-CNF guards still need whole-receiver proof output; certificate existence, arbitrary ER representation and SAT decision/separation are separate gaps.

This audit uses the unchanged SPECIALIZE assessment `drafts/literature/2026-10-11-balanced-slot-compiler-critical-audit.md`, the complete canonical argument, and the complete `compiler.py`, `checker.py` and `run_suite.py` code. **All-size geometry and actual arguments.** In `expose`, descent before the first rotation locates the original lowest common ancestor. At its cut, the left isolation loop reduces the right-boundary distance to the last slot by one each time; the right loop does the same to the first slot on the left boundary. No loop expands an off-boundary block. The two junction rotations leave the selected leaves as siblings. A saved rotation is an invertible change of a three-block parenthesization. Replacing or exchanging the two exposed leaves retains that shape, so reversing the stored path/direction list restores every original split. For k=1 neither isolation nor junction is needed; the same reasoning covers an interior cut with unchanged outer depth d. The union of the two original boundary paths and their junction has at most 2k+2 internal skeleton positions. Rotation reuses these positions and keeps each off-path block intact, so the stated d+3k+2 bound is valid independently of leaf labels and of cancellation. Forward rotations number at most 2(k−1)+2≤2h, and inverse rotations number the same. Thus even counting the local cancellation chain generously gives at most 4h+3 events.

Inspection of `rotate`, `rewrite` and `combine` identifies every growing template argument: associativity receives three actual blocks; symmetry receives the old and new local trees; congruence receives old/new children and their literal sibling; cumulative transitivity receives the initial, previous and next whole trees. The cancellation transitivity arguments are X(a,a), Z and X(Z,Z), of slot height at most one. None is a previously proved E formula. All therefore have height at most H above the supplied terms or Z.
