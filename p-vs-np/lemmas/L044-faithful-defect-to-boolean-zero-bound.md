# L044 — Faithful AND-defect bounds imply universal Boolean-zero witness bounds

## Hypotheses

Work over the free associative F₂ algebra, using precisely the explicit affine-edge acyclic ABP encodings and universal premise W of L041, and the universal-over-supplied-collections premise V of L042. An input graph G has full bit length E, including its entire original-variable/name dictionary, all affine label entries, designated endpoints and framing. Let its original alphabet be x₁,…,x_n, retaining explicitly listed unused names. Its ordered path polynomial is f_G; an empty path contributes 1. Constants, n=0, arbitrary nonextremal or coincident endpoints, dead vertices, zero/parallel edges and cancellation are allowed. Implicit graphs and circuit-valued labels are excluded.

Assume V: there is one polynomial p bounding the aggregate full encoding of some Boolean/commutator-only affine-ABP AND-defect witnesses for every eligible supplied faithful collection in L042. The bound's argument is exactly

    M=2+T+S+E_val,        T=2+|K|+|π|,

with all supplied representatives, root associations and full input dictionaries included, and no sought H graph, witness degree h or witness-size bound included. All original/constant roots are exact, signed complements are formally 1+r_v, fidelity holds on every original Boolean assignment, and each extension has a defining clause and a derived signed unit in final inference ancestry. V concerns every supplied eligible collection, rather than an existential choice of representatives. It supplies neither a constructor nor a fidelity tester. Witnesses may have nonlinear placeholder dependence and cancellation, but cannot use original-clause or extension-axiom placeholders.

## Conclusion

V implies the full universal W of L041. There is one polynomial q such that **every** Boolean-zero input G above has some explicit affine-F₂ ABP H' of full length at most q(E+2), with exactly the original alphabet and the complete Boolean/pair-once commutator placeholder dictionaries, satisfying formally

    H'(x,0)=0,          H'(x,B_x)=f_G(x),
    B_x=(x_i²+x_i; x_i x_j+x_j x_i for i<j).

This supplies the converse to L043's conditional implication. It establishes neither V nor W, nor a constructor, faithful-representative existence, an NP=coNP-only converse or a P-versus-NP resolution. The separate critical audit on 2026-10-10, step 119, found no substantive error within these hypotheses; the proof remains informal. The checked literature does not supply an exact matching theorem; this is local conditional progress, not certified originality.

    K=(x₁) ∧ … ∧ (x_n) ∧ (a) ∧ (¬a),       z ↔ (a AND a).

Its audited refutation π derives z from (z∨¬a) and (a), derives ¬z from (¬z∨a) and (¬a), and resolves these two signed units to the empty clause. Supply exact original roots y_i for y=(x,a), the exact constants 0 and 1, and

    r_z=aa+f_G,       r_¬z=1+aa+f_G.

Every other negative signed root is its exact formal complement, in particular r_¬a=1+a and r_¬x_i=1+x_i. Constant complements may alias the two constant roots. L043's endpoint-safe operation applies to each actual supplied copy: a connector enters at its designated entrance and leaves at its designated exit. This establishes the two displayed root polynomials with their actual affine labels, zero/parallel edges, unequal lengths and cancellations. Now assume G is Boolean-zero. For every scalar assignment (b,α) to (x,a),

    r_z(b,α)=α²+f_G(b)=α.

This is exactly the recursively defined value of a AND a. Originals/constants are exact, and exact complements establish fidelity for every signed root. Fidelity holds on **all** assignments, independently of the contradictory host units. Thus this supplied collection is eligible for V whenever G is Boolean-zero. We construct its graphs without deciding that promise; the promise is used solely to justify this invocation. The sole formal AND defect is

    r_z+r_a r_a=(aa+f_G)+aa=f_G.                         (1)

The ordered word aa cancels in F₂; no free-ring commutativity, idempotence or original-clause witness is used.

**Exact extraction and output ledger.** Since the Boolean-zero assumption establishes eligibility, V supplies a witness H for the sole AND node, with aggregate length and hence individual full length ≤p(M), satisfying

    H(x,a,0)=0,       H(x,a,B_y)=f_G(x).                (3)

Use precisely L042's anchor-elimination operation: set a, its Boolean placeholder σ_a and every anchor-pair placeholder τ_(i,a) to zero; retain every x_i, σ_i and τ_ij for i<j≤n. The original order was preserved with a last, so these retained axiom indices have exactly the required pair-once meaning. Reindex them explicitly if the serialization convention requires it. L042's audited generator check applies unchanged to (3): under ψ:a↦0, the anchor Boolean axiom a²+a and every x_i a+a x_i vanish, whereas all B_x axioms are fixed. Anchor elimination followed by B_x substitution therefore equals B_y substitution followed by ψ, and zero-placeholder substitution commutes with it as well. The right side f_G contains no a.     H'(x,0)=0,        H'(x,B_x)=ψ(f_G)=f_G.             (4)

This reuse covers arbitrary ordered nonlinear placeholder products and cancellation. No formula-specific property of L042's former f_F is used; being x-only is enough. Clause/extension placeholders cannot occur by V. Delete their optional registry metadata and rebuild framing for **exactly** the challenge original alphabet and B_x placeholders, including every unused allowed symbol. For completeness, let J=|H| denote its supplied full length and set R=J+L³+1. The transformed graph has O(J+1) actual records/entries.     |H'|≤D(J+L³+1)².                                   (5)

This deliberately loose bound covers complete output framing even if the input witness used compact shared dictionaries. It includes unchanged unused challenge names and all axiom/placeholder entries, not merely the reachable labels. It does not use a degree bound or an algorithm producing H.

Do not assume p itself monotone. Choose fixed A≥1 and integer d≥0 with p(t)≤A(t+1)^d for every nonnegative integer t, by bounding its absolute coefficients. From (2),

    J≤p(M)≤A(CL³+1)^d.

    q(L)=D(A(CL³+1)^d+L³+1)².                            (6)

Equations (4)–(6) establish exactly W's polynomial **full-bit** witness bound in E+2. All constants/exponents arise from the fixed encodings and V's single polynomial, not from G, its alphabet, endpoints, chosen H or an assumed constructor.     ev_x ∘ φ = ψ ∘ ev_y,       zero_x ∘ φ = ψ ∘ zero_y.
