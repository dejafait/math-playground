# L037 — Alternative affine-ABP assembly from supplied reduced data

## Hypotheses

Work in the free associative algebra over F₂. Retain the complete L025 hypotheses, replacing every supplied representative and relation circuit by an explicit acyclic affine-edge ABP. In detail, F is a width-at-most-three CNF; π is a valid ER refutation with fresh acyclic definitions z iff a AND b for signed earlier literals, sorted-set clauses, weakening and selected-pivot resolution. T=2+|F|+|π| counts binary encodings, including literals and references. The original axiom list A consists of the ordered falsity products of original clauses, x_i²+x_i, and x_i x_j+x_j x_i for i<j. Their placeholders t are distinct and independent. No extension-axiom placeholders occur.

An explicitly numbered abstract value DAG has S nodes, including original x, constants, exact signed nodes r_i=1+r_a, and reduced AND nodes with earlier signed operands a,b. Every value used by the proof has a supplied x-only representative r_i; original variables and constants have their exact values. A nonextension proof variable absent from F has value zero. Each AND node has a supplied ABP H_i over x,t satisfying, formally,

    H_i(x,0)=0,        H_i(x,A(x))=r_i+r_a r_b.                 (1)

Keep d,h≥1 bounding the formal root degrees of the representatives and H_i before substitution, as in L025. H_i may use every kind of original placeholder, nonlinearly; the representatives need not be faithful extension functions on every assignment. E=E_ABP≥2 charges the aggregate full explicit graph, label, alphabet, original-axiom dictionary, abstract-node and root encoding. If framing is not supplied, construct and charge it first; it has polynomial size in T. Graphs have finite explicit affine labels, may have constants, parallel edges, unequal path lengths, zero polynomials, nonextremal designated endpoints or equal endpoints; an empty path contributes 1. No circuits as labels, implicit graphs, formula promise, promise-verification algorithm or general existence of these data is assumed.

## Conclusion

Put N=2+T+S+E. There is a deterministic polynomial-time construction of an explicit acyclic affine-edge ABP C over the original x,t such that

    C(x,0)=0,                     C(x,A(x))=1.                 (2)

The graph has O(N⁸) vertices plus edges and full binary encoding O(N⁸ log(N+2)), with absolute constants for a fixed explicit encoding. Its complete formal degree in x,t is O(N⁸), before axiom substitution. This degree bound charges graph size and does not claim L025's sharper bound in semantic d,h. The d,h promises are retained but are not needed by this alternative construction. Writing every proof-line output separately still costs polynomial total size.

This closes the conditional final-assembly representation obligation for these supplied data. It does not construct the data for arbitrary ER proofs, convert L025's prescribed polynomial, prove every-certificate hardness, establish a full ABP-to-propositional simulation, or transfer proof size to unrestricted SAT decision. No P-versus-NP candidate or certified novelty is claimed. A separate critical audit on 2026-10-08 found no substantive error within the stated hypotheses; its all-input checks and independent finite replay are recorded below.

## Proof

**Source scope and local graph operations.** The pre-existing EXPLORE assessment `drafts/literature/2026-10-07-alternative-affine-abp-certificate-assembly.md` covers precisely this conditional target. Li–Tzameret–Wang, *Characterizing Propositional Proofs as Non-Commutative Formulas*, arXiv:1412.8746v4 (11 September 2015), Theorem 3.4 and Lemma 3.5, printed pp.16–17, supplies tree-like formula witness extraction, not the ER-DAG graph bound sought here. Its disjoint-ancestry cost cannot be imported for proof reuse. Raz–Shpilka, *Deterministic Polynomial Identity Testing in Non Commutative Models*, author-hosted 16-page manuscript, Lemma 2 Step 1, printed p.5, supplies disjoint serial/parallel graph composition. The new issue below is an ordered recurrence and its explicit proof-reference graph, not a reproof of either source theorem.

Normalize every finite affine label into parallel constant-1 or single-symbol edges by collecting F₂ coefficients. Record counts remain O(E). Use disjoint copies for a product of independently supplied graphs, with 1-edges between designated exits and entrances; use disjoint branches with fresh endpoints for a sum. This computes the ordered product or sum by finite distributivity. Nonextremal endpoints cause no extra accepting paths: after leaving a designated exit internally, a path cannot return to that exit to take its external connector, since that would be a cycle. The same argument covers coinciding endpoints; the empty internal path is allowed. Dead vertices and parallel edges can be kept. These operations apply to H_i graphs as well as x-only graphs, without testing their polynomials.

For any two x-only supplied or explicitly concatenated graphs f,g, use L035 to construct W(f,g), with W(0)=0 and W(A)=fg+gf. For a single x-only representative q, use L036 to construct B(q), with B(0)=0 and B(A)=q²+q. The audited L035/L036 hypotheses allow all graph cases above. Neither construction will ever take H_i, a Boolean witness, a commutator witness or a recursively constructed proof witness as an operand. All uses are on x-only graphs of explicitly bounded size.

**Original and defining-clause seeds.** An original clause starts with its own placeholder; if the input list needs sorting or duplicate deletion, use the corrections below. For a reduced definition with operand values a,b and output z, take independently constructed B(a),B(b),W(b,a) and the supplied H=H_i. Put

    M=a W(b,a)b+B(a)bb+a B(b),
    D₁=B(a)b+a W(b,a)+H(1+a),
    D₂=a B(b)+H(1+b),
    D₃=M+Hab.                                                (3)

L024's defining-clause identities apply: these witnesses vanish at t=0 and substitute respectively to z(1+a), z(1+b), (1+z)ab. To check applicability, its required Boolean and pair values are exactly the L035/L036 outputs and its relation is (1); no bound h on the newly constructed local witnesses is assumed or needed. For its unused w-slots take w=0 and zero commutator witnesses; choose its local witness-degree parameter large enough for the constructed inputs. Its separate B_z and K_zw outputs are not used. Each displayed expression contains a fixed number of disjoint graph factors, so its ABP size is polynomial in the supplied input. Every occurrence of H is copied as its entire graph; no nonlinear sector is deleted. Exact signed representatives supply 1+a and 1+b. Sorting and duplicate deletion convert these definition-order lists to the actual source clauses.

**Local changes of factor lists.** Write f(L) for the ordered product of reduced falsity factors of a literal list L, with f(empty)=1. The factor of a positive literal on value p is 1+p; the negative literal has factor p. All these factors have x-only supplied graphs or constant-size wrappers around them. Let prefixes/suffixes themselves be products of independently copied factor graphs.

For an adjacent swap PuvR to PvuR, the local correction is

    P W(u,v) R.                                             (4)

For deletion PqqR to PqR, it is

    P B(q) R.                                               (5)

Each correction vanishes at zero and substitutes to the sum of the old and new list products. Summing corrections along a deterministic sequence of adjacent swaps and duplicate deletions therefore witnesses the difference of its initial and final products, by cancellation of intermediate products. No correction recursively depends on an earlier correction. Bubble sorting followed by duplicate deletion uses O(T²) changes on a list of length at most 2T. Complementary literals are distinct and retained; they are never deleted as duplicates. In particular, this procedure does not assume that a tautological clause's reduced product is identically zero. Moving a selected pivot to the front is another sequence of such swaps, with the remaining order preserved.

**The right-multiplied resolution recurrence.** Inductively write Q_j(A)=f(D_j) and Q_j(0)=0 for each earlier proof line j. These are polynomial invariants for now, not recursively copied graphs. Consider parents j,l with selected literals v and NOT v. Let p be the reduced positive value of v. Let E_j,E_l be the local pivot-movement corrections from (4), and let U,V be the products of the remaining lists. Then

    (Q_j+E_j)(A)=(1+p)U,       (Q_l+E_l)(A)=pV.

Construct W(U,V) directly from the x-only residual-product graphs. The new ordered identity is

    (1+p)UV+pVU+p(UV+VU)=UV.                                (6)

Indeed its five expanded terms are UV,pUV,pVU,pUV,pVU; the two identical pairs cancel in F₂. No factor has been commuted. Consequently, if E_out is the local correction normalizing the concatenated residual lists to the encoded resolvent, set

    L_k=E_j V+E_l U+p W(U,V)+E_out,
    Q_k=L_k+Q_j V+Q_l U.                                    (7)

Then Q_k(A)=f(D_k) and Q_k(0)=0. Both recursively reused roots are multiplied on the right. In particular, (7) does not replace UQ_l by Q_l U in L025's formula while leaving its old correction unchanged; it uses the different verified identity (6). All commutator calls in (7) concern x-only U,V, never Q_l or E_l. Opposite signs remaining in either residual list are harmless. If the same earlier record supplies both parents, the two occurrences become separate transitions and their multiplicities are preserved.

For weakening from j, let V be the product of newly added factors, append them to the old list and normalize. With E_out the resulting local correction use Q_k=Q_j V+E_out. Set inclusion of sorted clauses ensures this appending procedure produces the desired clause. An initial original or definition clause uses its seed plus its normalization correction, with no parent. Thus in all cases

    Q_k=L_k+sum_(j,occurrence) Q_j M_(j,k),                  (8)

where each referenced j<k, there are at most two occurrences, and every M_(j,k) is an x-only product of at most T factor graphs. Every L_k is a sum of independently built local blocks and satisfies L_k(0)=0. For resolution these are exactly (7), including pivot corrections followed by their right multipliers. All original, definition, normalization, weakening and resolution cases have now been covered. Induction proves the invariants without a placeholder-linearity assumption.

**One explicit graph for all reused lines.** Introduce a fresh source α, one fresh vertex v_k per proof record, and a fresh final sink ω. For each additive local block in L_k, place an independently copied graph between α and v_k, using two 1-connectors. For each parent occurrence j in (8), place an independently copied graph for M_(j,k) between v_j and v_k, again using two 1-connectors. These multiplier blocks contain only x and constants. All copies are mutually disjoint except for their specified exterior endpoints. Finally put a 1-edge from the last proof vertex to ω and designate α,ω as roots. There is no direct edge from α to a proof vertex except through a stated local block.

The graph is acyclic: any passage between proof vertices strictly increases the proof-record index, local blocks can only enter from α and exit to their assigned vertex, and ω has no outgoing edge. Each block is internally acyclic and admits no return from its designated exit. A topological ordering can interleave each transition block after its earlier endpoint and before its later endpoint, ordering all internal vertices as in the input; the partial order is consistent for the preceding reason.

The path polynomial from α to v_k equals its local-block sum plus the sums of paths ending at parent vertices followed by the corresponding multiplier block. This is precisely (8), including repeated parent occurrences and parallel transitions. Induction in k therefore identifies that path polynomial with Q_k. Branching and reconvergence sum all proof ancestry paths automatically; no earlier Q_j graph is copied or stored as a circuit-valued edge. The last clause is empty, so Q_last(A)=1 and Q_last(0)=0, proving (2).

A supplied H_i may have placeholder-free paths whose contributions cancel when t=0. We do not assert that every accepting path of C contains a placeholder, nor that C is placeholder-linear. The zero identity follows from the polynomial identities of complete local blocks and (8); it retains all such cancellations and all nonlinear H terms.

**Record ledger, complete degree and bit complexity.** Charge the full axiom framing in E, and set e=E+2. After label normalization and dense renaming, each representative/H graph and the total supplied collection have O(e) vertex/edge/symbol records. An x-only product of at most 2T factors has O(Te) records, by making a separate copy for each factor occurrence; this is a bounded list product, not proof-ancestry unfolding.

L035's exact graph ledger gives O(e³) records for a pair of representative factors. L036 gives O(e⁵) for a Boolean factor witness, since its Q is O(e). A swap or deletion correction, even with its surrounding context and one extra residual multiplier from (7), therefore has O(e⁵+Te) records. There are O(T²) such corrections per source record, hence O(T³) total. The fixed-number defining seeds (3) cost O(e⁵) records each. Original seeds are single-placeholder graphs.

There is at most one W(U,V) call per resolution record, whose operands have O(Te) records and singleton labels. L035's marked-edge ledger therefore bounds its records by O((Te)³); prefixing p adds O(e). Across at most T records these blocks cost O(T⁴e³). There are at most 2T parent transitions, each costing O(Te) records. The source, sink, proof vertices and two connectors per local/transition block have O(T³+1) additional records. In aggregate a conservative bound is

    O(T³(e⁵+Te)+T e⁵+T⁴e³+T²e+T³+1)=O(N⁸).               (9)

This ledger counts independently copied local witnesses, entire nonlinear H graphs, context factors, every transition occurrence, connectors and isolated vertices. It requires no sharing across independently written input roots. Reusing one constructed local graph could save size but is unnecessary and is not assumed. The number of abstract nodes/root associations is charged in S+E; unused supplied data need not appear in the output.

Store original symbol names and the full axiom list in the charged dictionary, then use dense symbol and vertex identifiers on all output edges. There are O(N⁸) graph records, each with O(log(N+2)) reference/framing bits and at most one symbol; the original dictionary costs O(E). Root references and list lengths fit the same allowance. This proves the full bit bound, with no unit-cost long identifiers. Parsing, label normalization, topological ordering, bounded list sorting, marked-edge loops, graph copying and renumbering all take polynomial bit time in N. No path or word enumeration, PIT, promise validation, or search for alternative witnesses is part of this general construction.

Every path in a finite DAG with V_C vertices has at most V_C−1 edges. With affine labels, every expanded word on such a path has length at most V_C−1 in the independent original x,t. Finite summation can only cancel words, not raise degree. Thus deg C≤V_C−1=O(N⁸), including every nonlinear placeholder sector. This bound does not infer syntactic degree from semantic d,h; it follows from the actual output graph. Substituting original axioms raises degree by at most a factor three, although (2) gives the actual final value 1. Selecting any proof vertex as output gives its invariant; copying the whole graph at most T times is a conservative polynomial bound for separate line outputs.

**Critical audit: resolution and proof reuse.** With pivot-positive parent first (interchange the two parent roles if necessary), substitution in the right-multiplied terms of (7) gives (1+p)UV+pVU; the correction adds pUV+pVU. Expanding the first term yields UV+pUV, and all four other terms cancel in equal ordered pairs. This does not use p²=p, pU=Up or UV=VU. These facts establish (8) for every permitted rule by induction on the actual record order, without bounding the number of ancestry paths.

For the graph induction, contract each disjoint block to its entrance-to-exit possibility. A cycle traversing an exterior connector would require either returning to α, leaving ω, or returning from a higher proof index to a lower one; none is permitted. A cycle internal to one block is excluded by its input DAG. Unreachable/dead vertices do not affect this argument. Every path from α to v_k has a unique last entry: a local block for k, or a parent-occurrence block j→k. Within that last block its segment is exactly a path between the designated endpoints. Leaving a designated exit internally cannot return to that exit, and entering any other block requires its exterior connector. Conversely any earlier accepting path and any such block path concatenate to a valid path, because the copies are disjoint and proof indices increase. Hence finite distributivity gives (8) with the correct multiplication order and occurrence multiplicity. Coincident block endpoints contribute the allowed empty path, unreachable endpoints contribute zero, and nonextremal endpoints introduce no further accepting segments. The fresh source has no incoming edge and the fresh sink has no outgoing edge, so no empty accepting path bypasses a local block. Induction at zero substitution uses L_k(0)=0, not a per-path placeholder property; induction at A gives the final constant 1.
