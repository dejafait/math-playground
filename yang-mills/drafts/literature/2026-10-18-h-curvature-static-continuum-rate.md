Ginovyan–Sahakyan, [*On the trace approximations of products of Toeplitz matrices*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.3568v1),
v1, 14 May 2014; published *Statistics & Probability Letters* 83
(2013), 753–760. Inspected definitions, Theorems 1–2, printed p. 6,
