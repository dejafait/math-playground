# Isolated coordinate-Haar cutoff interpretation

WHY IT FAILS: [L018](../lemmas/L018-coordinate-haar-contraction-and-forest-change.md)
shows that changing the exact rooted forest changes the quadratic density
insertion, while the complete fixed-mesh response coefficient is invariant.
The [evaluated test](../drafts/2026-10-04-coordinate-haar-contraction.md)
finds sizable sampled contraction differences for the original two probes
at one admitted mesh, with explicit statistical qualifications. Thus an
isolated M2 estimate cannot be assigned a forest-independent ultraviolet
meaning without the other action, insertion and flow terms. The exact
density example alone does not prove those particular contractions differ;
the random estimates alone do not prove a cutoff asymptotic. This stops
the isolated interpretation, not the full combined-response mechanism or
the Yang--Mills target. The fixed-mesh expansion and forest representation
are retained.
