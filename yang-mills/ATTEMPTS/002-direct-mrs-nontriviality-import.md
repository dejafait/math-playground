# Direct import of physical nontriviality from MRS

Tested 2026-09-24. The proposed shortcut was to use the published ultraviolet construction as an already-established source of a positive centered gauge-invariant reflection norm at fixed infrared cutoff.

## WHY IT FAILS

The [primary-source audit](../foundations/03-mrs-ultraviolet-scope.md) identifies an explicit missing positivity step and no observable lower bound in the cited main statement. Thus that statement cannot be inserted as a physical-nontriviality theorem in this notebook. This is a failure of the proposed import, not a counterexample to the construction or to Yang–Mills existence. An observable comparison with a strictly positive free leading coefficient and a cutoff-uniform smaller remainder remains a distinct, unproved possibility; the audit records its precise threshold and remaining requirements.
