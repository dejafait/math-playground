# Signed E,E sector: uniform global Hodge-energy remainder shortcut

WHY IT FAILS: [L160](../lemmas/L160-signed-remainder-hodge-energy-norm-obstruction.md)
proves that the full-link signed remainder E has Hodge-energy-normalized
norm Theta(N^(3/2)), rather than O(1). Its exact gradient/co-closed block
identity retains an off-diagonal prefix map. A constant axial edge mode
with the lowest transverse Dirichlet frequency produces the squared
norm N/lambda+2(N-1)+lambda(N-1)(2N-1)/6; the actual pinned upper
endpoint is essential. This defeats only a global uniform energy bound
for each E line in L156's E,E contraction. It neither bounds nor gives
growth of that signed contraction or either actual probe: compact
residual support and complete action gauge identities may remove the
packet's large endpoint contribution in the actual order. Keep the
unchanged complete cubic-scale target, its full tensors and both
insertion subtractions; continue with the exact Hodge blocks before
using inequalities. L158–L159's mixed bounds remain valid.
