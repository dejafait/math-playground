# Projection-side prefix: uniform axial norm-Taylor absorption fails

WHY IT FAILS: [L208](../lemmas/L208-projection-prefix-axial-smoothness-obstruction.md)
tests the actual finite VP=q Phi P block, rather than declaring it a
smooth coefficient in L207. Its transverse Dirichlet mode of energy 6
gives first and second interior axial differences at least 3/4 and
9/14. Every norm-C² interpolation therefore needs derivative bounds
at least 3/(4a) and 9/(14a²); even a right finite transverse energy
inverse square preserves the second-derivative obstruction. L207's
uniform-coefficient remainder cannot certify this factorwise use.
These are factor bounds, not a lower bound for the Taylor error or
either signed actual-probe response. Keep deterministic exterior
multipliers separate and evaluate the intervening finite Green
contraction in its original order. Plateaus, all four blocks, upper
jumps, full heat sums and possible signed cancellations remain.
ATTEMPTS/039–044 and their independent conclusions are unchanged.
