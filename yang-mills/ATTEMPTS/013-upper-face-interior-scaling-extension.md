# Extending the interior axial estimate to the upper pinned face

WHY IT FAILS: [L029](../lemmas/L029-upper-face-axial-action-loop.md)
retains the three surviving upper-face links and proves that its
quadratic Wick coefficient on the external common-path slice grows
as Theta(N). The corresponding smooth lifted column has an O(1),
nonvanishing limit for fixed relative modes; L028's interior O(a)
column estimate is false there. The complete termwise estimate is
only O(a^(-2)), not the sought O(1). This rules out carrying the
interior polynomial and difference estimates across the pinned jump;
it does not rule out a bounded signed upper-face response. Actual
probe cancellations, improved signed estimates and L019's inter-group
cancellations remain possible. Evaluate the full upper-face contraction
before assigning a power or logarithmic coefficient; neither a growing
Wick coefficient nor this upper bound proves signed growth.
