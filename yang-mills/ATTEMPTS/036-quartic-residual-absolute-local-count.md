# Quartic residual: the direct absolute local count does not close

WHY IT FAILS: [L149](../lemmas/L149-quartic-residual-local-wick-reduction.md)
retains the complete quartic insertion and its twelve connected
pairings. The direct absolute product of its local coefficient,
flowed external matrix and rough forest pair gives only O(a^-4)
after summing the supported placements. The separate insertion
subtractions have only an O(a^-3) estimate by this method. Neither
meets the required o(a^-3). This records insufficiency of that count,
not actual response growth or impossibility of a sharper absolute
bound. A signed contraction must control the complete normalized
sum through its order-a term, including both subtractions; simply
assuming bounded raw covariance would repeat ATTEMPTS/011. The
quartic target remains open and the signed mechanism is not stopped.
