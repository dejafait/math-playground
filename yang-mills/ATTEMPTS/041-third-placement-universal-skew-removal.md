# Third placement: universal cyclic removal leaves prefix transport

WHY IT FAILS: [L188](../lemmas/L188-third-placement-skew-commutator-split.md)
separates the exact projected Q_3 trace into a multiplication commutator
and a prefix-transport bracket. Constant multiplication kills only the
first. Its all-normal-end-row pinned-incidence fixture, with synthetic
S=P, A=M/2 and a full symmetric supported B, gives Q_3=-3/8 entirely
from transport. Thus skewness, symmetry and cyclicity cannot universally
remove the complete trace. This is a limitation of an algebraic shortcut,
not a result about actual-probe growth or convergence. A residual-specific
estimate, or joint cancellation with the retained sextic term, remains
independently testable. ATTEMPTS/039 and ATTEMPTS/040 are unchanged.
