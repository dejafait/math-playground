# Termwise transverse transport/projection commutation

WHY IT FAILS: [L096](../lemmas/L096-other-other-transverse-transport-commutator-obstruction.md)
proves that the actual endpoint-averaged transport multiplier has a
projection commutator bounded below by 1/100 for all sufficiently fine
meshes. The bound also holds for the active diagonal transport after
its centered transverse difference, before the generator's 1/a factor.
An exact compact gradient packet acquires nonzero 34 circulation because
the two active output filters differ. Thus scalar coefficient smoothness
does not justify an O(a) gain by commuting individual multipliers past P.
The full transverse generator still annihilates gradients through its
cross-component terms. This stops only the termwise operator shortcut,
not joint signed summation, a cubic bound, or the complete scaled limit.
No signed growth is established. Preserve ATTEMPTS/019–028. The separately
bounded curvature subtraction offers an independent scaled-limit test.
