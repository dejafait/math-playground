# Separate first absolute moments of the projected alternating kernels

WHY IT FAILS: [L109](../lemmas/L109-other-other-projected-alternating-kernel-moment-obstruction.md)
retains both original paths and all transverse planes in the complete
frozen gh alternating map. For every nonzero frozen exterior vector,
a diagonal symbol is not differentiable at zero, so its kernel has
infinite first absolute moment. The corresponding finite periodic
moments tend to infinity without an established rate. Thus a parity
error cannot be closed by assuming uniform first absolute moments
for these factors separately and applying absolute row/column sums.
This fails before any physical-boundary estimate is invoked. It does
not rule out a signed or quadratic commutator bound, a joint closed-
trace moment, or cubic control of the pinned trace. No periodic-to-
pinned replacement or signed response growth follows. Preserve stops
028--031; test a different joint energy estimate retaining circulation
and covariance together before taking absolute values.
