# Automatic frozen cancellation of the direct first pair

WHY IT FAILS: [L070](../lemmas/L070-first-pair-frozen-circulation-symbol.md)
retains the two endpoint placements, both left Green weights, the
direct covariance symbol and the alternating right paths. For a
constant exterior field the remaining circulation/path symbol has
angular mean 2u_1 gamma (R.v), with gamma>0. Summing transverse
planes or invoking odd-frequency/skew symmetry does not annihilate
it identically. This stops that automatic local cancellation argument,
not the actual O(N²) estimate: no pinned-trace approximation or
nonzero original-probe coefficient has been proved. The next distinct
test must retain the actual signed exterior heat and axial contraction.
L066's unrestricted norm obstruction and ATTEMPTS/019–021 remain
preserved; neither they nor this symbol test establish signed growth.
