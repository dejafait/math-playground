# Fixed-mass transverse H cancellation does not close the cubic estimate

WHY IT FAILS: [L099](../lemmas/L099-other-other-h-transverse-quartic-scale-vanishing.md)
transfers the full-generator frozen cancellation to the actual trace,
but proves only a^4 R_H,tr -> 0. Its undifferentiated prefix block is
O(1); the absolute transverse-mass error is O(N^4 sqrt(epsilon)),
leaving O(N sqrt(epsilon)) after cubic scaling. The localization and
finite-range approximation constants depend on epsilon, so their
fixed-mass limits cannot be used with epsilon=N^-2 without a new
uniform estimate. The two-orientation cancellation itself is valid;
it is the quantitative transfer at cubic scale that remains missing.
Park this fixed-parameter closure mechanism, not the complete trace
or the approved other/other target. No signed growth or failure of
the complete limit follows. Preserve L096/ATTEMPTS/029's different
termwise-filter obstruction. The separately cubic-bounded R_L offers
an independently testable scaled-limit target within the same group.
