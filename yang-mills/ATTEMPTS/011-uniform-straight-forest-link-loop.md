# Uniform raw local loop in the straight forest

WHY IT FAILS: [L026](../lemmas/L026-straight-forest-local-link-loop-growth.md)
proves j/8 <= Var_F(x_mu) <= j+8 for interior transverse edges in
the lower-rooted direction-1 forest, and hence ||C_p|| = Theta(a^(-1))
on fixed compact interior plaquettes. Thus an O(1) estimate of the
raw link-coordinate loop cannot complete L025's nonradial action
bound in this forest. The failure concerns that absolute matrix
bound: it does not prove a nonzero tensor contraction, power growth
of either probe response, or failure of the full matching route.
Any bounded contraction must exploit the growing directions, the
ordered tensor, or the cross-term cancellations retained in L019.
Keep the exact forest law and test those cancellations rather than
repeating the disproved raw-loop bound or applying heat damping to
an unflowed pair.
