# Complete residual tensor: absolute norm still misses cubic scale

WHY IT FAILS: [L183, section 6](../lemmas/L183-effective-cubic-residual-retraction.md)
forms the full effective residual's antisymmetric three-form and its
exact Gaussian norm before inequalities. The tilt acts by sums of
eigenvalues; the exact retraction cancellation removes its Lz-slot
weight, leaving both prefix-slot weights. The available full-spectrum
count then gives O(N^4), versus O(N^3) sufficient for L189's co-closed
initial interval. This stops the spectral-symmetrization/absolute-norm
method as currently estimated, not the sharper norm question or the
joint response. The upper bound proves neither growth nor failure of
convergence. The signed initial pairing E[I rho_H]-E[dI[W]] is a
different test, allowing differentiated probe heat factors before
inequalities. ATTEMPTS/039–042 remain intact.
