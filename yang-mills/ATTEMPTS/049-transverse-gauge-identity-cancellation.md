# Direct gauge-identity cancellation for the transverse coefficient

Stopped on 2026-10-25 after the second transverse attempt. The actual
Wilson logarithmic bound is unresolved, not disproved.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L280 section 8](../lemmas/L280-local-relative-coulomb-source-coefficient.md)
tests the exact source-weighted phase identities through quartic order
on the full finite relative edge domain, together with Haar invariance
and both quotient-density determinants. A gauge-invariant comparison
phase adds lambda times the square of the total Wilson action. All
orbit and measure identities persist, but the complete mixed Gaussian
coefficient changes by at least a constant times N^4, using L003's
positive reflected coefficient. Thus those identities alone cannot
force logarithmic growth. The comparison term is nonlocal and is
**not** a counterexample for the specified Wilson action. No signed
transverse kernel remainder of that action was controlled. Reopening
requires a new Wilson-specific locality or kernel estimate, not another
orbit-identity rearrangement. ATTEMPTS/047–048 remain separate evidence;
neither this stop nor those stops is a global prohibition on the target.
