# Applying the upper-source curvature-array bound to two interior levels

WHY IT FAILS: [L085](../lemmas/L085-other-other-two-level-covariance.md)
proves that the actual full two-interior-level curvature covariance has
Hilbert–Schmidt norm Theta(N^2), with squared norm at least
9 N^3(N-1)/100. Thus the O(N^(3/2)) upper-source array estimate cannot
be reused after replacing the upper vertex by a second interior one.
This is a complete-spectrum obstruction to that specific estimate,
not growth of either signed probe response or failure of its a^3 limit.
The exact two-level covariance and both connected classes remain in
L085(5)–(6); signed contraction with the actual observable derivatives
is a distinct unresolved mechanism. The prior stops 019–025 remain.
