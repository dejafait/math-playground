# Upper-face cancellation by axial annihilation

WHY IT FAILS: L030's axial integral does not kill the leading residual.
[L031](../lemmas/L031-upper-face-axial-projection-test.md) gives an
explicit compact test on which P N_2 P is nonzero, with a positive
unweighted pairing against the compressed triplet. Thus neither
P N_2=0 nor P N_2 P=0 can justify a zero value for I_i. Moreover the
actual signed trace projects only its first mode index; replacing its
second index by axial-zero modes drops terms. Stop this particular
projection-annihilation shortcut. A cancellation in the full weighted
sum remains possible and has not been tested to completion. The core
test supplies no sign or nonvanishing result for either original probe.
