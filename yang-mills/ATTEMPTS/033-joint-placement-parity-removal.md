# Removing the joint placement coefficient by undifferentiated parity

WHY IT FAILS: [L125](../lemmas/L125-other-other-joint-placement-first-moment.md)
proves that L121/L122's combined tensor is a first joint spatial moment,
not L118's zeroth diagonal. Fourier differentiation changes the relevant
odd symbol into an even integrand; the component-phase derivative is
tracked explicitly. L125(4) gives a strictly positive four-entry
same-plane combination for the actual ordered (rho=2,nu=3) generator
stencil. Thus the entire first-moment tensor cannot be declared zero by
the undifferentiated parity argument. The positivity test is not its
contraction with either physical probe and gives no signed growth or
failure of complete compensation. Keep all other tensor components,
generator placements and the remaining seven canonical coefficients.
This stops only automatic tensor removal; a joint identity with the
generator-displacement and exterior-circulation coefficients is still
a distinct, independently testable mechanism. Preserve ATTEMPTS/028--032.
