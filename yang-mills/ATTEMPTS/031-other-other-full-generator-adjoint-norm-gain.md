# Full-generator adjoint norm gain

WHY IT FAILS: [L108](../lemmas/L108-other-other-full-transverse-generator-adjoint-obstruction.md)
proves ||Qcal (G_tr+G_tr^T) Qcal/2||>=1/(16a) on the actual
pinned spaces. Exact compact co-closed packets retain the complete
cross-component generator and all endpoint/clover filters. Its
surviving 24 entry gives a nonzero symmetric quadratic form; the
packet error is O(1) before the Rayleigh quotient is scaled. Thus
adjoint symmetrization of the full generator alone cannot supply
the O(1) bound that would make this part of the H trace cubic.
The exact anticommutator/commutator identity remains valid. This
stops only that sufficient norm shortcut: the actual alternating
trace factors may still cancel the large symmetric generator.
Neither signed response growth nor failure of the cubic limit is
proved. Preserve ATTEMPTS/028--030. A trace-specific spatial parity
identity with controlled kernel moments is a different test; it
must retain the full prefix, both coefficient levels and pinned
covariance rather than assume an O(a) projection commutator.
