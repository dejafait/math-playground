# Uniform smallness of the upper conditional quartic defect

WHY IT FAILS: [L050](../lemmas/L050-upper-face-noise-defect-spectrum.md)
computes L049's complete boundary defect as P/N+b_N(Delta_T)(I-P).
The zero-divergence sector retains an O(1) defect at high frequency.
More decisively, a four-vertex potential field gives an actual product
Q=u(v) cross u(v') with P Q=0 and Z/q_A>=7/16 for every N>=4.
Thus neither an operator-norm o(1) bound nor uniform pointwise relative
smallness on the actual Q range can justify replacing the cubic pair
by the restricted quartic. This stops only that shortcut: the test is
not an actual-probe cumulant or evidence of cutoff growth, and R and E
may still compensate it. The exact fractional-energy representation
permits a different signed-contraction test with all residual and
observable derivatives retained. Smooth low-mode suppression remains
valid but cannot be assigned to all internal modes of Q.
