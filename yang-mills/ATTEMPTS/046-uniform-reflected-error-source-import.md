# Direct source import of the uniform reflected-error estimate

Reviewed 2026-10-25 for L003's exact Q, q and c_box/2 threshold along a
fixed physical-scale trajectory; no counterexample was attempted.

## WHY IT FAILS

The [completed comparison](../drafts/literature/2026-10-25-uniform-reflected-error-source-coverage.md)
checks Faria da Veiga–O'Carroll, arXiv:2205.07376v2, Theorem 4,
Remark 16 and section VI.5 against L003. Its periodic, differently
normalized source bounds are upper stability estimates; they give no
stated Gaussian-error allowance for the exact centered pinned-box
observable or a physical trajectory. Existing MRS and perturbative
assessments also fail that import test. This stops direct import from
the inspected results, not the bound, cited theorem, or Yang–Mills.
No absence/novelty claim is made. The different fixed-mesh coefficient
diagnostic needs separate source approval; no coefficient or uniform
remainder was derived.
