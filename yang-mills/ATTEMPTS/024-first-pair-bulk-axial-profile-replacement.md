# Uniform bulk replacement of the level-weighted axial profile

WHY IT FAILS: [L075](../lemmas/L075-first-pair-relative-axial-resolvent.md)
evaluates the actual Dirichlet inverse on the base row S. Its upper-face
correction has middle-level relative size sech(N eta/2), with positive
limit for every fixed physical transverse frequency. A mode in the
range of T also retains a strictly nonzero correction after the actual
u_1 multiplication and normalized constant-edge average. Thus neither
high-frequency localization nor the odd axial displacement justifies
a uniformly negligible bulk-distance replacement. This parks only that
replacement. It does not prove a nonzero signed heat sum, exclude a
contracted small-error estimate, or identify the diagnostic's coefficient.
The original path operator depends on level and remains inside L075's
complete kernel formula; further work must estimate that actual order.
ATTEMPTS/019–023 remain preserved.
