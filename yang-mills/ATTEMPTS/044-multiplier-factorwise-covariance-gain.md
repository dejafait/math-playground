# Actual multiplier: a factorwise covariance commutator does not gain

WHY IT FAILS: [L197](../lemmas/L197-multiplier-covariance-low-mode-obstruction.md)
uses the actual relative covariance and the actual modal multiplier
V S e_(0,1,1,1). Its covariance commutator has operator norm
Theta(N^3), including after only the multiplier's upper normal row
is deleted. Two exact co-closed constant-axial modes give a nonzero
N^(-3) matrix-element limit. Thus the proposed o(N^3), in particular
O(N^(5/2)), factorwise covariance estimate cannot recover the missing
half power. This is a low-physical-frequency obstruction, with no
claim about actual-probe mode weights, [M,Z], a signed response,
growth or failure of its limit. L194's residual and both rough action
slices must remain joint before estimates; cancellations between the
ordered residual/covariance commutators remain possible. Preserve
ATTEMPTS/039–043 and L196's independent transport bound.
