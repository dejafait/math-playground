# Linear operator gain for the actual reverse-prefix block

WHY IT FAILS: [L094](../lemmas/L094-other-other-reverse-prefix-operator-obstruction.md)
proves ||C G_T1^T Qcal||>=c N^2 on the actual finite relative spaces.
An exact gradient column produces a compact component-1 adjoint
prefix with a full 1/a amplification; unequal clover/output filters
leave a transverse curl, rather than a gradient removed by Qcal.
The compact-packet proof retains both axial ends and all coefficient
placements. Thus an O(N) bound analogous to L093's first orientation
cannot supply the reverse cubic trace estimate. This is not growth
of the signed trace or failure of the complete a^3 limit. Its actual
alternating factors and covariance weights can still yield a signed
gain, and the H diagonal provides an independently testable trace.
Keep stop evidence 019–027; this stop concerns only the displayed
operator-norm shortcut, not the complete other/other target.
