# Uniform mixed residual bound for the full right vector

WHY IT FAILS: [L066](../lemmas/L066-mixed-transverse-residual-obstruction.md)
proves ||R_0T||=Theta(1/a) for the actual direct transverse block.
Unequal clover/endpoint filters at the retained carrier
(0,pi/2,pi,0) leave a non-gradient cross-component generator output.
A compact right axial profile supported where u_1=x_1>0 retains it
after the constant left axial projection. This stops an O(1)
full-vector extension of L065, whose component-1 bound remains valid.
It does not imply growth of L064's actual signed response: paths,
co-closed cross covariance, endpoint multipliers, inverse covariances
and heat coefficients still have to be composed in their actual order.
Continue only with a test of that composition or a materially different
signed mechanism, rather than another unrestricted residual norm.
The original failures ATTEMPTS/018–020 remain preserved.
