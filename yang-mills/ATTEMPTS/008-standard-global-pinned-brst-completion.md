# Standard global BRST completion with pinned boundary

The proposed completion of L014 would replace its linear auxiliary/ghost
functional with standard smooth BRST-exact Landau gauge fixing of all
compact SU(2) Wilson links. The prior assessment is
[compact normalization](../drafts/literature/2026-10-04-compact-brst-gauge-fixing-normalization.md).
The boundary-specific applicability result is
[L015](../lemmas/L015-boundary-pinned-compact-brst-normalization-vanishes.md).
This is a known obstruction applied locally, not a new no-go theorem.

WHY IT FAILS: Pinning transformations at boundary vertices leaves the
compact group SU(2)^((N-1)^4), with a nonempty ghost sector and no manifold
boundary. The full nonlinear BRST algebra preserves the restrictions,
the trace-potential Landau data are smooth globally, and positive
auxiliary width makes all bosonic coefficients integrable. Therefore
Neuberger's cited vanishing theorem applies: the gauge-fixed denominator
and gauge-invariant numerators are zero. They cannot define normalized
Wilson expectations by division. Removing the positive width supplies
no nonzero normalization without a separately justified changed
prescription.

Stop this standard smooth global completion. The exact forest measure,
the local free identities and research on the continuum target remain
available. This failure supplies no interacting reflected-error bound
or mass-gap conclusion; the required error remains <= c_box/2. A direct
Ward formulation on the existing forest quotient is a different finite
measure mechanism requiring its own screening.
