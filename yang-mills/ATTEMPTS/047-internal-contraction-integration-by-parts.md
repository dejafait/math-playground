# Direct internal-contraction rearrangement for the reflected coefficient

Stopped after two exploratory attempts on 2026-10-25. The full signed
logarithmic-growth target remains unresolved, not disproved.

## WHY IT FAILS

[L279 sections 6–7](../lemmas/L279-fixed-mesh-exact-reflected-coefficient.md)
retain the complete source coefficient and all relative boundaries.
The separated quadratic response estimate leaves internal forest
covariances uncontrolled. Gaussian integration by parts absorbs the
explicit Haar trace but preserves exactly its expectation and mixed
source derivative; the resulting quartic does not vanish pointwise,
as checked on the actual Wilson jets. This does not exclude cancellation
after Gaussian integration or a logarithmic bound. It supplies no
controlled residual, so another rearrangement alone is not justified.
A local nonlinear gauge-coordinate representation is a different
proposed mechanism, requiring separate literature review of the full
Jacobian, insertions and boundary hypotheses before mathematical use.
Neither the previous scalar-only/bulk-only stops nor this stop is a
global ban on the coefficient or a counterexample to Yang–Mills.
