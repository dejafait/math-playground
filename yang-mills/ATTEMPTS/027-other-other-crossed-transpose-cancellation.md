# Automatic cancellation of crossed residual orientations

WHY IT FAILS: [L091](../lemmas/L091-other-other-crossed-residual-block-compression.md)
proves that, in the actual complete exterior sum, P V Q and Q V P
give equal traces for either choice of base covariance. They therefore
add with factor 2 in each of L091(4)'s crossed pairs. Antisymmetry of
the alternating matrices and symmetry of the residual and exterior
coefficients do not provide automatic transpose cancellation. This
does not prove either actual pair is nonzero, divergent or unable to
cancel the other sectors. L091 additionally bounds the gradient-base
pair by O(N^3); its coefficient and the joint six-sector limit remain
open. A signed estimate using the actual generator/prefix composition
is a distinct mechanism. Preserve the previous stop evidence 019–026.
