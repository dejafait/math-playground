Stopped on 2026-10-27 by the complete infrared calculation in
[L296](../lemmas/L296-bare-heat-tail-infrared-obstruction.md), with
the stop confirmed by its separate conceptual audit on 2026-10-28.
This is the exact source-dependent

with L280's unchanged Wilson jets and full density.

## WHY IT FAILS

The source-dependent endpoints are correct, but L296(6),(23) give a
negative complete response of order N^(1/2)(log N)^2 at r of order
N^2 log N. The full cubic and quartic responses tend to zero there;
the retained density supplies the leading term. At twice that time
the whole response tends to zero. Thus a complete signed shell is
unbounded, including on L296(24)'s ordinary dyadic subsequence, and
the proposed O(1) terminal infrared bound fails at that terminal
scale. This is stronger than a bad bound for one selected group.
It does not estimate the uncut kappa_a, forbid cancellations across
scales, address a different fixed physical terminal time, or rule out
effective vertices/density that change with scale. Such a replacement
would need a materially different, separately assessed test. Retain
ATTEMPTS/047--049 as distinct earlier failures; none refutes Yang--Mills.
