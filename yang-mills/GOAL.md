# Yang–Mills existence and mass gap

Apply ../GOAL.md as the shared research policy.

## Exact target and traps

For every compact simple gauge group, construct a nontrivial quantum Yang–Mills theory on R^4 satisfying the axioms in Clay’s official problem statement and establish a positive mass gap. A lattice or finite-volume result alone is insufficient.
