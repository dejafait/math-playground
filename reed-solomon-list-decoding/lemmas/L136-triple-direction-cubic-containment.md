# L136 — Cubic containment for each triple of complete dual directions

## Hypotheses

Retain the pinned setup of L135. Thus q=137,
T={16,64,70,102,112,118,121} is a subset of the auxiliary F_137,
E=F_65537, F=F_(65537^28), H=<19139> has order 1024, and k=64.
Let C be an arbitrary 61-subset of H and let sigma be any bijection
from (T times F_137) disjoint union four dummy points to H minus C.
Write x_(t,y)=sigma(t,y). These 959 actual field labels are distinct.
Let K be E or F; no embedding of F_137 into K is assumed.

For every P=(u,v) in F_137^2, let g_P in K[X] have degree at most two,
with zero polynomials and repeated g_P permitted. For each (t,y),
assume g_P(x_(t,y)) has a common value d_(t,y) as P ranges over
A_(t,y)={(u,v):u+tv=y}. Set g_0=g_(0,0), and assume

\[
 V=\operatorname{span}_K\{g_P-g_0:P\in\mathbf F_{137}^2\},
 \qquad \dim_K V=2,\qquad W=g_0+V.
\]

Use L135's retained directions: all seven when there is no exceptional
evaluation level set, and the six obtained by omitting the entire
direction containing the unique exceptional level set otherwise.
For a retained t, let

\[
 D_t=\{\ell_{t,y}^{*}:y\in\mathbf F_{137}\},\qquad
 \ell_{t,y}=\{g\in W:g(x_{t,y})=d_{t,y}\},
\]

where the star denotes the point dual to the projective closure of
the affine line in the projective completion of W.

## Conclusion

For every three distinct retained directions a,b,c, there is a nonzero
homogeneous form Q_(a,b,c) of degree three over K vanishing on
D_a union D_b union D_c in the projective dual plane. Each union has
411 distinct K-rational points. The form may depend on the triple;
reducible, singular and nonreduced cubics are allowed, with no uniqueness
or descent of individual factors asserted.

This is a specialization of a cited cyclic light-multinet theorem.
It does not exclude span two, put all retained components on a single
cubic, handle span three or deletions, prove a retention cap, or transfer
to RS lists. The separate critical audit on 2026-10-28 accepts this
specialization with its stated hypotheses and qualifications.

## Proof

**Imported theorem and scope.** The pre-invocation SPECIALIZE assessment
is [the cyclic-cubic assessment](../drafts/literature/2026-10-27-l135-cyclic-cubic-containment.md).
We use Gábor Korchmáros and Gábor P. Nagy, *Group-labeled light dual
multinets in the projective plane (with Appendix)*,
[arXiv:1710.00172v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.00172v1),
30 September 2017, Definition 2.1, printed p. 3, the algebraic terminology
on p. 4, and Proposition 4.5, p. 10. In the algebraically closed setting,
the proposition puts the three components of a finite cyclic light
dual multinet on a cubic. Component maps are injective, components are
disjoint, and the specified product triples must be collinear; additional
incidences are permitted. Algebraic containment permits singular or
reducible cubics. No long-line hypothesis from Theorem 1.1 is used.

The prior assessment inspected the proposition's setup and degeneration
cases and its referenced propagation argument. The latter is in
Gábor Korchmáros, Gábor P. Nagy and Nicola Pace, *3-nets realizing a
group in a projective plane*,
[arXiv:1104.4439v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.4439v3),
17 June 2011: standing assumptions on p. 3, Lamé's theorem in
Proposition 2.3, pp. 3–4, and the cyclic case in Proposition 6.1,
pp. 17–18. We retain the conservative positive-characteristic conditions
p>=5 and n<p from that reference. They hold below. The 2017 paper's
reference to the published Proposition 18 must not be confused with the
inspected arXiv numbering. No reproof of that propagation, subgroup
analysis or cubic classification is needed here. We prove only the
local labeling, scalar-extension and descent obligations.

**Coordinates and the cyclic labeling.** Choose a K-basis h_1,h_2
of V, so every g_P is uniquely g_0+z_(P,1)h_1+z_(P,2)h_2. In homogeneous
primal coordinates [z_1:z_2:w], the dual point to a retained line is

\[
 p_{t,y}=[h_1(x_{t,y}):h_2(x_{t,y}):g_0(x_{t,y})-d_{t,y}]. \tag{1}
\]

These are K-rational projective points: L135 guarantees that each
retained normal (the first two entries) is nonzero. It also guarantees
that the maps y -> p_(t,y) are injective and have disjoint images.

Fix any ordered triple of distinct retained a,b,c. All arithmetic in
the next two displays is in the auxiliary F_137. Put

\[
 \lambda=\frac{b-c}{b-a},\qquad
 \mu=\frac{c-a}{b-a}.
 \tag{2}
\]

The denominator and both numerators are nonzero. For any y_a,y_b,
the two auxiliary lines of directions a,b meet at the unique point

\[
 v=\frac{y_b-y_a}{b-a},\qquad
 u=\frac{b y_a-a y_b}{b-a},\qquad
 u+cv=\lambda y_a+\mu y_b.
 \tag{3}
\]

Let G be the additive group of F_137. It is cyclic of order 137,
generated by 1, since F_137 is a prime field. Define its three component
maps by

\[
 \alpha_1(r)=p_{a,\lambda^{-1}r},\qquad
 \alpha_2(s)=p_{b,\mu^{-1}s},\qquad
 \alpha_3(t)=p_{c,t}.                                  \tag{4}
\]

Multiplication by either inverse scalar is a permutation of F_137.
Consequently the three maps are injective, have disjoint images, and
their images are exactly D_a,D_b,D_c. Given any r,s in G, (3) provides
P on the auxiliary lines with indices lambda^(-1)r, mu^(-1)s, r+s.
The three primal evaluation lines all contain g_P, by the hypothesis
defining their common values. Thus their three distinct dual points
alpha_1(r), alpha_2(s), alpha_3(r+s) are collinear. Explicitly, they
lie on the dual line with equation
z_(P,1)Y_0+z_(P,2)Y_1+Y_2=0, using (1).

These are precisely the prescribed group triples in Definition 2.1,
written additively. The relabelings (2)–(4) act on an index set only;
they do not send auxiliary field scalars into K. Repeated g_P can repeat
a connecting dual line, and other triples can also be collinear.
Neither possibility violates the light-multinet hypotheses.

**Extension and all degeneracies.** Embed K in an algebraic closure
Kbar and retain the coordinates (1). A nonzero K-vector remains
nonzero over Kbar. Two such vectors represent different projective
points exactly when at least one of their 2-by-2 minors is nonzero;
this condition is unchanged under the field embedding. Collinearity
of three points is the vanishing of their 3-by-3 determinant and is
also unchanged. Thus (4) remains a cyclic light dual multinet of order
137 over Kbar, with all 411 points distinct.

If all 411 points lie on one projective line over Kbar, choose any
two of them. Their cross product gives a nonzero linear form L over K
whose zero line contains them. Uniqueness of the line through two
distinct points makes this the line containing the entire union.
The polynomial L^3 is a nonzero homogeneous cubic over K containing
the union, so the conclusion holds in this case directly.

Otherwise apply Proposition 4.5 as cited above to the noncollinear
configuration over Kbar. Its group is cyclic, its components meet
the injectivity and disjointness requirements, and the collinearity
condition has been proved for every group pair. Both permitted K
have characteristic p=65537>137=n, so even the referenced proof's
standing positive-characteristic conditions are satisfied. The full
proposition covers its reducible and singular cases; no unproved
ordinary-net, nonsingularity, irreducibility, faithful-action or
long-line assumption is inserted. It gives a nonzero homogeneous
degree-three form Qbar over Kbar vanishing on the union.

**Descent of the containing form.** Use the actual nonzero K-vectors
in (1) as representatives of the 411 points. Form the 411-by-10
matrix M over K whose row at a representative v is

\[
 \big(v_0^3,v_0^2v_1,v_0^2v_2,v_0v_1^2,v_0v_1v_2,
       v_0v_2^2,v_1^3,v_1^2v_2,v_1v_2^2,v_2^3\big).
\]

The nonzero coefficient vector of Qbar is in the kernel of M over
Kbar, so rank_(Kbar)(M)<=9. Matrix rank is preserved under field
extension: every minor has its determinant in K, and that determinant
vanishes over K if and only if it vanishes over Kbar. The largest
nonzero-minor size is therefore the same in both fields. Hence
rank_K(M)<=9, and rank-nullity gives a nonzero vector in ker_K(M).
Its ten coefficients define the required nonzero homogeneous cubic
Q_(a,b,c) over K. The ten formal monomials are linearly independent,
so a nonzero coefficient vector is a nonzero polynomial. Rescaling
point representatives only rescales rows by nonzero cubes and changes
neither vanishing nor the kernel. No factor of Qbar has been descended.

The argument applied to an arbitrary ordered retained triple, including
any triple of the six available in the exceptional case. It only uses
the separation and concurrency supplied by L135, not an arbitrary
direction-omission test or the working identities (W1)–(W5). Choosing
different triples can produce different forms; the proof makes no
compatibility or common-cubic assertion. This proves the conclusion.

**Separate critical audit, 2026-10-28.** The pre-invocation
[audit coverage](../drafts/literature/2026-10-27-l136-critical-audit-coverage.md)
is SPECIALIZE. The audit accepts the proof without changing its
hypotheses or conclusion. The following checks concern its local
applicability and descent, not a reproof of the imported theorem.

All labels x_(t,y) belong to E and hence to either permitted K; all
entries of (1), including d_(t,y), therefore belong to K. The affine
line equation is h_1(x)z_1+h_2(x)z_2+g_0(x)-d=0, which checks the
sign of its third dual coordinate. Its first two coefficients are
not both zero by L135, even in the exceptional case after its prescribed
entire direction is omitted. L135 supplies all pairwise distinctions,
not just distinctions within each class. Thus three selected classes
have exactly 3*137=411 distinct points, independent of repetitions
among the coefficient images g_P.

To check (3) independently, substitution gives
u+av=((b-a)y_a)/(b-a)=y_a and
u+bv=((b-a)y_b)/(b-a)=y_b. Substitution of c gives
((b-c)y_a+(c-a)y_b)/(b-a). The three pairwise distinct directions
make b-a, b-c and c-a nonzero in F_137, so both inverse
permutations in (4) exist. For each of the 137^2 ordered pairs (r,s),
this constructs one auxiliary P with designated third index r+s.
Its coefficient coordinates satisfy all three primal equations.
Consequently all three dual points satisfy the displayed dual-line
equation, whose coefficient vector (z_(P,1),z_(P,2),1) is nonzero.
This proves the required incidence for every pair, including zero
indices, without assuming the converse incidence or transporting
F_137 scalars into K.

The audit rechecked Definition 2.1, the algebraic terminology and
Section 4/Proposition 4.5 in the versioned 2017 source cited above.
Its noncollinear setup is met after the separate line-cube branch;
its cyclic theorem allows the singular and reducible cases. The
referenced 2011 Proposition 6.1 and its standing characteristic
conditions were also checked. Here 65537>137 and 65537>=5. The
2011 ordinary-net theorem is not applied directly to this light
multinet. No classification requiring a long line is substituted.

For extension, a nonzero 2-by-2 minor distinguishing two K-vectors
remains nonzero, and a zero incidence determinant remains zero.
This verifies preservation even when some coordinates vanish. In the
wholly collinear branch, two distinct points give a nonzero cross
product over K. Its line is the unique line through them after
extension, so it contains all 411 points; its cube is nonzero since
K[Y_0,Y_1,Y_2] is an integral domain.

In the remaining branch, the nonzero cubic provided over Kbar has
ten formal degree-three coefficients and supplies a nonzero kernel
vector for exactly the displayed 411-by-10 matrix. The minor test
proves extension-invariant rank in every case, including rank below
nine; no nonsingularity of a chosen minor is presumed. Rank at most
nine in a ten-dimensional domain gives kernel dimension at least
one over K. Distinct formal monomials make the resulting form
nonzero, regardless of reducibility, repeated factors or any finite
field polynomial-function issue. Row rescaling by a nonzero cube
does not change this kernel. Thus neither Galois averaging nor
descent of factors or uniqueness is needed.
