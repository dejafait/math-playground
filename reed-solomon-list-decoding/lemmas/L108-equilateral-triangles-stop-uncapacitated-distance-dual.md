# L108 — Equilateral triangles stop the uncapacitated distance dual

## Hypotheses

Let V be all 68-subsets of a fixed 1024-point set, H=binomial(1024,68),
and i(A,B)=|A\B|. Use L098's exact integer
N=floor(65537^28/2^128)+1 and fixed pairs c_i, with
c_0=1, c_1=...=c_4=0 and c_i>=0 for 5<=i<=68.
Explicitly, for 5<=i<=68, c_i=alpha*a_i/(2*g_i),
g_i=binomial(68,i)binomial(956,i),
alpha=2(N-1)/(U-1), U=binomial(1024,64)/binomial(68,64),
where a_i is L021's exact positive vector. In particular N>2,
alpha>0 and sum_i g_i*c_i=N, as established in L098.

On every unordered distinct pair e={A,B}, prescribe
d_e=(N-2)c_{i(A,B)}. Keep zero-demand edges at i=1,...,4.
Let W be the edge/triangle incidence matrix of the complete graph on V:
every unordered triple of distinct supports is a column, with three
entries one. The test is membership of d in {Wz:z>=0}, without capacities,
PSD, labeled RS restrictions or any identification of source variables
with different t-s.

## Conclusion

The distance-dependent dual cone is exactly the nonnegative orthant:

    lambda_i+lambda_j+lambda_h>=0 for every actual distinct triangle
    if and only if lambda_i>=0 for every i=1,...,68.

Consequently no such dual separates d. In fact no unrestricted edge dual
separates d, by constraint-preserving S_1024 averaging, and the finite
Farkas alternative implies d belongs to the uncapacitated triangle cone.
This stops this diagnostic, not the capacitated shared-triple problem.
It gives no scalar-capacity witness, full source lift, PSD extension,
packing, RS list, uniform list bound or change to the 955–957 bracket.
The local specialization is REPRODUCTION of the assessed finite cone
method; the separate critical proof review below retains this limited stop.

## Proof

**Imported input and why this is a necessary relaxation.** Import the
finite Farkas alternative for nonnegative solutions of Ax=b from
Dukes–Garaschuk, *On the cone of weighted graphs generated by triangles*,
[arXiv:1608.06017v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1608.06017v1),
Section 2.1, Theorem 2.1, printed p.3. Section 2.2, p.4, specifies the
complete edge/triangle incidence matrix, including zero-weight edges;
Section 2.3, p.5, supplies automorphism averaging of dual tests.
These primary passages were inspected in the prior SPECIALIZE assessment
`drafts/literature/2026-10-21-distance-dependent-triangle-dual.md`.
Use the standard sign convention: Wz=d,z>=0 is feasible exactly when
every u with W^T u>=0 has <u,d>>=0. No strict feasibility or SDP duality
is needed. Only the following finite applicability and geometry require
local proof; no general cone theorem is reproved.

L098's Boolean moment identity, normalized by s_0=N/H>0, says that the
sum of normalized third-support moments on an edge equals N*c_i.
For A distinct from B the summands at Z=A and Z=B are distinct, each
equal to c_i with coefficient one. Removing both leaves (N-2)c_i,
as in L107. The remaining moment for each unordered distinct triple is
nonnegative and independent of its presentation, hence defines a
nonnegative column weight for W. This proves the necessary cone
condition for any full feasible moment assignment. It divides only by
s_0, never by a possibly zero c_i. There is no edge at i=0, where the
two repetitions would instead coincide. Discarding capacities enlarges
the necessary problem; the converse conversion to a full source
assignment is not being asserted.

**All actual triangles and the action.** A coordinate permutation acts
bijectively on V, unordered edges and unordered distinct triangles,
preserving incidence and half-distance. Thus it preserves W's column
cone and d. Two unordered edges with the same i are in one orbit:
their four regions A intersection B, A\B, B\A and outside A union B
have respective sizes 68-i,i,i,956-i, and bijections of these regions
give a coordinate permutation taking one edge to the other.
Different i cannot be in one orbit. Averaging any feasible edge dual
over the finite group S_1024 therefore yields weights lambda_i.
Each triangle inequality remains nonnegative, since its permuted
triangles are still columns. Also the pairing with d is unchanged:
permutation invariance of d permits reindexing the finite edge sum.
In particular a strict negative pairing would remain strict after
averaging. This uses the ordinary unlabeled cone only; it makes no
claim about averaging labeled RS constraints.

**Realizable equal-side triangles force every sign.** Fix any
1<=i<=68. Choose disjoint sets K,P,Q,R of respective sizes
68-i,i,i,i. They exist because 68+2i<=204<=1024. The three supports
K union P, K union Q and K union R are distinct, have size 68 and
have every half-distance equal to i. Thus the complete triangle system
contains 3*lambda_i>=0, forcing lambda_i>=0 over the reals.

This construction also lies in the exact original orbit ranges of
foundations/04. Anchor at A=K union P, take B=K union Q and Z=K union R.
Then (i,j,t,s)=(i,i,i,0), so h=i+j-t-s=i. Its ranges hold:
max(0,2i-68)<=i<=i and max(0,2i-956)=0<=s=0<=i.
Its third-support multiplicity at a fixed edge is

    D(i,i,i,0)=binomial(956-i,i)>0,

since 956-i>=i for i<=68. Hence no impossible orbit, repeated vertex
or distance-only surrogate was used. In particular the equal-side
triangles at null-edge distances 1,...,4 remain columns of W.

Conversely if every lambda_i>=0 then its sum on every actual triangle
is nonnegative, regardless of that triangle's orbit or t-s. This proves
the exact dual-cone characterization without enumerating the remaining
triangles or imposing a distance-only primal lift. A subset of necessary
dual inequalities suffices for this nonseparation conclusion; a proposed
separator would have required checking every triangle instead.

**Pairing with all unordered multiplicities.** Each support has
g_i=binomial(68,i)binomial(956,i) distinct neighbors at distance i.
Summing over H supports counts an unordered edge twice, giving H*g_i/2
edges in that class. These exhaust the edges because
sum_(i=1)^68 g_i=H-1; this follows by partitioning all 68-subsets by
intersection size with a fixed one. Therefore

    <lambda,d> = H*(N-2)/2 * sum_(i=1)^68 g_i*c_i*lambda_i >= 0.

All factors in each summand are nonnegative. The required separator
threshold is strictly below zero; the actual minimum over dual-feasible
vectors is exactly zero, attained at lambda=0. Zero-demand classes
contribute zero, without deletion or division. By the averaging argument
there is also no noninvariant strict separator. The cited finite Farkas
alternative now gives a nonnegative uncapacitated triangle weighting.
This is an exact symbolic certificate, so no optimizer, numerical script
or large certificate is required.

The proof does not bound individual triangle weights by any c_i, impose
Boolean lower scalar rows, or verify a t-s-preserving source completion.
The full source system can therefore still fail despite this cone pass.
L105's independent-row result, L106's restricted-support failure and
L107's sufficient-theorem failure retain their scopes. All numerical-run
stops in ATTEMPTS/081–082,088–090 remain in force.

**Separate critical audit (2026-10-22, step 405).** Reuse the prior
SPECIALIZE coverage in `drafts/literature/2026-10-22-l108-critical-audit.md`,
including the precise finite Farkas import above. The audit imports

sum_(i=1)^68 g_i*c_i=alpha*(U-1)/2=N-1; the repeated coordinate

For fixed distinct A,B, the normalized homogeneous contraction in L098
has exactly one summand for each support Z. The actual repeated terms
have indices (i,0,0,0) and (i,i,i,i), respectively, with D=1 each.
Boolean union identifies each with c_i. Thus precisely 2*c_i is removed,
including when c_i=0. At A=B these terms coincide and only one repeated
support exists; that row is the objective and supplies no edge of W.
The normalization divides only by N/H>0. A common nonnegative moment
on each unordered distinct triple then contributes once to each of its
three edges, with no extra factor of three or six. This verifies the
necessary relaxation without presuming a representing distribution.

Independently reconstruct the equal-side geometry by its eight atoms.
In membership-bit order 000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111 their sizes are

    956-2i, i, i, 0, i, 0, 0, 68-i.

They are nonnegative for every 1<=i<=68 and sum to 1024. Every support
has 68 points, all three are distinct, and all six presentations have
(i,j,t,s)=(i,i,i,0), with t-s=i and third distance i. In particular
i=68 has empty common core but remains a legitimate triangle; i=1,...,4
are actual triangles of the complete support graph despite forbidden
code distances. At a fixed edge A,B, these indices force Z to contain
all of A intersection B and none of either pair-only region. Its i
remaining points can be any subset of the 956-i outside points, giving
exactly binomial(956-i,i)>0. This checks the source coefficient by direct
set choices, not only by substituting a claimed multiplicity formula.

For an arbitrary feasible edge dual u, define
u_bar(e)=|S_1024|^(-1)*sum_sigma u(sigma(e)). A permuted triangle has
exactly its three permuted edges, so the sum of u_bar on any triangle
is an average of nonnegative sums. Moreover d_sigma(e)=d_e, and changing
variables in each finite sum gives <u_bar,d>=<u,d>. Bijections of the
four pair regions show u_bar is constant precisely on the half-distance
classes. This argument preserves all actual triangle constraints and
strict negativity, without requiring transitivity on triangle classes
or averaging any actual-label RS restriction. The equal-side constraints
force every one of its 68 coordinates nonnegative. Conversely such
coordinates satisfy every triangle inequality; no omitted triangle can
break that direction. Thus checking only equal-side triangles suffices
for this exact characterization of invariant duals. It would not suffice
for verifying a proposed negative separator.

Each class has H*g_i/2 edges, because the neighbor count g_i is symmetric
in its endpoints. The classes exhaust H*(H-1)/2 edges by sum_i g_i=H
including g_0=1. All 68 coefficients H*(N-2)*g_i*c_i/2 in the full
pairing are nonnegative, including four zeros. Hence the invariant
minimum is exactly zero, attained at the zero dual. The averaging
argument rules out an unrestricted strict separator too. Apply precisely
the finite nonnegative-system alternative to W: it has finitely many
rows and columns, although enormously many. There is no infinite-limit,
strict-feasibility or SDP-duality hypothesis. This retains uncapacitated
cone membership, not an assertion that every dual itself is pointwise
nonnegative or that triangle weights obey scalar capacities.
