### Exact inspected source content

Use Q,d,t for the design parameters, to avoid confusing them with the
auxiliary q=137 and the RS parameter k=64. The support conditions are
row size at most Q, column size at least d, and pairwise column overlap
at most t.

**BDWY.** [Version 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1009.4375v2), Theorem 3.2,
p. 8, gives rank at least n-(Qtn/(2d))^2 for complex matrices. Theorem
8.3, p. 28, transfers a lower bound valid for every complex matrix with
one exact support to characteristic zero or p>P0(n,m). Its proof uses
the integer Nullstellensatz identity (2): the product of supported
variables, raised to a power and multiplied by an integer alpha, lies
in the ideal generated by the forbidden entries and rank minors.
Transfer requires alpha nonzero in the destination field. The text
asserts effective bounds via Theorem 8.2 but does not display an evaluated
P0 or a certificate alpha for the present sizes. Neither 65537>P0 nor
65537 not dividing alpha is verified. No effective-Nullstellensatz
reproof or audit of Theorem 8.2's coefficient conventions is imported.

**DSW.** The [journal version](https://www.math.ias.edu/~avi/PUBLICATIONS/DvirSaWi2015.pdf),
Theorem 1.3, p. 3, gives
rank at least n/(1+Q(Q-1)mt/(nd^2)), hence at least
n-mtQ(Q-1)/d^2. Corollary 1.4 gives the denominator
1+Q(Q-1)t/d. Theorem 1.5, pp. 3–4, assumes d<=nt and gives
rank at least n-6mn^2t^3/d^4. Definition 1.1 allows arbitrary fields,
but the proof uses complex scaling (Corollary 2.4, p. 10), the Hermitian
Lemma 3.5 with its real-eigenvalue proof (pp. 13–14), and A'*A'
in Section 3.2 (pp. 14–15). Those steps do not give a finite-field
application. No characteristic-transfer theorem was located in either
the journal text or arXiv:1211.0330v1. The PDF extraction renders
non-strict inequality symbols incorrectly in places; the matching
arXiv statements specify the bounds above with >=.
