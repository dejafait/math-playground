## Primary comparisons actually inspected

Irit Dinur, Yuval Filmus and Prahladh Harsha, *Agreement Tests on Graphs
and Hypergraphs*, [published primary text](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/full/10.1137/21M1397684),
published online 5 March 2025: Theorem 1.2 assumes d<=t<k<=n,
rho in (0,1), and 2k-t<=n-rho(k-t). Local functions are indexed by every
k-subset; the test chooses a uniform t-set and two k-extensions having
exactly that intersection. Small failure gives one global function agreeing
on most k-sets, with constants depending on d,t/k,rho. The whole statement
and mu/nu sampling definitions were read. Neither those laws nor polynomial
structure of the glued function is available here. No application is made.

Venkatesan Guruswami and Swastik Kopparty, *Explicit Subspace Designs*,
[March 2013 author version](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~sk1233/subspace-designs.pdf):
Lemma 10, printed p. 7, gives Wronskian independence when the strict degree
bound is below the characteristic. Theorem 17, pp. 11–12, assumes
s<=t<=m<char(F_q); for the multiplicity-t root spaces it bounds summed
intersection dimensions by (m-1)s/(t-s+1). Its full proof uses derivative
evaluation ranks and multiplicities of a nonzero Wronskian. Ordinary
agreements here provide neither these derivatives nor the structured folded
evaluations of the alternative construction. No cap is imported.

Gabor Korchmaros, Gabor P. Nagy and Nicola Pace, *k-nets embedded in a
projective plane over a field*, [arXiv:1306.5779v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1306.5779v1),
24 June 2013: Section 2 and Proposition 3.1, pp. 3–4, use plane-curve
pencils to give constant cross-ratio. Proposition 4.1, pp. 5–6, gives
kappa^N=(kappa-1)^N=1 for N=n(n-1). Theorems 4.3/5.2, pp. 8–9,
require characteristic p>3^phi(n(n-1)) for their stated characteristic-zero
conclusions; Theorem 4.4 has a separate gcd condition. Relevant complete
proofs and characteristic branches were read. No plane-net embedding of
the coefficient assignments, or needed numeric hypothesis, is certified
here; p>n alone is insufficient for that citation. This is motivation,
not an imported obstruction.
