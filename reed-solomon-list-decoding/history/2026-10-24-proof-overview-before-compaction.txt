# Reed–Solomon grand list-decoding challenge: argument overview

No complete candidate resolution has been developed. The [source audit](foundations/01-target-and-source-audit.md) records the website target and primary claims. The [pinned list model](foundations/02-pinned-list-model.md) fixes readable ArkLib definitions at an immutable commit; comparison with the unavailable July ABF26 PDF remains incomplete.

## Argument assembled

For closed simultaneous column-Hamming balls, L001 counts the common k-subsets on which a whole polynomial tuple agrees with its center. Distinct tuples cannot share one, giving B_m(t/n)<=floor(binomial(n,k)/binomial(n-t,k)) for 0<=t<=n-k, independently of m. At the next grid point there is an explicit q^m list. L011's monic root-product center attains binomial(n,k) at t=n-k for every m. Thus, when epsilon*q>=1, t_star=n-k exactly when q>=epsilon^(-1)binomial(n,k), including equality. The necessary field condition at this endpoint is exponential at a fixed prize rate; it does not locate the general smaller-field boundary.

The safe real radii form [0,(t_star+1)/n), so t_star/n is a grid maximum and (t_star+1)/n an unsafe real supremum. No attained largest real radius follows. This convention is already explicit in pinned ArkLib; the unavailable ABF comparison remains qualified.
