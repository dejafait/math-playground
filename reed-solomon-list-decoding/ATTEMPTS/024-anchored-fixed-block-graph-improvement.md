attains the exact ordinary cap 10696 in every pair-conflict plus

outside bijection. The requisite anchored forbidden incidence never

does not test nonanchored L033 triples, L031 quadruples, full-neighborhood
admissibility, whole-family conversion, cross-core consistency or an RS

renewed optimization requires a materially different restriction and
