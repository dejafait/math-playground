# Universal exclusion of the selected triple pattern — refuted

WHY IT FAILS: Three disjoint antipodal pairs in the order-1024 subgroup
give a rank-two overlap-quadratic matrix. Its kernel has three nonzero
entries and constructs distinct degree-less-than-64 messages at one
compatible center for every m>=1. The canonical proof and full exact
certificate are in
[L020](../lemmas/L020-triple-support-realizability-and-subgroup-degeneracy.md).
Thus excluding every 68-support triple with a 61-point common core and
two points in each pair-only overlap is false. This does not reject
the determinant restriction, which also forbids explicit configurations,
or a quantitative extremal-family approach. That separate step needs
coverage and a discriminating threshold comparison; the 955–957 bracket
and C001b's stopped nested-floor certificate remain unchanged.
