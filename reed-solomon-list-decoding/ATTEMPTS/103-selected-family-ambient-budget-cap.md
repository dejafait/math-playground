# Direct single-core cap from an ambient configuration budget

WHY IT FAILS: The proposed cap 1532495 for pair-packing seven-subset
families avoiding L020's nonsingular triples is already false by
[L030](../lemmas/L030-all-core-shared-moment-extension.md). Its audited
construction has 2571353 blocks on every core and avoids the entire
counted overlap pattern. L124's smaller ambient budget cannot force
the cap under these assumptions, regardless of completion multiplicity.
This applies ATTEMPTS/021's existing exact-family obstruction, supplies
no new counterexample and does not refute RS safety. Additional RS
restrictions or cross-core compatibility change the hypotheses and
require separate assessment; this historical stop is not a global ban.
