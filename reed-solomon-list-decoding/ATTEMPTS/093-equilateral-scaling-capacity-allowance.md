# Equilateral-only conditional capacity certificate — parked

WHY IT FAILS: [L110](../lemmas/L110-equilateral-scaling-capacity-allowance-failure.md)
uses every actual equilateral third-support class. Its maximum allowance
is (N-2)/89469195005112 at actual distances (5,5,5), between 2.38*10^82
and 2.39*10^82 against required bound one. All 28,215 permitted side
classes were evaluated exactly. This bound is too loose to certify
capacities; it neither proves an actual capacity violation nor rejects
scaling/capacitated primal feasibility. Park this certificate. A future
capacity mechanism needs materially different marginal information and
its own discriminating test. Factor existence, Boolean lower rows,
source lift, PSD and RS realization remain open. ATTEMPTS/082 and
L107–L109 keep their scopes; the 955–957 bracket is unchanged.
